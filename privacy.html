Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Luxura Habitat collects the name, phone number, email address and message you provide when you submit an enquiry. We use this information to respond to your request, arrange property consultations and, where you separately agree, send relevant property communications.

Service providers and sharing

Enquiry forms are processed through Web3Forms. We may share the details necessary to fulfil your specific enquiry with our team and, where relevant, a developer or authorised project representative. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or to meet legal and record-keeping requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent by emailing projectinfoindia@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already completed or processing required by law.
