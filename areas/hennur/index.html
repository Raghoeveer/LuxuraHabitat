The Investment Case

Why Hennur–Thanisandra Doesn't Follow the Market

Most residential micro-markets in Bangalore correlate with broad market cycles — they appreciate fast in bull runs and correct sharply in downturns. Hennur–Thanisandra is one of the few zones that has maintained steady 14–18% annual appreciation through three distinct market headwinds since 2019, because its demand is employment-anchored rather than speculative.

🏢

Manyata Tech Park Anchor

80,000+ employed professionals at one campus. Accenture, IBM, Dell, Cognizant and dozens of Fortune 500 tenants create an unbroken corporate rental demand that insulates the belt from speculative cycles.

🛣️

Outer Ring Road Access

Direct ORR connectivity via the Nagavara junction provides east-west access to Whitefield, Marathahalli, and Electronic City without entering the CBD — making this the best-connected North Bangalore zone for multi-corridor workers.

🚇

Metro Expansion (Nagavara)

The proposed Nagavara Metro station on the Yellow Line will provide mass rapid transit access for the first time, expected to reprice the zone by 15–25% in the 18 months following confirmation.

✈️

Airport Access via Hebbal

The Hebbal flyover and NH-44 provide a direct, uncongested route to KIAL in 35–45 minutes — critical for Manyata's large multinational executive population with frequent international travel needs.

🏫

School Ecosystem

Canadian International School, Ryan International, and multiple premium CBSE schools within the Hennur–Thanisandra catchment make this one of Bangalore's top family destination zones.

📉

Tight Vacancy Rates

Rental vacancy in the Manyata catchment consistently runs at 4–6% — among the lowest in all of Bangalore. Corporate demand from Manyata tenants prevents the oversupply cycles that periodically affect speculative zones.

Market Intelligence

Hennur–Thanisandra Property Prices: 2021–2026

Unlike appreciation-only corridors, Hennur–Thanisandra delivers a blended return: solid capital appreciation (14–18% annually) plus meaningful rental yield (3.5–4.5%) driven by Manyata Tech Park corporate demand.

The Manyata Rental Market

Manyata Tech Park is the defining economic force in this corridor. Its 80,000+ employees generate demand for 2 BHK rentals at ₹22,000–₹36,000/month and 3 BHK at ₹38,000–₹55,000/month — consistently above the North Bangalore average. Projects like Goyal Orchid Salisbury on Thanisandra Main Road are purpose-built for this tenant profile.

At the premium end, Sattva La Vita's 4 BHK row villas in Hennur command ₹75,000–₹1,20,000/month from CXO-level Manyata tenants who require the privacy and space of a villa format — a product that is genuinely scarce in North Bangalore.

Common Questions

Hennur–Thanisandra Real Estate: FAQ


Is Hennur a good area to invest in 2026?

Yes — particularly for buyers looking for a blended return of rental income plus capital appreciation. The Manyata Tech Park anchor creates sustained, employment-backed rental demand that has kept vacancy rates below 6% and prices appreciating at 14–18% annually since 2021. See our full market analysis.

What is the rental yield in Hennur–Thanisandra?

Gross rental yields range from 3.2–4.5% depending on configuration. 2 BHK apartments at ₹1.0–1.2 Cr yield 2.8–3.5%; 3 BHK at ₹1.5–2.0 Cr yield 3.3–4.1%. Row villas like those at Sattva La Vita command executive rents of ₹75,000–₹1,20,000/month. Full data in our rental yield guide.

How close is Orchid Salisbury to Manyata Tech Park?

Goyal Orchid Salisbury on Thanisandra Main Road is approximately 4–6km from Manyata Tech Park — a 10–15 minute drive under normal conditions. This proximity is the primary reason Orchid Salisbury commands consistent corporate rental demand from Manyata's 80,000+ employees.

What is the difference between Orchid Salisbury and Sattva La Vita?

Orchid Salisbury offers high-rise 2 & 3 BHK apartments from ₹1.03 Cr — suited for mid-budget end-users and rental investors. Sattva La Vita offers 4 BHK row house villas at a premium price point — designed for CXO-level buyers and executive tenants. Our comparison guide covers the full decision framework.

What is the Nagavara Metro impact on Hennur property prices?

The proposed Nagavara Metro station on the Yellow Line will be approximately 3–4km from the Thanisandra Main Road corridor. Based on Bangalore's history, metro station confirmation typically triggers 15–25% price appreciation in surrounding areas within 18 months. The station confirmation timeline is expected by 2028.
