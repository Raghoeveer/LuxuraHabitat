The Investment Case

Why Devanahalli is Different From Every Other Bangalore Corridor

Most of Bangalore's growth corridors are driven by IT employment. Devanahalli is unique because its primary demand driver is an international airport — generating NRI demand, aerospace professionals, and long-horizon land investors rather than the typical IT rental profile.

✈️

Airport Anchor

KIAL Terminal 2 expansion will handle 45 million passengers annually, directly generating 40,000+ jobs in the immediate catchment area by 2028.

🛣️

STRR Connectivity

The 280km Satellite Town Ring Road will connect Devanahalli to Whitefield, Sarjapur, and Electronic City without entering central Bangalore — dramatically expanding the buyer pool.

🚇

Namma Metro Expansion

The northward metro extension towards the airport will permanently reprice land along the alignment. Every confirmed metro corridor in Bangalore has delivered 20–28% appreciation within 18 months.

🏭

Aerospace SEZ

The 12,000-acre KIADB Aerospace SEZ adjacent to the airport will create a second employment cluster rivalling Manyata Tech Park in scale by 2030.

🌿

Low Density & Green

Unlike congested IT corridors, Devanahalli still offers large plotted developments, villa communities, and open-space-first design at pricing below comparable Hyderabad or Pune airport zones.

🌐

NRI & HNI Demand

Airport proximity, clear title plotted land, and a proven appreciation track record makes Devanahalli the top destination for NRI and HNI investors in Bangalore real estate.

Market Intelligence

Devanahalli Property Prices: 2022–2026

Appreciation in Devanahalli has been driven by a virtuous cycle of infrastructure announcements, airport expansion milestones, and growing NRI interest. The plotted segment has materially outperformed apartments on capital returns.

The Two Product Markets

Villa Plotted Development — Led by projects like Assetz Palmscape on IVC Road, this segment has compounded at 20–26% annually since 2022. Devanahalli land remains significantly underpriced relative to Hyderabad and Pune airport corridors for equivalent infrastructure quality.

Luxury Apartments — Projects like Sattva Aeropolis and Sattva City (Doddajala) target end-users and rental investors in the aviation and aerospace professional segment. Gross rental yields of 3.2–4.0% are driven by corporate demand from the airport zone.

Infrastructure Roadmap

What's Coming to Devanahalli: 2026–2030

Devanahalli's appreciation cycle is still in its mid-phase. The most transformative infrastructure events are 2–4 years away — the window to invest before full realisation remains open.

2024–2025 (Completed)

KIAL Terminal 2 — Phase 1 Operational

Phase 1 of the expanded T2 terminal is operational, increasing annual passenger capacity by 15 million. Immediate employment creation across aviation and allied services.

2026 (Underway)

STRR — Devanahalli Alignment Tendering

The Satellite Town Ring Road alignment through the Devanahalli-IVC Road corridor is in active tendering. Land acquisition along the alignment is creating near-term appreciation for adjacent properties.

2027 (Planned)

KIADB Aerospace SEZ — Foundation Works

KIADB's 12,000-acre Aerospace SEZ expected to begin infrastructure works. The SEZ will host major aerospace manufacturers, MRO facilities, and defence contractors.

2028–2029 (Expected)

Namma Metro Airport Extension — Station Confirmation

Metro alignment to KIAL expected to have station locations confirmed, triggering the next major repricing event for the corridor.

2030 (Target)

KIAL Terminal 2 — Full 45 MPPA Capacity

Full T2 capacity of 45 million passengers per annum, combined with STRR and metro, will establish Devanahalli as a genuinely integrated urban node — no longer a peripheral suburb.

Existing Connectivity Today

  • ✓NH-44 (Bellary Road): 6-lane highway to Hebbal and city centre. Travel time: 35–50 min under normal traffic.
  • ✓IVC Road: Connects Devanahalli to Hoskote and the Whitefield-ORR belt. Key road for Assetz Palmscape's location.
  • ✓BSRP Suburban Rail: Under active development. Will significantly cut city-to-airport commute times once operational.
  • ✓Nandi Hills Access: Direct access to a premier weekend destination — a strong lifestyle draw for villa buyers.

Common Questions

Devanahalli Real Estate: FAQ


Is Devanahalli a good investment in 2026?

Yes — for buyers with a 5+ year horizon. Devanahalli has compounded at 20–26% annually on villa plots since 2022, driven by airport expansion, the planned STRR, and growing NRI demand. See our full market trends analysis.

Which is better in Devanahalli — a plot or an apartment?

It depends on your goal. Plots (like Assetz Palmscape) offer pure capital appreciation — ideal for NRIs and long-horizon investors. Apartments offer monthly rental yield (3–4%) with more moderate appreciation. See our full ROI comparison.

How far is Devanahalli from Bangalore city centre?

Approximately 35–40km from MG Road via NH-44. Under normal traffic: 45–60 minutes. Once the STRR and metro extension are operational (estimated 2028–2030), this improves further and Whitefield to Devanahalli becomes 35–45 minutes without entering the city.

What are the best projects to buy in Devanahalli in 2026?

Based on our independent verification: Assetz Palmscape for villa plotted investment, Sattva Aeropolis for airport-facing apartment end-use, Sattva City for integrated township living. See our project comparison guide.

Is NRI buying allowed in Devanahalli plotted developments?

Yes. NRIs can purchase approved residential plots through FEMA-compliant channels. The plotted format is actually preferred by NRIs — lower GST, no maintenance of under-construction units, and a cleaner appreciation story. Our advisors guide you through payment channels and registration.

What is the STRR and how does it affect Devanahalli?

The Satellite Town Ring Road is a 280km orbital highway planned at 25–35km from central Bangalore. For Devanahalli, it creates direct road access to Whitefield, Sarjapur and Electronic City without going through the city — making the airport corridor accessible to Bangalore's entire IT workforce. See our infrastructure deep-dive.
