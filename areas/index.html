Yelahanka – North Bangalore

★ Most Active Corridor

Why Yelahanka is Booming

North Bangalore's fastest-growing luxury corridor. Proximity to KIAL airport, the upcoming Namma Metro Blue Line (Phase 2B), and BSRP rail are creating a multi-modal hub attracting every Tier-1 developer simultaneously.

Schools & Green Lifestyle

Home to Canadian International School, Vidyashilp Academy, and Stonehill International. Wide roads, the Jarakabande Kaval forest reserve, and low density make it the top family destination in Bangalore.

Active Premium Projects

We are currently advising buyers on Sattva Lumina, Brigade Eternia, Concorde Mayfair, and Assetz Zen & Sato — four flagship launches across the Yelahanka-Bagalur corridor from ₹1.5 Cr onwards.

Kanakapura Road Corridor

South Bengaluru Premium

Lifestyle & Connectivity

Seamless access to NICE Ring Road and metro connectivity (Namma Metro Green Line, Silk Institute / Konanakunte Cross). Known for expansive green landscapes, premium gated communities, and a rapidly developing social infrastructure with international schools and healthcare facilities.

Proximity to Tech Hubs & Schools

25-minute commute to Electronic City; close to top-tier educational institutions including DPS South, Greenwood High, and proximity to upcoming IT corridors along Mysuru Road.

Infrastructure Trends

Namma Metro Phase 2 extension, NICE Road connectivity upgrades, and several large-format retail developments are set to significantly boost property values over the next 3-5 years.

South Bengaluru Hub

Established Premium

Lifestyle & Connectivity

The beating heart of Bengaluru's premium residential market. Tree-lined avenues, heritage neighbourhoods, and a mature social ecosystem with the finest dining, retail, and cultural destinations within arm's reach.

Proximity to Tech Hubs & Schools

Central to Koramangala, HSR Layout, and BTM tech startup corridors. National Public School, Bishop Cotton's, and Christ University all within a 15-minute radius.

Infrastructure Trends

Namma Metro connectivity expansion, flyover decongestion projects, and peripheral ring road development are elevating accessibility while preserving the area's premium residential character.

Sarjapur Road Corridor

IT Corridor Premium

Lifestyle & Connectivity

Bengaluru's fastest-evolving IT corridor, connecting Outer Ring Road to Electronic City. Thriving with premium apartment complexes, vibrant F&B culture, and a young, aspirational community driving rapid social infrastructure growth.

Proximity to Tech Hubs & Schools

Direct access to Wipro, Infosys, and RMZ Ecoworld campuses. Orbis School, Inventure Academy, and Greenwood High serve families in this rapidly growing micro-market.

Infrastructure Trends

Proposed Signal-Free Corridor, metro Phase 3 feasibility studies, and aggressive commercial development are positioning Sarjapur Road for 15-20% appreciation over the coming decade.

Explore Sarjapur Road Projects

Devanahalli – Airport Corridor

Growth Frontier

Lifestyle & Connectivity

The northern growth frontier anchored by Kempegowda International Airport. Large plotted developments, aerospace SEZ proximity, and a lifestyle oriented towards open spaces and weekend farmhouse communities.

Proximity to Tech Hubs & Schools

Minutes from the KIAL Aerospace SEZ and upcoming IT Investment Region. Canadian International School and Akash International School cater to families in the corridor.

Infrastructure Trends

Airport expansion, Namma Metro Orange Line, Satellite Town Ring Road, and the upcoming Business Park are catalysts for long-term capital appreciation in this high-growth zone.

Explore Devanahalli Corridor

Whitefield – ITPL Corridor

Tech Hub Core

Lifestyle & Connectivity

Bengaluru's original IT hub, now matured into a self-sustaining township with premium malls, multiplexes, hospitals, and a thriving community of tech professionals. The Purple Line metro extension has dramatically improved connectivity.

Proximity to Tech Hubs & Schools

ITPL, Embassy Tech Village, and Prestige Tech Park within 10 minutes. Whitefield Global School, Vydehi School, and Ryan International offer excellent K-12 education options.

Infrastructure Trends

Purple Line metro operational, elevated expressway to KR Puram, and the Peripheral Ring Road project are solving legacy congestion challenges while driving premium property development.

Explore Whitefield Projects

Hennur – Thanisandra Belt

Emerging Premium

Lifestyle & Connectivity

A rapidly urbanising belt in North Bengaluru offering excellent value appreciation. Wide arterial roads, proximity to Manyata Tech Park, and a growing retail ecosystem make this an attractive option for first-home buyers and investors.

Proximity to Tech Hubs & Schools

10 minutes from Manyata Tech Park and Kirloskar Business Park. Ebenezer International School, Harvest International, and Delhi Public School (North) serve the community.

Infrastructure Trends

Hennur Road widening, Outer Ring Road connectivity improvements, and proposed metro extension are key catalysts. Early-stage investors stand to gain significant appreciation as infrastructure matures.
