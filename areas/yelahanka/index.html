Market Overview

Why Yelahanka is Bangalore's Most Strategic Address

A decade ago, Yelahanka was a quiet North Bangalore suburb. Today, it is home to four simultaneous luxury project launches from Brigade, Sattva, Concorde, and Assetz — all backed by the same thesis: a once-in-a-generation infrastructure convergence.

✈️

Airport Proximity

Kempegowda International Airport (KIAL) is a 25-minute commute from Yelahanka. As KIAL doubles capacity with Terminal 2, the radius of premium residential demand around the airport is expanding — and Yelahanka sits squarely within it.

🚇

Metro Blue Line (Phase 2B)

The Namma Metro Blue Line Phase 2B will bring metro connectivity directly into Yelahanka. Historically, every area in Bangalore that gets metro connectivity sees a 20–35% appreciation in property prices within 3 years of the announcement.

🏢

Employment Hubs

Manyata Tech Park (housing 100,000+ employees) is accessible in under 25 minutes. The upcoming KIADB Aerospace Park and the planned IT Investment Region along the Nandi Hills corridor add a second employment axis to the north.

🏫

Elite Schools Cluster

Yelahanka has one of Bangalore's highest concentrations of premium international schools: Canadian International School, Vidyashilp Academy, Stonehill International, and DPS North — all within a 10-minute radius of the key residential zones.

🌿

Green Lifestyle

Unlike the congested southern IT corridors, Yelahanka offers wide 100-foot roads, proximity to the Jarakabande Kaval forest reserve, and a far lower population density — making it the preferred choice for families who won't compromise on quality of life.

📈

Capital Appreciation

The Yelahanka-Bagalur corridor registered a 22–28% increase in residential property values between 2022 and 2024. With the infrastructure runway still incomplete, most analysts project continued appreciation through at least 2028.

The Two Micro-Markets: Yelahanka vs. Yelahanka New Town

It is important to understand that "Yelahanka" broadly refers to two distinct micro-markets. Old Yelahanka is a more established residential neighbourhood with a mix of resale apartments, independent houses, and plots. Yelahanka New Town and the extended Bagalur Main Road Corridor is where all the premium new launches are concentrated.

For luxury buyers, the relevant micro-market is the Bagalur Main Road stretch — which is where Sattva Lumina, Brigade Eternia, Concorde Mayfair, and Assetz Zen & Sato are all positioned. Base prices here range from ₹7,500 to ₹10,000+ per sq.ft depending on the developer brand and the specific sub-location.

What is Driving Price Growth?

Three compounding forces are driving prices in the luxury segment specifically:

  • Limited supply of RERA-approved luxury inventory
  • Strong demand from NRI buyers attracted by the airport corridor
  • Developer confidence reflected in simultaneous Tier-1 launches

Infrastructure Roadmap

The Projects That Will Change Yelahanka Forever

Infrastructure is the single most reliable predictor of long-term property appreciation. Here is what is in the pipeline for Yelahanka's corridor.

2024–2025

Namma Metro Blue Line Phase 2B — Station Confirmed

A dedicated Yelahanka metro station is part of the Phase 2B alignment. This is the single most significant infrastructure catalyst for the corridor — directly comparable to what the Purple Line did for Whitefield prices.

2025

Bengaluru Suburban Rail Project (BSRP) — Devanahalli Line

The BSRP will connect Yelahanka to the heart of the city and to Devanahalli Airport on a dedicated rail corridor, creating a multi-modal transport hub for the north.

2025–2026

Outer Ring Road (ORR) and NH-44 Signal-Free Upgrades

Planned flyovers and signal-free stretches along NH-44 (the Bellary Road) between Hebbal and the airport will further reduce commute times from Yelahanka to the city's tech hubs.

2026–2028

KIAL Terminal 2 Completion

Kempegowda International Airport's Terminal 2 is designed to triple the airport's passenger capacity. This expansion will dramatically increase the airport's employment catchment area, boosting demand for housing in Yelahanka.

2027+

KIADB Aerospace & IT Investment Region

A planned 12,000-acre Aerospace SEZ and ITIR near Devanahalli will eventually house millions of square feet of commercial space, creating a massive long-term employment sink on Yelahanka's doorstep.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Yelahanka a good area to buy property in Bangalore?

Yes — for the specific segment of luxury apartments (₹1.5 Cr and above), Yelahanka is arguably the strongest buy in Bangalore in 2026. The combination of confirmed metro connectivity, airport proximity, and a supply-constrained luxury segment creates ideal conditions for both end-users and investors. However, buyers should be selective about sub-location: the Bagalur Main Road corridor carries a significant premium over Old Yelahanka for good reason.

What is the average price per sq.ft in Yelahanka in 2026?

In the luxury new-launch segment (projects like Assetz Zen & Sato, Brigade Eternia), prices currently range from ₹8,500 to ₹11,500 per sq.ft. The integrated township segment (Sattva Lumina) offers a slightly more accessible entry between ₹7,500 and ₹9,200 psf. Resale apartments in established parts of Yelahanka are available between ₹4,500 and ₹6,500 psf. All figures are indicative; contact us for verified current pricing.

How far is Yelahanka from the airport?

Yelahanka is approximately 18–25 kilometres from Kempegowda International Airport (KIAL), translating to a 20–30 minute drive under normal traffic conditions on the NH-44 (Bellary Road) highway. This makes it the closest established residential zone to the airport in Bangalore — a key driver of NRI and corporate housing demand in the area.

What is the rental yield in Yelahanka for luxury apartments?

Luxury 3 BHK apartments (2,000+ sq.ft) in Yelahanka are currently achieving monthly rents of ₹55,000–₹75,000, translating to a gross rental yield of approximately 4.0–4.5%. This is above the Bangalore average and is largely sustained by demand from senior corporate employees working at Manyata Tech Park, KIAL-related businesses, and international families enrolled at the corridor's premium schools.

Which projects in Yelahanka are RERA approved?

All four projects we currently advise on are fully RERA registered. Assetz Zen & Sato (RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/080525/007728), Brigade Eternia, Sattva Lumina, and Concorde Mayfair have all been independently verified by our team. We always recommend buyers verify RERA registration directly on the Karnataka RERA portal before making any payment.
