Common Questions

Sattva Group Projects: FAQ


Is Sattva Group the same as Salarpuria Sattva?

Yes. Sattva Group is the current name of the Bengaluru developer that began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva.

Which Sattva projects are near Bangalore Airport?

Sattva Aeropolis, Sattva Park Cubix, Sattva City, Sattva Vasanta Skye and Sattva Vasanta Cove are all on the Devanahalli airport corridor.

Which Sattva project has the lowest entry price?

Among the projects we cover, Sattva Lumina in Yelahanka lists homes from ₹38 Lakh, and Phase 1 resales at Sattva Park Cubix start from about ₹34 Lakh.

Does Sattva sell plots?

Yes. Sattva Ekatra off Doddaballapur Main Road, Sattva Green Groves in Nelamangala and Sattva Kaveri Siri in Mandya are plotted developments.
