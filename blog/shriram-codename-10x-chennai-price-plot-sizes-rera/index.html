3. TNRERA and DTCP Status: The Detail Most Listings Skip

This is the section to read most carefully. Shriram Codename 10X is registered under TNRERA number TN/01/LAYOUT/0036/2020. What most aggregator sites will not tell you is that this registration covers Codename 10X as a phase within a larger master layout, the Shriram OneCity / Shriram Earth development promoted by Shriram Properties Limited, rather than as a fully standalone, independently numbered project. We are stating that plainly rather than glossing over it, because a buyer comparing RERA numbers across projects should understand they are looking at a phase-level registration, not a separate one-off project registration issued solely for this specific 9.73-acre parcel.

In practical terms, this is a common and legitimate structure for large master-planned communities that release land in phases, but it does mean you should ask your sales contact to walk you through exactly which survey numbers and plot numbers fall under this specific TNRERA registration before you pay any token amount. The DTCP Layout Approval Number is 04/2020, a separate approval from the state's Directorate of Town and Country Planning confirming the layout itself, road widths, plot subdivisions, and mandatory open space have been sanctioned. Local body approval sits under RC.No 4093/2018/A4, issued by the Valarpuram Panchayat / Sriperumbudur Panchayat Union. All three numbers, TNRERA, DTCP, and the local body reference, are separate approvals that should each be independently verifiable. We would strongly encourage every buyer to check the TNRERA registration directly on rera.tn.gov.in before booking, rather than taking any marketing material, including this one, at face value.

4. Buying a Plot vs. Buying an Apartment: What Actually Changes

If most of your property research has been on apartment projects, a plotted purchase involves a genuinely different set of considerations, and conflating the two is a common and costly mistake.

No fixed floor plan or possession date

An apartment purchase comes with a fixed unit layout and a RERA-declared possession date tied to the builder's own construction schedule. A plotted purchase like Codename 10X has neither. You are buying a registered parcel of land within an approved layout, and construction of your villa is entirely your own project, on your own timeline, with your own architect and contractor. There is no "possession date" in the apartment sense; there is only the date your plot registration completes, after which the land is yours to build on whenever you choose.

Registration and title, not UDS percentage

Apartment buyers spend a lot of attention on Undivided Share (UDS) of land, because in an apartment you own a fractional, undivided share of the total plot alongside every other flat owner. A plotted purchase sidesteps that calculation entirely. You are buying a specific, individually surveyed and demarcated plot with its own boundaries, registered in your name via a standard sale deed, exactly as you would for any independent piece of land. This generally makes title and boundary verification more straightforward than UDS math, but it also means you, not a builder's legal team, are typically responsible for commissioning your own construction-stage approvals (building plan sanction, patta transfer if applicable) once you are ready to build.

No maintenance charges until you build

Apartment owners pay maintenance from possession, whether or not they move in. A vacant plot in a maintained gated layout may carry a modest common-area maintenance charge for roads, drainage, and security, but nothing close to an apartment's ongoing clubhouse-and-lift maintenance bill, until you actually build and occupy your villa.

Construction cost is separate and your own decision

The pricing table above covers land only. Building a villa is a separate cost entirely under your control, meaning your total outlay, and your ability to phase spending over time (buy the plot now, build in two years), is far more flexible than an apartment's fixed, all-in price.

6. Location Snapshot

Valarpuram sits roughly a 5-minute drive from the 8-lane Chennai-Bengaluru Highway (NH4) and the proposed Chennai-Bengaluru Expressway. It is close to the Sriperumbudur Industrial Hub (100+ manufacturing MNCs including Hyundai and Saint-Gobain) and the Oragadam SEZ and SIPCOT Tech Parks (Renault Nissan, Daimler, Royal Enfield). Public transit access includes Thirumazhisai Metro Rail Station and the Kuthambakkam Bus Terminus, both roughly 10 minutes away, with Sevvapet Road Railway Station around 20 minutes out and Chennai International Airport roughly 50 to 60 minutes away via outer ring connections.
