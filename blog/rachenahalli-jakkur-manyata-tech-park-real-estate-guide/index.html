Rachenahalli & Jakkur: Living Behind Manyata Tech Park

By the Luxura Habitat Team•September 12, 2026•10 Min Read

Ask most Bangalore homebuyers where to live near Manyata Tech Park, and the answer defaults to Thanisandra or Hennur Main Road. Rachenahalli, the small lake-side locality directly behind the tech park's rear gates, rarely comes up, despite being closer to the campus than either of those better-known addresses. This guide covers what living in Rachenahalli and neighbouring Jakkur actually looks like: connectivity, schools, price trends, and who this corridor genuinely suits.

1. Where Exactly Is Rachenahalli?

Rachenahalli sits in North Bangalore, wedged between Jakkur and the rear boundary of Manyata Tech Park, along Rachenahalli Main Road (Pin: 560064). It's best understood not as a standalone suburb but as the immediate hinterland of Manyata's Block G/H side, the entrance most tech park employees rarely use because most residential development and retail so far has clustered around the Nagawara/Hebbal side instead. That imbalance is exactly why the corridor is worth a closer look: it offers proximity without the traffic congestion that has built up on the more established approach roads.

2. Connectivity: How Close Is "Behind Manyata," Really?

The honest answer is that Rachenahalli's proximity to Manyata Tech Park is genuinely short in a straight line, roughly 1.5 km to the nearest campus blocks, but the actual drive time depends heavily on which gate you use and what time of day you're travelling. Key connectivity points for the area:

  • Manyata Tech Park (nearest blocks): ~1.5 km
  • Nagawara Metro Station, Blue Line: ~3.4 km
  • Hebbal Flyover (Outer Ring Road / Bellary Road junction): ~8.1 km
  • Kempegowda International Airport (via Bellary Road): approximately 25-30 minutes under normal traffic
  • Karle Town Centre and Kirloskar Business Park: both within a few kilometres, contributing additional employment density to the immediate area

Unlike Thanisandra Main Road, which funnels traffic through some of North Bangalore's more congested junctions during peak hours, Rachenahalli's internal roads are comparatively less trafficked today, largely because there's simply less built-up residential density pulling commuters through them yet. That's a genuine short-term advantage, but it also means the road network itself is less tested at scale; as more residential projects come up in the immediate vicinity, it's worth watching whether internal road capacity keeps pace.

3. Social Infrastructure: Schools, Healthcare & Retail

Rachenahalli and neighbouring Jakkur benefit from spillover social infrastructure built up over the last decade to serve Manyata's workforce and the broader Hebbal-Yelahanka belt:

  • Schools: North Hills International School sits in the immediate neighbourhood; St. George Public School, Vibgyor School and Delhi Public School are all within the wider Jakkur-Thanisandra catchment.
  • Healthcare: Aster CMI Hospital (~5 km), Manipal Hospital Hebbal (~6.2 km) and Bangalore Baptist Hospital are all accessible without crossing into the more congested Hebbal signal-heavy stretch.
  • Retail & Leisure: Phoenix Mall of Asia, Elements Mall and RMZ Galleria Mall all sit within a reasonable drive, giving the area mall-grade retail access despite its comparatively low-key residential profile today.
  • Green Space: Rachenahalli Lake itself is a genuine, walkable neighbourhood amenity, one of the more underrated lake-adjacent addresses in North Bangalore.

4. Price Trends & What You Get for Your Budget

Because Rachenahalli hasn't yet built up the multi-project density of Thanisandra Main Road or Hennur Main Road, per-square-foot pricing here tends to run below those more established addresses for comparable high-rise apartment product. As an example, Meenakshi High Life, currently under construction on Rachenahalli Main Road, prices its 2 BHK apartments (1,096-1,292 sq. ft.) from approximately ₹1.37 Cr, at an average base rate of ₹12,400-12,500 per sq. ft. For comparison, established Thanisandra-side and Hennur-side launches from Tier-1 developers often quote base rates in a comparable or higher band despite being further from the tech park by road. Whether that gap persists as more residential supply reaches this corridor is the key variable for both end-users and investors to track over the next 2-3 years.

5. The Trade-Off: Newer Corridor, Thinner Track Record

The flip side of Rachenahalli's relative affordability is that it doesn't yet have the multi-project resale history that makes valuation straightforward in more established micro-markets. Buyers in Thanisandra or Hennur Main Road can point to several completed, resold projects when benchmarking a new launch's pricing; in Rachenahalli, that comparable data set is thinner because there are fewer large, delivered residential communities to reference yet. This isn't necessarily a red flag, many of Bangalore's now-established corridors looked exactly like this a decade ago, but it does mean buyers should weight developer track record, RERA registration and construction-stage verification more heavily than they might in a corridor with a longer transaction history, simply because there's less market data to lean on otherwise.

6. Jakkur vs Rachenahalli: What's the Difference?

Jakkur and Rachenahalli are frequently used interchangeably in property listings, but they're distinct localities with slightly different characters. Jakkur is the larger, more established of the two, home to Jakkur Aerodrome, Jakkur Lake and a wider spread of independent houses and mid-rise apartments built up over the last 15-20 years, with more retail and small commercial establishments already in place along its main roads. Rachenahalli is smaller and more residential-in-progress, positioned closer to Manyata's rear boundary and centred on its own lake. In practice, most new high-rise launches marketed as "Rachenahalli" or "behind Manyata Tech Park" sit on Rachenahalli Main Road itself, while Jakkur functions as the broader administrative and social-infrastructure anchor for the immediate area, the postal and civic address the corridor shares, and the source of most of its established schools and retail. When evaluating a specific project, it's worth confirming which of the two it actually falls under and how far it sits from each locality's own core, since "Jakkur-Rachenahalli" as a combined label can span a wider area than a single project's own walking-distance claims suggest.

7. Investment Outlook: What Could Change This Corridor's Trajectory

Three factors are worth watching if you're evaluating Rachenahalli or Jakkur from an investment, rather than pure end-use, lens. First, the pace of new residential supply: as more large-format apartment projects complete and register resales, the corridor's comparable-pricing data set will thicken, which typically supports more confident valuation and, historically in Bangalore, tends to accelerate price discovery once a critical mass of delivered inventory exists. Second, metro connectivity: the Nagawara Metro Station on the Blue Line, roughly 3.4 km away, is the corridor's nearest rapid-transit anchor; any future extension or feeder-bus network improving last-mile access from Rachenahalli to that station would meaningfully strengthen the area's connectivity profile beyond its current road-only access. Third, Manyata Tech Park's own expansion plans: as one of Bangalore's largest employment hubs, any announced expansion of blocks facing the Rachenahalli side specifically (as opposed to the Nagawara/Hebbal side, where most existing development has concentrated) would directly reinforce the walk-to-work thesis this corridor is built on. None of these are guaranteed, but they're the concrete signals worth tracking rather than treating the corridor's investment case as static.

8. Who Should Consider Rachenahalli & Jakkur

This corridor makes the most sense for three buyer profiles. IT and tech professionals working within Manyata Tech Park's rear-facing blocks or at Kirloskar Business Park, for whom the short physical distance translates into real time savings on a daily commute. End-users who are price-sensitive relative to Thanisandra and Hennur Main Road and are comfortable trading a slightly less established micro-market for a lower entry price today. And buyers who specifically value proximity to Rachenahalli Lake and a comparatively quieter residential setting over the denser, more built-up feel of the established corridors nearby. Buyers whose top priority is a deep resale comparable history or an extensive existing social infrastructure footprint may still be better served by Thanisandra Main Road or Hennur Main Road for now.

Our Honest Assessment

Rachenahalli and Jakkur represent a genuine, underrated proximity play on Manyata Tech Park, close enough to matter for a daily commute, with real lake-adjacent green space and growing access to mall-grade retail, at pricing that currently undercuts the more established Thanisandra and Hennur Main Road addresses on the other side of the same tech park. The trade-off is a thinner resale track record and a road network that hasn't yet been stress-tested by large-scale residential density the way the established corridors have. For end-users prioritising commute time and current affordability over deep comparable transaction history, this corridor is worth a serious site visit, ideally timed to the actual morning and evening commute windows rather than a quiet midday drive-through, to verify the proximity claim holds up in practice.
