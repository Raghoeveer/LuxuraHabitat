Vajram Vivera: Price, Layout & RERA Review

By the Luxura Habitat Team•July 30, 2026•7 Min Read

Just off the Yelahanka corridor, on Kogilu Main Road, Vajram Vivera takes a different approach to the high-rise, high-density format most of its neighbours use — just 3 towers on 6.2 acres, at a density of 55 units per acre. This is a deep-dive review covering layout, pricing, possession, RERA status, and our honest assessment of its investment merit.

1. The Developer: Vajram Group

Vajram Group (formerly Salarpuria Vajram) is a Bengaluru-based developer known for design-led residential communities across the city. Vajram Vivera is positioned as a boutique, low-density project rather than a mass high-rise launch — a differentiated pitch compared to the larger-scale developments elsewhere on the corridor.

2. Location: Kogilu Main Road, Near Yelahanka

Vajram Vivera is located opposite KRN Complex on Kogilu Main Road (Airport Road, NH-207), North Bengaluru, immediately adjacent to the Yelahanka corridor. Key proximity metrics:

  • RMZ Galleria Office: ~3.5 km
  • Sattva Horizon: ~4.0 km
  • Manyata Tech Park: ~6.4 km
  • National Public School (NPS): ~0.6 km
  • REVA University: ~4.3 km
  • Galleria Mall: ~3.5 km · Mall of Asia: ~5.5 km
  • Sparsh Hospital: ~2.8 km · Regal Hospital: ~4.7 km

3. The Project: What You Actually Get

The project spans 6.2 acres across just 3 towers (2B + G + 17 Floors), an intentionally low-density format — 55 units per acre, well below the density of most competing launches. It's an IGBC Platinum Pre-Certified development, with 85% of the land kept as open green space and 1,000+ native trees. Amenities span 2.5 acres across 2 Grand Clubhouses, including a 4,500 sq.ft co-working space, a learning centre, a swimming pool, gymnasium, biophilic lounge with reflexology path, open-air theatre, community BBQ zone and a dedicated medical clinic — 55+ amenities in total.

Unit Sizes

Vajram Vivera offers 3 & 4 BHK configurations. The 3 BHK units range from 1,573 to 2,406 sq.ft. with 2-side open, cross-ventilated layouts. 4 BHK layouts — named Aura, Breeze and Celeste — are available, with exact carpet area and pricing available on request.

Across the five 3 BHK sizes on offer, pricing works out to roughly ₹14,200–14,700 per sq.ft. — consistent with a boutique, low-density positioning rather than a mass-market launch. For context, that puts Vivera's entry price above Century Kindle's 2 BHK (₹1.18 Cr) but broadly in the same band as Assetz Zen & Sato's 3 BHK entry (₹2.70 Cr), while offering meaningfully lower density than either.

5. Possession Timeline

Vajram Vivera is a new launch, currently under construction, with an estimated possession of Mid 2029 — consistent with a project at this early stage, and subject to RERA-mandated updates as construction progresses.

Our Honest Assessment

Vajram Vivera's clearest point of differentiation is density: at 55 units per acre across just 3 towers, it's positioned well below the scale of most other Yelahanka-corridor launches, and that boutique format — plus IGBC Platinum certification and 2 Grand Clubhouses' worth of amenities — should support stronger long-term privacy and resale appeal for buyers who value that over sheer unit count. The main open item for buyers today is that 4 BHK pricing and exact 4 BHK carpet areas aren't public yet, so that diligence has to happen directly with the sales team. With RERA registration (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/031125/008217) already in place and a Mid-2029 possession, this remains an early-stage, under-construction project — the usual site-visit and construction-progress checks apply.
