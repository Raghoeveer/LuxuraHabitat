Century Kindle: Price, Layout & RERA Review

By the Luxura Habitat Team•July 29, 2026•7 Min Read

Of the five Tier-1 launches currently active on the Yelahanka corridor, Century Kindle is the most accessible on price — and the newest to enter our verified listings. This is a deep-dive review covering layout, pricing, possession, RERA status, and our honest assessment of its investment merit.

1. The Developer: Century Real Estate

Century Real Estate (Century Group) is a Bangalore-based developer with a legacy of 50+ years, a land bank of 3,000+ acres, and a delivered portfolio of 20+ million sq. ft. across the city. That scale and local track record puts it in the same conversation as the other Tier-1 names already active in Yelahanka — Sattva, Brigade, Concorde and Assetz.

2. Location: Yelahanka New Town, Opposite Attur Lake

Century Kindle sits opposite Attur Lake in Yelahanka New Town, with direct frontage on NH-44 (International Airport Road). Key proximity metrics:

  • Kempegowda International Airport: ~15 minutes
  • KIADB Aerospace Park: ~10 minutes
  • Yelahanka Business Park: ~12 minutes
  • STRR (Satellite Town Ring Road): ~5 minutes
  • Hebbal: ~30 minutes
  • Manyata Tech Park: ~35 minutes
  • Schools: Canadian International School, Vidyashilp Academy, Stonehill International School, DPS North

3. The Project: What You Actually Get

Phase 1 spans 15 acres across 14 towers of 18 floors, delivering 450+ apartments in 2, 3 and 4 BHK configurations. Planned amenities include a swimming pool, gymnasium, kids' play area, multipurpose hall, indoor and outdoor sports courts, a jogging track, and landscaped gardens.

Unit Sizes

Three configurations are on offer: a 2 BHK (1,160–1,300 sq. ft.), a 3 BHK (1,760 sq. ft.), and a 4 BHK (2,790 sq. ft. and above). Detailed floor plans for the 2 BHK and both 3 BHK toilet variants are available on the project page; 4 BHK plans are available on request.

For context, that ₹1.18 Cr 2 BHK entry point sits meaningfully below the corridor's other new launches — Assetz Zen & Sato starts at ₹2.70 Cr for a 3 BHK, and Brigade Eternia and Concorde Mayfair are both positioned as 3 & 4 BHK-only developments. Century Kindle is currently the only Yelahanka Tier-1 launch offering a sub-₹1.2 Cr entry point.

5. Possession Timeline

The indicative possession timeline for Phase 1 is October 2028, consistent with the project's pre-launch status and typical RERA-mandated construction schedules for a 14-tower development of this scale.

We encourage all prospective buyers to independently verify this registration number on the official Karnataka RERA website before finalising a booking — a step we recommend regardless of developer reputation.

Our Honest Assessment

Century Kindle's biggest advantage is price positioning: it is the only Tier-1 Yelahanka launch right now with a sub-₹1.2 Cr entry point, which opens the corridor up to a buyer segment the other four projects don't really serve. The NH-44 frontage and Attur Lake-facing location are genuine amenities, and Century's scale as a developer removes most execution-risk concerns. With RERA registration (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/200326/008542) already in place, the main remaining diligence item for buyers is the usual site-visit and construction-progress check that applies to any pre-launch project.
