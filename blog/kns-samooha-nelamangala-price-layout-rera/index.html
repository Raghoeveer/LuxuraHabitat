KNS Samooha: Price, Plot Sizes & RERA Review

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 1, 2026•8 Min Read

Off Tumkur Road, roughly 2 minutes from the Bengaluru-Mysuru Expressway at Nelamangala, KNS Samooha is one of the largest plotted launches this corridor has seen — a 150-170 acre layout with around 2,500 plots, rolled out across three separate phases. This is a deep-dive review covering plot sizes, pricing, layout infrastructure, and RERA status for all three phases, plus our honest assessment of its investment merit.

1. The Developer: KNS Group

KNS Group (KNS Infra) is a Bengaluru-based developer with 18+ years of experience and 35+ completed projects. As with its Mysore Road launch KNS Sampada, Samooha isn't KNS Group's first project in this belt — sister developments KNS Alora, KNS Amora and KNS Ananta are already established nearby in the Kanegowdanhalli micro-market, giving buyers a visible local track record to check rather than taking a first-time launch on faith.

2. Location: Nelamangala, Tumkur Road

KNS Samooha sits in Kanegowdanhalli, off Tumkur Road, West/North-West Bengaluru. Key proximity points:

  • Bengaluru-Mysuru Expressway (Nelamangala): ~2 minutes
  • Kanegowdanhalli State Forest: ~2 minutes
  • Challaghatta Metro Station: ~15 minutes
  • Kengeri Metro Station & Bus Terminal: nearby
  • CHRIST University (Kanegowdanhalli Campus), Tattva School, Don Bosco Institute of Technology, ACS College of Engineering: in the immediate vicinity
  • Rajarajeshwari Hospital, Trust Med Multispecialty Hospital & Diagnostics: nearby for healthcare access
  • Wipro Kawasaki Precision Machinery, Bosch Rexroth, Featherlite Office Systems: established employers on the same corridor

3. The Layout: What You Actually Get

The project spans 150-170 acres with approximately 2,500 exclusive plots ranging from 600 to 2,400 Sq.Ft, released across three phases. The layout plan reserves two Civic Amenity sites (CA-1 & CA-2) and a dedicated green zone with a clubhouse, multi-sport court and children's play area, connected by 9M and 12M wide internal roads — with an existing 15M boundary road being widened to an 18M master-plan road. Every plot is covered by 24/7 gated security with CCTV, and the layout includes its own Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) and overhead water tank (OHT).

Plot Sizes

KNS Samooha's core inventory is four plot dimensions: 30x40 (1,200 Sq.Ft), 30x45 (1,350 Sq.Ft), 30x50 (1,500 Sq.Ft) and 40x60 (2,400 Sq.Ft), with the 30x40 size further split into Premium, Super Premium, Ultra Premium and Exclusive tiers — likely reflecting differences in road frontage, corner positioning, or proximity to the clubhouse and green zone. A smaller number of entry-tier and odd-dimension plots (as compact as 600 Sq.Ft) are also part of the wider layout, which is what the advertised "from ₹46 Lakhs" headline price refers to.

At a base rate of ₹5,750 per Sq.Ft before premiums, KNS Samooha prices in a similar band to other BMRDA-approved layouts on this stretch of Tumkur Road. Worth noting: the widely advertised "from ₹46 Lakhs" headline price applies to the smallest, entry-tier 600 Sq.Ft plots — the bulk of the 1,200 Sq.Ft inventory prices closer to ₹84-94 Lakhs, so don't anchor your budget to the headline figure alone.

5. Project Status: Three Phases

KNS Samooha is being registered and released in three phases rather than as a single layout — each with its own separate RERA registration (see below). As with any multi-phase plotted launch, ask the developer directly which phase a specific plot belongs to, its current on-ground development status (road-laying, landscaping, utility connections) and the registration/khata timeline, rather than assuming completion from marketing renders alone.

6. Legal and RERA Verification

At Luxura Habitat, no project recommendation is made before legal verification. KNS Samooha's three phases each carry a distinct RERA number — don't conflate them when checking a specific plot:

  • Phase 1 RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1250/307/PR/060325/007551
  • Phase 2 RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1250/307/PR/210825/008014
  • Phase 3 RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1250/307/PR/290825/008039
  • Approval: BMRDA-approved layout plan
  • Advertiser/Agent RERA: This listing is marketed by an Authorized Channel Partner / RERA Authorised Advertiser (Karnataka: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/AG/170823/000045)

We encourage all prospective buyers to independently verify the relevant phase's registration number on the official Karnataka RERA website (rera.karnataka.gov.in) before finalising a booking — and to confirm in writing which of the three phases your specific plot falls under, since the phases carry separate approvals and, potentially, separate completion timelines.

Our Honest Assessment

KNS Samooha's clearest strengths are scale and location — at ~2,500 plots across 150-170 acres it's a genuinely large, master-planned layout, sitting 2 minutes from the Bengaluru-Mysuru Expressway and 2 minutes from Kanegowdanhalli's forest cover, with KNS Group's visible local track record from three sister projects already delivered nearby. The trade-off of that scale is the three-phase rollout: verify which phase your plot sits in and its specific RERA number before booking, rather than treating the project as a single monolithic approval. As with any plot purchase, appreciation depends more on your own build timeline and the corridor's continued infrastructure delivery than on the developer alone. If you want a BMRDA-approved plot on a genuinely improving expressway corridor and are comfortable with the plot-ownership tradeoffs (see our plots vs apartments guide), Samooha is worth shortlisting — just confirm the phase-specific RERA number independently and current site development status before booking.
