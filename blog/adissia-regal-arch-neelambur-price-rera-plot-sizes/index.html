Adissia Regal Arch Review: Price, RERA & Plot Sizes

By Narayanan Rajesh•October 4, 2026•14 Min Read

Adissia Regal Arch is a fully gated, British-themed villa plot community from Adissia Developers Pvt. Ltd. in Neelambur, on Coimbatore's eastern edge near the Avinashi Road flyover. It is a DTCP-approved, Tamil Nadu RERA-registered layout of 168 plots on 9.1 acres, and it launched at a pre-launch price from ₹23 Lakh per cent. This is our independent review: what the developer has confirmed, what the numbers actually mean for each plot size, what is still missing at this stage, and who we think the project suits.

If you only need the headline facts, here they are. Everything below is taken from the developer's own project overview and launch material, with our calculations clearly marked.

1. What Adissia Regal Arch Actually Is

The simplest way to describe Regal Arch is as a plotted layout that has borrowed the presentation of a high-end villa project. You are buying an open, DTCP-approved residential plot and you build your own home on it. What makes it different from most plotted layouts in Coimbatore is the common-area design: the renders show a British-style grand entrance arch with arched windows, a crest and lantern lamp posts, formal lawns edged with clipped hedges, and avenues lit by period-style lamp posts. The developer markets it as a "British-themed superior villa plot" community, and the theme carries through to the launch creatives, which use a Big Ben motif.

Underneath the theme, the planning numbers are straightforward. There are 168 plots on 9.1 acres, which is roughly 18 to 19 plots per acre before roads and open spaces are deducted. For context, the same developer's Adissia New Meadows layout in Kovaipudur places 225 plots on 11.25 acres, about 20 per acre, with much smaller plots. Regal Arch is a lower-density layout aimed at buyers who want a full-size villa plot rather than a compact one.

2. Location: Why Neelambur

Neelambur is the point on Coimbatore's eastern side where Avinashi Road, NH-544 and the city bypass come together. That junction is what drives most of the project's location pitch. The developer and listing data give these reference times:

  • Coimbatore International Airport: ~15 mins
  • Kathir College of Arts and Science: ~4 mins
  • GRD-CPF Matriculation Hr. Sec. School: ~5 mins (4.8 km)
  • Dindigul Thalappakatti restaurant: ~5 mins (2.5 km)
  • KPR Tech Park: ~9 mins
  • Sri Rani Complex: ~9 mins (7.9 km)
  • PSG iTech: ~10 mins
  • Thottipalayam bus stop: ~12 mins (10.3 km)

The project's location map also marks PSG College of Arts and Science, PSG Tech, the Sitra area near the airport, Fun Mall, Annapoorna, and Singanallur bus stand and railway station. The developer highlights direct access to NH-544, easy connectivity to Avinashi Road, quick access to Peelamedu and the city's IT corridor, and a position on the Coimbatore to Tiruppur industrial corridor. Sri Sakthi Tech Park is also close by.

Two infrastructure items are worth knowing. On the positive side, the 28 km bypass is set for a 4/6-lane upgrade intended to ease traffic and reduce accidents. On the uncertain side, the announced extension of the G D Naidu flyover from Goldwins is under review and should not be counted on. And on the negative side, traffic congestion and lane safety on the highway stretch near Neelambur are a genuine concern that locals raise, so visit at peak hours before you decide. 4. Pricing: What ₹23 to ₹28 Lakh per Cent Means for You

The developer quotes a pre-launch price from ₹23 Lakh per cent and a budget band of ₹23 to ₹28 Lakh per cent. That converts to roughly ₹5,280 to ₹6,430 per sq ft. Applying that band to each plot size gives these indicative totals (our calculation, before any premiums or statutory charges):

The spread between ₹23 and ₹28 Lakh per cent usually reflects plot position: corner plots, park-facing plots and plots on wider internal roads tend to sit at the top of a band like this, while interior plots sit at the bottom. Because the master plan is not yet published, you cannot yet see which plots fall where, which is one reason to ask for the plot-wise price list rather than relying on a band.

The launch banner compares the pre-launch rate with a "market price" of ₹30 Lakh per cent. That comparison is the developer's own, and we would treat it as marketing until you have checked recent registered values and the government guideline value for Neelambur. For comparison, Adissia New Meadows in Kovaipudur is priced at roughly ₹3,850 to ₹4,370 per sq ft, so Regal Arch carries a clear premium that you are paying for the Avinashi Road location, the larger plot sizes and the themed common areas.

Budget separately for Tamil Nadu stamp duty and registration charges (calculated on the higher of guideline value or sale value), any position premium, legal verification fees, loan processing fees and any layout maintenance charges. Request the official plot-wise price list before you compare options.

5. RERA, DTCP and Legal Checks

Regal Arch is registered with Tamil Nadu RERA under TN/11/Layout/0852/2026, dated 4 March 2026. You may also see it written as TNRERA/11/LO/0852/2026 on portals; both are notations of the same registration. This is a different number from the developer's other Coimbatore layout, Adissia New Meadows, which is registered as TN/11/LO/1533/2026, so do not mix them up when you check the TNRERA portal. The developer also states the layout is DTCP approved.

Our standard checklist before any booking:

  • RERA: confirm on rera.tn.gov.in that TN/11/Layout/0852/2026 is attached to this layout and check the registered completion date.
  • DTCP: get the approval number and the sanctioned layout, then match your plot number, dimensions and road width against it.
  • Title: get a title opinion covering at least 30 years, plus an encumbrance certificate, patta and chitta for the land.
  • Agreement: make sure the booking agreement states the plot number, the all-inclusive price, the payment schedule and the timeline for roads and amenities.

6. Loans and the Buying Timeline

The developer says plot loans are available from 10+ banks and lenders, including HDFC, Axis and LIC, with loan eligibility of around 70%. Plot loans differ from home loans: the loan-to-value ratio is usually lower, tenures are shorter, and lenders typically require a DTCP-approved layout, which this project has. If you plan to build soon, ask lenders about a composite plot-plus-construction loan, which can bring better terms.

The buying timeline is short. Self-funded buyers can complete registration within 15 days of booking; loan buyers register when the bank sanctions and disburses. That is fast for a pre-launch project, and it means you hold registered title to your plot early rather than waiting on construction milestones, which is a real advantage of plotted projects over under-construction apartments.

7. Amenities and the British Theme

The developer markets 20+ lifestyle amenities. The renders we reviewed show a floodlit pickleball court, a fenced box cricket pitch with turf, a formal landscaped park and lawn, a covered pavilion with table tennis and seating next to a kids' play area, a garden pergola with flower-bed landscaping, and the grand entrance arch with lamp-lit avenues. That is a sports-and-garden mix rather than a clubhouse-and-pool package; we did not see a clubhouse or swimming pool in the material, so ask if either matters to you.

One practical point on the theme: a British streetscape only stays coherent if the homes built on the plots fit it. The source material does not say whether the developer will impose elevation or design guidelines. Ask, because the answer affects both your design freedom and how the layout will look in ten years.

8. What Is Still Missing

Every pre-launch project has gaps, and it is better to know them up front. For Regal Arch, the main ones are: the master plan has not been published, so plot positions, road widths and amenity locations are not yet visible; the completion date for layout roads and amenities is not stated in the source material; and while one portal lists April 2026 possession and a ready-to-move status, that does not match the developer's pre-launch positioning, so treat it as unconfirmed. None of these is a red flag on its own, but all three should be answered in writing before you pay a booking amount.

9. Who This Project Suits

Regal Arch fits a buyer who wants a mid-size or large villa plot on the Avinashi Road and airport side of Coimbatore, works in or near the eastern IT parks or the Tiruppur industrial belt, likes the idea of a design-led gated community, and is comfortable buying at pre-launch with the checks above in place. It also suits NRIs from Coimbatore who want a registered land asset close to the airport that they can build on later.

It is a weaker fit for a buyer who needs to build immediately with full infrastructure in place (Adissia New Meadows is ready to construct and cheaper per sq ft), wants a clubhouse and pool, or wants an apartment with rental income from day one. Our Honest Assessment

Adissia Regal Arch gets two big things right: location and plot size. Neelambur's position at the meeting point of Avinashi Road, NH-544 and the bypass gives it access to the airport, two kinds of employment and the city's main growth corridor, and plots of 3.44 to 5.50 cents are genuinely villa-sized in a market where many layouts sell 2 to 3-cent plots. DTCP approval, an active TNRERA registration and a 15-day registration timeline also put it ahead of many unregistered pre-launch layouts.

The trade-offs are price and certainty. At ₹23 to ₹28 Lakh per cent you are paying a clear premium over the developer's own Kovaipudur layout, the master plan and amenity timeline are not yet public, and the highway traffic near Neelambur is a real quality-of-life issue. Our view: it is a strong shortlist candidate for end-users and long-horizon investors on the eastern side of the city, provided you see the master plan, pick your plot position carefully and verify the title before paying.
