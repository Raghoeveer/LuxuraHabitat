Concorde Sienna Hennur: Price, Layout & RERA Deep-Dive

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 4, 2026•8 Min Read

Concorde Sienna is a new pre-launch tower from Concorde Group on Hennur Main Road, Kannuru — the same North Bengaluru corridor that's home to Sattva La Vita and Goyal Orchid Salisbury. Unlike most of its neighbours, Sienna is a single vertical tower rather than a multi-block layout — a design choice that shapes almost everything about how it will look, live, and price. Here's what the numbers and floor plans actually say.

3. Why a Single Tower Changes the Math

Most large layouts in this price band spread units across several 12–16 storey blocks, which lets a developer stage construction and phase amenities. Concorde Sienna instead concentrates roughly 269–270 homes into one ~92-metre tower on a comparatively compact 3.05-acre plot. The upside is density-efficient open space — up to 70% of the plot stays free for landscaping, the sky-deck clubhouse and sports courts, which is a high ratio for a plot this size. The trade-off is that a single-tower format means one shared lift core, one construction timeline with no phase buffer, and a smaller total unit count than most nearby multi-block projects — worth factoring in if liquidity (i.e. how many comparable resale units exist nearby) matters to your exit plan.

4. Amenities Worth Actually Weighing

Beyond the standard clubhouse-and-gym checklist, two amenities stand out on the master plan: a genuine sky-deck infinity pool rather than a ground-level pool, and a dedicated co-working space — a increasingly common but still not universal amenity that matters if you or a tenant works hybrid. The master plan also shows the site backing onto a lake and nala buffer on its western edge, which should keep that side of the tower's views open longer-term, though buyers should independently verify the buffer's exact status with the developer before relying on it as a permanent view guarantee.

5. RERA & Legal Status

Concorde Sienna is registered under Karnataka RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/220626/008740. As a pre-launch project, the exact possession date has not yet been published in the material we reviewed — buyers should request the RERA-registered possession date directly and confirm it against the state RERA portal before booking, standard practice for any pre-launch purchase.

Our Honest Assessment

Concorde Sienna is a straightforward, well-specified single-tower project in a corridor — Hennur Main Road — that already has credible Tier-1 competition from Sattva and Goyal. Its case rests on three things: a genuinely high open-space ratio for its plot size, pre-launch pricing that undercuts what nearby resale/near-completion inventory typically commands, and a sky-deck-plus-co-working amenity set that leans toward working professionals rather than large joint families. It suits a buyer prioritising a compact, well-amenitised 2 or 3 BHK over sheer scale — but because it's a single tower with a comparatively small unit count, it's worth comparing rental and resale liquidity against larger multi-block neighbours like Sattva La Vita before committing at the pre-launch stage.
