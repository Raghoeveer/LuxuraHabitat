Godrej Properties' Q2 FY27 Results: Record Sustenance Sales Even as Profit Dips 62%

By the Luxura Habitat Team•October 31, 2026•7 Min Read

Godrej Properties' board approved its unaudited Q2 and H1 FY27 results on October 23, 2026, with an investor call the same evening. The headline numbers are a genuinely mixed picture: booking value came in at about ₹8,008 crore from 3,658 units across 6.2 million sq ft, down roughly 6% year on year from ₹8,505 crore in the same quarter last year, while net profit fell about 62% to roughly ₹154 crore, according to reporting from Kotak Neo, Investywise, Outlook Business and Upstox covering the filing. The stock fell over 4% in the immediate aftermath. But buried inside that headline is a genuinely strong number: the company's highest-ever quarterly sustenance sales, the revenue from projects it has already launched rather than brand-new bookings, up 83% year on year and 43% quarter on quarter.

1. Two Very Different Stories in One Quarter

Total income for the quarter actually rose 38% to ₹2,575 crore, and collections from customers climbed 31% to ₹5,318 crore, both pointing to a business that is converting sales into cash at a healthy clip. The profit decline sits awkwardly next to those numbers, but it is explained by how real estate accounting works: revenue and profit are recognised against specific projects as construction milestones are completed, a schedule that does not move in lockstep with how much gets sold or collected in any given quarter. A booking-value dip combined with a sharp profit fall in the same quarter that collections and sustenance sales both improved is a reporting-period mismatch more than it is evidence of weakening demand.

2. Why Record Sustenance Sales Is the More Telling Number

A company can post a strong booking-value quarter simply by launching a large new project and front-loading sales into it; that doesn't necessarily say much about ongoing demand for everything it has already sold. Sustenance sales, by contrast, come from buyers choosing to purchase units in projects that were already live, sometimes months or years into construction, with no fresh launch marketing push behind them. An 83% year-on-year jump in that number, on top of the 43% sequential rise, suggests Godrej's existing, already-launched portfolio is converting buyer interest at an accelerating pace, independent of how many new projects it chose to launch this particular quarter.

3. Reading This Against Godrej Plots Coimbatore

None of these figures include Godrej Plots Coimbatore, which remains at the pre-launch stage. Based on the project's own page, it is a 44-acre plotted development opposite the Coimbatore Golf Club in Chettipalayam, South Coimbatore, offering 700-plus plots across four indicative typologies from about 744 to 2,250 sq ft. Plots start from roughly ₹37.99 Lakhs* onwards for a Cottage-typology plot, and Expression of Interest has been open since July 2026, though the official commercial launch is still pending final DTCP and TNRERA approvals, with the registration number yet to be issued.

  • A record sustenance quarter is a reasonable, not conclusive, signal. Strong resale-style demand across Godrej's already-launched portfolio nationally is a healthier underlying indicator than a single launch-driven quarter, but it doesn't substitute for checking Godrej Plots Coimbatore's own TNRERA filing, final layout and price sheet once they're released.
  • The profit dip is a corporate accounting detail, not a demand signal. Buyers evaluating Godrej as a developer shouldn't read the 62% profit fall in isolation; it needs to be read alongside the simultaneous rise in collections, total income and sustenance sales, which point the other way.
  • Pre-launch pricing at Godrej Plots Coimbatore can still move. As with any pre-launch project, the ₹37.99 Lakhs* onwards figure is indicative and subject to change once the official layout, TNRERA registration and commercial launch are confirmed.

The Bottom Line

Godrej Properties' Q2 FY27 result is a case study in why a single headline number rarely tells the full story: booking value and profit both fell, but collections, total income and sustenance sales all rose, with sustenance sales hitting an all-time quarterly high. That is useful context on the developer's underlying demand strength for anyone evaluating Godrej Plots Coimbatore, Godrej's first plotted development in Coimbatore, though the project's own TNRERA status, final layout and pricing still need to be confirmed independently once officially released.
