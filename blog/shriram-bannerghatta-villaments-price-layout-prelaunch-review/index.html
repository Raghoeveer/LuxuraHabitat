Shriram Bannerghatta: Price, Layout & Pre-Launch Review

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published August 29, 2026•12 Min Read

Shriram Properties, better known in Bangalore for large apartment communities, has quietly moved into a different product category on Bannerghatta Road: the villament, a duplex, land-attached home inside a gated 5-acre community. Shriram Bannerghatta is currently at an exclusive pre-launch stage, which means the usual review checklist looks a little different here, no confirmed RERA number, no finalised unit count, no possession date, but the developer has already shared confirmed configurations, tentative pricing and a full master plan. This review walks through everything that is actually known today, is explicit about what isn't, and gives our honest read on whether registering interest now makes sense.

1. What Exactly Is a "Villament"?

Villament, a portmanteau of villa and apartment, is the term Shriram Properties uses for this product, and it's worth defining precisely because it's easy to conflate with either a plotted villa or a regular apartment. A villament is a duplex, low-rise, land-attached home, meaning it has its own private ground-floor entrance, an individual private garden or deck, and internal stairs connecting two full levels, but it sits within a single, unified gated community with shared amenities, security and maintenance, rather than being sold as an independent plot where the buyer manages construction. The developer's own positioning captures this directly: "Experience the privacy and spaciousness of a villa with the convenience of a thoughtfully planned community."

In practical terms, this puts Shriram Bannerghatta in a different competitive set from both a typical high-rise apartment tower (no private garden, shared common walls, elevator-dependent access) and a raw villa plot (no shared amenities, buyer bears full construction risk and timeline). For a buyer who has outgrown an apartment but doesn't want the effort of building or maintaining a stand-alone villa, that middle position is the entire value proposition.

2. Location: Bannerghatta Road, South Bengaluru

The project sits on the Bannerghatta Road corridor in South Bengaluru. Per the developer's own location graphic, the immediate surroundings include RV Institute of Technology, KS School of Engineering & Technology and Holiday Village Resort, with the DMart on Kanakapura Road and a second DMart near Hulimavu both shown as nearby retail anchors. The pin itself sits closest to Doddakammanahalli, with Hulimavu, Akshayanagar, Anjanapura and Konanakunte all visible as surrounding localities, and Begur Fort and the HRBR Layout stretch further to the east. Bannerghatta Road is one of South Bengaluru's rapidly developing corridors, and its position also puts it within a reasonable distance of the neighbouring Kanakapura Road belt, where established healthcare anchors like Fortis Hospital Bannerghatta already serve residents; see our Bannerghatta Road investment guide for a deeper look at the corridor's growth story.

One honest caveat: the developer's own location graphic does not label exact drive-time distances to any of these landmarks, unlike some of the other pre-launch material we've reviewed for nearby projects. We've described what's genuinely shown on the map rather than estimating commute times we can't verify, and recommend confirming exact distances directly with the sales team or via your own maps app before treating any specific landmark as a firm commute-time anchor.

3. The Master Plan: A 5-Acre, Cluster-Based Layout

Shriram Bannerghatta spans 5 acres, organised into multiple villament clusters arranged around a central clubhouse and swimming pool, with perimeter gardens and a children's play area positioned along the community's edges. The developer's own master plan graphic shows several distinct cluster groupings of varying sizes wrapped around internal roads leading from a single main entrance, a layout designed to give each cluster a degree of separation and privacy while keeping the shared clubhouse, pool and multi-purpose court centrally accessible to every resident.

What the master plan does not yet confirm is a final, official total unit count. The graphic shows individual unit numbers within each cluster, but since this is explicitly labelled "coming soon" pre-launch material, we're treating those as indicative cluster sizes rather than a confirmed final unit count, and recommend asking the sales team directly for the finalised total before booking.

4. Confirmed Configurations and Floor Plans

Two duplex configurations are confirmed, both following the same broad ground-floor-plus-upper-level layout logic:

  • 3 BHK Duplex Villament (2,000 sq. ft.): Ground floor includes a master bedroom with attached bath, dining area, modern kitchen, a guest bedroom, powder room, entrance foyer and a large private deck opening onto the garden, plus private parking. The upper level adds two further bedrooms (including a second ensuite), a family lounge, a walk-in wardrobe and a balcony.
  • 4 BHK Duplex Villament (3,000 sq. ft.): Follows the same ground-floor logic as the 3 BHK but adds a dedicated staff room. The upper level carries a fourth bedroom (also ensuite) alongside the same family lounge, walk-in wardrobe and balcony arrangement.

A Simplex option is also mentioned by the developer as available on request, though no floor plan or price has been disclosed for it in the material we've reviewed; if a single-level layout matters to you, ask the sales team directly for details before assuming it matches either duplex plan.

Working out a rough per-square-foot rate from these figures puts the 3 BHK at roughly Rs 15,000-16,500/sq. ft. and the 4 BHK at roughly Rs 12,700-14,200/sq. ft., both broadly consistent with premium low-rise, land-attached product on a growth corridor in South Bengaluru, though the range is wide enough (and explicitly tentative) that we'd treat it as directional rather than something to build a firm budget around just yet. Note too that both figures are described by the developer as tentative and subject to change, which is standard for a pre-launch project but does mean a buyer registering interest today should not treat either number as locked in.

On financing, a purchase in the Rs 3-4.25 Cr band typically sits in a segment where lenders expect a larger down payment proportion and closer income documentation than a mass-market apartment purchase, so buyers should start conversations with their bank or a home-loan advisor early, even at the pre-launch, registration stage, so financing isn't a bottleneck once final pricing and booking terms are released.

6. Amenities: The Mind, Body, Soul Framework

The developer's own pre-launch material groups amenities under a "Mind, Body, Soul" theme rather than a flat checklist. Mind covers landscaped spaces, community zones and indoor recreation and social areas; Body covers a fitness centre, swimming pool, sports facilities and jogging or wellness spaces; and Soul covers green landscapes, leisure zones, kids' spaces, relaxation areas and community lawns. In practice, this maps to a fairly standard premium-community amenity set, a clubhouse and pool, a multi-purpose sports court, a children's play zone and individual unit gardens layered on top of shared perimeter gardens, but organised thematically rather than as a bare list, which is consistent with how the developer is positioning the whole project around lifestyle and wellness rather than just square footage.

7. RERA and Legal Status: What's Genuinely Pending

This is the section we'd urge every prospective buyer to read carefully. Shriram Bannerghatta has no RERA registration number yet; the developer has explicitly described the project as being at the pre-launch, registration-in-progress stage. That also means there is no confirmed possession date, no finalised total unit count, and no tower or floor count disclosed in the material we've reviewed. We have not invented or estimated any of these figures for this review, and any source that quotes a specific RERA number, possession date or unit count for this project today should be treated with caution until it's independently verifiable on the Karnataka RERA portal.

None of this makes the project untrustworthy, pre-launch registration of interest ahead of a formal RERA-backed launch is a common and legal practice, but it does mean the risk profile is meaningfully different from booking into an already-RERA-registered, under-construction project. Registering interest now can offer early access to unit selection and pricing before wider release; it should not be treated as equivalent to a booking with the legal protections a RERA registration provides.

8. Who This Project Actually Suits

Shriram Bannerghatta is a strong fit for a buyer who specifically wants villa-style privacy, a private entrance, garden or deck and a duplex layout, but doesn't want the construction risk, land-title diligence or ongoing maintenance overhead of a stand-alone villa plot, and is comfortable with the higher price band that comes with a large-format, low-density product on Bannerghatta Road. The 3 BHK at 2,000 sq. ft. suits a family wanting extra space without moving to the largest layout; the 4 BHK at 3,000 sq. ft. with its fourth ensuite bedroom and staff room suits a larger household or a buyer who values a dedicated staff space.

It's a weaker fit for a buyer who needs a firm possession date to plan around, since none has been announced, or for someone who wants the legal certainty of an already-RERA-registered project before committing any funds or serious time to a decision. It's also a poor fit for someone assuming this is a budget or mid-market purchase; at Rs 3-4.25 Cr tentative, this sits firmly in the premium segment.

Our Honest Assessment

What stands out about Shriram Bannerghatta is the product itself: genuine duplex, land-attached villaments with private decks and gardens are a distinctly different offering from the high-rise apartment towers that dominate most Bangalore growth corridors, and the confirmed 3 BHK and 4 BHK layouts, plus the Mind-Body-Soul amenity framing, suggest a project built around a specific, differentiated lifestyle pitch rather than a generic checklist. The 5-acre scale is also a genuine point in its favour: large enough for a proper clubhouse, pool and multiple amenity zones, small enough to stay low-density and exclusive rather than sprawling.

The honest trade-off is that this genuinely is an early-stage registration, not a booking with the usual RERA-backed protections, and every buyer should weigh that explicitly: no RERA number yet, no possession date, no finalised total unit count, and tentative pricing that is explicitly subject to change. If the villament format and Bannerghatta Road location are a strong fit for what you're looking for, registering interest now is a reasonable way to get early access to pricing and unit selection, provided you go in with clear eyes about what "pre-launch" actually means, and independently verify the RERA registration on the Karnataka RERA portal the moment it's issued before making any financial commitment. As always, enquire directly for the latest confirmed details before booking.
