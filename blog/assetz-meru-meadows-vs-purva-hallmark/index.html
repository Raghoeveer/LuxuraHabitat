Assetz Meru & Meadows vs Purva Hallmark: Which Fits You?

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 13, 2026•7 Min Read

Two of the newest launches on the Kanakapura Road corridor happen to land at almost exactly the same price for almost exactly the same unit size: Assetz Meru & Meadows' entry 3 BHK (1,897 sq ft, from ₹2.74 Cr) and Purva Hallmark's compact 3 BHK (1,900–1,950 sq ft, from ₹2.76 Cr) are within a couple of lakhs of each other on a per-unit basis. That makes this a genuinely useful head-to-head for anyone shopping this exact price band on this exact stretch of road — not a comparison we're forcing for the sake of having one.

The Core Trade-Off: Scale vs. Boutique Density

Once you look past the price match, the two projects are built on almost opposite philosophies:

  • Assetz Meru & Meadows is a larger-scale high-rise: 468+ residences across two towers on ~5 acres, with 87% open space and a ~31,000 sq ft clubhouse. It's a bigger community with a correspondingly bigger amenity program — 30+ amenities including tennis, padel tennis, pickleball, a pet park and co-working space.
  • Purva Hallmark (also marketed as Purva Diamond Vajarahalli) is a boutique, low-density pre-launch: approximately 250 residences on ~3.6 acres, positioned around privacy features like only 4 homes per floor and twin lift cores per tower. It's a smaller, more exclusive community by design.

Neither approach is objectively "better" — it depends on whether you value a larger amenity-rich community or a smaller, more private one at the same price point.

Developer Track Record: Assetz Property Group vs Puravankara Limited

Since both projects sit in the same price band, developer track record is one of the more decision-relevant differences between them. Assetz Property Group is described in its own project materials as an established Bangalore-headquartered developer with a portfolio of residential and commercial developments across the city, known for contemporary architecture and quality construction — but as a privately held company, it doesn't publish the kind of standardised, publicly-verifiable financial disclosures a listed company does.

Puravankara Limited, by contrast, is a listed company: founded in 1975 by Ravi Puravankara, headquartered in Bengaluru, and listed on both the NSE and BSE since 2007 under the ticker PURVA. It operates through three brands — Puravankara (luxury), Provident Housing (mid-segment) and Purva Land (plotted developments) — with projects across Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi, Mumbai, Coimbatore, Pune, Mangaluru and Goa, plus an international presence in Sri Lanka, Dubai and the UAE. In FY2024, the group reported annual sales bookings of ₹5,914 Crore, a 90% year-on-year increase — figures that are publicly disclosed and auditable because of its listed status, a genuine structural advantage over an unlisted developer when it comes to independently verifying financial health before committing ₹2.7+ Cr.

That said, listed status isn't the same as project-completion track record on this specific site: Purva Hallmark is pre-launch, so there's no delivered inventory at Vajarahalli yet to inspect, while Meru & Meadows is already under construction with a fixed, RERA-registered possession date. Weigh corporate transparency against construction-stage certainty according to what matters more to you.

Price Per Square Foot: A Closer Look

Because the two entry-level 3 BHKs land at almost the same total price but slightly different areas, it's worth comparing them on a per-square-foot basis rather than sticker price alone. Assetz Meru & Meadows' 1,897 sq ft unit at ₹2.74 Cr works out to approximately ₹14,444 per sq ft on super built-up area. Purva Hallmark's entry 3 BHK, at roughly 1,900–1,950 sq ft for ~₹2.76 Cr, works out to approximately ₹14,150–14,530 per sq ft depending on where in that size range the final unit lands — effectively the same rate. That confirms the two projects are genuinely comparable on a like-for-like basis rather than one carrying a hidden premium over the other once size is adjusted for; the real differentiators are density, amenities, construction stage and metro proximity, not price efficiency.

Amenity Program: Built vs Planned

The two projects' amenity lists reflect very different project stages, and reading them side by side is informative in its own right. Assetz Meru & Meadows' 30+ amenities are attached to a project already under construction with a fixed clubhouse footprint (~31,000 sq ft) and a sports-heavy lineup: infinity pool, kids' pool, gym, spa, yoga room, tennis, padel tennis, pickleball, badminton, a dedicated pet park, amphitheatre, co-working space and café among others.

Purva Hallmark's amenities, as a pre-launch project, are described by the developer as "planned" rather than built: a multi-level clubhouse with indoor games, co-working spaces and event halls, a temperature-controlled swimming pool, gymnasium, badminton and basketball courts, jogging and cycling tracks, 24/7 three-tier security with video door phones, vehicle-free pedestrian zones, EV charging points, solar lighting and rainwater harvesting. Notice the emphasis: Purva Hallmark's list leans more toward security and sustainability infrastructure (EV charging, solar lighting, rainwater harvesting, three-tier security) than sports variety, while Meru & Meadows leans more toward recreational breadth (padel tennis, pickleball, a dedicated pet park). Neither list is inherently better; they reflect two different amenity philosophies, and only one of the two projects has these amenities built and inspectable today.

Registered vs Pre-Launch: What That Means for Your Booking

This is the single most consequential structural difference between the two projects, and it's worth spelling out plainly rather than leaving it as a line item in a table. Assetz Meru & Meadows carries an active RERA registration (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/130625/007828), which means the project has cleared the regulatory disclosures Karnataka RERA requires before a developer can legally accept bookings or advertise the project for sale — the promoter's registered land title, the approved layout, the escrow arrangement for buyer payments, and a committed possession date are all part of that registration.

Purva Hallmark, by its own developer disclosure, does not yet have a RERA number: registration is "in process," and under RERA rules a project cannot be formally booked or legally advertised for sale until that registration is granted. Any pre-launch "booking" at this stage is typically structured as an expression of interest or a refundable token, not a binding sale agreement, precisely because the legal framework that protects a buyer's payment doesn't fully apply until registration completes. That's not a reason to rule Purva Hallmark out on its own — plenty of legitimate projects go through a pre-launch phase before registering — but it does mean the diligence burden on a buyer is meaningfully higher at this stage: confirm the registration has actually gone through, with paperwork in your own name specifically, before any payment beyond a clearly-marked refundable token moves. The two projects' possession dates — November 2030 for Meru & Meadows versus an indicative December 2031 for Purva Hallmark — are also roughly 13 months apart, which is worth factoring into your own timeline whether you're buying to live in or planning to sell/rent shortly after handover.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Assetz Meru & Meadows if...

You want a larger community with a fuller amenity program (30+ amenities, dedicated pet park, co-working space), you're comfortable with a project that's already under construction with a confirmed 2030 possession date, and you'd rather buy from a project where pricing is already established rather than a pre-launch stage where numbers can still shift.

Choose Purva Hallmark if...

Walk-to-metro convenience (~280m to Thalaghattapura Metro) is a priority, you prefer a smaller, more private low-density community (~250 homes vs 468+), and you're comfortable with pre-launch-stage pricing and the additional diligence that involves — including confirming RERA status before committing, since pre-launch projects often begin marketing before full registration is finalised.

Our Honest Assessment

This is one of the closer price-and-size matchups we've seen on the Kanakapura Road corridor, which makes it a genuinely useful comparison rather than a forced one. If we had to generalise: Assetz Meru & Meadows is the safer pick for buyers who want more amenities and a project already past the pre-launch pricing-uncertainty stage, while Purva Hallmark's metro proximity and lower density will appeal more to buyers prioritising exclusivity and walk-to-transit convenience over amenity scale. Either way, verify current RERA status and get a written, dated price quote before booking with either developer — pricing on both projects, especially Hallmark's pre-launch figures, is subject to change.
