Devanahalli ROI Explained: Land Appreciation vs Rental Yield

By Narayanan Rajesh•July 23, 2026•7 Min Read

In the Devanahalli real estate market, investors constantly debate between two distinct return models: land appreciation through plotted development, and rental yield through apartment ownership. This is not a trivial distinction — the two strategies suit entirely different risk profiles and investment horizons.

1. Understanding the Two Strategies

Strategy A: Land Appreciation (Plotted Development)

Buying a pre-approved plot in a gated community like Assetz Palmscape means you are acquiring land rather than a built apartment. The return model is almost entirely capital appreciation — land in Devanahalli has compounded at 20–26% annually since 2022, well above any risk-adjusted alternative.

The key advantages are:

  • No construction risk: You buy land; the developer delivers the infrastructure
  • Flexibility: Build immediately, hold as-is, or sell the plot post-development
  • Lower GST burden: Approved plots attract significantly lower GST than under-construction apartments
  • NRI preference: Plots are the preferred vehicle for NRIs looking to repatriate funds into Bangalore real estate

The downside: no rental income during the holding period. The return is entirely backend-loaded.

Strategy B: Rental Yield (Apartment Ownership)

Devanahalli apartments — particularly those in integrated township projects near the airport — generate 3.2–4.0% gross rental yields, driven by corporate tenants from the aviation, aerospace, and logistics sectors. Projects like Sattva Aeropolis are specifically designed with this tenant profile in mind.

3. The Tax Dimension

For resident Indians, rental income is taxed as income, reducing the net yield by 20–30% depending on the tax bracket. For NRIs, TDS rules apply at 30% on rental income, making the post-tax yield on apartments considerably less attractive. This is why NRIs almost universally prefer the plotted development model in Devanahalli — a clean, tax-efficient capital appreciation play.

4. The Hybrid Strategy: What Experienced Investors Are Doing

The most sophisticated buyers in Devanahalli right now are pursuing a hybrid — purchasing a plot in a development like Assetz Palmscape while simultaneously holding a 2 BHK apartment in an airport-adjacent integrated township for monthly cash flow. The plot provides the long-term appreciation engine; the apartment provides the carry income that offsets holding costs.

Which is Right for You?

There is no universal answer. If your primary goal is monthly income and you have a 3–5 year horizon, the apartment route with a strong corporate rental catchment makes sense. If you have a 7+ year horizon, are an NRI or HNI looking to preserve and grow capital, and can absorb a zero-income holding period, the plotted development route in Devanahalli is one of the highest-conviction buys available in Indian real estate today.
