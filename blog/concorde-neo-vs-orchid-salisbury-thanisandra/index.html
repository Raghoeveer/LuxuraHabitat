Concorde Neo vs Orchid Salisbury: Which Thanisandra Project Fits You?

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 7, 2026•7 Min Read

Two of the most closely watched pre-launch and ongoing projects on Thanisandra Main Road now sit almost directly opposite each other in positioning: Concorde Neo (a new 6-tower pre-launch by Concorde Group) and Goyal Orchid Salisbury (an established 7-tower community from Goyal & Co). Both sell the same 2 & 3 BHK product on the same road to a similar buyer — which makes this one of the more genuinely useful head-to-head comparisons in the corridor.

2. Concorde Neo: The Sky-Deck Newcomer

Concorde Neo is the newer of the two, still in pre-launch with an expected possession window of December 2028 to October 2029. Its headline differentiator is the Sky Evolve Clubhouse — a ~7,000 sq ft clubhouse positioned on the 17th and 18th floors rather than at ground level, paired with a dedicated co-working space and business lounge. With ~78% open space across 6 towers on 4.02 acres, it's built around a slightly lower density than a typical 7-tower layout of comparable scale.

Best for: Buyers who want to get in at the earliest pre-launch pricing, prioritise a sky-deck clubhouse and co-working amenities, and are comfortable with a possession timeline roughly 1–2 years further out than an active project.

3. Orchid Salisbury: The Established Scale Play

Goyal Orchid Salisbury is the larger, more established of the two — 660+ apartments across 7 towers, already under construction rather than pre-launch. The Goyal & Co pedigree matters here: they're a Tier-1 Bangalore developer with a track record of on-time delivery, and the project's phased possession schedule (early units from Mid-2027, final handover by December 2028) means some buyers could move in well before Concorde Neo even breaks ground on possession.

Best for: Buyers who want a shorter time-to-possession, a larger existing community with more resale/rental comparables already in the market, and the reassurance of construction progress they can inspect today.

Our Honest Assessment

This isn't a case of one project being objectively better — it's a trade-off between price-at-entry and time-to-possession. Concorde Neo's pre-launch pricing and sky-deck clubhouse make a strong case for buyers willing to wait, while Orchid Salisbury's active construction status and larger, more liquid unit base suit buyers who want certainty sooner. Both sit on the same RERA sub-jurisdiction (Karnataka RERA zone code 1251/309) and the same road, so the deciding factor for most buyers will come down to timeline tolerance rather than location or developer credibility — both are legitimate Tier-1 options.
