Manyata Tech Park Effect: How Rental Yields Are Holding Strong in Hennur–Thanisandra

By Narayanan Rajesh•July 23, 2026•7 Min Read

Manyata Tech Park, located at the Nagavara junction in North Bangalore, is the single most powerful rental income driver in the Hennur–Thanisandra real estate belt. With 80,000+ employees and a campus spanning over 165 acres, Manyata generates more consistent, sustainable rental demand than any other single employer in Bangalore — including the combined effect of multiple smaller IT parks in the Whitefield and Electronic City corridors.

1. Understanding the Manyata Tenant Profile

The Manyata tenant is typically a senior or mid-level IT professional earning ₹12–35 LPA. They have specific housing requirements:

  • 2 BHK: Young professionals or couples seeking 900–1,200 sqft, within a 10–15 minute commute from the park
  • 3 BHK: Managers and senior engineers with families, seeking 1,400–1,800 sqft in gated communities with school proximity
  • Budget: Willing to pay ₹22,000–₹55,000/month in rent depending on configuration and amenities

This is the tenant profile that Goyal Orchid Salisbury on Thanisandra Main Road is purpose-built to serve — high-rise gated community living with premium amenities, directly in the Manyata catchment zone.

Hennur–Thanisandra consistently achieves the tightest vacancy rates in North Bangalore. The combination of Manyata Tech Park's employment density, Hebbal's connectivity, and the relative scarcity of new supply keeps occupancy above 93% year-round.

4. The Row House Premium: Sattva La Vita

Sattva La Vita offers 4 BHK row house villas in Hennur — a product category that commands a meaningful premium over apartments for rental income. Senior executives, startup founders, and CXO-level professionals actively seek row house accommodation in Bangalore, particularly in gated communities by Tier-1 developers. Monthly rents for 4 BHK row houses in Hennur currently range ₹75,000–₹1,20,000 — significantly outpacing the per-sqft yield of apartments.

The Investment Case

If your primary objective is reliable, corporate-backed rental income in Bangalore, Hennur–Thanisandra is arguably the most defensible micro-market in the city. The combination of Manyata's employment anchor, tight vacancy rates, and a proven track record of 14–18% annual capital appreciation since 2021 creates a genuinely compelling blended return profile.
