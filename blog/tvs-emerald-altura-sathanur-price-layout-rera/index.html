TVS Emerald Altura: Price, Layout & RERA Review

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 12, 2026•7 Min Read

TVS Emerald Altura is one of the newer high-rise launches on the Bagalur Main Road corridor in Sathanur, North Bangalore, and it has generated an unusually sharp spike in buyer interest since its price list went public. This is a deep-dive review covering layout, pricing, possession, RERA status, and our honest assessment of its investment merit.

1. The Developer: TVS Emerald

TVS Emerald Housing & Estates Pvt. Ltd. is the residential real estate arm of the TVS Group, a name with decades of manufacturing and engineering credibility behind it. Altura is TVS Emerald's high-rise entry into the Sathanur micro-market, positioned as a Japandi-inspired community with a scale (975 residences) that's meaningfully larger than most boutique projects we cover in the North Bangalore corridor.

2. Location: Sathanur, Bagalur Main Road

TVS Emerald Altura sits in Sathanur, on the Bagalur Main Road corridor of North Bangalore. The project's own positioning leans heavily on this corridor's proximity to established and emerging employment hubs:

  • Thanisandra Main Road and Bagalur Main Road: direct corridor access
  • Hebbal and Outer Ring Road: within reach
  • Manyata Tech Park and Hebbal business district: key workplace hub
  • KIADB Aerospace Park: growing airport-linked employment corridor
  • Kempegowda International Airport: approximately 20 minutes by road
  • Thanisandra and Nagawara commercial catchments: nearby retail and daily convenience

3. The Project: What You Actually Get

The project spans 10.06 acres and holds 975 residences across 12 high-rise towers built to a 2B+G+12 format, with more than 70% of the land kept as open, landscaped space. Amenities are organised around dual clubhouses, a swimming pool, work-friendly community spaces, 100% DG power backup, and 24/7 gated security. The overall design language is Japandi-inspired, a departure from the more conventional amenity-heavy branding used by several nearby projects.

Unit Sizes

TVS Emerald Altura offers four distinct configurations. 2 BHK Classic units run approximately 1,131 sq.ft.; 2 BHK Premium units run approximately 1,223 sq.ft. 3 BHK units span approximately 1,510 to 1,588 sq.ft., with a larger 3 BHK Luxe configuration at approximately 2,123 sq.ft. for buyers who want more room without moving to a 4 BHK price bracket.

Altura's ₹1.4 Cr entry point puts it toward the accessible end of new high-rise launches on this side of North Bangalore, and it's a meaningful part of why search interest in the project has climbed so sharply since launch benefits went live. As always, we'd treat a published starting price as a floor to verify rather than a number to book against directly, since it typically applies to a specific floor range and facing rather than the whole 2 BHK Classic inventory.

5. Possession Timeline

TVS Emerald Altura is a new launch, currently in the RERA-linked delivery schedule with phase-wise progress updates as construction proceeds. The developer has not published a specific possession date on record at the time of writing; we'd recommend requesting the current construction-linked payment schedule directly, and cross-checking the delivery timeline against the RERA registration before booking.

At Luxura Habitat, no project recommendation is made before legal verification. For TVS Emerald Altura:

  • Project RERA Number: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/040426/008572
  • Status: RERA-registered, new launch
  • Advertiser/Agent RERA: This listing is marketed by an Authorized Marketing Partner of TVS Emerald / RERA Authorised Advertiser (Karnataka: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/AG/170823/000045)

We encourage all prospective buyers to independently verify this registration number on the official Karnataka RERA website before finalising a booking, a step we recommend regardless of developer reputation or brand recognition.

Our Honest Assessment

TVS Emerald Altura's strongest case is scale plus price: 975 units across 12 towers give it a level of amenity infrastructure that smaller boutique projects on this corridor can't match, and a ₹1.4 Cr entry point keeps the 2 BHK Classic configuration within reach for a wider set of North Bangalore buyers than most comparable launches nearby. The trade-off of that scale is density; buyers who prioritise a low-unit-count, boutique feel should look elsewhere on the same corridor. As a fresh launch without a published possession date, the usual diligence applies in full: confirm the construction-linked payment schedule directly with the developer and independently verify the RERA registration (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/040426/008572) before booking.
