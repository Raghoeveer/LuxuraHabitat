Aparna Wonder Woods: Price, Layout & RERA Review

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 1, 2026•7 Min Read

Off Kanakapura Road at Mallasandra, Aparna Wonder Woods is a 14.9-acre, 1,261-unit high-rise township — one of the largest single launches this corridor has seen. This is a deep-dive review covering configurations, pricing, construction technology, amenities and RERA status, plus our honest assessment of its investment merit.

1. The Developer: Aparna Constructions

Aparna Constructions is a Tier-1 developer with 29+ years of experience in residential construction. Wonder Woods is the company's large-format entry into the Kanakapura Road corridor, built using advanced Shear Wall / Mivan construction technology — a system generally associated with faster build cycles and tighter dimensional precision than conventional construction.

2. Location: Mallasandra, Off Kanakapura Road

The project sits at Mallasandra, off Kanakapura Road, in South Bengaluru — on the same broader corridor as Anjanapura and JP Nagar 9th Phase, within reach of the NICE Road network and the established social infrastructure of JP Nagar and Banashankari. The site also sits close to Turahalli Reserve Forest, giving the township a genuinely green, lower-density setting relative to inner South Bangalore.

3. The Project: What You Actually Get

Wonder Woods spans 14.9 acres with 1,261 apartments across 6 towers. Every home is designed to be 100% Vaastu compliant with no common walls — a combination of scale and privacy-first planning that's uncommon at this unit count. The township is anchored by a grand 49,663 Sq.Ft multi-level clubhouse and 40+ lifestyle amenities, including a swimming pool, gymnasium, tennis, badminton and squash courts, cricket practice nets, a jogging and skating track, a business lounge, preview theatre, library, party lawn, creche and pet park. On the sustainability side, the project is IGBC Gold Pre-Certified with solar lighting, rainwater harvesting, an STP, EV charging provisions and 100% power backup.

Unit Sizes

Four configurations are on offer: a 2 BHK (1,292–1,297 sq.ft), a 2 BHK + Study (1,457–1,459 sq.ft), a 3 BHK (1,572–1,876 sq.ft, which includes a more compact 2.5 BHK layout within that size band), and a 3 BHK + Study (2,090–2,092 sq.ft).

5. Possession Timeline

The target possession date for Aparna Wonder Woods is February 2031, as per the project's RERA registration — a long runway that reflects the scale of a 1,261-unit, 6-tower township. Buyers should treat this as an early-stage, ground-up launch and factor the extended construction timeline into their planning.

Our Honest Assessment

Wonder Woods' clearest strengths are scale and developer credibility: a 29+ year Tier-1 builder committing 14.9 acres and 1,261 units to a single corridor is a meaningful vote of confidence in Kanakapura Road's growth, and the Vaastu-compliant, no-common-wall design plus Mivan construction are genuine differentiators. The trade-off is the February 2031 possession date — a long horizon that suits buyers planning for the medium term more than those wanting a near-term move-in. If you want a large-scale, amenity-rich township from an established builder and are comfortable with an extended construction runway, Wonder Woods is worth shortlisting — see how it stacks up against the corridor's other flagship in our Wonder Woods vs Sattva Forest Ridge comparison, and independently verify the RERA registration (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/080626/008706) before booking.
