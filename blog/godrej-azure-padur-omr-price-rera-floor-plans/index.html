Godrej Azure Padur: Price, RERA & Floor Plans Review

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published September 5, 2026•12 Min Read

Godrej Azure price is one of the first things buyers search for once this Padur launch comes up in conversation, and it's easy to see why: a sea-view apartment on Chennai's OMR IT Corridor, from a listed national developer, is a genuinely uncommon combination. This review covers the developer, the location, the real unit configurations and pricing, the floor plan situation, possession timeline, amenities and RERA verification, so you can decide whether this project is worth pursuing before you contact the sales team.

1. The Developer: Godrej Properties

Godrej Properties is part of the Godrej Group, one of India's oldest and most recognisable conglomerates, and is among the country's leading listed real estate developers with a nationwide portfolio spanning residential, commercial and township projects. A listed, well-capitalised developer name is a meaningful risk-reducer for a buyer evaluating a new-launch project, since it usually comes with more institutional oversight on construction-linked disclosures and RERA compliance than a smaller, first-time developer would offer.

2. Location: Padur, OMR

Godrej Azure sits in Padur, on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR), Chennai's established IT Corridor. The project is positioned close to two of the corridor's largest employment clusters, the SIPCOT IT Park and Siruseri IT Park, both major concentrations of IT and ITES companies. At a glance, the proximity profile disclosed by the developer looks like this:

  • IT hubs and workplaces: SIPCOT IT Park (3.9 km), Siruseri IT Park (5-10 mins)
  • Educational institutions: Hindustan University (0.95 km), Sri Chaitanya School (1.9 km)
  • Hospitals: Supreme Specialty (0.5 km), Unittas Hospital (1.5 km)
  • Transport: Kilambakkam Bus Terminus (22.1 km), Chennai Airport (30.2 km)

A sub-1 km hospital and a sub-1 km university are genuinely convenient for daily life, and the under-10-minute reach to two separate IT parks is the strongest argument for this location if your household includes someone commuting to OMR's IT corridor. The bus terminus and airport distances are the two weaker points on this list, both over 20 km, so factor in a longer drive for intercity or air travel. We've written a dedicated locality deep-dive, Padur, OMR: Sea-View Apartments Buying Guide, if you want the fuller picture on this corridor and how it compares with nearby OMR micro-markets.

3. The Project: 8.8 Acres, 60%+ Open Space, Sea-View Residences

Godrej Azure is planned across 8.8 acres, with 60%+ of the land kept as open space. The project's central positioning claim is that every residence comes with a private balcony offering panoramic views of the sea and surrounding greenery, a genuine differentiator on this stretch of OMR, where most new residential supply faces inland rather than toward the coast. The source marketing material we reviewed did not disclose a specific unit count or tower structure, so if that level of detail matters to your decision, ask the sales team directly for the exact number of towers and units before booking.

Importantly, this is a new-launch project. That status means construction has not necessarily progressed to a stage where every element of the marketing material (renders, floor plans, master plan) reflects final, as-built reality. Treat published imagery and layouts as indicative, particularly the promised sea-view sightlines, which can vary meaningfully by floor and tower position, and confirm current construction status and your specific unit's actual view before booking, a standard precaution for any new-launch purchase regardless of developer.

4. Unit Configurations and Floor Plans

The project spans three configuration tiers, and floor plan artwork is available for all three:

  • 2 BHK Premium (1,020-1,050 sq.ft): a single floor plan variant, marketed as the entry-level sea-view configuration.
  • 3 BHK Elite / Royale (1,250-1,470 sq.ft): a mid-tier configuration with a meaningful size range, suggesting more than one layout variant within this tier, though the source material we reviewed presented it as one named floor plan.
  • 3 BHK Grandeur & 4 BHK Duplex (1,950-2,722 sq.ft): the top tier, explicitly including a 4 BHK Duplex layout at 2,722 sq.ft. The 3 BHK Grandeur and 4 BHK Duplex are grouped under one floor plan image and one price band in the source material, so ask the sales team to clarify which specific sqft and price applies to the 3 BHK Grandeur versus the 4 BHK Duplex before booking, since a nearly 800 sq.ft spread sits inside this single tier.

Bed and bathroom counts per configuration were not disclosed in the material we reviewed beyond the BHK label itself, unlike some comparable pre-launch projects that specify bath counts down to the half-bath. Ask for this detail directly if it affects your decision, particularly for the 4 BHK Duplex, where the layout across two levels is likely to matter more than usual.

At roughly ₹7,470-₹7,690 per sq.ft on the 2 BHK entry price, Godrej Azure is priced at a genuine premium over comparable inland OMR launches, which is consistent with the sea-view positioning and the Godrej brand. The jump from the 2 BHK's ₹78.49 Lakh entry point to the 3 BHK Elite/Royale's ₹96 Lakh-₹1.13 Crore band, and again to the top tier's ₹1.47-₹2.09 Crore range, is a steep but fairly linear per-sqft progression rather than an unusual premium concentrated in one tier.

On financing, the 2 BHK at ₹78.49 Lakhs sits at the upper end of what a salaried buyer in the mid-income bracket can comfortably finance under a conventional 80-85% loan-to-value ratio over a 20-year tenure; the 3 BHK and 4 BHK tiers will typically need a higher down payment, a co-applicant, or both to keep EMIs within a comfortable debt-to-income ratio. Budget separately for stamp duty, registration, GST on the under-construction component and monthly maintenance, none of which were disclosed in the source material we reviewed and should be confirmed directly with the sales team before booking.

6. Amenities

The amenity list spans two broad categories in the material we reviewed: sports and fitness facilities including a swimming pool, gymnasium, jogging track, yoga deck, meditation area and a dedicated cricket practice area; and family and recreation facilities including a children's play area, a senior citizen zone, a modern clubhouse, a multipurpose hall, landscaped gardens and community spaces. This is a solid, family-oriented amenity spread, though it is lighter on named sports courts (no dedicated tennis, badminton or basketball facility was disclosed) than some comparable large-format Chennai launches. If specific sports facilities matter to you, confirm the final clubhouse amenity list with the sales team, since amenity specifics can still evolve at the new-launch stage.

7. Possession Timeline

As a new-launch project, Godrej Azure's possession is targeted for May 2030, roughly four years out from this review's publication date. That is a longer runway than most of the pre-launch and new-launch Chennai projects we've reviewed, which typically target a two-to-three-year horizon. A timeline this far out means the usual construction-risk caveats apply more strongly than they would for a project already mid-construction: ask specifically for a construction-linked payment schedule tied to actual build milestones, not just calendar dates, and confirm what recourse you have if the timeline slips, before committing any booking amount.

8. Legal and RERA Verification

At Luxura Habitat, no project recommendation is made before legal verification. Godrej Azure is registered under TNRERA number TNRERA/35/BLG/0354/2025. We recommend verifying this number independently on the official TNRERA portal (rera.tn.gov.in) before finalising a booking, as we do for every project we cover. This project-level registration is separate from the individual RERA agent registration used to market this listing, so don't conflate the two when checking documentation.

9. Who This Project Actually Suits

Godrej Azure is a strong fit for a buyer who specifically wants a sea-view residence within a short commute of OMR's IT corridor, is comfortable paying a premium for that combination, and can accommodate a possession date roughly four years out with a listed developer's construction-linked payment schedule. It's a weaker fit for a buyer on a tighter budget who would be better served by a comparably positioned inland OMR launch at a lower per-sqft rate, or for anyone who needs possession within the next two years.

Our Honest Assessment

This project has real fundamentals in its favour: a listed, well-capitalised developer, a genuinely differentiated sea-view positioning on a corridor where that's rare, proximity to two major IT employment hubs, and a healthy 60%+ open-space ratio. Where we'd urge caution: it's a new-launch project with a possession date roughly four years out, the source material we reviewed didn't disclose a specific unit or tower count, bed and bathroom counts per configuration weren't specified, and the 3 BHK Grandeur and 4 BHK Duplex are bundled under one price band despite a wide sqft spread between them. As always, verify TNRERA/35/BLG/0354/2025 yourself on the TNRERA portal, confirm current construction progress directly with the developer, and ask for your specific unit's actual sea-view sightline in writing before booking rather than relying on the marketing renders.
