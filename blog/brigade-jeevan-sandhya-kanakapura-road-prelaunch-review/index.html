Brigade Silvercrest: Pre-Launch Review & What to Verify

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 1, 2026•7 Min Read
Pre-Launch Notice: Brigade Silvercrest is currently in pre-launch stage. RERA registration is not yet complete, and all pricing, unit and possession figures below are indicative and sourced from the developer's current marketing material — not a final, RERA-registered offering. Treat this as an early-look review, not a booking recommendation.

Update (August 2026): this project was previously covered on our site under its pre-launch code name, Brigade Jeevan Sandhya. It is now being marketed as Brigade Silvercrest — same developer, location and configuration.

On Kanakapura Road, near Vajarahalli Metro Station, Brigade Group is pre-launching a boutique, single-tower ultra-luxury project: just 135 homes across 20 floors. Here's an honest, early-stage look at what's known so far — and what still needs to be verified before anyone commits.

1. The Developer: Brigade Group

Brigade Group (Brigade Enterprises Limited) was founded in 1986 by M. R. Jaishankar and has been listed on the BSE and NSE since 2013 (ticker: BRIGADE). The company's first project, Brigade Towers, was Bengaluru's first private high-rise building on completion in 1989, and Brigade has since delivered 300+ projects covering roughly 100 million Sq.Ft across South India. That's a long, publicly-verifiable track record for a pre-launch project to lean on.

2. Location: Kanakapura Road, Near Vajarahalli Metro

The project sits on Kanakapura Road, close to Vajarahalli Metro Station on the Green Line — on the same broader corridor as JP Nagar and Banashankari. Key proximity points from the developer's location map:

  • Vajarahalli Metro Station: Nearby, Green Line
  • Doddakallasandra, Konanakunte Cross, JP Nagar & Banashankari Metro Stations: along the same corridor
  • NICE Road Junction: ~2.5 km, with signal-free access onward to Electronic City, Mysore Road and Bannerghatta Road
  • Forum South Mall: ~10 minutes
  • Gopalan Innovation Mall: ~20 minutes
  • Electronic City: ~30 minutes via NICE Road
  • Kempegowda International Airport: ~50 km / ~90 minutes
  • Art of Living International Center & Pyramid Valley: established wellness and spiritual landmarks on the same Kanakapura Road corridor

3. The Project: A Deliberately Boutique Scale

Brigade Silvercrest is planned as a single 20-floor tower on approximately 2 acres, with just 135 ultra-luxury units — a notably low-density unit count for a tower of this height. Configurations are 3 and 4 BHK homes ranging from approximately 2,100 to 2,600 Sq.Ft. The developer's marketing material positions this explicitly as an "ultra-luxury" address rather than a mass-market launch.

4. Pricing

Indicative pre-launch pricing starts at approximately ₹3 Crore onwards for 3 BHK homes, with 4 BHK pricing available on enquiry. As with any pre-launch project, treat this as a starting reference point — final pricing, floor-wise premiums and payment schedules will only be confirmed at formal launch.

5. What's Still Unconfirmed

Several details aren't yet public at this stage:

  • RERA registration: Not yet filed/approved as of this review. No RERA number exists yet — be wary of any listing that shows one.
  • Detailed amenity list and specifications: The developer's current material mentions a clubhouse, swimming pool, gym and landscaped gardens, but a full specification sheet isn't yet public.
  • Detailed floor plans and tower elevations: Not yet released publicly.
  • Final possession date: December 2030 is the developer's current indicative target, subject to change once RERA registration formalizes the commitment.

6. A Note on the Project Name

This project was initially marketed under the internal pre-launch code name "Brigade Jeevan Sandhya," which is how we first covered it. It is now being positioned under its official name, Brigade Silvercrest — same developer, same Kanakapura Road location, same configuration. If you come across older listings or discussions referencing "Jeevan Sandhya," they refer to this same project. As with any pre-launch listing, rely on the developer's official channels and RERA filings once available, rather than assuming a third-party listing is authoritative.

Our Honest Assessment

Brigade Silvercrest's clearest strengths are its boutique scale — just 135 homes is genuinely low-density for an ultra-luxury Kanakapura Road tower — and Brigade Group's long, listed track record of 300+ delivered projects. The trade-off is that this is a very early-stage listing: no RERA number, no public floor plans, and a limited published amenity list. If you want a large-format alternative with a confirmed RERA registration on the same corridor, compare against Aparna Wonder Woods. If Silvercrest's boutique positioning appeals to you, registering early interest is reasonable — but as with any pre-launch project, hold off on binding payments until RERA registration is confirmed and independently verified on the Karnataka RERA portal.
