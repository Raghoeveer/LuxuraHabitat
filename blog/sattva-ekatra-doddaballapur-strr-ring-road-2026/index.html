Bengaluru's Satellite Town Ring Road Nears a November Milestone: What It Means for Sattva Ekatra, Doddaballapur

By the Luxura Habitat Team•November 11, 2026•9 Min Read

Bengaluru's Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR), the planned ring corridor meant to connect 12 satellite towns around the city without forcing every vehicle through the core, has quietly become one of the more consequential infrastructure stories for North Bengaluru's outer growth corridors. A Swarajyamag news brief dated August 2026, citing an earlier Times of India report, said nearly half of the full roughly 288 km corridor was expected to become operational by around November 2026. We could not independently confirm from our own research whether that specific deadline has been hit as of this week, and readers should treat it as a reported target rather than a verified fact, but the direction of travel on STRR is unambiguous: large sections are moving from planned to built. For Sattva Ekatra (also marketed as Sattva Thippapura), the RERA-registered plotted layout on Doddaballapur Main Road, this is about as direct a piece of location-relevant infrastructure news as a project in this corridor gets, since the project's own listings place the site roughly a minute from STRR itself.

1. What's Already Open, and What's Still Being Built

The segment of STRR that matters most for Doddaballapur, the Dobbaspet-Doddaballapur-Hoskote stretch, is not a future promise. It was inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in March 2024, and multiple 2026 property-market reports describe this six-lane section as operational and tolled since shortly after. One widely cited property guide from August 2026 explicitly calls the Doddaballapur bypass one of the more cleanly de-risked pockets of the wider STRR project, precisely because it is already built and carrying traffic rather than sitting in a tendering queue. The part of the story that is still unfolding is the rest of the 288 km ring, where the August 2026 report's "nearly half operational by November 2026" claim applies; other sections of the corridor, including segments like the Bannerghatta stretch, have gone through their own rounds of tendering, package awards and construction over 2025 and 2026, with 18 overbridges planned along the route, including dedicated crossings for two-wheelers and cyclists, per an earlier 2026 report.

2. Why a Ring Road Matters Differently for Plotted Land

A metro line mainly changes commute times for people who already live or plan to live along its route. A ring road changes something more structural for a corridor like Doddaballapur: it is usually the single biggest driver of where logistics parks, warehousing, light manufacturing and ancillary industry choose to locate next, well before it meaningfully shifts apartment demand the way a metro extension does. That matters more for a plotted layout than for a finished apartment, because a plot's value is tied directly to its road frontage, its distance from a functioning arterial, and the pace of commercial activity building up around it, rather than to amenities inside a single building. For a corridor like Doddaballapur, already home to the Integrated Textile Park and within reach of Foxconn's upcoming manufacturing investment, a completing ring road is one of the clearer structural tailwinds a plotted-land buyer can point to, separate from any single developer's marketing claims.

  • The Doddaballapur stretch is already open. This isn't a "wait and see" story for this specific segment; the six-lane Dobbaspet-Doddaballapur-Hoskote section has been tolled traffic since shortly after its March 2024 inauguration.
  • The wider ring is still under construction. Treat the "nearly half by November 2026" figure as a reported target from an August 2026 story, not an independently confirmed, fully delivered milestone.
  • Ring roads tend to pull industry first, housing second. Expect logistics and manufacturing interest to build along STRR before any apartment-style demand shift becomes visible, which is a different, often earlier, signal for plotted-land buyers to watch.
  • None of this changes a specific project's RERA facts. Infrastructure progress is useful context, not a substitute for checking a project's own registration, layout approval and cost sheet.

3. Reading This Against Sattva Ekatra's Own Numbers

Sattva Ekatra, listed under the working name Sattva Thippapura, is a plotted residential development by Sattva Group on Doddaballapur Main Road (NH-44 Extension) in Thippapura, Doddaballapur, offering 500+ serviced plots across roughly 25 acres. Unlike several projects in this corridor, it is already RERA registered, under Karnataka RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1250/301/PR/180926/008945 (commonly shortened to PR/180926/008945), with possession currently expected around June 2028. Buyers choose from five plot sizes, 600 sq ft (20'x30'), 1,200 sq ft (30'x40'), 1,350 sq ft (30'x45'), 1,500 sq ft (30'x50') and 1,800 sq ft (30'x60'), plus odd-dimension corner plots, with indicative starting prices from about Rs 48 lakh for the smallest plot up to about Rs 1.55 crore for the largest, before development charges, PLC and taxes. According to the project's own listings, the site is roughly a minute from STRR itself, about 7 minutes from Doddaballapur Railway Station, around 10 minutes from the Integrated Textile Park, about 20 minutes from Foxconn, roughly 30 minutes from Devanahalli, and about 35 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

That location profile is exactly the kind of corridor where a completing ring road should matter most: the project doesn't rely on a single future metro extension or a distant airport-linked thesis, it sits directly on a main road that already feeds into an operational, tolled stretch of STRR, with an established textile park and an incoming large manufacturing employer within a short drive. Sattva Group itself began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva, giving it close to four decades of delivery history across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata and Goa, which is a reasonable, though not sufficient on its own, reason to weigh the group's track record alongside the corridor's infrastructure story. As with any plotted purchase, buyers should still independently verify the sanctioned layout, exact plot dimensions and facing, title documents, and the full cost sheet, including development charges and taxes, before booking, and confirm the live RERA status on rera.karnataka.gov.in.

The Bottom Line

The Satellite Town Ring Road's Doddaballapur stretch isn't a future infrastructure promise for Sattva Ekatra, it's already open and carrying tolled traffic, while the wider 288 km corridor continues working through its remaining sections toward the reported November 2026 target. For a RERA-registered plotted layout that sits roughly a minute from the ring road, with its own textile park and manufacturing-employer proximity, that is a genuinely relevant, if incremental, piece of context layered on top of the project's own registered price, plot sizes and possession timeline.
