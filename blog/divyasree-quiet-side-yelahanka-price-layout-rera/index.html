Divyasree The Quiet Side Review: Price, Floor Plans & RERA Status

By Narayanan Rajesh•Updated November 10, 2026•12 Min Read

Divyasree The Quiet Side is one of the more talked-about new launches in North Bangalore this year, and for a simple reason: it puts just 270 homes on 5.5 acres in Yelahanka New Town. In a market where new towers routinely pack 8 to 10 homes onto every floor, a project with 4 homes per floor and no common walls stands out. This review, updated in November 2026 after the launch, covers what the project offers, what it costs, and what is still unconfirmed.

Latest Update (10 November 2026)

  • Launched: Divyasree announced the launch on 5 November 2026, the date its website gave for the start of booking. News coverage dated 8 November 2026 puts the estimated gross development value at over ₹800 crore. Full status timeline on the project page booking section.
  • RERA: Karnataka RERA PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/291026/009050, dated 29 October 2026, is now cited by news coverage of the launch that refers to K-RERA records. A research microsite says the application was acknowledged on 12 October 2026. Check the number and completion date on the K-RERA portal before paying.
  • A third unit count: the launch announcement speaks of about 260 homes on 5 acres, against 236 homes on about 4.52 acres in the RERA record and 270 homes on 5.5 acres on the marketing microsites. The developer also claims 85% of the land as open space, while research portal data shows 60%. Side-by-side table on the project page.
  • Plan names and carpet areas: a brochure-based research microsite lists a Grand 3 BHK of 1,993 sq ft (1,195 sq ft carpet), a Luxe 3 BHK with a staff room of 2,290 to 2,339 sq ft (1,372 to 1,406 sq ft carpet), and duplex penthouses of 2,697 to 3,261 sq ft. It quotes prices from ₹2.59 Cr*. See sizes by plan.
  • Exact site: the Fire NOC (17 June 2025) and a June 2026 title report name Survey Nos. 5/3 & 6/1, Mandalakunte Village, Yelahanka Hobli (PIN 560064), for 3 high-rise blocks A, B and C. A broker site places it off Mother Dairy Road, behind NCC Urban Parkview. See the location section.
  • Sources disagree on the basics: 270 homes and 5.5 acres on the marketing microsites, 236 homes on a broker site and a research portal, 4.52 acres on a research portal and 5 acres 4 guntas (about 5.1 acres) in the title report. Possession is 2030 onwards on the microsites but 30 December 2031 on the portals quoting RERA.
  • Prices reported elsewhere: from ₹2.25 Cr* (broker site, 3 BHK 1,968 sq ft) and ₹2.5 Cr* (Divyasree launch ad) and ₹2.59 Cr* (a research microsite) up to ₹2.85 Cr* to ₹3.3 Cr* (a research portal). Our headline stays at ₹2.65 Cr*, per the microsite price table. Comparison table on the project page pricing section.

3. Design and Specifications

The low-density plan is the core of the pitch. Per the developer's pre-launch fact sheet, each floor has just 4 homes with no common wall, which allows double-side ventilation in the rooms. The specification list includes a 4x8 ft main door with 4-way digital access, Italian marble in the living, dining and 2 bedrooms, wooden flooring in the master bedroom, Kohler or equivalent fittings, 9.7 ft ceilings and 8 ft windows with St. Gobain glass. Model apartments are expected to open at the official launch.

4. Master Plan and Amenities

The 3 towers sit around a central clubhouse, a swimming pool and landscaped greens, spaced to avoid the tower-facing congestion common in denser projects. Parking is in the basement, keeping the podium largely vehicle-free, with walking paths and a jogging track running through the landscaping.

The amenity list covers the essentials well: clubhouse, swimming pool, gymnasium, yoga pavilion, aerobics room, dance and music room, mini theatre, indoor games room, multipurpose hall, kids' activity zone and play area, senior citizen seating, 24/7 CCTV, video door phones, high-speed lifts, power backup and water storage. With only 270 households sharing them, these facilities should feel far less crowded than in a 1,000-unit township.

6. RERA, EOI and Payment Plan

Divyasree The Quiet Side carries Karnataka RERA registration PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/291026/009050, dated 29 October 2026, and Divyasree announced the launch on 5 November 2026. Check the number and its completion date on the K-RERA portal yourself. Under RERA, a developer must register a project before selling units in it. Before the launch, an Expression of Interest let early buyers secure priority unit selection, preferred facings and higher floors, and early pricing, usually through a post-dated cheque in favour of the developer. That stage has now given way to booking, so ask whether any EOI or launch offers are still running. Do not sign an agreement for sale or pay a large booking amount until you have checked the RERA number and its completion date on rera.karnataka.gov.in.

A construction-linked payment plan is expected: a booking amount at reservation, stage-wise payments tied to construction, and a final payment at handover. One microsite also advertises a 20:5 flexi plan without a breakup, so ask for it in writing.

7. Possession Timeline

The marketing microsites show possession from 2030 onwards, but the RERA record gives a completion date of 30 December 2031. That makes this a long-horizon purchase. Budget for rent or EMIs on a current home through 2031, and treat the RERA date as the one that binds the developer.

Our Honest Review: Pros and Cons

What works:

  • Genuinely low density: about 49 to 52 homes per acre whichever count you use, with 4 homes per floor and no common walls.
  • Fire NOC, KSPCB consent and a title report are already on record.
  • Large homes from 1,900 sq ft, with premium specs like 9.7 ft ceilings, Italian marble and 8 ft windows.
  • An established Yelahanka New Town address with schools, hospitals and the airport within easy reach.
  • Amenities shared by relatively few households.

What to weigh:

  • RERA was registered only on 29 Oct 2026, and the record lists fewer homes (236) on less land (about 4.52 acres) than the marketing material.
  • Possession is 2030 onwards at best, and the reported RERA date is 30 December 2031.
  • A research site puts the rate about 58% above the top of the Yelahanka average and gross rental yield at only 2.0% to 2.9%.
  • At roughly ₹13,500 to ₹14,000 per sq ft, pricing sits at the premium end for Yelahanka.
  • Sizes and prices changed during pre-launch, and published plan sizes still differ between sources.
  • Published location landmarks for the project are not fully consistent, so verify the site in person.

Who Should Consider It

The Quiet Side suits end-users who want a large, private home in a calm part of North Bangalore, can wait until 2031, and value low density over a long amenity list. It suits investors with a long horizon who are comfortable with launch-stage risk. It is a weaker fit for anyone who needs a ready home soon or wants RERA protection from day one.

Our Assessment

Divyasree The Quiet Side offers something increasingly rare in Bangalore: big homes in a small, quiet community, from a known developer, in an established suburb. The pricing reflects that. The sensible approach is to get the official launch cost sheet and floor plans, match the plan name and RERA carpet area, and hold off on any binding commitment until you have checked the RERA number and completion date on the portal. You can see the full configuration cards, master plan, amenities and location tables on the Divyasree The Quiet Side project page.
