Divyasree The Quiet Side Review: Price, Floor Plans & RERA Status

By Narayanan Rajesh•Updated October 5, 2026•12 Min Read

Divyasree The Quiet Side is one of the more talked-about pre-launch projects in North Bangalore this year, and for a simple reason: it puts just 270 homes on 5.5 acres in Yelahanka New Town. In a market where new towers routinely pack 8 to 10 homes onto every floor, a project with 4 homes per floor and no common walls stands out. This review, updated in October 2026 with the latest pricing and project details, covers what the project offers, what it costs, and what is still unconfirmed.

3. Design and Specifications

The low-density plan is the core of the pitch. Per the developer's pre-launch fact sheet, each floor has just 4 homes with no common wall, which allows double-side ventilation in the rooms. The specification list includes a 4x8 ft main door with 4-way digital access, Italian marble in the living, dining and 2 bedrooms, wooden flooring in the master bedroom, Kohler or equivalent fittings, 9.7 ft ceilings and 8 ft windows with St. Gobain glass. Model apartments are expected to open at the official launch.

4. Master Plan and Amenities

The 3 towers sit around a central clubhouse, a swimming pool and landscaped greens, spaced to avoid the tower-facing congestion common in denser projects. Parking is in the basement, keeping the podium largely vehicle-free, with walking paths and a jogging track running through the landscaping.

The amenity list covers the essentials well: clubhouse, swimming pool, gymnasium, yoga pavilion, aerobics room, dance and music room, mini theatre, indoor games room, multipurpose hall, kids' activity zone and play area, senior citizen seating, 24/7 CCTV, video door phones, high-speed lifts, power backup and water storage. With only 270 households sharing them, these facilities should feel far less crowded than in a 1,000-unit township.

6. RERA, EOI and Payment Plan

Divyasree The Quiet Side does not yet have a Karnataka RERA number; registration is under process. Under RERA, a developer must register a project before selling units in it. A pre-launch Expression of Interest is common and lets you secure priority unit selection, preferred facings and higher floors, and early pricing, usually through a post-dated cheque in favour of the developer. That is not the same as a sale. Do not sign an agreement for sale or pay a large booking amount until the RERA number is issued and you have checked it on rera.karnataka.gov.in.

Once launched, a construction-linked payment plan is expected: a booking amount at reservation, stage-wise payments tied to construction, and a final payment at handover.

7. Possession Timeline

Launch is expected in 2026, with completion in 4 to 5 years and possession from 2030 onwards. That makes this a long-horizon purchase. Budget for rent or EMIs on a current home through roughly 2030, and treat the date as tentative until it appears in the RERA registration.

Our Honest Review: Pros and Cons

What works:

  • Genuinely low density: 270 homes on 5.5 acres, about 49 per acre, with 4 homes per floor and no common walls.
  • Large homes from 1,900 sq ft, with premium specs like 9.7 ft ceilings, Italian marble and 8 ft windows.
  • An established Yelahanka New Town address with schools, hospitals and the airport within easy reach.
  • Amenities shared by relatively few households.

What to weigh:

  • RERA is not yet issued, so there is nothing to legally book into today.
  • Possession is around 2030, and that date is tentative.
  • At roughly ₹13,500 to ₹14,000 per sq ft, pricing sits at the premium end for Yelahanka.
  • Sizes and prices have already changed during pre-launch, and dimensioned floor plans are yet to be released.
  • Published location landmarks for the project are not fully consistent, so verify the site in person.

Who Should Consider It

The Quiet Side suits end-users who want a large, private home in a calm part of North Bangalore, can wait until around 2030, and value low density over a long amenity list. It suits investors with a long horizon who are comfortable with pre-launch risk. It is a weaker fit for anyone who needs a ready home soon or wants RERA protection from day one.

Our Assessment

Divyasree The Quiet Side offers something increasingly rare in Bangalore: big homes in a small, quiet community, from a known developer, in an established suburb. The pricing reflects that. The sensible approach is to register an EOI if the product fits your needs, get the official cost sheet and floor plans, and hold off on any binding commitment until the RERA number is published and verified.
