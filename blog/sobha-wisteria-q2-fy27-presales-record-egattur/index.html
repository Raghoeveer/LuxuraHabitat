Sobha's H1 FY27 Presales Hit an All-Time High: What It Means for Sobha Wisteria Buyers in Egattur

By the Luxura Habitat Team•October 21, 2026•7 Min Read

Sobha Limited filed its real estate operational update for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, with the stock exchanges on October 2, 2026. According to an HDFC Securities note on the filing dated October 5, 2026, Sobha's Q2 FY27 presales came in at about Rs 2,210 crore, its second-highest quarter on record, just behind the Rs 3,660 crore it posted in Q1 FY27. Taken together, that puts the company's H1 FY27 presales at an all-time high of roughly Rs 5,860 crore, up 47% year on year. The company's management, led by Managing Director Jagadish Nangineni and CFO Yogesh Bansal, was scheduled to walk analysts through the numbers on an investor call on October 19, 2026.

1. The Numbers: A Record Half, Even After a Record Quarter

Following a record-setting Q1 FY27 with a strong Q2 is a harder thing to pull off than most single-quarter headlines suggest, since it rules out the number simply being a one-off spike. Sales volume for the quarter rose a modest 2% year on year, while the average selling price climbed about 14% year on year, which indicates this period's growth leaned more on realization per unit than a sharp jump in the number of homes sold. At roughly 59% of the company's full-year FY27 guidance of about Rs 10,000 crore in presales, Sobha is comfortably ahead of pace at the halfway mark of its financial year.

2. New Launches This Quarter, and What's Coming Next

Sobha launched new projects in Calicut and Mysore during Q2 FY27, continuing its pattern of spreading launches across both its home Bengaluru-Kerala markets and newer geographies. Brokerage estimates following the update point to a further pickup in Q3 FY27, with new launches potentially spanning a further phase of its Crystal Meadows project, a second phase in Gurugram, a Hyderabad launch and a Whitefield, Bengaluru project. None of the brokerage's named upcoming launches refer to Sobha Wisteria in Chennai, so that project's own launch timing remains separate from this quarter's disclosed pipeline.

3. Reading the Results Against Sobha Wisteria in Egattur

A listed developer's quarterly presales number and a specific pre-launch project's own facts are two different things, and it's worth being precise about which is which. The 47% H1 FY27 growth is a corporate-level figure covering every Sobha project selling nationally this half year, not a claim about any single, unlaunched development. Sobha Wisteria, also marketed as Sobha OMR, in Egattur on Chennai's OMR IT corridor, remains a pre-launch project: based on the project's own page, it is indicatively planned as roughly 650 homes on 8.5 acres, offered only as 3 and 4 BHK apartments from about 1,750 to 2,700 sq ft, with 3 BHK homes priced from around Rs 1.69 crore onwards and possession expected by December 2029. Its TNRERA registration number is still to be advised, so none of these figures are final until the developer's official brochure and registration are out.

  • A healthy parent company is a reasonable, not sufficient, signal. A developer posting record presales and an accelerating average selling price is generally a better financial backdrop for an upcoming launch than a developer under pressure, but it doesn't substitute for checking the specific project's own RERA filing, price sheet and construction timeline once they're released.
  • Pre-launch pricing can move once a project is formally launched. Pre-launch indicative pricing, like the Rs 1.69 crore onwards figure currently associated with Sobha Wisteria, is not unusual to see adjusted once a project's formal TNRERA registration, master plan and brochure are released, so buyers evaluating it now should treat today's numbers as a starting reference rather than a locked-in quote.
  • OMR's Egattur micro-market benefits from the same demand base driving the company's broader numbers. Egattur sits minutes from the SIPCOT IT Park in Siruseri, one of the stronger employment-linked micro-markets along OMR, which is part of why a Tier-1 developer like Sobha is entering it in the first place.

The Bottom Line

Sobha's H1 FY27 update shows a developer whose core business is accelerating rather than slowing, with record or near-record presales in back-to-back quarters and pricing power that is holding up alongside volume. For Chennai buyers specifically tracking Sobha Wisteria in Egattur, that is useful context on the developer's financial health, but the project's own pricing, configuration and RERA status still need to be confirmed independently once they are formally announced.
