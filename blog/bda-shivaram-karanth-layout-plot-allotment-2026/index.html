BDA Shivaram Karanth Layout 2026: Site Allotment Explained

By the Luxura Habitat Team•September 15, 2026•8 Min Read

The Bengaluru Development Authority has taken a concrete step toward allotting sites in Dr Shivaram Karanth Layout, one of North Bengaluru's largest and longest-pending government layouts. The Karnataka High Court has allowed BDA to invite applications, and BDA's own notification dated August 31, 2026 lists 2,815 sites open for general-public allotment. Chief Minister DK Shivakumar symbolically handed over plot allotment documents to landowners at an event the same day, where BDA distributed plots to more than 600 landowners. If you've been tracking this layout, or you're weighing a BDA site against private developments in the same corridor, here's what actually changed and what still hasn't.

1. What BDA Has Actually Opened Up

  • 2,815 sites for general-public allotment, per the August 31, 2026 notification.
  • Online applications open until October 15, 2026 at 12 noon, giving prospective applicants roughly six weeks from the notification date.
  • Provisional site rate of Rs 64,584 per sq metre for the standard 9x12 metre sites, which make up the bulk of the 2,815 (2,500 sites), alongside 300 sites of 12x18 metres and 15 of 15x24 metres.
  • Landowner allotments are running in parallel, with BDA having already distributed plots to over 600 landowners as part of the August 31 event, separate from the general-public application window.

2. The Court Angle You Shouldn't Skip

The Karnataka High Court's order allows BDA to call applications, but site allotments themselves remain subject to further court orders, with BDA citing mounting loan interest costs as part of the context for moving forward now. This is a meaningful caveat: applying for a site is not the same as receiving a guaranteed, litigation-free allotment. Shivaram Karanth Layout has a long history of land-acquisition disputes and court involvement, including earlier orders on regularising existing buildings within the layout. Anyone applying should go in with realistic expectations about timeline and treat the process as still evolving rather than fully resolved.

3. The Scale of the Layout

Dr Shivaram Karanth Layout spans approximately 3,069 acres across 17 villages in North Bengaluru, including Avalahalli, Ramagondanahalli and Jarakabande Kaval. That places it squarely within the broader Yelahanka and Bengaluru airport growth corridor, one of the more actively developing parts of the city thanks to KIADB Aerospace Park, the airport itself, and the metro and STRR infrastructure being built out in the same belt.

4. BDA Plots vs Private Developments: What to Weigh

A BDA layout plot has genuine advantages: government backing, typically lower entry pricing than an equivalent private plot in the same micro-market, and clean legal title once the process concludes. But the current court-order caveat, the application deadline, and the sheer scale of a 3,069-acre, multi-village layout mean the actual allotment and site-possession timeline for a general-public applicant is difficult to predict with confidence today.

If you want a more predictable, RERA-registered path into the same broad North Bengaluru corridor, several private developments are already live. Interestingly, Vajram Chrysanthemum sits in Avalahalli, Yelahanka, one of the very villages that forms part of Shivaram Karanth Layout, and is a bookable, RERA-registered apartment project today rather than an application in progress. For buyers specifically looking for plots rather than apartments, Luxura Habitat also markets several private plotted developments in the wider North Bengaluru and Doddaballapur belt, including KNS Samooha and Sattva Thippapura.

The Bottom Line

BDA's August 31 notification is real progress on a layout that has been stuck in various stages of acquisition and litigation for years, but it isn't yet a finished process. If you're set on a BDA site, apply within the window and track the court proceedings closely. If you'd rather not wait on an outcome that's still subject to further court orders, the same North Bengaluru corridor already has private, RERA-registered options you can evaluate today. Talk to our team if you want help comparing the two paths.
