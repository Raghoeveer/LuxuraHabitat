Sobha Wisteria (Sobha OMR) Review: Price, Sizes & RERA Status

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published October 12, 2026•Updated November 8, 2026•15 Min Read

Representative image. Sobha has not released official project images yet.

If you have searched for Sobha Wisteria or Sobha OMR in the last few weeks, you have probably noticed two things: the project is being talked about everywhere on Chennai's IT corridor, and almost every website describes it slightly differently. This review pulls together what the launch listings actually say about Sobha Wisteria in Egattur, works through the price per square foot, explains the RERA position, and flags exactly where the published numbers disagree, so you can walk into a sales conversation knowing what to ask.

One thing to be clear about upfront: Sobha Wisteria is a pre-launch project. There is no TNRERA registration number yet, no official brochure in public circulation and no released master plan. Everything below is based on launch listings, checked against each other, and we say so wherever a figure is not settled.

Latest Update (8 November 2026)

A pre-launch price circular shared with channel partners this month gives the clearest numbers yet, and we have updated this review and the project page to match. The short version:

  • Base price: ₹12,999 to ₹13,999 per sq ft*, on a selective, limited set of units, first come first served. Floor rise, PLC, facing, car parking, clubhouse, infrastructure, GST and registration are extra. See the price section of the project page.
  • Five layouts, 1 to 4 BHK: 665 sq ft (1 BHK, 1 toilet), 1,150 sq ft (2 BHK, 2 toilets), 1,550 sq ft (3 BHK, 2 toilets and a washbasin), 1,850 sq ft (3 BHK, 3 toilets and a powder room) and 2,300 sq ft (4 BHK, 4 toilets, powder and pooja rooms). This replaces the 3 and 4 BHK only, 1,750 to 2,700 sq ft version we led with earlier.
  • Base prices from about ₹86.4 L* to ₹3.22 Cr* on our arithmetic, before the extras. A property portal's news item from 7 November reports the same rate band under the name Sobha Codename Bayview.
  • EOIs are being collected. Channel partners have been asked to start collecting expressions of interest. One channel-partner site says the EOI is refundable and that the first 200 units should get pre-launch pricing; Sobha has not published the terms. See status and booking.
  • Still no TNRERA number as of 8 November 2026, and land area (8.5, 9 or 9.5 acres), unit count (about 600, 650 or 906) and possession (December 2029 or 2030) still differ by source.

The full comparison is on the project page price section.

Even the lower end of the base rate sits well above the area. A research portal puts Egattur apartment rates at roughly ₹8,250 to ₹12,300 per sq ft and the OMR-wide average near ₹7,400 per sq ft, while a Reddit commenter says other flats nearby sell for ₹6,500 to ₹8,000 per sq ft. Part of that gap is the Sobha brand premium. Part of it is that the extras have not been added yet: floor rise, PLC, parking, clubhouse and infrastructure charges, plus GST, stamp duty and registration, all come on top of the base figure.

Two questions to ask before comparing: first, is the rate on saleable or carpet area? A property portal lists carpet areas of about 465 to 1,610 sq ft for the five layouts, around 70% of the saleable size, so the effective rate on carpet area is roughly 40% higher. Second, which units actually qualify for the pre-launch rate, and what does each extra charge come to in rupees?

What the EMI looks like

As a rough guide, on a 2 BHK with a base price of about ₹1.55 Cr and a 20 percent down payment (about ₹31 lakh), a loan of about ₹1.24 Cr over 20 years at around 8.5 percent works out to an EMI in the region of ₹1.08 lakh a month, before the extra charges are added. Under a construction-linked plan, you would pay pre-EMI interest only on the amount disbursed until possession, so the full EMI usually starts closer to handover. For the 4 BHK at about ₹3 Cr base, the same structure points to an EMI of roughly ₹2.1 lakh a month. These are estimates for planning, not a loan offer; your bank's rate and the final cost sheet will decide the real figure.

4. Floor Plans and Home Sizes

The November 2026 unit sheet lists five layouts: a 665 sq ft 1 BHK with one toilet, a 1,150 sq ft 2 BHK with two toilets, a 1,550 sq ft 3 BHK with two toilets and a washbasin, a 1,850 sq ft 3 BHK with three toilets and a powder room, and a 2,300 sq ft 4 BHK with four toilets, a powder room and a pooja room. These are saleable sizes. A property portal pairs them with carpet areas of about 465, 805, 1,085, 1,295 and 1,610 sq ft, and the same portal labels the 2,300 sq ft plan as a 3 BHK with four toilets, while other listings call it a 4 BHK. Earlier launch listings showed only 3 BHK (1,750 to 2,150 sq ft) and 4 BHK (2,350 to 2,700 sq ft) homes. Floor plan drawings have not been released publicly. When they are, look for:

  • Bathroom count: the unit sheet already answers part of this. The 1,550 sq ft 3 BHK has two toilets and a washbasin, while the 1,850 sq ft 3 BHK has three toilets and a powder room, so check which bedroom goes without an attached bath in the smaller plan.
  • Utility and staff room: many Sobha large-format homes include a utility area, and some 4 BHKs include a staff room with its own toilet. Confirm whether this is counted as a bedroom.
  • Balcony share of the area: deep balconies are pleasant in Chennai's climate but inflate the super built-up figure. Ask for the carpet area of each room.
  • Orientation: on OMR, east and north-facing living rooms stay cooler through the afternoon. If you are early in the queue, this is one of the real advantages of booking at pre-launch.

5. Amenities

The amenity list across the two listings is long and fairly typical of a large Sobha community: a clubhouse, swimming pool and children's pool, gymnasium, mini theatre, open amphitheatre, yoga and meditation deck, indoor games room, cafeteria, multipurpose hall, tennis court, half basketball court, skating rink, jogging and cycling track, kids' play area, party lawn, senior citizens' sit-out and landscaped gardens with water features. On the services side, the listings mention 24/7 CCTV security, rainwater harvesting, a sewage treatment plant and underground electrical cabling.

What is missing at this stage is the one thing that tells you how good the amenities will really be: the size of the clubhouse in square feet and the share of the site kept as open space. On an 8.5-acre site with 650 homes, the open-space ratio will decide whether the community feels green or crowded. Ask for both numbers.

6. Location: Egattur on OMR

Satellite screenshot shared in a property-news post. The pin is near Novotel Chennai Sipcot; the exact Sobha Wisteria site boundary is not confirmed.

Egattur is on Rajiv Gandhi Salai between Navalur and Kelambakkam, on the stretch next to SIPCOT IT Park in Siruseri. Its biggest advantage is the short commute to the large employers in and around SIPCOT, such as TCS and Cognizant. Listings put SIPCOT at anywhere from under 1 km to about 5 km away depending on the source and route. The Marina Mall is between 0.5 and 3 km by different counts, Chettinad Health City about 4.5 to 5 km, and Chennai airport 20 to 29 km, or roughly 30 to 45 minutes outside peak hours. Rail is distant: Taramani MRTS is about 19 km away and the nearest operating metro station, St Thomas Mount, about 20 km. There is a full distance table in the location section of the project page.

The main thing to watch is traffic on OMR itself, which is heavy at peak hours between Sholinganallur and the city. Corridor 3 of Chennai Metro Phase 2, the Purple Line from Madhavaram to SIPCOT, is the single biggest infrastructure change coming to this stretch. Its nearest planned stations are Navallur and Siruseri, and CMRL's project status page targets completion of Phase 2 by the end of 2028. We look at the locality in more detail, including schools and rents, in our Egattur, OMR buying guide.

7. RERA Status: What Pre-Launch Means

Sobha Wisteria does not have a TNRERA registration number yet. Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, a project of this size generally has to be registered before it can be advertised or sold, so pre-launch activity usually takes the form of an "expression of interest" (EOI), where buyers pay a refundable amount to get priority when bookings open.

If you are offered an EOI, ask four things in writing: whether the amount is fully refundable, how many days refunds take, whether the EOI fixes the price or just your place in the queue, and what happens if the final plan (unit count, tower height, possession date) differs from what you were told. Once the TNRERA number is issued, check it on the official portal at rera.tn.gov.in and compare the registered details against what you were sold.

What is being said about booking so far comes from one channel-partner microsite, not from Sobha. It mentions tentative EOI post-dated cheques of ₹3 lakh for 1 and 2 BHK, ₹4 lakh for 3 BHK and ₹5 lakh for 4 BHK, and expects a 10:10:80 payment plan: 10% at booking, 10% at agreement and 80% in construction-linked instalments. It also announced a launch date of 15 March 2026, which has passed without a TNRERA registration. In November 2026, channel partners were asked to start collecting EOIs at the pre-launch base rate, and another channel-partner site describes the EOI as refundable, with the first 200 units expected to get pre-launch pricing. Under the RERA Act 2016, a developer cannot accept a booking or payment before registration, so treat any request for a booking amount now with caution. We track the status in the status and booking section of the project page.

8. The Developer: Sobha Limited

Sobha Limited was founded in 1995 by P.N.C. Menon and is headquartered in Bengaluru. It listed on Indian stock exchanges in 2006 and has been active in Chennai since 2012, when it launched its first projects in the city. Sobha's main selling point is backward integration: it makes its own concrete, runs its own interiors, glazing and metal works units, and builds with its own teams instead of subcontractors. That is why Sobha homes generally carry a resale premium over similar projects nearby, and it is the strongest reason to consider Wisteria at a higher rate per sq ft than its neighbours. A marketing-partner site credits Sobha with over 500 completed projects across 27 cities in 14 states, about 7,621 employees (March 2026) and 1,456 quality checks on each home before handover.

9. Who Sobha Wisteria Suits

  • A good fit: buyers who want anything from a 1 BHK to a 4 BHK from a branded developer, work along OMR or in SIPCOT, value build quality over the lowest price, and can wait until around 2029 or 2030 for possession.
  • A weaker fit: buyers with an all-in budget under about ₹1 Cr (even the 1 BHK starts near ₹86 L* before extras), investors looking mainly for rental yield at a rate well above the area average, and anyone who needs to move in within two years.

Our Honest Assessment

Sobha Wisteria has a lot going for it: a developer with a real reputation for construction quality, a location next to Chennai's largest IT cluster with a metro line planned to end nearby, and a full spread of layouts from 1 to 4 BHK. If the 8.5-acre, 650-home version holds up, the density is also lower than most high-rise launches on OMR.

The caution is equally real. The project has no TNRERA number yet, the published figures do not agree, the base rate of ₹12,999 to ₹13,999 per sq ft is well above the local range before any extra charges, and possession is at least three years away. None of these are reasons to ignore the project, but they are reasons not to pay anything non-refundable until the registration is out.
