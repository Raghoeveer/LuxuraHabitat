Sattva Vasanta Cove: Price, Layout & RERA Review

By Narayanan Rajesh•August 24, 2026•10 Min Read

Off Bellary Road in Devanahalli, Sattva Vasanta Cove is a compact new launch that breaks from the multi-configuration norm of the airport corridor: it's Studio and 1 BHK only, on a modest 1.3 acres. That narrow focus makes it easy to evaluate quickly, but also means the numbers matter more than usual. At 200 units on 1.3 acres, this is a dense, efficiency-first product aimed squarely at first-time buyers and rental-yield investors rather than end-user families. This review works through the developer's own configuration table to lay out pricing, floor plans, possession, RERA status and who this project actually makes sense for.

1. The Developer: Sattva Group

Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group) is one of Bengaluru's most established real estate developers. In Devanahalli alone, the group's portfolio already includes the larger-format Sattva Aeropolis and the integrated township Sattva City. Vasanta Cove is the developer's smallest-ticket entry into the same corridor, built around a single, focused Studio/1 BHK format rather than a broad configuration spread. That focus is deliberate: rather than competing on unit variety, Sattva is betting that a well-branded, well-located, small-format product can capture the segment of buyers and investors who are priced out of the corridor's larger apartments but still want a tier-one developer name on their title deed.

2. Location: Off Bellary Road, Devanahalli

Sattva Vasanta Cove sits off Bellary Road (NH-44) in Devanahalli, North Bengaluru, within the immediate vicinity of the KIADB Aerospace Park, an upcoming Foxconn facility and Prestige Tech Cloud. Key proximity metrics from the developer's own location data:

  • Kempegowda International Airport: ~5–10 mins by road
  • KIADB Aerospace Park: Nearby
  • Foxconn (upcoming facility): Nearby
  • Prestige Tech Cloud: Nearby
  • NH-44 / Bellary Road: Direct access
  • Metro Phase 2B: Upcoming access

This is a more industrial/aerospace-employment-driven location story than the airport-travel-convenience pitch typical of other Devanahalli launches, which is worth keeping in mind if you're evaluating this primarily as a rental play. Direct NH-44 access also means residents aren't dependent on internal Devanahalli roads to reach the highway, a small but real convenience during peak traffic hours when arterial roads elsewhere in the corridor can back up.

3. What You Actually Get

Sattva Vasanta Cove spans 1.3 acres across 2 towers, each rising 2 basements + ground + 12/13 floors, for a total of 200 units: 163 Studio apartments and 37 1 BHK apartments. There is no 2 BHK or larger configuration in this project; buyers looking for a family-sized home should look at Sattva Aeropolis or Sattva City instead.

Verified Unit Sizes (developer's configuration table)

  • Studio: 533-625 sq.ft. · 163 units
  • 1 BHK: 887-952 sq.ft. · 37 units

The clubhouse is sized at roughly 12,000 sq.ft. across G+2 levels, with co-working space, an influencer/creative zone and indoor game lounges: a clear nod to the young-professional, work-from-home-adjacent buyer this project is designed for, alongside a podium swimming pool, outdoor gym, yoga/meditation deck, jogging tracks, an amphitheatre, party lawn, bar lounge, tree decks and a summer lounge. On the safety side, the developer lists 100% power backup, 24/7 CCTV surveillance and covered parking as standard across both towers.

Two towers and 200 units on 1.3 acres is a tighter footprint than most Devanahalli launches, which typically spread larger unit counts across bigger parcels. That density is the direct consequence of the Studio/1 BHK-only format: smaller average unit sizes mean more homes fit on the same land, which is also what keeps the entry price low. It's a trade-off worth naming plainly rather than glossing over: buyers get a genuinely compact ticket size, but should expect a busier, more tightly packed community than a lower-density project would offer.

At roughly ₹59 Lakhs for a 533 sq.ft. Studio, Sattva Vasanta Cove works out to approximately ₹11,000-11,100 per sq.ft. at the entry end. That's a meaningfully lower ticket size than Sattva Aeropolis's larger-format inventory in the same corridor, which is the project's core positioning: a smaller cheque size to get into a Sattva-branded, airport-corridor address. As with any pre-completion project, the per-sq.ft. rate typically climbs at higher floors and better-facing units, so treat the entry price as a floor, not a flat rate across the whole configuration.

5. Who Sattva Vasanta Cove Is Actually Built For

Devanahalli's growth story has moved beyond the airport itself. The KIADB Aerospace Park, Foxconn's upcoming facility and Prestige Tech Cloud are drawing a large manufacturing and technology workforce to the immediate vicinity of Vasanta Cove, and that workforce needs housing close to the workplace. A Studio/1 BHK-only project sitting directly off Bellary Road is well positioned to capture exactly that demand, which is the clearest lens for evaluating this project: not as a family home, but as a housing product for a specific, growing employment base.

Two buyer profiles fit this project best. The first is the young professional or first-time buyer who wants a Sattva-branded address at the lowest possible entry price into the corridor, without needing the space of a 2 or 3 BHK. The second is the rental-yield investor: Studio and 1 BHK formats are typically the first configurations to lease out to single professionals and dual-income couples working nearby, and as KIADB Aerospace Park and Foxconn ramp up hiring, Vasanta Cove's proximity, combined with direct NH-44 access and a full lifestyle clubhouse, positions it to see steady rental interest from that workforce over time. Buyers evaluating this purely for personal end-use as a family home, or who want more breathing room than a Studio or 1 BHK offers, are better served by Sattva Aeropolis or Sattva City in the same portfolio.

6. Possession Timeline

Target possession for Sattva Vasanta Cove is December 2030, per its RERA registration: a longer runway than several other Devanahalli launches, reflecting its early launch stage. Buyers should factor this extended timeline into their investment horizon, particularly rental-yield investors who are underwriting a specific holding period before the unit is ready to lease.

Our Honest Assessment

Sattva Vasanta Cove is a narrow, focused bet: a well-known developer, a genuinely small ticket size, and a location that's more about proximity to Devanahalli's growing aerospace and manufacturing employment base than the airport-travel convenience story other projects lean on. The trade-offs are real too. Studio and 1 BHK only, a fairly dense 200 units on 1.3 acres, and a long runway to December 2030 possession are all things to weigh against the lower entry price before committing capital.

For a young professional or first-time investor who wants Sattva's brand and Devanahalli's growth story at the lowest possible entry price, this is worth a closer look, and it's a reasonable candidate for a rental-yield play given the aerospace and manufacturing hiring building up around it. Families or buyers wanting more space are better served by Sattva Aeropolis or Sattva City in the same corridor. With RERA registration PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/041225/008310 confirmed and no conflicts found, the project clears our basic legal check, but as always, verify current pricing and construction status directly before booking.
