What Is Assetz Codename Paradise?

Assetz Codename Paradise is the pre-launch working title for a low-density, Balinese-themed 4 BHK villament community developed by Assetz Property Group on Hennur Road, North Bangalore. The project has since been officially branded as Assetz Mizu & Ki, though many aggregator listings, brochures and earlier marketing material still reference the Codename Paradise name.

If you're seeing both names online and wondering whether they're different projects — they are not. "Codename Paradise" and "Assetz Mizu & Ki" refer to the same 188-unit development across 8.2 acres, with 55% open space and Balinese-themed landscaping.

Why Was It Called "Codename Paradise"?

Like several Assetz projects before it, this development carried an internal working title — "Codename Paradise" — during its pre-launch marketing phase. This is common practice among Bangalore developers: a codename is used while land acquisition, approvals and initial marketing are underway, before the formal brand name is finalised. Once Assetz settled on the brand identity, the project was renamed to Assetz Mizu & Ki, with "Mizu" and "Ki" being Japanese words for water and life energy, reflecting the project's nature-oriented, Balinese design theme.

You may still encounter the "Codename Paradise" name on third-party aggregator sites (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com), older broker brochures, and some YouTube walkthroughs. All of these refer to the same project.

Location & Connectivity

Assetz Codename Paradise (now Mizu & Ki) sits off the Hennur Road corridor in North Bangalore, with connectivity to:

  • Manyata Tech Park and Karle Town Centre — the corridor's core IT employment hubs
  • Kirloskar Business Park
  • Bagalur Aerospace SEZ / KIADB Park
  • Thanisandra Main Road and the Outer Ring Road (ORR)
  • Upcoming Nagawara Metro Station and a short drive to Thanisandra Railway Station
  • A toll-free, signal-free route to Kempegowda International Airport via the Hennur-Bagalur Road

Schools in the vicinity include Delhi Public School (DPS) North, Legacy School and Vidyashilp Academy. Healthcare is covered by Aster CMI Hospital and Motherhood Hospital.

What Do You Get? The Villament Format

Every unit in Assetz Codename Paradise is a 4 BHK villament — roughly 3,060 sq ft of super built-up area — with a private backyard of up to 500 sq ft, a large private terrace, and a dedicated 2-car garage. The project is built as a low-rise B+G+4 structure housing just 188 homes across 8.2 acres, which works out to roughly 23 units per acre. That's a fraction of the 100+ units per acre typical of high-rise apartment projects on the same corridor.

The 55% open space ratio with Balinese-themed landscaping — gardens, water features and shaded pavilions — is a genuine differentiator. RERA & Legal Status

As of the time of writing, Assetz Codename Paradise / Assetz Mizu & Ki is at pre-launch stage with its project RERA registration still awaited. This means the usual RERA-based protections — locked-in possession date, escrow requirements, penalty clauses — are not yet in place.

  • Project RERA Number: Awaited (pre-launch)
  • Advertiser/Agent RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/AG/170823/000045

We recommend verifying RERA status independently on rera.karnataka.gov.in before paying any non-refundable amount. Search under "Registered Projects" for "Assetz Mizu and Ki" or "Assetz Codename Paradise."

Our Honest Take

Assetz Codename Paradise pairs a credible developer with a genuinely differentiated product — low-density villaments with private gardens and 2-car garages on Hennur Road are not marketing fluff.
