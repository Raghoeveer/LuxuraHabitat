Yelahanka's Flyover Nears Completion: What It Means, and Doesn't, for Goyal Riviera Glade Buyers

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published November 27, 2026•10 Min Read

Yelahanka's long-running flyover project is, by most late-2026 accounts, finally approaching the finish line, though exactly how close depends on which report you read. A real-estate trade blog dated October 21, 2026 put the elevated road at about 85% complete with a December 2026 completion target; a Deccan Herald report on a Greater Bengaluru Authority review of stalled flyover projects cited 80%; and an earlier account put progress closer to 70% against a September 2026 target that had already slipped by the time it was reported. Construction on the project began back in 2022, and it has missed more than one announced deadline since. For anyone evaluating Goyal Riviera Glade, the 112-villa gated community on MVIT Road in Yelahanka, this long-delayed piece of infrastructure is worth understanding properly, both what it is and, just as importantly, what it is not.

1. What the Flyover Actually Is, and Isn't

Here is the honest geography: the flyover under construction sits on Doddaballapur Main Road, one of the radial roads running out of Yelahanka town. Goyal Riviera Glade's own project page describes its access differently, via MVIT Road, off Bellary Road (Airport Road), near Bettahalasur and Chikkajala. These are two different roads radiating from Yelahanka in different directions, so it would be inaccurate to claim this specific flyover sits on Riviera Glade's direct commute route, and we won't make that claim.

What the flyover does matter for is Yelahanka town itself. Both Doddaballapur Main Road and Bellary Road converge on and around Yelahanka's town centre and railway station area, and congestion at that junction affects anyone travelling through the locality, including residents heading from MVIT Road towards the railway station, local markets, or onward connections. A Deccan Herald report separately noted that the BBMP chief had directed speedier road infrastructure works specifically in Yelahanka, suggesting civic authorities see the locality's road network, not just this one flyover, as a priority. Read together, these are signs of a locality catching up on infrastructure that lagged its residential growth, not evidence that any single project is about to get dramatically faster.

2. Why This Still Matters for Riviera Glade Buyers

Villa buyers at this price point are rarely purchasing for a daily office commute alone; many of Riviera Glade's intended buyers, described by the developer as HNIs, CXOs, entrepreneurs and business owners, are weighing the locality's long-term trajectory as much as today's travel times. Yelahanka's congestion has been a well-documented drag on the area's appeal relative to more built-out North Bengaluru micro-markets, and a steady, if slow, drumbeat of civic attention, this flyover, the BBMP directive, prior metro and underpass work near the Yelahanka airbase, is the kind of signal that supports a multi-year investment thesis even when no single project changes your commute tomorrow.

The caution is equally important: this project has already missed a September 2026 target and, on the most optimistic account, is not expected before December 2026. Treat any "almost done" framing with the same scepticism its own construction history earns. If you are buying at Riviera Glade specifically for improved connectivity, verify the state of Doddaballapur Main Road yourself on a site visit rather than relying on any single completion estimate.

4. The Homes Themselves

Riviera Glade offers only one home type across four size variants, each a Ground + 2 triplex with living, dining, kitchen, one bedroom and a covered car bay on the ground floor, three bedrooms on the first floor, and an open terrace with a toilet on top (Lotus and Olive also include a terrace pantry). Every villa has its own private garden or backyard and no common walls with neighbouring units, which is unusual at this density and is one of the project's genuine differentiators against stacked villa or row-house formats elsewhere in North Bengaluru.

5. The Sylvan Forest Clubhouse and Amenities

The community is anchored by the Sylvan Forest Clubhouse, which the developer lists as including a grand ballroom, mini theatre, social lounge, cafeteria, recreation zone, lifestyle arena, swimming pool, gymnasium, yoga room and badminton court. Outdoor amenities include sand dunes, a pickleball court, a half-basketball court, box cricket, a kids' play area, a celebration lawn, a community court, a social square, a bloom park, a literary garden, a walking trail and a dedicated paw park for pets. For a 112-villa community, this is an unusually extensive amenity list, though buyers should confirm which elements are delivered at possession versus built out in a later phase.

6. Connectivity, in the Developer's Own Figures

  • Kempegowda International Airport: about 18 km, roughly 25 minutes via Bellary Road (Airport Road)
  • Hebbal: about 12 km, roughly 20 minutes
  • Manyata Tech Park: about 14 km, roughly 25 minutes
  • Yelahanka Railway Station: about 5 km

These are the developer's own distance and time estimates, and as with any North Bengaluru project, peak-hour traffic at Yelahanka's town junctions can add meaningfully to any of them, which is exactly why the flyover's eventual completion, whenever it actually lands, is relevant background even though it sits on a different road.

7. Who Riviera Glade Suits

This is a fit for buyers who want the space, privacy and garden access of an independent villa without compromising on a gated community's security and amenities, and who are comfortable with a long-dated possession target of December 2030. It suits North Bengaluru's HNI and business-owner segment specifically, given the price band starting above Rs 6 Cr. It is a weaker fit for anyone prioritising an immediate, congestion-free commute today, since both the flyover and the broader Yelahanka road network remain work in progress.

8. Questions to Ask Before You Book

  1. The current completion date registered against PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/040326/008503 on the Karnataka RERA portal
  2. Exact plot size, built-up area and backyard/garden dimensions for your chosen variant (Rose, Iris, Lotus or Olive)
  3. Which Sylvan Forest Clubhouse amenities are complete at possession versus planned for later phases
  4. A recent, independent read on travel times from MVIT Road to Hebbal and the airport during peak hours, rather than relying solely on the developer's stated times
  5. Total out-the-door cost, including registration, stamp duty, corpus fund and any preferred-location or corner charges

The same checklist, brochure and current price list are available via the enquiry form on the Goyal Riviera Glade project page.

Our Assessment

Yelahanka's flyover progress, whatever the true percentage, is a genuine sign that the locality's infrastructure is slowly catching up with years of residential growth, and it is fair context for anyone weighing a long-term purchase in the area. It is not, however, a reason to expect Goyal Riviera Glade's own commute times to change once this particular structure opens, since the project's access road is a different one. Buy Riviera Glade for its space, privacy and clubhouse, underwritten by a 2030 possession date you should treat as a planning horizon rather than a promise, and watch Yelahanka's broader road and metro works as a slow-moving tailwind rather than a reason to pay a premium today.
