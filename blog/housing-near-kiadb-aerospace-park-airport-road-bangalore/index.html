Housing Near KIADB Aerospace Park: Airport Road's Next Employment Hub

By the Luxura Habitat Team•July 30, 2026•6 Min Read

Most coverage of Bangalore's airport corridor focuses on the airport itself — flight connectivity, the coming metro line, retail and hospitality spillover. Less discussed is a quieter but equally important demand driver sitting just off NH-44: the KIADB Aerospace Park, a dedicated aerospace and defence manufacturing hub that's steadily anchoring a distinct category of housing demand along the corridor. If you're evaluating property here, this is the search you should be running before "airport road real estate" — it points to a more specific, more durable buyer pool.

What Is KIADB Aerospace Park?

Developed by the Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board, the Aerospace Park sits near Devanahalli, close to Kempegowda International Airport, and hosts a cluster of aerospace, defence and precision-manufacturing companies alongside MRO (maintenance, repair and overhaul) facilities. Unlike a generic IT park, its tenant base skews toward engineers, technical staff and long-tenure employees on multi-year projects — a workforce profile associated with steadier housing demand than the more transient IT-services crowd.

Why This Matters for Buyers and Investors

Three factors make housing near the Aerospace Park worth a closer look on its own terms, rather than folding it into generic "near the airport" searches:

  • Employer concentration, not just proximity. A cluster of aerospace and defence manufacturers creates a defined catchment of working professionals who need housing within a practical commute — a more predictable demand signal than airport footfall alone.
  • Longer tenures, steadier rental demand. Manufacturing and defence-sector roles tend to have longer average tenures than IT services, which historically translates to more stable occupancy for landlords compared to high-churn IT-corridor rentals.
  • Under-priced relative to the demand driver. Because most search and marketing attention on this corridor centres on the airport and STRR, projects within a genuine 10–15 minute commute of the Aerospace Park itself haven't yet seen the same price discovery as, say, Devanahalli's airport-adjacent land parcels.

What "Near KIADB Aerospace Park" Actually Means on the Ground

On NH-44, a handful of projects sit within a genuine short commute of the Aerospace Park gates. Century Astoria, Century Real Estate's new pre-launch in Immencity New Town, is roughly 10 minutes from the park by road, alongside its ~15-minute distance to Kempegowda International Airport itself — making it one of the more direct options for anyone specifically targeting this employment base rather than the airport generally. Sattva Aeropolis, further along the corridor, is a comparable airport-proximate option worth cross-checking on price and configuration.

What to Check Before You Buy

If the Aerospace Park's employment base is your actual investment thesis (rather than a side benefit), a few diligence points matter more here than elsewhere on the corridor:

  • Actual drive-time, not straight-line distance. NH-44 traffic patterns during shift-change hours can meaningfully affect the real commute — verify this at the time of day relevant to shift workers, not just during a site-visit mid-morning.
  • RERA status of any project you shortlist. Pre-launch airport-corridor projects should always be checked against the Karnataka RERA portal directly — see our Century Astoria RERA review for how we approach this.
  • Rental comparables specific to the corridor, not city-wide averages — talk to a local advisor about what similar configurations are actually renting for near the park today.

Our Take

The Aerospace Park is a genuine, under-discussed demand driver on Bangalore's airport corridor — distinct from, and complementary to, the airport itself. For buyers specifically targeting this employment base, proximity to the park gates (not just to the airport) should be the primary filter, with Century Astoria and Sattva Aeropolis both worth shortlisting on that basis. As always, treat this as one input among several — verify RERA status, current pricing and actual commute times directly before committing.
