Meenakshi High Life: Price, Floor Plans & RERA Review

By the Luxura Habitat Team•September 12, 2026•11 Min Read

Tucked behind Manyata Tech Park on Rachenahalli Main Road, Meenakshi High Life is a 313-unit, twin-tower high-rise from Meenakshi Infraa Projects that leans hard into one pitch: walk-to-work distance from one of Bangalore's largest IT campuses. This is a deep-dive review covering unit sizes, pricing by configuration, amenities, project status and RERA verification, plus our honest assessment of whether the location premium is worth paying for.

1. The Developer: Meenakshi Infraa Projects

Meenakshi Infraa Projects (marketed under the Meenakshi Realty brand, with the tagline "Building Conscious Homes") is the developer behind Meenakshi High Life, also referred to on marketing collateral as "High Life Towers." The project is structured as two high-rise towers over 2 basements, a ground floor and 14 upper floors (2B+G+14), spread across approximately 3.7 acres. At roughly 313 total units, that works out to a density of about 53-54 units per acre, a relatively low-density layout for a high-rise apartment project in this part of North Bangalore, which typically translates into more generous common areas and landscaping per resident than denser towers on the same footprint.

2. Location: Rachenahalli, Behind Manyata Tech Park

Meenakshi High Life sits on Rachenahalli Main Road, close to the Jakkur / Hebbal extension corridor of North Bangalore (Pin: 560064). The project's central pitch is proximity: this stretch of Rachenahalli sits directly behind Manyata Tech Park, one of Bangalore's largest IT parks with a workforce well into six figures across its many blocks. Key proximity points, drawn from the developer's own location material:

  • Manyata Tech Park: ~1.5 km
  • Nagawara Metro Station (Blue Line): ~3.4 km
  • Hebbal Flyover: ~8.1 km
  • Aster CMI Hospital: ~5 km
  • Manipal Hospital, Hebbal: ~6.2 km
  • Bangalore Baptist Hospital: nearby, on the same general corridor
  • Rachenahalli Lake & North Hills International School: in the immediate neighbourhood

The project's own location map also places it within reach of several established North Bangalore retail and workplace anchors, including Phoenix Mall of Asia, Elements Mall, RMZ Galleria Mall and Kirloskar Business Park, along with schools such as St. George Public School, Vibgyor School and Delhi Public School in the wider Jakkur-Thanisandra belt. For a broader read on this corridor's infrastructure and rental dynamics, see our Manyata Tech Park rental yield guide.

3. Configurations, Sizes & Pricing

Meenakshi High Life offers four configurations, 2 BHK, 2.5 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK, at an average base rate of approximately ₹12,400-12,500 per sq. ft. of super built-up area. We cross-checked the developer's stated size ranges against individual unit floor plans shared in the project's own sales material: Unit A11 (2 BHK + 2T) measures exactly 1,108 sq. ft., inside the quoted 1,096-1,292 sq. ft. band; Unit C3 (3 BHK + 3T) measures 1,466 sq. ft.; and Unit C6 (3 BHK + 3T) measures 1,499 sq. ft., both inside the quoted 1,466-1,711 sq. ft. band for the 3 BHK category. This consistency between the headline size ranges and the individual floor plans is a reasonable sign that the quoted figures aren't inflated marketing numbers.

The 2.5 BHK category (~1,265 sq. ft.) is worth flagging separately: it sits between the 2 BHK and 3 BHK size bands and typically denotes a compact third room usable as a study or small bedroom rather than a full third bedroom. If your requirement is genuinely three full bedrooms, confirm the exact room dimensions for this configuration against the 3 BHK options before booking. The 4 BHK, at roughly 3,004 sq. ft., includes a home theatre room, dedicated store and multiple balconies in the layout we reviewed, positioning it as the project's premium, low-inventory configuration rather than a mainstream offering.

4. Amenities

Meenakshi High Life's amenity list leans toward wellness and family convenience rather than a sprawling sports-first clubhouse:

  • Leisure & Wellness: Heated swimming pool, infinity pool, toddler pool, health spa, clubhouse, gymnasium, salon, card room, terrace garden and sensory relaxation gardens
  • Family & Convenience: Creche/day-care centre, children's play area, in-community cafe and a multi-purpose hall
  • Sustainability & Security: EV charging point provisions, rainwater harvesting, solid waste management and 24x7 CCTV-backed security

The presence of a dedicated creche/day-care centre and a health spa alongside the more standard clubhouse-and-gym combination stands out, both are amenities more commonly marketed toward dual-income households with young children, which fits the project's positioning toward Manyata's working professional base.

5. Project Status & Possession Timeline

Meenakshi High Life is currently under construction, with an expected possession date of August 2027. As with any under-construction project, we recommend asking the developer directly for the current construction stage (foundation, structure, or finishing) and a documented payment-linked construction schedule before booking, rather than relying on marketing renders or brochure timelines alone.

7. How Meenakshi High Life Compares on the Manyata Belt

Most of the established apartment inventory near Manyata Tech Park sits on the Hennur-Thanisandra side of the corridor: projects like Concorde Neo and Orchid Salisbury on Thanisandra Main Road, or Concorde Sienna on Hennur Main Road, typically a 15-25 minute drive from the tech park depending on traffic. Meenakshi High Life's Rachenahalli address, by contrast, sits directly behind the campus at a claimed ~1.5 km, which, if commute time genuinely holds up during peak hours, is meaningfully closer than most alternatives in this price band. That proximity is also the main variable worth stress-testing on an actual site visit: Rachenahalli's internal roads and the final approach to the tech park's rear gates can behave very differently from a straight-line distance figure during the 9-10 AM and 6-7 PM peak windows.

8. Financing & Cost Considerations

At an average base rate of ₹12,400-12,500 per sq. ft., a 2 BHK at Meenakshi High Life sits in the ₹1.37-1.60 Cr range before registration, GST, parking and other developer charges, which on Karnataka properties this size typically add another 8-10% on top of the base price. For a ₹1.5 Cr unit with a 20% down payment (₹30 lakh) and the remainder financed over 20 years at a home loan rate in the 8.3-8.8% band prevailing through 2026, buyers should budget an EMI in the roughly ₹1.02-1.06 lakh per month range, before factoring in the construction-linked payment plan that under-construction projects like this one typically use (where the loan disburses in tranches tied to construction milestones rather than as a single lump sum). Since possession is expected only in August 2027, most buyers will also carry pre-EMI interest payments on the disbursed amount during construction, on top of any existing rent, so it's worth stress-testing this combined monthly outflow against your household budget before committing, rather than budgeting only for the post-possession EMI figure quoted by a sales team.

9. Who Should Consider Meenakshi High Life

Based on the pricing, configuration mix and location profile, three buyer types stand out as the best fit. First, IT and tech professionals working within Manyata Tech Park itself, or at nearby campuses like Kirloskar Business Park, for whom the ~1.5 km distance genuinely converts into a walk or a very short commute rather than a marketing claim. Second, end-users prioritising a lower entry price than the established Thanisandra and Hennur Main Road addresses, since the 2 BHK starting price here undercuts most comparable RERA-registered launches on that side of the corridor. Third, families who specifically value the creche/day-care and health spa amenities alongside the more usual clubhouse and pool, since that combination is less common on standard project amenity lists in this price band. Buyers who prioritise an established, multi-project resale market with several years of comparable transaction history should weigh the Rachenahalli location's relative newness against these advantages before deciding.

Our Honest Assessment

Meenakshi High Life's clearest strength is genuine proximity to Manyata Tech Park at a price point (2 BHK from ₹1.37 Cr) that undercuts most comparable Hennur-Thanisandra apartment launches, while still offering a fairly complete lifestyle amenity set including a creche and health spa. The relatively low density (~53-54 units/acre across just 313 units) is also a point in its favour versus denser high-rise developments nearby. The trade-offs: this is a single-project location without an established multi-project micro-market around it yet, so resale liquidity and comparable pricing data are thinner than in a more built-out corridor like Thanisandra or Hennur Main Road, and August 2027 possession means a genuine multi-year construction-risk window. If walk-to-work proximity to Manyata is your primary driver and you're comfortable with under-construction timelines, Meenakshi High Life is worth shortlisting, just verify the RERA registration (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/200923/006268) independently, confirm the current construction stage on a site visit, and cross-check the actual peak-hour commute to your specific block within Manyata Tech Park before booking.
