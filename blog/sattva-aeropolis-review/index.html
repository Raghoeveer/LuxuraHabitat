Sattva Aeropolis Review: Price, Floor Plans, RERA and Our Verdict

By Narayanan Rajesh, Luxura Habitat•Published October 1, 2026•14 Min Read

The stretch of North Bangalore around Kempegowda International Airport has become the city's most talked-about growth story. Land in the Devanahalli belt has climbed from about ₹4,500 per sq ft in 2024 to roughly ₹7,000 to ₹9,600 in 2026, and the KIADB Aerospace Park next door now hosts Boeing and RTX, with a new Wistron plant expected to bring around 3,000 jobs. Sattva Aeropolis is built to ride exactly this wave.

This review looks at the project from the buyer's side. We set out both RERA phases, read the 3 BHK layout off the builder's floor plan, work out the real rate per square foot and the all-in cost, and are candid about the trade-offs of buying into a 1,001-home township in the far north of the city.

The short answer: Sattva Aeropolis offers one of the most accessible branded entries on the airport corridor, from about ₹45.77 Lakh for a Studio and ₹60 Lakh to ₹90 Lakh for 1 to 3 BHK homes. It suits airport-belt end users and rental investors. The main trade-offs are density (about 91 homes per acre) and the far-north location, and Phase 1's RERA completion date of July 2026 has passed, so verify your tower's handover status before you pay.

1. The developer: Salarpuria Sattva

Sattva Group began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva and is one of Bengaluru's larger developers across homes, office parks and hospitality. Aeropolis is one of several Sattva bets on the airport corridor, alongside Sattva Park Cubix, Vasanta Skye and Sattva City. An established name matters on a 1,001-home build, but it reduces delivery risk rather than removing it.

  • Ask for progress against milestones: compare on-site progress with what was declared on the RERA portal.
  • See a finished floor: MIVAN quality varies with the site team, so inspect a completed sample.
  • Clarify sequencing: on a two-phase project, find out exactly what is delivered at your handover and what comes later.

2. Location: the strongest corridor, with a long reach to the rest of the city

Aeropolis sits at Boovanahalli, Udayagiri, off NH-44 and beside the KIADB Aerospace Park. Roughly 50,000 professionals work within about 10 km of the airport, and the Namma Metro Blue Line to the airport is under construction. For anyone whose work is in the north, that is hard to beat. For anyone commuting east or south, it is a real cost.

7. RERA and possession

Both phases are registered with Karnataka RERA. Phase 1 (PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/110522/004869) carried a completion date of 31 July 2026. As of this review, that date has passed, so do not rely on the project-level date. Ask for the Occupancy Certificate status of your specific tower, whether any extension has been filed on the portal, and which amenities will be running when you get your keys. Phase 2 (PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/250925/008119) has a RERA possession date of October 2028.

9. Pros and cons

What works

  • Sub-₹1 Cr entry, including a 3 BHK at about ₹90 Lakh
  • About 15 minutes to the airport, beside a growing aerospace employment hub
  • Established developer with several projects on the same corridor
  • MIVAN construction in Phase 1 for consistent finishes
  • Two phases give a choice between nearer handover and lower ticket size

What to weigh

  • High density, about 91 homes per acre
  • Phase 1's RERA date has passed; tower-level status must be verified
  • Long commutes to Whitefield, ORR and Electronic City
  • Compact third bedroom in the 3 BHK
  • Maintenance quality depends heavily on the facility manager and residents' association

Rental yields on the corridor run about 3.2% to 3.8% gross. On a ₹60 Lakh 1 BHK that implies roughly ₹16,000 to ₹19,000 a month, and on an ₹81 Lakh 2 BHK roughly ₹21,500 to ₹25,500, before maintenance and vacancy. Our Devanahalli ROI guide goes deeper on returns.

10. Who should buy, and who should skip

Good fit: airport, aerospace and logistics professionals; first-time buyers who want a branded home under ₹1 Cr; and investors targeting the airport-corridor rental pool. Phase 2's Studios suit the lowest budgets and frequent-traveller tenants.

Probably skip: buyers who want low-density, boutique living, and anyone commuting daily to the eastern or southern tech belts.

Our verdict

Sattva Aeropolis combines an accessible, sub-₹1 Cr entry with one of Bengaluru's strongest infrastructure corridors and an established developer. That is a compelling package for airport-belt end users and investors. Its trade-offs, the scale and the far-north location, are matters of fit rather than flaws. The one thing to do before anything else is to pin down the real handover status of your Phase 1 tower, since the RERA date has passed. Then check the details against the live Sattva Aeropolis project page and run the checklist above.

Sattva Aeropolis FAQs

Project basics

Sattva Aeropolis is an apartment township by Salarpuria Sattva (Sattva Group) at Boovanahalli, near Kempegowda International Airport in Devanahalli. It covers about 11 acres with roughly 1,001 homes in 11 towers of ground plus 11 floors, built in two RERA-registered phases.

Salarpuria Sattva, now branded Sattva Group, which has been building in Bengaluru since 1986. The RERA promoter entity is Trinayani Realtors Pvt. Ltd.

Phase 1 has 1, 2 and 3 BHK homes from about ₹60 Lakh, MIVAN construction and a RERA completion date of 31 July 2026. Phase 2 has 554 Studio and 1 BHK homes from about ₹45.77 Lakh with RERA possession in October 2028.

About 1,001 homes on roughly 11 acres is around 91 homes per acre. Expect a busy, amenity-led community rather than a low-density enclave.

A clubhouse with swimming pool, gymnasium, outdoor gym, yoga deck, library, indoor games, multipurpose hall, kids' play area, sports courts and landscaped walkways, shared across both phases. Ask which will be running at your handover.

Floor plans and price

Phase 1: 1 BHK 666 to 672 sq ft, 2 BHK 906 to 943 sq ft, 3 BHK 1,012 sq ft. Phase 2: Studio 448 to 500 sq ft, 1 BHK 550 to 658 sq ft.

About ₹45.77 Lakh for a Phase 2 Studio, ₹58.50 Lakh for a Phase 2 1 BHK, ₹60 Lakh for a Phase 1 1 BHK, ₹81 Lakh for a 2 BHK and ₹90 Lakh for a 3 BHK, all before GST, stamp duty and other charges.

The 1,012 sq ft 3 BHK plan shows three bedrooms (about 10'0" x 11'0", 11'0" x 10'0" and 9'0" x 8'0"), two toilets, an 11'0" x 10'0" living room, a separate dining area, a 10'0" x 6'7" kitchen and a balcony.

Dividing the starting prices by the stated Phase 1 sizes gives roughly ₹8,600 to ₹9,000 per sq ft. Phase 2's smaller homes work out higher, roughly ₹8,900 to ₹10,600 per sq ft. Confirm whether the sizes are carpet or super built-up.

On an ₹81 Lakh 2 BHK, add about ₹4 Lakh GST if under construction, about ₹4.5 Lakh stamp duty, ₹0.8 Lakh registration and roughly ₹2 to 4 Lakh for parking, deposits and charges. Budget about ₹92 Lakh to ₹95 Lakh before interiors.

GST applies to under-construction homes, generally 5% without input tax credit, or 1% for homes that qualify as affordable (up to ₹45 Lakh and 60 sq m carpet in Bengaluru). It does not apply to a completed home sold after the Occupancy Certificate. Check how your unit is invoiced.

MIVAN uses aluminium formwork to cast walls and slabs in concrete together. It gives uniform finishes, fewer cracks and good carpet efficiency, but walls are structural, so you cannot easily remove or move them later.

Under RERA, a buyer who stays in the project is entitled to interest for the delay period, and one who withdraws can claim a refund with interest. Check the agreement's delay clauses and any extension filed on the RERA portal.

Buying decision

For airport-corridor investors it is one of the more accessible branded options: sub-₹1 Cr entry, an established developer and strong employment growth nearby. Weigh that against high density and the far-north location.

With corridor yields of about 3.2% to 3.8% gross, a ₹60 Lakh 1 BHK implies roughly ₹16,000 to ₹19,000 a month and an ₹81 Lakh 2 BHK roughly ₹21,500 to ₹25,500, before maintenance and vacancy.
