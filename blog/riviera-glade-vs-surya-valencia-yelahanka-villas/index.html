Riviera Glade vs Surya Valencia: Which Yelahanka Villa Fits You?

By Narayanan Rajesh•August 24, 2026•9 Min Read

North Bengaluru's Yelahanka villa corridor has two genuinely distinct options for buyers who want an independent home rather than an apartment: Goyal Riviera Glade on MVIT Road (a new-launch, ultra-premium 4 BHK-only community) and Surya Valencia near Rajankunte (an established, more accessibly priced 4 & 5 BHK Spanish-style villa community). Both sit in the same broad Yelahanka villa belt but target noticeably different budgets, which makes this comparison genuinely useful rather than a coin-flip. Beyond the headline price gap, they also differ in density, villa structure, amenity mix and how far along each project actually is, all of which matter more than the price tag alone once you're deciding where to put your money.

2. Riviera Glade: The Ultra-Premium Newcomer

Riviera Glade sits on MVIT Road in Bettahalasur / Chikkajala, Yelahanka, near the Sir M. Visvesvaraya Institute of Technology, with Kempegowda International Airport roughly 20-30 minutes away and Manyata Tech Park, the KIADB Aerospace Park and the Outer Ring Road each around 20 minutes out. It's priced almost double Surya Valencia's entry point, and the product justifies at least part of that gap: it's exclusively 4 BHK (no smaller or larger options), spread across four villa variants (Rose at ~3,190 sq ft, Iris at ~3,300 sq ft, Lotus at ~3,475 sq ft and Olive at ~3,550 sq ft), built at a lower density of roughly 12 villas per acre, and every villa is a Ground + 1 + Terrace triplex with no common walls with its neighbours: a structurally independent format rather than a shared-wall row layout.

The Sylvan Forest Clubhouse anchors the amenity offering, with a grand ballroom, indoor games, media room, social lounge and cafeteria, alongside a swimming pool, gymnasium, squash and pickleball courts, a half-basketball court, box cricket, a yoga room, a bloom park, a literary garden, sand dunes, walking trails, a pet park and a celebration lawn. That's a genuinely amenity-dense package for a project of only 112 villas, and it's a meaningful part of what the higher price is buying beyond just square footage and land.

Best for: HNI buyers and business owners with a ₹6.5 Cr+ budget who prioritise privacy, low density and structural independence over a shorter possession timeline (Riviera Glade's expected possession is December 2030) or immediate resale liquidity.

3. Surya Valencia: The Established, Accessible Villa

Surya Valencia sits near Rajankunte, Yelahanka, close to the Taj Urban Resort and Angsana Oasis, with access to the airport, the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) and the Peripheral Ring Road (PRR). It is already RERA registered across two phases and offers a wider size range: 4 BHK Estate Villas from ₹3.54 Cr at 3,345 sq ft, and larger 5 BHK Regal Edition villas from ₹4.59 Cr at 3,893 sq ft, both with a triple-height living room ceiling and a private backyard, at roughly half of Riviera Glade's entry price. The Regal Edition adds a private swimming pool, a pre-fit lift, home automation and a sky pergola on top of the shared features.

Its own amenity package is anchored by a 40,000 sq ft clubhouse managed by Illesium Clubs, with an azure pool, party lawn and amphitheatre, yoga and meditation corner, outdoor fitness area, putting green, reflexology walk and landscaped gardens across 40+ named facilities. With 122 villas already allocated across both phases, it also has a larger, more established base for future resale and rental comparables than a brand-new launch like Riviera Glade can offer at this stage.

Best for: Buyers who want a genuine villa lifestyle without the ultra-premium price tag, families needing a 5 BHK option, and buyers who value an already-registered, more established project over a brand-new launch.

4. Location & Connectivity Compared

Both projects sit within the broader Yelahanka villa belt, but they lean on different parts of the corridor's growth story. Riviera Glade's MVIT Road location trades on established proximity to Manyata Tech Park and the Outer Ring Road, alongside the KIADB Aerospace Park and the airport, putting it closer to existing IT and aerospace employment clusters today. Surya Valencia's Rajankunte location leans more on the STRR and PRR ring-road pipeline, infrastructure that is still being built out but is expected to connect this stretch to other peripheral employment hubs over the next several years without a central-city commute. Practically, that means Riviera Glade's connectivity story is more established right now, while Surya Valencia's is more dependent on infrastructure that is still in progress.

5. Amenities & Density Compared

Riviera Glade's amenity package is built around exclusivity: 112 villas at roughly 12 per acre means fewer households sharing the Sylvan Forest Clubhouse's ballroom, sports courts and pet park, and the no-common-walls layout adds a further layer of privacy most row-villa projects can't offer. Surya Valencia's 40,000 sq ft Illesium-managed clubhouse is larger in absolute terms and includes resort-style features like the azure pool and reflexology walk, but it's shared across a denser 122-villa layout across two phases. Neither is objectively better: it's a straightforward trade-off between a smaller, more exclusive amenity base and a larger, more resort-scaled one shared among more households.

Our Honest Assessment

This comparison isn't close on price, and it shouldn't be treated as one: Riviera Glade and Surya Valencia serve different budgets within the same Yelahanka villa corridor. If your ceiling is closer to ₹4-5 Cr and you want an already-registered project with a 5 BHK option, Surya Valencia is the more practical choice, and its two-phase RERA registration and larger 122-villa base give it a firmer footing for buyers who want to see an established project rather than a fresh launch. If you have a ₹6.5 Cr+ budget and want the lowest density, most exclusive villa product currently launching in this corridor, Riviera Glade's case is strong, provided you're comfortable with its longer runway to possession and a newer, less-tested track record on the ground.

Both are legitimate options from credible North Bengaluru developers; the deciding factor is budget and timeline, not developer trust. If you're weighing this decision purely on connectivity, lean toward Riviera Glade for infrastructure that's already in place today; if you're comfortable underwriting a multi-year infrastructure story for a lower entry price, Surya Valencia's Rajankunte location is the more affordable bet on the same broader corridor.
