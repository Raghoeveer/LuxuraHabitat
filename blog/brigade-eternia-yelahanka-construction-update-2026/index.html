Brigade Eternia Construction Update: Inside the Yelahanka Site as Towers Take Shape

By the Luxura Habitat Team•November 19, 2026•9 Min Read

Brigade Enterprises' latest construction disclosures for Brigade Eternia, its 1,124-unit lakeside residential development in Yelahanka, North Bengaluru, show the project moving steadily through its structural phase. Updates tracked on the developer's own project listing and picked up by property trackers through October 2026 show that Tower C has completed the slab casting for its first floor, while window installation and internal tiling work are simultaneously underway across several other towers on the site. For a New Launch project that is still some years from possession, this kind of granular, tower-by-tower progress disclosure matters more to a booked buyer than any brochure claim, because it is a verifiable account of what is physically happening on site today, not a projection of what will happen eventually.

1. What the October Update Actually Shows

Per the available construction tracking, Tower C's first-floor slab has been cast, a structural milestone that confirms the tower has moved past its foundation and plinth stage into vertical construction. Elsewhere on the roughly 14-acre site, work crews are simultaneously installing windows and laying tiles in units across towers that are further along in the build cycle. That means different blocks within the same 1,124-unit, 12-tower development are currently sitting at visibly different stages: some are still casting floor slabs, while others are already receiving windows and finished flooring. That spread is entirely ordinary for a project phased across multiple towers on a single large site, but it is useful information for a prospective buyer. A unit in a tower that has already reached the fit-out stage is, in practical terms, closer to being move-in ready than a unit in a tower that has only just begun casting its upper floors, even though both carry the same project brand, the same amenities, and broadly the same marketed possession date.

2. Why a Construction Milestone Beats a Launch Announcement

Brigade Eternia was launched with considerable fanfare in 2025, positioned as a roughly Rs 2,700 crore lakeside development aimed squarely at North Bengaluru's airport-corridor demand. Launch-stage marketing numbers, unit counts, renderings, projected amenities, are necessarily aspirational; a slab-casting and tiling update eighteen months later is not. It is the kind of fact a site engineer can confirm on a walkthrough, and it is the clearest signal available to a buyer that a project is actually progressing rather than sitting idle after its launch event. This distinction matters more in North Bengaluru specifically, where the sheer volume of new launches along the airport corridor over the past three years means buyers increasingly have to differentiate between projects on execution, not just on brochure promises. Brigade's own Q2 FY27 results, reported on October 29, 2026, showed the company's consolidated EBITDA margin expanding to roughly 38% alongside residential collections of about Rs 1,633 crore running close to pre-sales of around Rs 1,827 crore for the quarter, a combination that generally indicates a developer with the cash flow discipline to keep construction funded across its pipeline, Eternia included, rather than one stretching thin across too many simultaneous launches.

3. What Brigade Eternia Offers, and Where Pricing Stands Today

Brigade Eternia occupies roughly 14 acres in Yelahanka, within reach of the Bengaluru airport corridor that has anchored much of North Bengaluru's recent residential demand. The development comprises 1,124 units spread across 12 towers, built around a central lake-facing open space, and offers 3 and 4 BHK apartments designed with 2-side open layouts intended to maximise cross-ventilation. Per the project's own current listing, 3 BHK units range from about 1,653 to 2,111 sq. ft., while 4 BHK units run from roughly 2,714 to 2,926 sq. ft. On pricing, the project's own page lists 3 BHK apartments starting at Rs 2.25 crore, with the development overall positioned from Rs 2.47 crore across its configurations; exact 4 BHK pricing is available on request from the developer, and all figures remain subject to the usual "onwards" caveat attached to under-construction inventory. The project carries RERA registration under number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/070325/007559, and its own disclosed possession target is Mid 2029, a timeline buyers should treat as the authoritative reference point over any secondary estimate found elsewhere online.

4. What to Verify Before You Treat This as a Done Deal

A construction update is encouraging, but it is not a substitute for direct verification. Buyers should ask Brigade specifically which tower and floor a shortlisted unit sits in, since, as this update shows, towers on the same site can be months or quarters apart in actual progress despite sharing one possession date on paper. It is also worth asking for the project's current RERA-filed quarterly progress report, which is a more complete and legally mandated account of construction status than any single tower-level update picked up by a property portal. Given that Brigade Eternia remains firmly in its under-construction phase with possession several years out, buyers should also factor in the usual risks of early-stage bookings, construction-linked payment plans, the possibility of schedule slippage on individual towers, and the importance of confirming final carpet area and specification at each milestone rather than relying solely on the floor plans quoted at launch.

Yelahanka's Brigade Footprint, and How Eternia Compares

Brigade Eternia is not the only large North Bengaluru launch competing for buyer attention in this price band. For a side-by-side look at how it stacks up against another prominent Yelahanka entrant, see our comparison of Century Kindle versus Brigade Eternia, which weighs the two projects on configuration, pricing and amenities. Within Brigade's own broader Bengaluru portfolio, buyers evaluating Eternia alongside the builder's other live developments can also look at Brigade Banashankari in South Bengaluru and Brigade Silvercrest, both of which offer a useful read on how the developer's execution has played out on projects at different stages of their own construction cycles.

The Bottom Line

Tower C's first-floor slab cast and the window and tiling work now visible across other towers are small facts individually, but together they are a meaningfully better signal of Brigade Eternia's real-world progress than anything in the project's original launch materials. The picture they paint, a large, multi-tower site advancing on a staggered but active schedule, backed by a developer whose latest company-wide results show healthy collections and improving margins, is a reasonably encouraging one for buyers who have already booked or are considering a unit here. It does not remove the need for ordinary diligence: confirm your specific tower's progress, check the RERA-filed status, and treat the Mid 2029 possession date as a target to track rather than a guarantee.
