Surya Valencia Rajankunte: Price, Villas & What to Verify

By Narayanan Rajesh•August 24, 2026•10 Min Read

Surya Valencia is a newly launched, RERA-registered, Spanish-themed villa community near Rajankunte, Yelahanka: a genuinely uncommon product type for this stretch of North Bengaluru, which is otherwise dominated by high-rise apartment supply. Here's an independent look at the location, pricing, villa specs, amenities and what a serious buyer should verify before booking.

2. Location: Rajankunte, Yelahanka

Surya Valencia sits near Rajankunte in Yelahanka, North Bengaluru, close to the Taj Urban Resort and Angsana Oasis, with easy access to the airport, the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) and the Peripheral Ring Road (PRR). This is a quieter, more resort-adjacent stretch of the Yelahanka corridor than the denser apartment belt closer to the city, which fits the low-rise, private-pool villa positioning: buyers here are typically trading some proximity to daily conveniences for space, privacy and a genuinely different living format.

The STRR and PRR are both material to how this corridor develops over the next several years. Once operational, they connect Rajankunte to other employment and residential clusters around Bengaluru's periphery without routing through the city centre, which is the same infrastructure logic driving land-value appreciation across North Bengaluru's other growth corridors. Buyers evaluating Surya Valencia purely as an investment should treat these road projects as a multi-year tailwind rather than an immediate convenience.

At roughly ₹10,600/sq ft for the 4 BHK and ₹11,800/sq ft for the 5 BHK Regal Edition, Surya Valencia prices as a premium independent-villa product rather than a budget layout, consistent with the private-pool, resort-clubhouse positioning. The roughly ₹1 Cr jump for the Regal Edition buys a genuinely distinct package (private pool, automation and a pre-fit lift), not just 548 extra sq ft, so buyers should weigh whether those specific features justify the premium for their use case rather than treating it purely as a per-sq.ft. upgrade.

4. The Villa Format Itself

Both villa types are laid out across four levels: a ground floor with parking, living/dining and kitchen; a first floor and second floor with bedrooms and a family lounge; and a terrace/third floor with a multi-purpose room or additional terrace bedroom. That vertical stacking is standard for independent villas on a compact plot footprint, and it's worth understanding before you buy: unlike a single-level home, day-to-day movement between the kitchen, bedrooms and terrace involves stairs (or the reserved lift space) across all four levels, which matters more for elderly residents than it does for a young family.

Every villa reserves space for a lift, and both configurations get the same signature triple-height living room ceiling: a genuinely differentiating architectural feature that few villa projects in this price band offer as standard. On the 5 BHK Regal Edition, that lift comes pre-fit rather than merely reserved as space, which is one of the concrete, checkable differences buyers are paying the roughly ₹1 Cr premium for. The private backyard, at 300-700 sq ft depending on plot position, is usable outdoor space that apartment buyers in the same corridor simply don't get, though the range is wide enough that it's worth confirming the specific backyard size for the exact plot you're considering rather than assuming the top end of the range.

5. The Clubhouse & Master-Planned Amenities

The master plan lists over 40 named facilities anchored by a 40,000 sq ft clubhouse managed by Illesium Clubs, including an azure pool, party lawn with amphitheatre, yoga and meditation corner, outdoor fitness area, putting green, reflexology walk, and multiple landscaped gardens and trellised walkways. This level of amenity curation is closer to what large apartment townships offer than what most standalone villa layouts provide, and it's a meaningful part of Surya Valencia's pitch: buyers get the privacy and space of an independent villa without giving up the shared clubhouse lifestyle that's typically the preserve of large apartment complexes.

6. Who Surya Valencia Is Actually Built For

Rajankunte's investment case rests on the same logic driving the rest of North Bengaluru's periphery: proximity to the airport corridor combined with upcoming ring-road infrastructure (STRR and PRR) that connects this stretch to other employment clusters without a central-city commute. Layered on top of that is a genuinely scarce product type. Spanish-themed, private-pool independent villas with a full resort-style clubhouse are uncommon in this belt, which is otherwise dominated by high-rise apartment supply, and that scarcity is itself part of the long-term demand case for buyers with a multi-year horizon.

The clearest fit is a buyer who specifically wants an independent villa rather than an apartment: more privacy, a private backyard, and (on the Regal Edition) a private pool, while still getting shared clubhouse amenities most standalone villa layouts don't offer. It's a weaker fit for buyers prioritising short possession timelines, since both phases run out to 2028 and 2030, or for anyone comparing strictly on a per-sq.ft. basis against high-rise apartment inventory in the same corridor, since the villa format itself commands a premium independent of location.

7. Developer, RERA & Possession: What We Verified

Surya Valencia is developed by Surya Developers, based in Yelahanka, Bengaluru, and the project is split into two separately RERA-registered phases. Phase I covers 44 villas across 3.64 acres under RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/171224/007305, with possession scheduled for December 2028. Phase II covers 78 villas across 5.32 acres under RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/020125/007343, with possession scheduled for December 2030. Always cross-check both numbers directly on the Karnataka RERA portal before any booking amount changes hands, and confirm which phase your specific villa falls under: the possession date differs by two years between the two.

Our Honest Assessment

Surya Valencia's core appeal is real: Spanish-themed independent villas with private backyards, triple-height ceilings and (on the Regal Edition) a private pool are a genuinely scarce product on the Yelahanka-Rajankunte corridor, which is otherwise almost entirely apartment supply. The 40,000 sq ft Illesium-managed clubhouse adds township-level amenity scale that most villa layouts skip, and the location's STRR/PRR-linked growth story is a reasonable multi-year tailwind for buyers willing to hold through both possession timelines.

With a named developer, dual RERA registration and published possession dates for both phases, this listing clears the basic diligence bar that many pre-launch projects don't. The honest caveats are the price point, well above apartment inventory in the same corridor, and the possession runway, out to December 2028 for Phase I and December 2030 for Phase II. Buyers should confirm which phase a specific villa unit belongs to before booking, since the two carry possession timelines two years apart, and should independently verify both RERA numbers before any payment changes hands.
