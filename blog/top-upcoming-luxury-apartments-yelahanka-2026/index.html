Top 3 Upcoming Luxury Apartments in Yelahanka (2026 Reviews)

By Narayanan Rajesh•July 22, 2026•7 Min Read

Yelahanka has transitioned from a quiet suburb to the absolute center of luxury real estate in North Bangalore. Driven by the expansion of the Kempegowda International Airport and the imminent arrival of the Namma Metro Blue Line, tier-1 developers are aggressively launching premium residential projects to cater to HNIs, NRIs, and senior tech executives.

If you are looking to invest upwards of ₹1.5 Cr to ₹3 Cr in Yelahanka this year, you are likely overwhelmed by the options. Based on our strict criteria—legal clarity, construction quality, developer track record, and lifestyle design—here are the top 3 luxury apartment projects currently defining the market in 2026.

1. Assetz Zen & Sato: The Kyoto-Inspired Retreat

Assetz Property Group has taken a wildly different approach to luxury with Zen & Sato. Instead of packing thousands of units into a massive concrete jungle, they have opted for a low-density, Kyoto-minimalist design.

  • The Vibe: Tranquil, balanced, and green. It features only 4 towers across ~7 acres, dedicating 74% of the land to open spaces and Japanese-style gardens.
  • Configurations: Strictly spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK units, designed for cross-ventilation and natural light.
  • Pricing: Starting from ₹2.70 Cr onwards.
  • Why it makes the top 3: It caters perfectly to buyers who want genuine exclusivity and low crowd density without moving out to the extreme periphery of the city.

Dive deeper: Read our full, unbiased Assetz Zen & Sato Review or check the project details here.

2. Brigade Eternia: The Corporate Standard

Brigade Group requires no introduction in Bangalore. Brigade Eternia brings their signature corporate reliability and premium finish to Yelahanka.

  • The Vibe: Modern, cosmopolitan, and highly efficient. It appeals strongly to IT professionals working in Manyata Tech Park who want a hassle-free, premium lifestyle.
  • Amenities: Expect world-class clubhouses, extensive sports facilities, and smart-home ready infrastructure.
  • Investment Angle: Properties by Brigade consistently command premium rental yields and offer excellent liquidity when you decide to sell.

For buyers who prioritize brand reliability and a community of like-minded corporate professionals, Brigade Eternia is currently the safest bet in the luxury segment.

3. Concorde Mayfair: The Boutique Luxury Experience

While massive townships have their appeal, a growing segment of buyers is looking for "boutique luxury." This is where Concorde Mayfair shines.

  • The Vibe: Intimate, highly personalized, and architecturally striking. It doesn't aim to be the biggest project in Yelahanka, but rather the most thoughtfully designed.
  • Target Audience: Perfect for established families and senior executives who value privacy and bespoke amenities over crowded mega-clubhouses.
  • Location Edge: Strategically positioned to offer a quieter residential experience while remaining minutes away from Yelahanka's arterial roads and international schools.

How to Choose?

Choosing between these three comes down to your primary driver. Want low-density Zen living? Assetz. Want guaranteed corporate-grade liquidity and amenities? Brigade. Prefer private, boutique luxury? Concorde.
