Prestige's ₹2,850 Cr Twin Bengaluru Launch: Parklane & Garden Breeze

By Narayanan Rajesh•September 29, 2026•8 Min Read

Prestige Estates Projects has unveiled two new Bengaluru residential developments with a combined revenue potential of ₹2,850 crore, according to reports from Business Standard, EquityBulls and Whalesbook. The first is Prestige Parklane in Devanahalli's KIADB Hi-Tech, Defence & Aerospace Park, with an estimated Gross Development Value (GDV) of roughly ₹1,750 crore. The second is Garden Breeze, a 10-acre phase within the larger Prestige City township on Sarjapur Road, with an estimated GDV of ₹1,100 crore. Prestige Parklane is a project we already track closely on this site, so the twin launch is a useful moment to separate the genuine signal in this news from what it doesn't change for a buyer evaluating it.

1. The Two Projects, Side by Side

Garden Breeze spans 10 acres within The Prestige City, Sarjapur, across 4 towers of Ground+30/22 floors and 2 basements, comprising 655 apartments in 2 and 3-Bedroom configurations from 1,081 sq ft up to 1,789 sq ft, with an approximate saleable area of 0.93 million sq ft. Prestige Parklane, on the other side of the city in Devanahalli, is considerably larger in unit count: 9 towers across 11.91 acres of a 32.26-acre master plan, with 1,788 apartments across six configurations from a 1 BHK Classic to a 3 BHK Ultima. Together, the two launches point to Prestige Group deploying capital across both of Bengaluru's fastest-growing residential arcs, the established Sarjapur IT corridor in the east and the airport-led Devanahalli belt in the north, within a similar window.

2. Prestige Parklane: What's Actually Confirmed

Since Prestige Parklane is a project we track directly, here's what its own listing confirms rather than what the wider launch story implies. It occupies 32.26 acres inside KIADB's Hi-Tech, Defence and Aerospace Park, Phase 2, at Muddenahalli Village, Devanahalli, of which 11.91 acres form the residential precinct, nine towers of 3 basements plus Ground plus 24 upper floors arranged around a landscaped central park. It launched on 25 September 2026 and carries Karnataka RERA registration PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/150926/008941, granted 15 September 2026, with completion declared as 31 December 2030 per the filing.

Six configurations are on offer: 1 BHK Classic (567-571 sq ft) from around ₹56 Lakhs* onwards, 2 BHK Optima (828-880 sq ft), 2 BHK Ultima (1,087-1,103 sq ft), 3 BHK Aspire (1,445-1,469 sq ft), 3 BHK Premia (1,597-1,662 sq ft), and 3 BHK Ultima (1,757-1,801 sq ft) up to around ₹1.64 Cr* onwards, spread across 32 distinct layouts. As with any new launch, treat this pricing as indicative until Prestige Group's formal price list is confirmed, and independently verify the RERA status on rera.karnataka.gov.in before booking.

3. Garden Breeze: Context, Not a Site Recommendation

We want to be upfront that Garden Breeze is not a project marketed on this site, and we have no independent facts to add beyond the publicly reported GDV, unit count and configuration range summarised above. We're mentioning it here only because the twin-launch framing is the actual news story, and because it's a useful data point on how Prestige Group is currently allocating its Bengaluru launch pipeline across two very different micro-markets and price bands. If Sarjapur Road specifically interests you, that's a conversation for our advisory team separate from anything on this page.

4. What This Doesn't Change: Prestige Springwood

It's worth noting that Prestige Group also has a third live listing in the same Devanahalli corridor as Parklane: Prestige Springwood, a pre-launch collection of just 60 exclusive 4 BHK row villas on 9 acres in Yerthiganahalli, priced from roughly ₹10 Cr* to ₹12.5 Cr* for 4,000 to 5,000 sq ft layouts. Springwood is not part of this particular ₹2,850 crore twin-launch story, and as of its own listing, its RERA application has not yet been filed; its EOI phase began 16 September 2026 with formal launch and bookings targeted from 25 October 2026. The point isn't to conflate it with Parklane or Garden Breeze, it's the opposite: three Prestige Group listings in or near the same corridor, at three very different stages, formats and price points, is exactly the situation where checking each project's own RERA status and timeline individually matters most.

5. What a Twin Launch Actually Signals

A developer bringing two sizeable projects to market in different corridors within a similar window is generally a sign of active capital deployment and confidence in near-term absorption, which is a reasonable, mildly positive signal about a large builder's execution pace. It is not, on its own, a reason to book at either project. Prestige Parklane's case rests on its own RERA filing, its 32.26-acre master plan, its pricing across six configurations, and its December 2030 possession date, exactly as it would whether or not Garden Breeze had launched in the same news cycle.

The Bottom Line

Prestige Estates continuing to launch at scale across Bengaluru is a genuinely useful signal of developer momentum heading into the festive season, and Prestige Parklane's KIADB Aerospace Park location, RERA registration and configuration spread give it a credible foundation independent of this broader news.
