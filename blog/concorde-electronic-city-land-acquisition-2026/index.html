Concorde Enters Electronic City: What the ₹600 Crore Land Deal Signals

By the Luxura Habitat Team•October 25, 2026•6 Min Read

Concorde Group has acquired a roughly 4-acre land parcel in Electronic City, Bengaluru, for a new residential development, in a deal brokered by Colliers India, according to coverage from The Realty Today and Housing.com. The acquisition combines an outright purchase of about 2 acres with a further acre secured through a joint development agreement, and the estimated gross development value of the planned project is around Rs 600 crore. Concorde's own reported land cost for the outright-purchase portion is approximately Rs 56 crore.

1. What's Actually Been Confirmed

Coverage of the deal describes a purely residential development planned across roughly 5.25 lakh sq ft of built-up area, made up of premium 2 and 3 BHK apartments supported by amenities. Beyond that broad outline, the details that typically matter most to a prospective buyer, exact unit sizes, a price list, a RERA registration number and a launch date, haven't been disclosed yet. That's standard for a project still at the land-acquisition stage, but it also means any pricing or possession claims circulating about this specific site right now are speculation rather than fact.

2. Why Electronic City Is a Notable Shift for Concorde

What makes this acquisition worth a closer look isn't the deal size alone, it's the location. Concorde's current flagship Bengaluru projects are all concentrated in the city's north: Concorde Mayfair on Bellary Road in Yelahanka, Concorde Neo on Thanisandra Main Road, and Concorde Sienna on Hennur Main Road. Electronic City, by contrast, is a mature, well-established IT corridor in south Bengaluru with its own long-standing employment base and residential demand. A developer with a strong North Bengaluru track record choosing to acquire land in the south suggests either confidence that the Electronic City micro-market still has room to absorb premium supply, or a deliberate strategy to diversify beyond a single corridor rather than concentrate further in an increasingly crowded North Bengaluru new-launch belt.

3. What This Means If You're Considering Concorde

This Electronic City acquisition doesn't change anything about Concorde's existing, RERA-registered projects in North Bengaluru, but it is a useful data point on the developer's financial standing and willingness to expand into new corridors. If you're evaluating Concorde as a developer rather than this specific, unlaunched Electronic City site, its track record is easier to verify today at projects that are already RERA-registered and under construction, such as Concorde Sienna on Hennur Main Road or Concorde Mayfair in Yelahanka, both of which have published floor plans, pricing and possession timelines you can check against the Karnataka RERA portal.

The Bottom Line

Concorde's Electronic City land buy is a credible, well-brokered first step into a new Bengaluru corridor for the developer, but it remains a land acquisition, not a launched, RERA-registered product. Electronic City buyers should treat this as a market to watch rather than one to act on immediately, and anyone evaluating Concorde's reliability as a builder can do so today through its existing North Bengaluru projects.
