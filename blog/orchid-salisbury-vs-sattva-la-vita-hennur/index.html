Goyal Orchid Salisbury vs Sattva La Vita: Which Hennur Project Fits You?

By the Luxura Habitat Team•July 27, 2026•6 Min Read

Two of the most discussed projects in the Hennur–Thanisandra real estate belt — Goyal Orchid Salisbury (a high-rise apartment community) and Sattva La Vita (luxury 4 BHK row house villas) — serve entirely different buyer profiles. This guide breaks down which suits your specific needs.

2. Orchid Salisbury: The Manyata Belt Apartment

Goyal Orchid Salisbury on Thanisandra Main Road is designed around the Manyata Tech Park professional. With 660+ apartments across 7 high-rise towers, it offers the scale and amenities that a major corporate rental community requires — swimming pools, clubhouse, children's play areas, and enough building management infrastructure to maintain quality over time.

The Goyal and Co pedigree is relevant here: they are a Tier-1 Bangalore developer with a track record of on-time delivery and post-possession maintenance quality. In the Hennur belt, where several smaller developers have struggled with delayed possessions, the Goyal name is a meaningful differentiator.

Best for: Mid-budget investors (₹1–2 Cr range), families needing the Manyata catchment, buyers upgrading from older HSR/Koramangala apartments.

3. Sattva La Vita: The Row Villa Premium

Sattva La Vita occupies a niche that very few projects in North Bangalore fill: a Tier-1 developer row house community in the Hennur–Thanisandra belt. Row villas by Sattva command a specific kind of buyer — senior executives, entrepreneurs, and large families who want the privacy and space of an independent house but are not willing to sacrifice the security, maintenance, and amenities of a gated community.

The 4 BHK row house format means each home is typically 2,500–3,500 sqft, across 3–4 floors, with a private terrace and sometimes a small garden. This is exactly the product that Manyata's CXO population — earning ₹50L+ annually — actively seeks and fails to find in most of Bangalore's premium gated communities.

Best for: HNI buyers, CXO-level professionals, families requiring 4 bedrooms, rental investors targeting the executive segment (₹80K–₹1.2L/month rents).
