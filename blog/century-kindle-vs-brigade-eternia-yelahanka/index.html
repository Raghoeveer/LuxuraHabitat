Century Kindle vs Brigade Eternia: Which Yelahanka Launch Fits You?

By the Luxura Habitat Team•July 29, 2026•7 Min Read

Two of the most prominent new launches on the Yelahanka corridor — Century Kindle and Brigade Eternia — sit less than a few kilometres apart on the same NH-44/Airport Road stretch, but target noticeably different buyers. This guide breaks down which one fits your budget and requirements.

2. Century Kindle: The Accessible Entry Point

Century Kindle's defining feature in this comparison is configuration breadth: it is the only one of the two offering a 2 BHK, starting at approximately ₹1.18 Cr — roughly half of Brigade Eternia's 3 BHK starting price. Phase 1 spans 15 acres across 14 towers with 450+ apartments in 2, 3 & 4 BHK layouts, positioned opposite Attur Lake with direct NH-44 frontage.

As a pre-launch project from a large, established developer (Century Real Estate — 50+ years, 20+ million sq.ft delivered), it carries meaningfully lower execution risk than a smaller developer at the same price point, while still undercutting the corridor's other new launches on entry price.

Best for: First-time buyers and young professionals wanting a 2 BHK in the Yelahanka corridor, rental investors targeting the sub-₹1.5 Cr segment, buyers who want scale and amenities without a 3 BHK+ budget.

3. Brigade Eternia: The Larger-Format Corporate Play

Brigade Eternia is a larger, more mature development — 1,124 units across 12 towers on 14 acres, offered exclusively in 3 & 4 BHK configurations (1,653–2,926 sq.ft.), starting around ₹2.47 Cr. It leans into a corporate-professional positioning with a co-working space, learning centre and an extensive clubhouse-led amenity set, and sits closer to Manyata Tech Park (~6.4 km).

Brigade Group's scale and delivery track record make this a reasonable choice for buyers who specifically want a 3 or 4 BHK and are comfortable with the higher entry price and slightly longer possession timeline (Mid 2029) that comes with a 1,124-unit, 12-tower project.

Best for: Families needing 3+ bedrooms from day one, corporate/HNI buyers who value a larger integrated clubhouse and co-working amenities, buyers prioritising proximity to Manyata Tech Park over entry price.
