Concorde Hennur Apartments: Our Honest Review

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published September 15, 2026•10 Min Read

If you searched "Concorde Hennur", you already know the developer and the locality, just not necessarily the project's registered brand name: this launch is officially sold as Concorde Sienna. That naming gap trips up a fair number of buyers, especially when they get to the RERA verification step and the certificate shows a name they don't recognise. So before anything else, we're clearing that up, then walking through what we actually liked about this tower, what gave us pause, and the numbers you need before you call the sales team.

First, the Naming: Why "Concorde Hennur" and "Concorde Sienna" Are the Same Project

Concorde Group has filed this pre-launch project under the marketing name Concorde Sienna, and that is the name printed on the RERA certificate, the brochure, and every official document you'll be asked to sign. But because it's a Concorde-developed tower sitting directly on Hennur Main Road, a large share of prospective buyers simply search "Concorde Hennur" instead — the developer plus the locality, which is how most people actually describe a project to a friend or an agent before they know its formal name. If that's how you landed here, you're in the right place, and the facts below apply to both names equally: it's one tower, one RERA filing, one price list.

What We Liked

  • A single-tower format genuinely delivers 70% open space on a compact 3.05-acre plot, rare for a project this tall
  • Established developer with two other live North Bengaluru projects (Concorde Mayfair, Concorde Neo) to cross-check build quality against
  • Sky-deck clubhouse, infinity pool and a dedicated co-working lounge are a genuine fit for the professional buyer this corridor attracts
  • RERA registration is already in place at the pre-launch stage and independently verifiable today
  • Pre-launch entry pricing from ₹1.26 Cr undercuts several established stretches of the wider Hebbal-Manyata belt

What Gave Us Pause

  • Just 2 lifts and 2 staircases are planned to serve roughly 270 apartments across a 28-29 floor tower — worth asking about at peak hours
  • The July 2030 possession date circulating on listing platforms is market-reported, not the developer's own RERA-filed date
  • Carpet area and maintenance figures come from pre-booking sheets, not an official filing, so they need direct confirmation
  • Only 2 and 3 BHK configurations are on offer — no 1 BHK or 4 BHK option if your budget or family size sits outside that band

Location: What Hennur Main Road Actually Offers Today

The site sits on Hennur Main Road in Kannuru Village, Bangalore East, a corridor wedged between the established Hebbal-Manyata belt and the still-developing Thanisandra-Bagalur stretch. That in-between position is exactly why it's drawing attention right now: prices haven't caught up to Hebbal yet, but the connectivity is already close to it. Here's the proximity snapshot we verified against the project's own material:

  • Connectivity: Kannur Junction (1.2 km), Outer Ring Road / Hebbal interchange (short drive), Kempegowda International Airport (16.5 km), Yeshwanthpur Metro (nearest connected station)
  • Employment hubs: Manyata Tech Park (7-10 km via ORR and Hebbal)
  • Schools: Legacy School, Vibgyor High
  • Hospitals: Aster CMI Hospital, Manipal Hospital Hebbal
  • Shopping: Bhartiya Mall of Bengaluru (3.2 km), Decathlon (3.6 km)

The site itself backs onto a lake and nala buffer along its western edge, which the developer is using as a natural green boundary rather than building right up to it — a genuinely useful detail if you care about what's immediately outside your windows, and one that doesn't always show up in a project's headline marketing.

The Project Itself: A Single Landmark Tower, Not a Sprawling Layout

Concorde Sienna (the name you'll see on the actual RERA certificate) is a single vertical tower rising to approximately 92 metres — 2 basements, ground, and either 28 or 29 floors depending on the block. That's a deliberate design choice: instead of spreading a similar unit count across several mid-rise blocks, Concorde Group opted for one landmark structure on the 3.05-acre plot, which is what frees up close to 70% of the surface for landscaped greenery, sports zones and the sky-deck clubhouse. Total inventory is capped at roughly 269 to 270 apartments, exclusively 2 BHK and 3 BHK, so despite the height, density per floor stays genuinely low at 6 apartments per floor.

That low per-floor density is real, but it comes with a trade-off we think is worth naming plainly: with only 2 lifts and 2 staircases serving the entire tower's 28-29 floors, the vertical circulation math is tighter than it looks on paper. Six apartments waiting on two lifts during a 9 AM weekday isn't unusual in single-tower Bangalore high-rises, but it's exactly the kind of detail that doesn't show up in a brochure render and is worth asking the sales team to walk you through directly, ideally by comparing it against how Concorde Mayfair or Concorde Neo handle the same math in their own towers.

We want to flag something upfront: the carpet area figures above, and the estimated monthly maintenance (roughly ₹4,800-4,900 for a 2 BHK and ₹6,200-7,500 for a 3 BHK), come from pre-booking sheets circulating in the market rather than the developer's own official RERA filing. They're a useful planning reference, but they're not the number you should sign against. Ask the sales team for the RERA-declared carpet area and the developer's own maintenance estimate for your specific unit before you book, not the version currently doing the rounds online.

Amenities: Built for a Professional, Not a Sprawling Family Township

The amenity list leans deliberately toward working professionals rather than the everything-included township model some larger developments go for: a sky-deck clubhouse with an infinity pool and a separate kids' pool, a gymnasium, a yoga and meditation studio, spa/sauna/steam facilities, indoor and outdoor sports courts, a banquet hall and an indoor amphitheatre. The standout for us is the dedicated co-working lounge — a genuinely practical addition for a corridor that's increasingly home to IT professionals commuting to Manyata Tech Park, and not something every single-tower project in this price band bothers to include. Smart-home provisions and EV charging points round out the list, both table stakes at this price point now but still worth confirming are actually wired in at handover rather than left as a "provision" that requires a separate cost later.

RERA Verification — Do This Before You Book

The project carries Karnataka RERA registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/220626/008740, filed under the name Concorde Sienna. Confirm this independently on the RERA Karnataka portal before signing anything — not because we doubt the registration, but because that's the standard we'd apply to any project regardless of developer reputation, and because the name mismatch (Hennur vs. Sienna) is precisely the kind of detail that makes it worth double-checking you're looking at the right certificate for the right tower.

Our Verdict

Concorde Hennur, officially Concorde Sienna, earns a place on our shortlist for this corridor: a genuinely low-density single-tower format, an amenity package that actually suits the buyer this location attracts, and pre-launch pricing that's still competitive against the wider Hebbal-Manyata belt. Where we'd tell a buyer to slow down and ask direct questions is the lift-to-unit ratio across a 28-29 floor tower, and the gap between the market-reported 2030 possession estimate and whatever date the developer eventually files with RERA. Neither is a dealbreaker, but both are worth a direct conversation with the sales team rather than taking the brochure at face value. If you want a lifestyle-first, single-tower home on Hennur Main Road and you're comfortable with a pre-launch timeline, it's worth a site visit.
