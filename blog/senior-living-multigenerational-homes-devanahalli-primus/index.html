Senior Living & Multi-Generational Homes in Devanahalli: A Buyer's Guide

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published September 12, 2026•10 Min Read

Search for senior living apartments in Bangalore and most results are either small, elderly-only assisted living facilities on the far edge of the city, or generic apartment listings that use "senior citizen zone" to describe a bench in a garden. Genuinely organised, operator-run senior and multi-generational housing, engineered for accessibility from the ground up rather than retrofitted, is still a small category in this market. This guide explains what that category actually looks like when it's done properly, using Devanahalli's Brigade Orchards Laurel & Maple, operated by Primus Senior Living, as a working example, and gives you a framework for evaluating any similar project you come across.

1. What "Accessibility-First Design" Actually Means

The phrase gets used loosely, so it's worth being specific about what should actually be present in a genuinely accessibility-engineered home, rather than a standard apartment with a few add-ons:

  • Wider doorways throughout the unit, not just at the main entrance, sized to comfortably accommodate a walker or wheelchair.
  • Lever-type door handles instead of round knobs, which are meaningfully easier to operate for anyone with reduced grip strength or arthritis.
  • Dual-height door viewers, positioned for both a standing adult and a seated wheelchair user, rather than a single peephole at standard eye height.
  • Level flooring with gentle slopes in place of raised thresholds between rooms, removing a common trip hazard and wheelchair obstruction.
  • Sliding doors in select bathrooms, which take up less swing space than a hinged door and are easier to operate one-handed.
  • Grab bars in showers and toilet areas, installed during construction rather than added later, which matters because retrofitted grab bars are often weaker if they aren't anchored into proper wall backing.

At Brigade Orchards Laurel & Maple, all six of these are present according to the developer's own specification, which is a genuinely more complete list than most projects that use "senior-friendly" as a marketing term without the underlying engineering. If you're evaluating any project on this basis, ask for the specification sheet in writing rather than relying on a verbal assurance from the sales team.

2. The SOS-to-Nursing-Station Model

Beyond passive accessibility features, the more distinctive element in a genuine senior-living format is an active emergency response system: an in-unit SOS call bell wired directly to an on-site nursing station or healthcare facility, rather than a generic intercom to the security desk. This is the feature that most changes the risk calculus for a family with an elderly parent living semi-independently, since it converts a potential medical emergency from "hope someone notices in time" into a system with a defined, immediate escalation path.

When evaluating this feature at any project, ask specifically: is the nursing station staffed 24x7, or only during certain hours? What is the actual response time commitment, if any? Is this service included in the base purchase price, or billed as a recurring charge by the operator (in Laurel & Maple's case, Primus Senior Living)? None of these operational specifics are typically disclosed in glossy marketing material, so they're worth asking for directly, in writing, before you book.

3. Senior Living vs. Multi-Generational Living: An Important Distinction

It's worth being precise about terminology, since "senior living" and "multi-generational living" describe genuinely different products:

  • Senior living (sometimes called assisted living) typically means smaller-format residences, often studio or 1 BHK, designed exclusively for elderly occupants, frequently bundled with meal service, housekeeping and more intensive care options.
  • Multi-generational living, the format used at Brigade Orchards Laurel & Maple, means regular family-sized 1, 2 and 3 BHK homes, built with the accessibility and healthcare-linked features above, but intended for households where multiple generations live together or in close proximity, not an elderly-only community.

This distinction matters because it changes who the product actually suits. A family wanting to buy a full-sized home where an aging parent can live safely alongside adult children and grandchildren is a different buyer than someone specifically seeking a smaller, fully-serviced elderly-only unit. If you're searching with "senior living" as your keyword but what you actually want is the former, make sure you're not accidentally filtering yourself into a category of smaller, elderly-only inventory that doesn't fit your household.

4. How to Evaluate the Operator, Not Just the Developer

A defining feature of this category is the split between developer and operator. At Laurel & Maple, Brigade Enterprises Ltd. (in joint venture with Classic Group) is responsible for construction and the underlying real estate, while Primus Senior Living is the specialist operator handling day-to-day senior-living services. This is structurally similar to how a hospitality-branded residence might separate the real estate developer from the hotel brand operating it.

When evaluating any project with this structure, treat the operator's track record as a separate due-diligence item from the developer's: how long has the operator been running comparable communities, what is included in their service fee versus billed separately, and what happens if the operator relationship changes after you've bought in? A large, listed developer like Brigade reduces construction and title risk, but it does not automatically guarantee the ongoing quality of a separate operator's day-to-day service delivery, so evaluate both independently rather than assuming the developer's reputation covers the operator too.

5. Why Devanahalli Specifically, for This Category

Devanahalli's growth has been driven primarily by Kempegowda International Airport and the surrounding KIADB Aerospace Park and IT/industrial corridor, which has historically positioned it as a working-professional and investor market rather than a retirement destination. That's exactly what makes an organised multi-generational format here interesting: it's a location with genuine long-term appreciation and rental-yield fundamentals (see our Devanahalli ROI: Land Appreciation vs. Rental Yield guide for the fuller investment picture), combined with the kind of large-township amenity base, in Brigade Orchards' case a 135-acre master plan with a Signature Club Resort and sports arena, that supports the additional infrastructure a senior-living-adjacent format needs. A standalone senior-living project in a location without that surrounding township infrastructure would have a much harder time delivering the same amenity depth.

6. Pricing: What Premium Should You Expect to Pay?

Operator-run, accessibility-engineered, amenity-dense formats consistently carry a premium over a standard apartment of comparable size in the same micro-market. At Laurel & Maple, the market-listed average base rate runs around ₹9,950 per sq.ft, which sits above generic Devanahalli apartment pricing, reflecting both the additional construction specification and the operator infrastructure. Budget for this premium explicitly rather than comparing this category's pricing directly against a standard apartment's per-sqft rate without adjusting for what you're actually getting.

7. A Verification Checklist Before You Book

Whichever specific project you're considering in this category, verify the following before paying anything:

  • The project's RERA registration number, checked independently on the state RERA portal, not just taken from the sales brochure.
  • Written clarity on which senior-living or healthcare-linked services are included in the base purchase price versus billed separately by the operator on an ongoing basis.
  • The accessibility specification sheet for your specific unit and layout, since features can vary between configurations and layout variants within the same project.
  • A construction-linked payment schedule tied to actual build milestones, particularly important for a new-launch project where a firm possession date hasn't yet been disclosed.
  • The operator's own track record, separate from the developer's, if the project uses a split developer-operator model.

Our Honest Assessment

Genuinely organised, accessibility-engineered multi-generational housing is a real and underserved category in Bangalore, and Brigade Orchards Laurel & Maple, built by a listed developer inside a large, amenity-rich township and operated by a specialist senior-living operator, is a legitimate example of the category done properly rather than a marketing relabel of a standard apartment. That said, at the time of writing, this specific project's RERA registration and possession date were still pending disclosure, and the exact split of responsibilities and charges between Brigade, Classic Group and Primus hadn't been fully detailed in the material we reviewed, both reasons for extra diligence before booking, not reasons to avoid the category altogether.
