Brigade Enterprises Q1 FY27: 37% Profit Growth and an 11.5 MSF Launch Plan

By the Luxura Habitat Team•September 15, 2026•7 Min Read

Brigade Enterprises presented its Q1 FY27 results on August 14, 2026, and the numbers tell a story that's worth unpacking for anyone tracking the Bengaluru developer. Consolidated net profit rose 37% year-on-year to Rs 216.94 crore, up from Rs 157.95 crore in the same quarter last year, while profit before tax jumped 47% to Rs 285 crore. Total income, however, declined 11.5% to Rs 1,179.22 crore from Rs 1,332.86 crore. That combination, profit up sharply while revenue fell, isn't a contradiction once you look at the margins: EBITDA margin expanded to 36% from 28% a year earlier, on an EBITDA of Rs 425 crore.

1. Reading the Numbers Correctly

Real estate revenue recognition is lumpy by nature. A developer books revenue against specific projects as they hit construction or sales milestones, so quarter-to-quarter income can swing based on which projects happen to be at a recognition stage, independent of how strong actual demand is. Brigade's real estate segment brought in Rs 707 crore of the quarter's revenue, with leasing contributing Rs 328 crore (up 9% year-on-year) and hospitality Rs 144 crore. The takeaway isn't that Brigade's business shrank; it's that the company sold and executed more efficiently on a smaller recognised base, which is generally a healthier signal than growing revenue on thinning margins.

2. The Bigger Number: 11.5 Million Sq Ft of New Launches

The more consequential detail for prospective buyers is Brigade's forward guidance. The company has set a residential launch target of 11.5 to 12 million sq ft for FY27, with an estimated combined gross development value of Rs 12,000-13,000 crore, based on an assumed average portfolio pricing of roughly Rs 10,500 per sq ft. Of the flagship 11.5 million sq ft figure, Brigade has indicated about 4.5 million sq ft is earmarked for Bengaluru, with 3 million sq ft each planned for Chennai and Hyderabad. Alongside this, the company has reiterated a pre-sales target of around Rs 9,000 crore for FY27, roughly 20% growth over the prior year.

In plain terms: a large, well-capitalised developer with strengthening execution margins is planning a substantial wave of new Bengaluru inventory this fiscal year. That's useful context whether you're deciding when to book into an existing Brigade pre-launch or waiting to see what gets announced next.

3. What This Means If You're Considering a Brigade Project

  • More choice is coming, not less. With roughly 4.5 million sq ft planned for Bengaluru alone this year, buyers who don't find the right fit in Brigade's current pre-launch lineup are likely to see fresh options surface over the coming months.
  • Execution discipline is a good sign, not a red flag. A developer improving margins even on lower recognised revenue typically means tighter cost control and better realisations on units already sold, which matters for construction quality and delivery timelines on ongoing projects.
  • Guidance isn't a guarantee. Launch targets can slip due to approval delays or market conditions. Treat FY27 pipeline numbers as directional intent, not a confirmed launch calendar for any specific project.
