Sobha's Q2 FY27 Profit Fell 55% Even as Sales Hit a Record: What It Means for Wisteria Buyers

By the Luxura Habitat Team•October 23, 2026•7 Min Read

Sobha Limited's board approved its unaudited consolidated results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026, on October 19, 2026, followed by an analyst call the same evening with Managing Director Jagadish Nangineni and CFO Yogesh Bansal. We'd already covered the company's record H1 FY27 presales from its earlier operational update, but the full financial results released on October 19 tell a more complicated story: even as bookings hit an all-time high, reported net profit for the quarter fell sharply. According to NewsBytes and Kotak NEO's coverage of the filing, Q2 FY27 consolidated net profit came in at about Rs 32.95 crore, down roughly 55% from about Rs 72.5 crore in the same quarter last year.

1. The Numbers: Record Sales, Weaker Profit

The headline divergence is stark. H1 FY27 presales touched an all-time high of about Rs 5,860 crore, up 47% year on year, and Q2 FY27's real estate sales value alone rose 16% year on year to about Rs 2,206 crore, per Kotak NEO. Yet Q2 FY27 net profit fell to roughly Rs 33 crore from about Rs 72.5 crore a year earlier, a decline of around 55%. NewsBytes reports total income for the quarter fell to about Rs 1,017 crore from Rs 1,469 crore in Q2 FY26, while Univest's figures put revenue at around Rs 967 crore, down 31.3% year on year. The two sources don't fully agree on the exact revenue figure, which likely comes down to how each defines "revenue" versus "total income," but both point the same direction: a smaller recognised revenue base this quarter against a larger one a year ago.

2. Why Sales and Profit Can Move in Opposite Directions

This isn't necessarily a contradiction, and it's a useful pattern for any homebuyer tracking a listed developer's quarterly results to understand. Presales (sometimes called bookings) measure the value of homes sold during the quarter, which the company will typically collect and recognise as revenue over the following several years as construction progresses. Reported quarterly revenue and profit, by contrast, depend on which specific projects have reached a stage where accounting rules allow the company to recognise income now, plus the costs booked in that same quarter. A developer can have an excellent sales quarter while reporting a weaker profit quarter simply because the completed, revenue-recognisable projects in its pipeline were fewer or smaller this time around. Kotak NEO's data showing H1 FY27 PAT of about Rs 84 crore, down a comparatively mild 2.6% year on year, suggests Q1 FY27's stronger profit partly cushioned the half-year picture even after Q2's decline.

3. What This Means If You're Evaluating Sobha Wisteria in Egattur

It's worth being precise about what this quarter's numbers do and don't say. They are a corporate-level result covering Sobha's existing, revenue-recognising projects nationally. Sobha Wisteria, also marketed as Sobha OMR, in Egattur on Chennai's OMR IT corridor, remains a pre-launch project: it has not been formally launched, has no TNRERA registration yet, and is not part of this quarter's reported sales or revenue. Based on the project's own page, it is indicatively planned as roughly 650 homes on 8.5 acres, offered as 3 and 4 BHK apartments from about 1,750 to 2,700 sq ft, with 3 BHK homes priced from around Rs 1.69 crore onwards and possession expected by December 2029, all subject to change once the official brochure and TNRERA registration are released.

  • A profit dip at the parent company isn't, by itself, a reason to avoid a specific pre-launch project. Management described sales momentum as strong on the earnings call, and the same quarter's bookings were a record. One quarter's accounting profit is a narrower measure than the company's overall sales health.
  • It is a reason to ask sharper questions before booking. When evaluating any pre-launch project from a listed developer, it's reasonable to ask about that specific project's construction financing, expected handover schedule and how it fits into the company's broader execution pipeline, rather than relying only on the parent company's sales headlines.
  • Sobha Wisteria's own facts still come first. Price, configuration, RERA status and possession date should be verified against the project's own TNRERA filing and brochure once released, independent of how the parent company's quarter read.

The Bottom Line

Sobha's Q2 FY27 results show a developer with genuinely strong sales momentum, but a quarter where that didn't translate into matching profit growth, a reminder that presales and reported earnings are measuring different things on different timelines. For Chennai buyers tracking Sobha Wisteria in Egattur, the practical takeaway is to treat this as useful context on the parent company rather than a signal about the project itself, and to keep watching for the project's own TNRERA registration and official pricing.
