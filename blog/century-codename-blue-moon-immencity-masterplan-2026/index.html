Century Codename Blue Moon: Inside Century Real Estate's Immencity Push in North Bangalore

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published November 27, 2026•11 Min Read

Century Real Estate has spent 2026 pushing hard on its North Bangalore airport-corridor story. The developer unveiled Immencity, a 50-plus acre integrated township in Jakkur's wider NH-44 belt, as its flagship lifestyle destination, while trade press reported a roughly Rs 9,000 crore launch pipeline across ten-plus projects for the financial year and a run of strong pre-launch bookings at other Bangalore developments. Century Codename Blue Moon is one of the residential addresses sitting inside that Immencity push, and the attention Century Real Estate is putting behind the larger township is worth unpacking for anyone evaluating Blue Moon specifically, because a project's standing within a developer's broader masterplan says as much about its long-term prospects as its own floor plans do.

1. What Century Real Estate Actually Announced

Trade coverage through 2026 described Century Real Estate rolling out a high-impact outdoor teaser campaign across Bengaluru in February, followed by the formal unveiling of Century Immencity in Jakkur, North Bengaluru, positioned as a 50-plus acre mixed-use lifestyle destination. Separately, the company was reported to be targeting over Rs 3,000 crore in sales for the financial year, on the back of demand for plotted developments and luxury housing, and to have booked more than Rs 405 crore in pre-launch sales at a different Marathahalli project called "Fast Forward." None of these figures are about Blue Moon directly, but together they describe a developer putting real marketing and sales weight behind its North Bangalore pipeline, of which Blue Moon's home, Immencity New Town, is the centrepiece.

2. Where Blue Moon Sits Inside Immencity

Century Codename Blue Moon's own project page places it in "Immencity New Town," on NH-44, facing Attur Lake, in North Bangalore's airport corridor. That is the same address Century Real Estate uses for the broader Immencity masterplan it has been promoting through the year. Within this address, the developer has listed Blue Moon as a 15-acre, 14-tower, 450-plus unit development, with units across four named towers: Altair, Bellatrix, Deneb and Electra.

Here is a detail buyers should sit with before anything else: Century Real Estate also markets a separate project page for Century Astoria, at the identical address, with the identical 15-acre, 14-tower, 450-plus unit description, and the identical Karnataka RERA registration number, PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/160526/008662. That overlap strongly suggests Blue Moon and Astoria are two marketing names for the same underlying registered project, with "Codename Blue Moon" most likely the pre-launch label and "Astoria" the subsequent branded name, a common pattern once a project in Bangalore moves from pre-launch to formal launch. We flag this not as a criticism but as the single most important thing to verify directly with the developer before you book: ask explicitly whether you are buying into the same registered project under two names, or whether there genuinely are two distinct developments sharing a parcel, and get that answer in writing against the RERA number.

5. Connectivity on the Airport Corridor

Century Real Estate's own distance table for Blue Moon lists the following drive times, which buyers should treat as the developer's figures rather than independently verified times, since peak-hour traffic on NH-44 can add meaningfully to any of them:

  • Kempegowda International Airport: about 15 minutes, with direct access to NH-44
  • KIADB Aerospace Park: about 10 minutes
  • Immencity Business Park (within the same township): about 12 minutes
  • Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR): about 5 minutes
  • Hebbal: about 30 minutes
  • Manyata Tech Park: about 35 minutes

The pitch here is straightforward: Blue Moon sits inside a township that also contains planned business-park and retail space, so residents are meant to draw on amenities beyond the residential parcel itself. That is a genuine advantage of integrated townships generally, but it also means some of what is "included" in the lifestyle pitch, the business park, the retail frontage, may be built out over a longer timeline than the residential towers. Ask for a phase-wise construction schedule for the non-residential parts of Immencity, not just the towers you are buying into.

6. Amenities

The developer's amenity list for Blue Moon covers four broad categories: leisure and social spaces (multipurpose hall, library, indoor games, yoga and meditation room), kids and play (a dedicated play area and family-friendly outdoor zones), fitness and wellness (gymnasium, outdoor gym, jogging track and swimming pool), and sporting options (basketball, a cricket or play field, badminton and a general sports court). For a 450-plus unit, 14-tower community, this is a reasonably standard amenity set for the price band; ask which of these are open on possession day versus delivered in a later phase.

7. About Century Real Estate

Century Real Estate describes itself as a Bengaluru developer with more than 50 years of experience, a land bank exceeding 3,000 acres, a built and planned portfolio of more than 20 million sq ft, and more than 5,000 families housed across its projects, according to figures on its own Blue Moon page. The group's broader 2026 pipeline, the Immencity unveiling, the reported Rs 9,000 crore, ten-plus-project FY26 launch target, and strong pre-launch numbers at Fast Forward in Marathahalli, point to a developer in an active growth phase on the North Bangalore corridor specifically. That is useful context for Blue Moon buyers: a large, well-capitalised township sponsor reduces (though never eliminates) the risk of a stalled project, but it does not substitute for your own verification of this specific tower's RERA status, construction schedule and sale agreement terms.

8. Who Blue Moon Suits, and Who Should Wait

Blue Moon's airport-corridor position, lakefront-facing towers and township scale will appeal to buyers who want to be inside North Bangalore's fastest-growing employment belt, anchored by the airport, the KIADB Aerospace Park and the business parks coming up along NH-44, and who are comfortable buying at pre-launch stage with a 2028 possession horizon. It is a weaker fit for anyone who needs certainty today about which tower name, Blue Moon or Astoria, their unit will ultimately carry, or who wants a fully launched, RERA-stable project rather than a pre-launch one still finding its final branding.

9. Questions to Ask Before You Pay

  1. Is "Century Codename Blue Moon" the pre-launch name for the same RERA-registered project as Century Astoria, or a separate development? Get this confirmed against the RERA number in writing.
  2. What is the construction start date for the specific tower (Altair, Bellatrix, Deneb or Electra) your unit sits in?
  3. Does the quoted pre-launch price change once the project is formally launched and the RERA registration is updated?
  4. What is the phased delivery schedule for the Immencity Business Park and retail components relative to the residential towers?
  5. What exactly is included in the Rs 3.8 Cr to Rs 5.6 Cr starting price, and what is charged separately (car parking, clubhouse corpus, maintenance deposit)?
  6. What is the current completion date registered against PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/160526/008662 on the Karnataka RERA portal?

The same checklist, along with the brochure and the latest pricing, is available via the enquiry form on the Century Codename Blue Moon project page.

Our Assessment

Century Real Estate is clearly betting on North Bangalore's airport corridor, and Immencity is the vehicle for that bet. Century Codename Blue Moon benefits from being part of a scaled township with a well-capitalised sponsor and genuine connectivity advantages, airport, aerospace park and STRR access all inside a short drive. The one issue that needs resolving before you book is the overlap with Century Astoria: get written clarity on whether you are buying the same registered project under a different name, confirm the RERA completion date, and treat pre-launch pricing as the starting point for negotiation, not the final figure.
