How the ORR, Hebbal Flyover & Nagavara Metro Are Shaping Hennur Real Estate

By the Luxura Habitat Team•July 25, 2026•6 Min Read

Connectivity is the single most reliable predictor of long-term property appreciation in Bangalore. The Hennur–Thanisandra belt is one of the best-connected micro-markets in the city — not because of any single future project, but because it sits at the intersection of three existing high-capacity road networks, with two additional metro stations under development nearby.

1. The Outer Ring Road: Hennur's Backbone

The Outer Ring Road (ORR) is the 65km orbital highway that encircles Bangalore between 10–20km from the city centre. Hennur Road connects directly to the ORR at the Nagavara junction — making it straightforward to reach Marathahalli, Whitefield, Koramangala, and Electronic City without entering the CBD.

For Manyata Tech Park employees who live in Hennur or Thanisandra, this connectivity is essentially perfect: Manyata itself sits directly off the ORR, making the commute a matter of 10–20 minutes regardless of traffic in the city centre. This is a fundamental structural advantage that other North Bangalore corridors like Devanahalli or Yelahanka cannot replicate.

2. Hebbal Flyover: The City Gateway

The Hebbal flyover — one of Bangalore's most significant infrastructure investments — provides seamless access to:

  • MG Road / CBD: 25–35 minutes under normal traffic via the flyover and Bellary Road
  • Kempegowda International Airport: 35–45 minutes direct via NH-44 — critical for Manyata's multinational executive population
  • Yeshwanthpur / Rajajinagar: West Bangalore connectivity via the flyover and Ring Road

3. Namma Metro: Nagavara and Thanisandra Stations

The Namma Metro network is being extended northward with two stations directly relevant to this belt:

Nagavara Metro Station (Yellow Line / Phase 3)

The Nagavara station will serve as an interchange node linking the ORR belt to the metro network for the first time. For Orchid Salisbury residents on Thanisandra Main Road, this station will be within 2–4km — a reasonable e-bike or feeder cab commute. Metro proximity has consistently repriced property by 15–25% in Bangalore within 18 months of operational confirmation.

Thanisandra Main Road — Proposed Station

A station on Thanisandra Main Road itself — while still in planning alignment — would directly serve the corridor where Orchid Salisbury is located. If confirmed, it would represent a transformative connectivity event for the corridor.

The Connectivity Verdict

For buyers who need both Manyata access and airport connectivity, Hennur–Thanisandra is the only North Bangalore micro-market that delivers both without a prohibitive trade-off. This dual connectivity premium is one reason the corridor's property values have held up through market cycles that damaged other zones.
