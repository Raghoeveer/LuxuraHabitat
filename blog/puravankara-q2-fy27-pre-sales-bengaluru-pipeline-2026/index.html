Puravankara's Q2 FY27 Pre-Sales Jump 62% to Rs 2,136 Crore: What It Means for Purva Hallmark

By the Luxura Habitat Team•November 7, 2026•6 Min Read

Puravankara's operational update for the July-September 2026 quarter shows pre-sales of around Rs 2,136 crore, up roughly 62% from about Rs 1,322 crore in the same quarter a year earlier, according to reporting picked up by outlets including the Free Press Journal. Customer collections for the quarter rose about 24% year-on-year to around Rs 1,301 crore. Separately, reports say the company completed the sale of its commercial asset, Purva Zentech, to an entity backed by ICICI Prudential AMC around September 30, 2026, and incorporated a new wholly-owned subsidiary, Burgundyprime Realty Private Limited, on October 19, 2026, with an initial investment of Rs 99,990. A board meeting was reportedly scheduled for November 2, 2026 to approve the company's unaudited results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026. These are pre-sales and corporate-action figures from an operational update, not the final audited financial statements, so we have framed the numbers accordingly.

1. What Actually Moved

A 62% jump in quarterly pre-sales is a meaningful acceleration, not a marginal beat. Combined with collections growth of 24%, it points to a company that is both booking new sales at a faster clip and converting a reasonable share of that into actual cash in the door, which matters more to construction timelines than the booking number alone. The Purva Zentech sale to an ICICI Prudential AMC-backed buyer also suggests the company is actively recycling capital out of completed commercial assets, which can free up resources for its residential launch pipeline.

2. Where This Connects to Bengaluru's Pipeline: Purva Hallmark

Puravankara's current Bengaluru portfolio includes Purva Hallmark, a pre-launch project in Vajarahalli off Kanakapura Road, roughly 0.3 km from the Thalaghattapura Metro station. Per the project's own page, it is planned to offer 3 BHK homes of approximately 1,900 to 2,050 sq.ft starting around Rs 2.76 Cr, and 3 BHK + Staff (3.5 BHK) homes of approximately 2,100 to 2,250 sq.ft starting around Rs 3.05 Cr, at an indicative pre-launch base rate of about Rs 14,500 per sq.ft. The project's page notes these are pre-launch figures from the developer's marketing team, with RERA registration still in process at the time of writing, and an indicative possession target of December 2031. The Expression of Interest amount is Rs 5 Lakh, refundable, across all configurations.

To be clear, the Q2 FY27 update is a company-level result covering Puravankara's entire business across cities, not a Purva Hallmark-specific announcement. We're connecting the two because stronger group-wide pre-sales and collections generally improve a developer's ability to fund its active pipeline, of which Purva Hallmark is a part, alongside other Bengaluru projects in the group's portfolio such as Purva Codename Skye in Hennur and Purva Northern Lights in Bagalur.

3. What It Means for a Purva Hallmark Buyer

If you're an early registrant on a pre-launch project, developer-level financial health is one of the things worth tracking, since it has a bearing on whether construction stays funded on schedule after formal launch. That said, it doesn't replace the usual pre-launch checklist: confirm the RERA registration number once it's issued, get the final configuration and pricing in writing at launch rather than relying on pre-launch indicative figures, and independently verify the possession date on the Karnataka RERA portal once registered.

The Bottom Line

Puravankara's reported Q2 FY27 pre-sales growth of about 62%, alongside rising collections and a capital-recycling move through the Purva Zentech sale, points to a developer in a stronger financial position entering this quarter. For buyers tracking Purva Hallmark specifically, that's useful context heading into its formal launch and RERA registration, though the project-level fundamentals, final pricing and RERA status still need to be checked independently once available.
