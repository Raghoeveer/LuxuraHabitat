Sattva Thippapura, Doddaballapur: Price, Plots & RERA Review

By Narayanan Rajesh•August 13, 2026•9 Min Read

Sattva Thippapura is a new-launch plotted residential development from Sattva Group in Thippapura, Doddaballapur, on the northern edge of Bangalore. As a brand-new project, there's naturally more open than settled about it: final plot dimensions, per-square-foot rates and a confirmed RERA number are all still pending. This is an independently written review of what we do know, what's still to be confirmed, and our honest read on where it fits for a buyer weighing a new-launch plotted purchase.

1. The Developer: Sattva Group

Sattva Group is a Bangalore-headquartered developer with a portfolio spanning residential apartments, plotted developments and commercial projects across the city, including several other Sattva-branded communities in North Bangalore. Sattva Thippapura extends that portfolio into Doddaballapur, giving the project a level of developer credibility that a first-time or unknown builder wouldn't carry into a new launch of this scale.

2. Location: Thippapura, Doddaballapur

The project sits in Thippapura, on the Doddaballapur side of North Bangalore. Doddaballapur has a long-standing industrial base and is increasingly discussed as part of the broader North Bangalore growth corridor anchored by Kempegowda International Airport. Some reference distances:

  • NH-44 (Bangalore-Hyderabad Highway): ~8 km
  • Kempegowda International Airport: ~30 km
  • Bangalore city center: ~35 km
  • Doddaballapur Industrial Area: ~5 km, with the KIADB Industrial Park nearby
  • Manyata Tech Park: ~25 km

For a deeper look at why Doddaballapur specifically is drawing investment attention right now, including the region's industrial pipeline, see our companion piece: Doddaballapur Real Estate: Why This North Bangalore Corridor Is Worth Watching.

3. The Project: What's Confirmed So Far

Sattva Thippapura is planned across approximately 25 acres, offering 500 residential plots in three categories:

  • Standard plots: the entry-level category, starting from ₹84 Lakh onwards
  • Corner plots: dual road frontage, priced on request
  • Premium plots: larger, prime-positioned plots, priced on request

Exact plot dimensions and per-square-foot rates have not yet been published. That's normal for a project at this early a stage, but it does mean the numbers above should be treated as an indicative new-launch starting price rather than a locked-in rate card. Planned amenities include a swimming pool, a fitness center and yoga studio, landscaped gardens and parks, a children's play area, a clubhouse with indoor games, jogging tracks, 24/7 CCTV security and internal roads throughout the layout.

4. Pricing: Reading a New-Launch Starting Price Correctly

The ₹84 Lakh onwards figure for standard plots is worth unpacking for buyers newer to new-launch plotted purchases. It's typically the entry point into the cheapest available plot category, at the earliest stage of the project's pricing cycle: prices on most projects step up at each subsequent milestone (RERA registration, formal launch, construction progress) as risk to the developer, and therefore to the buyer, falls. The trade-off is the same one that applies to any pre-RERA purchase: you're committing based on a starting price and category, not a plot-specific rate card you can compare line by line yet.

Contact our team for the current, verified price list and available plot inventory. We don't publish speculative per-square-foot rates for projects still ahead of RERA registration and formal launch.

5. Legal and RERA Verification: What to Check Before Booking

At Luxura Habitat, we don't recommend a project before completing legal verification. On a plotted development specifically, RERA (the Real Estate Regulatory Authority) registration is the single biggest structural protection a buyer has:

  • Ring-fenced funds: a registered project must keep buyer collections in a project-specific account, released against verified progress rather than free to be diverted elsewhere.
  • Enforceable delivery commitments: the developer registers against a stated completion timeline, with penalties if it slips.
  • Accurate marketing: plot sizes, amenities and layout details become part of the legal project disclosure once registered.
  • A public record you can check yourself: once registered, anyone can verify a project's status and approvals on the Karnataka RERA portal.

For Sattva Thippapura specifically, here's where things stand as of this review: the project's RERA registration is currently in process. As with any pre-RERA new launch, confirm the current registration number, land conversion status and layout approval directly with our sales team, and independently verify the RERA number on the Karnataka RERA portal once it's issued, before converting any interest into a booking.

6. What Buying at This Early Stage Typically Involves

For buyers newer to the plotted-development process, the realistic sequence looks like this:

  1. You register interest and receive the current indicative price list and available plot categories.
  2. As RERA registration and formal launch progress, exact plot numbers, dimensions and final per-square-foot rates are published.
  3. You choose a specific plot, sign a booking agreement, and agree the payment schedule.
  4. Before final registration, you (or your advisor) verify the RERA certificate, land conversion order, layout approval and an encumbrance certificate for that specific plot, not just the layout as a whole.
  5. You proceed to the sale agreement and registration, paying applicable stamp duty and registration charges.

That fourth step is the one most first-time buyers skip, and it's the one that matters most: a project-level RERA registration doesn't automatically mean every individual plot is free of disputes.

7. Amenities and Layout: What's Planned

Beyond the plot itself, Sattva Thippapura's layout is planned around a fairly standard but genuinely useful gated-community amenity set: a swimming pool, a fitness center and yoga studio, landscaped gardens and parks, a dedicated children's play area, a clubhouse with indoor games, jogging tracks, 24/7 CCTV security, and internal roads throughout the layout. For a plotted development specifically, amenities matter differently than they do on an apartment project: you're not paying a maintenance premium bundled into a monthly charge on a fixed unit, you're buying into a shared, professionally maintained common area around your own self-built home. That distinction is worth understanding upfront if this is your first plotted purchase: your ongoing costs will typically include a layout maintenance charge for these shared amenities, separate from whatever you spend building and maintaining your own house.

8. Who This Project Suits, and Who Should Wait

Based on what's currently known, Sattva Thippapura is a reasonable fit for a fairly specific buyer profile: someone who wants a self-build option in North Bangalore's industrial corridor, is comfortable committing based on an indicative starting price and plot category rather than a locked rate card, and has a multi-year horizon rather than needing to build or resell in the next 12 months. It's a weaker fit for a buyer who needs a specific plot dimension confirmed today to plan construction, needs bank financing pre-approved against a known per-square-foot rate, or is uncomfortable with the standard pre-RERA risk that the project's registration, and therefore some of its legal protections, aren't yet in place.

It's also worth comparing this against the alternative product type directly: buying a ready plot from an established, already-RERA-registered layout elsewhere in North Bangalore, at a higher entry price but with full documentation already in hand, versus buying into a new launch like this one at an earlier, more attractive price point but with more paperwork still to be completed. Neither choice is objectively better; it depends on how much you value certainty today versus a lower entry price with more patience required.

Our Honest Assessment

Sattva Thippapura is a genuinely early-stage new launch: an established developer entering a locality with real industrial and infrastructure momentum, but with plot dimensions, final pricing and RERA registration all still to be confirmed. That combination cuts both ways. The upside is that new-launch, pre-RERA pricing is typically the most competitive a project will ever offer, and Sattva Group's track record reduces (without eliminating) execution risk relative to a first-time developer. The downside is straightforward: you're evaluating a starting price and a plot category, not a plot-specific rate card, and there's no RERA number yet to independently verify.

For buyers comfortable with that level of pre-launch uncertainty in exchange for early pricing and Doddaballapur's industrial growth story, it's worth a shortlist conversation. Buyers who need certainty on exact dimensions, final pricing and a confirmed RERA number today should ask us to flag them once those are published, rather than booking on today's indicative numbers alone.
