Park Cubix vs Sattva City: Which Devanahalli Township Fits You?

By the Luxura Habitat Team•August 7, 2026•7 Min Read

Sattva Group operates two large integrated townships in the Devanahalli corridor: Sattva Park Cubix, an established two-phase development with real ready-to-occupy inventory, and Sattva City, a newer, larger single-phase launch still under construction. Since both come from the same developer in the same micro-market, the choice comes down almost entirely to scale, timeline and configuration — a genuinely useful same-developer comparison.

2. Park Cubix: Proven Delivery, Smaller Entry Point

Park Cubix's core advantage is that part of it already exists — Phase 1 is Ready to Occupy, so a buyer can inspect real, finished construction rather than relying on renders and a promised timeline. It also offers a genuine 1 BHK entry point (~658 sq ft), the smallest and most accessible ticket size between the two townships, useful for first-time buyers or investors targeting rental yield on compact units near the airport.

Best for: Buyers who want to inspect finished construction before committing, first-time buyers wanting a 1 BHK entry point, and investors comfortable with a smaller 1,608-unit resale/rental pool.

3. Sattva City: Bigger Scale, Larger Homes

Sattva City is nearly triple the unit count (3,460 vs 1,608) on almost triple the land (50 vs 18 acres), with a configuration range that skews larger — up to 4 BHK at 2,623 sq ft, well beyond Park Cubix's 3 BHK ceiling of 1,498 sq ft. Its 4 clubhouses and 200+ amenities outscale Park Cubix's offering considerably, though as a newer, single-phase, under-construction project, every unit here carries construction-timeline risk that Park Cubix's Phase 1 buyers simply don't face.

Best for: Families needing a larger 3 or 4 BHK home, buyers who want the widest possible amenity set, and investors willing to accept pre-launch/under-construction risk for potentially the most competitive per-sq-ft entry pricing.

Our Honest Assessment

This is less a "which is better" comparison and more a "which stage of risk and scale do you want" comparison — both are Sattva Group townships in the same Devanahalli corridor, so developer trust isn't the differentiator. Park Cubix's Phase 1 offers something Sattva City genuinely cannot yet: inspectable, ready inventory with zero construction-timeline risk, plus the corridor's most accessible entry-level unit sizes. Sattva City counters with scale — nearly triple the units, a wider configuration range up to 4 BHK, and a much larger amenity base — but every unit there is still a construction-timeline bet. Buyers who prioritise certainty should lean Park Cubix Phase 1; buyers who prioritise scale, larger homes, and are comfortable waiting should lean Sattva City.
