Sattva Vasanta Skye Devanahalli: Price, Floor Plans, RERA and Buyer Guide

By Narayanan Rajesh, Luxura Habitat•Updated September 30, 2026•14 Min Read

Sattva Vasanta Skye is Sattva Group's 16-acre apartment township in Kannamangala Village, on Bellary Road in Devanahalli, about 10 km from Kempegowda International Airport. It offers 1,077 homes across Studio, 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK formats, from a 454 sq ft Studio to a 2,416 sq ft 4 BHK, with prices starting at ₹78 Lakhs onwards and possession expected in May 2031.

That range is the project's defining feature. Very few Devanahalli launches let a rental investor buying a Studio and a family buying a 4 BHK choose from the same gated community. This guide goes through the developer, the location, the master plan, every configuration with carpet areas read off the floor plans, pricing and the full cost of buying, specifications, amenities, possession, reviews, risks and a due-diligence checklist.

What changed in this update: An earlier version of this review listed 2.5 BHK homes, a ₹63 Lakh starting price and sizes up to 2,623 sq ft. Those figures came from a set of "Tower 1 to 9" plans that do not match the project's master plan. This update uses the Block and Wing floor plans that do match it, along with the latest published price and possession details.

1. The developer: Sattva Group

Sattva Group, known until recently as Salarpuria Sattva Group, was founded in 1986 and is one of Bengaluru's longest-running developers, with residential, office and hospitality projects across the city. In North Bengaluru its airport-corridor portfolio now includes Sattva Aeropolis, Sattva City, Sattva Vasanta Cove and Vasanta Skye. At 1,077 homes, Vasanta Skye is the largest of these by unit count.

A known developer does not remove construction risk, but it does mean a track record you can inspect. Before booking, visit a completed Sattva project in North Bengaluru to see how the finishes and common areas have held up.

2. Location and connectivity

The site is in Kannamangala Village, Devanahalli, on Bellary Road, opposite the National Public School. NH-44 and SH-104 are close by, so the drive to the airport is short and the run south towards Yelahanka and Hebbal is a straight highway. The planned Peripheral Ring Road should further improve access to business hubs and the city centre once built.

3. The master plan

The master plan wraps the residential blocks around the edges of the 16-acre site and keeps the middle open. The developer describes 11 residential towers of G+13 and G+14 floors; on the plan these appear as 16 numbered wings grouped into Blocks A, B and C.

  • Central court: the clubhouse and swimming pool sit at the centre, surrounded by gardens, a pond, kids' play zones and winding walking paths.
  • Sports frontage: a running track around a playfield and a row of multi-sport courts line the entrance side, keeping noise away from most homes.
  • Roads and safety: wide internal roads and fire driveways loop around every block, and the main entrance has large gates with full security and a drop-off plaza.
  • Density: 1,077 homes on 16 acres is about 67 homes per acre, which is moderate for a high-rise township.

What the plans tell you

  • Loading is consistent at about 36%. Carpet area works out to roughly 64% of super built-up across every format, from the 454 sq ft Studio (291 sq ft carpet) to the 2,358 sq ft 4 BHK (1,520 sq ft carpet). Compare projects on carpet area, not SBA.
  • Two kinds of 3 BHK. The 3 BHK + 2T (about 1,509 to 1,570 sq ft) is the value option. The 3 BHK + 3T (about 1,856 to 1,955 sq ft) adds a third bathroom, a larger living and dining area and deeper balconies of 121 to 163 sq ft.
  • Compact homes are clustered. Studios and most 1 BHKs sit in Block C's Wings 15 and 16, around a long central corridor. Good for rental management, but expect a busier corridor than in the family wings.
  • Ground floor 1 BHKs are larger. The 1,012 to 1,133 sq ft 1 BHKs in Blocks A and B are all ground floor units, so ask about privacy and what each one faces.
  • 4 BHKs take the corners. The 4 BHK + 4T homes in Wings 1, 13 and 14 include a dresser off the master bedroom and balconies of 135 to 207 sq ft.

5. Price and all-in cost

Prices at Sattva Vasanta Skye start at ₹78 Lakhs onwards. The developer has not published a configuration-wise price list, and the price of any specific home depends on its wing, floor and facing, so ask for a stamped cost sheet for the exact unit you are considering.

When you compare quotes, add the costs that sit on top of the base price:

  • GST: 5% on under-construction homes (without input tax credit).
  • Stamp duty and registration: in Karnataka these together add roughly 6 to 7% of the property value.
  • Other charges: car parking, clubhouse membership, electricity and water connection charges, legal fees and advance maintenance, which vary by project and should all appear in the cost sheet.

A useful rule of thumb is to budget 12 to 15% above the base price for the all-in cost. With possession in May 2031, most buyers will pay through a construction-linked plan, so confirm the payment schedule and which banks have approved the project for home loans.

6. Specifications

The published specifications list the main construction and finish materials:

  • Structure: RCC framed structure built with top-quality materials.
  • Flooring: good-quality marble flooring in all apartments.
  • Doors: laminated flush doors.
  • Electrical: PVC-insulated copper wiring throughout.
  • Bathrooms: chrome-plated fittings.
  • Balconies: a ceiling hook in every balcony for a swing chair.

Brochure specifications are a summary. The version the developer is legally bound to is the specification schedule attached to your agreement for sale, so read that annexure, including the brands of sanitaryware, electrical switches and windows, before you sign.

7. Amenities

Sattva Vasanta Skye lists 50+ amenities for residents of all ages, anchored by a premium clubhouse and a large swimming pool at the centre of the township.

  • Sports and fitness: gymnasium and outdoor gym, tennis, badminton and basketball courts, a running track with a playfield, jogging and walking paths.
  • Wellness: swimming pool and kids' pool, yoga lawn, meditation area, lotus pond and senior citizen seating.
  • Social: clubhouse, indoor games, multipurpose hall, gazebos and pergolas.
  • Kids and family: children's play areas, landscaped central court, gardens and lawns.

With a single clubhouse serving 1,077 homes, ask how large it is and what facilities are inside it, and check whether any amenities are shared with a neighbouring Sattva development.

8. Possession and RERA

Sattva Vasanta Skye is RERA approved under PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/080525/007730. The registration dates from May 2025 and the expected possession date is May 2031, which is a long construction window, typical for a township of this size.

Verify the registration yourself on the Karnataka RERA portal, and check the completion date and quarterly progress updates filed there. RERA also lets you see whether the promoter has filed its approvals and how many units have been booked. The advertiser for this listing is an authorised channel partner registered with Karnataka RERA as PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/AG/170823/000045.

9. Reviews and ratings

Third-party project listings report that Sattva Vasanta Skye holds a rating of 4.1 out of 5 on Google reviews, with buyers mostly citing its location near the airport, the price plan and the amenities, along with the Sattva Group's reputation. We have not independently verified that rating.

Keep in mind that reviews of an under-construction project reflect the launch pitch and the sales experience, not the finished homes. The most useful review you can get is your own: visit the site, then visit a completed Sattva project nearby and talk to residents there.

10. Who Sattva Vasanta Skye suits, and who it doesn't

Good fit:

  • Professionals working at the airport, the Aerospace SEZ or the KIADB parks around Devanahalli.
  • Investors looking for a Studio or 1 BHK in a large branded township with rental demand from airport and aviation staff.
  • Families who want a 3 or 4 BHK with township amenities and 80% open space, and can wait until 2031.
  • Buyers who want to start small and upgrade within the same community later.

Think twice if:

  • You need to move in within the next two or three years.
  • Your workplace is in central, south or east Bengaluru; Manyata Tech Park alone is about 30 km away.
  • You want a small, quiet community; this is a large township with over a thousand households.

11. Risks and watchpoints

  • Long timeline. Possession in May 2031 means five-plus years of construction risk and pre-EMI or rent-plus-EMI costs.
  • Inconsistent marketing material. Listings for this project have circulated different size ranges, price points and even floor plans that belong to a different layout. Rely only on the stamped plan and cost sheet for your unit.
  • Distance from the city's job centres. Appreciation depends partly on the airport corridor's continued growth and on roads like the Peripheral Ring Road being built.
  • Supply on the corridor. Several large launches in Devanahalli will deliver around the same time, which can hold back resale prices and rents in the first years after possession.
  • One clubhouse for 1,077 homes. Check its size and facilities before assuming the amenity experience will match the brochure.

12. Vasanta Skye vs Vasanta Cove

Sattva has two projects on the same corridor with almost identical names. Vasanta Skye is the 16-acre, 1,077-home township with Studio to 4 BHK homes. Sattva Vasanta Cove is a much smaller Studio and 1 BHK development with its own RERA number (PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/041225/008310) and a December 2030 possession date. Both now offer Studios, so check which project any price or floor plan refers to. Our Vasanta Skye vs Vasanta Cove comparison sets them side by side.

13. Due-diligence checklist before you pay

  1. Verify RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/080525/007730 and its completion date on the Karnataka RERA portal.
  2. Get a stamped floor plan and cost sheet for your exact unit, with carpet area stated.
  3. Confirm the all-in price: base, GST, parking, clubhouse, deposits and maintenance.
  4. Ask for the sanctioned building plan, title documents and list of approving banks.
  5. Read the specification annexure and the delay-compensation clause in the agreement for sale.
  6. Visit the site and a completed Sattva project, and drive your daily commute at peak hour.
  7. Pay only to the developer's designated project bank account, never in cash.

Our honest assessment

Sattva Vasanta Skye's strongest points are its scale and its range: a 16-acre township with 80% open space, a moderate density of about 67 homes per acre, and homes from a 454 sq ft Studio to a 2,416 sq ft 4 BHK, 10 km from the airport and backed by a developer active since 1986. At ₹78 Lakhs onwards with a RERA-registered May 2031 possession, it suits airport-corridor professionals, patient families and rental investors.

The trade-offs are time and distance: a long wait for possession, a long commute to the city's main tech parks, and marketing material that has not always been consistent. None of these is a deal-breaker if you go in with a stamped cost sheet, a verified RERA record and a realistic view of the timeline.
