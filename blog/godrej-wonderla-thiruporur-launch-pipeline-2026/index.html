Godrej's Rs 32,000 Crore H2 FY27 Push: What It Means for Godrej Wonderla, Thiruporur

By the Luxura Habitat Team•November 21, 2026•9 Min Read

Godrej Properties used its Q2 FY27 earnings call to lay out an aggressive second-half plan: the company intends to launch homes worth around Rs 32,000 crore between October 2026 and March 2027, roughly double the Rs 16,000 crore it launched in the first six months of the financial year. Management tied this to a sales guidance of Rs 22,500-25,000 crore for H2 FY27 and flagged that about 80% of the approvals needed for the quarter's launches were already in hand before the quarter began, with the rest expected by early-to-mid January. The company's Q2 FY27 booking value came in at Rs 8,008 crore from 3,658 units sold, even as EBITDA fell sharply on higher construction spending, a pattern consistent with a developer loading up on launch-stage costs ahead of a big push.

For Chennai buyers specifically, the headline figure is national and Godrej has not published a city-by-city breakdown of where that Rs 32,000 crore will land. But one piece of the puzzle is already public and sits squarely on Chennai's OMR corridor: Godrej's 47-acre plotted development at Thiruporur, marketed as Godrej Wonderla, right next to the Wonderla Chennai amusement park. The project is currently at the pre-launch stage, with a launch date targeted for December 2026, which falls inside this same H2 FY27 window the company just told investors about.

1. How the Thiruporur Land Fits the Pipeline

Godrej Properties announced on June 30, 2026 that it had bought a roughly 47-acre parcel off OMR in South Chennai, describing it as a primarily plotted residential project with a developable potential of about 1.2 million sq ft and an estimated revenue potential of around Rs 500 crore. That is the same 47-acre, OMR-facing parcel that Godrej Wonderla's own launch material describes: a plotted layout of 950-plus plots ranging from 750 to 2,400 sq ft, anchored by a 15,000-plus sq ft Victorian-themed clubhouse the developer calls the Grand Club, with more than 62 listed amenities including a temperature-controlled pool, pickleball and paddle courts, a skating rink, pet parks and a kids' play area.

Godrej's channel-partner briefing groups the plots into four bands: Cottage Plots (750-999 sq ft) from Rs 36.99 Lakh, Villa Plots (1,000-1,199 sq ft) from Rs 49.99 Lakh, Bungalow Plots (1,200-1,499 sq ft) also quoted from Rs 49.99 Lakh, and Mansion Plots (1,500 sq ft and above) from Rs 76.99 Lakh. Those are pre-launch starting prices on the developer's own material, not figures that originate from the news coverage of the land deal, and they are subject to change once TNRERA registration and the formal price list are issued. At the entry size in each band, that works out to roughly Rs 4,900 to Rs 5,100 per sq ft, a level that sits above many unbranded plotted layouts further out on OMR but is consistent with what a large, branded developer typically commands for a gated format with this scale of clubhouse.

Rs 500 crore of revenue potential from a single land parcel is a small slice of a Rs 32,000 crore half-year pipeline, so it would be a stretch to say the company's national numbers were built around this one Chennai project. What the Rs 32,000 crore figure does usefully confirm is that Godrej is actively accelerating launches across its FY27 pipeline rather than delaying them, and Thiruporur's own December 2026 launch target lines up with exactly that acceleration window.

2. Why Thiruporur, and Why Now

Thiruporur sits at the southern tail of Chennai's OMR IT corridor, in Chengalpattu district, with the OMR junction roughly 10 minutes away and SIPCOT Siruseri, the big IT and SEZ employment cluster anchoring this stretch of OMR, about 25 minutes out. The East Coast Road is also within easy reach. The area's profile changed meaningfully in December 2025 when Wonderla Chennai, a Rs 611 crore amusement park, opened right next door, giving the micro-market a destination landmark that most OMR plotted layouts further south do not have.

That combination, a recognisable landmark, a large branded clubhouse format, and continued southward IT-corridor spillover from Siruseri and Kelambakkam, is the kind of setup that tends to draw land buyers looking at a five-to-ten-year horizon rather than a quick resale. The flip side is that outer-OMR appreciation is more sensitive to how fast supporting infrastructure, like the planned ECR-OMR link road and the slower-than-hoped Chennai Metro Phase 2 extension toward Siruseri SIPCOT, actually gets built, so buyers should treat the location as a growth bet rather than an already-arrived one.

3. What Buyers Should Actually Check

  • RERA status first. Godrej Wonderla's TNRERA registration number had not been issued as of the project's pre-launch stage. Nothing beyond a refundable Expression of Interest should be paid before that number appears on rera.tn.gov.in.
  • Possession date is not yet committed. One property portal lists an indicative December 2030 handover, but this is unconfirmed by the developer; the binding date will be the one that appears in the RERA filing and your agreement for sale.
  • Price list is still provisional. The Rs 36.99 Lakh to Rs 76.99 Lakh band is from the developer's pre-launch material; the official, RERA-filed price list can differ once it is released.
  • Plot financing works differently from a home loan. Banks typically lend a smaller share of a plot's value than they would against a ready apartment, and will usually only finance plots inside an approved, RERA-registered layout, which is one more reason the registration step matters before you commit funds.

For context on how a plotted purchase compares with a ready apartment elsewhere in Godrej's own Chennai portfolio, see our review of Godrej Azure at Padur, a sea-facing 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartment project closer to the Kelambakkam-Siruseri job cluster. Buyers weighing land ownership and design freedom against a ready-designed, rental-ready apartment will find the two projects make for a useful comparison within the same developer's Chennai pipeline.

The Bottom Line

Godrej's Rs 32,000 crore H2 FY27 pipeline is a company-wide number, not a Chennai-specific promise, and it should be read as context rather than a reason to rush. What it does tell OMR buyers is that the developer behind the Thiruporur land is in an active launch phase rather than a cautious one, which is consistent with the December 2026 launch window already attached to Godrej Wonderla. As always on a pre-launch project, the news is the context, the project's own RERA filing is the fact.
