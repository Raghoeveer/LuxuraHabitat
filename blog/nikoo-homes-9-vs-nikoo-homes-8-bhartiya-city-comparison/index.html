Nikoo Homes 9 vs Nikoo Homes 8: Which Bhartiya City Phase Should You Buy?

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published September 25, 2026•11 Min Read

If you're comparing Nikoo Homes 9 vs Nikoo Homes 8, you're really asking a narrower question than it first appears: both phases sit inside the same 125-acre Bhartiya City township in KIADB, Bagalur, share the same mall, international school and commercial campus, and carry the same developer, Bhartiya Urban. The real differences that matter for a buying decision come down to inventory, pricing, configuration choice and each phase's own RERA and construction status. This guide walks through those differences point by point, plus a short note on how Nikoo Homes 7 fits into the same picture, so you can decide which phase actually suits your budget and timeline.

1. Same Township, Different Phases

Nikoo Homes 9 and Nikoo Homes 8 are both residential phases within Bhartiya City, developed by Bhartiya Urban. Whatever phase you buy into, you get access to the same township-wide infrastructure: the Bhartiya Mall of Centre, Chaman Bhartiya International School, and the Bhartiya Centre of Information Technology (BCIT) commercial campus. This is the single biggest reason these phases are worth comparing directly rather than against unrelated standalone projects elsewhere in the KIADB Bagalur corridor -- the township-level amenities don't change based on which phase number you buy into.

2. Availability: New Launch vs Resale

This is the most practically important difference. Nikoo Homes 9 is a new launch, meaning the developer is selling fresh inventory directly, across all eight configurations, at current new-launch pricing. Nikoo Homes 8, by contrast, is an earlier phase; based on the comparative positioning in the developer's own marketing material for Nikoo Homes 9, Nikoo Homes 8 availability is now largely limited to resale units rather than fresh developer inventory. If you specifically want first-owner status, full configuration choice and a construction-linked payment plan, Nikoo Homes 9 is the more straightforward option. If you want a home that may already be closer to possession-ready, a Nikoo Homes 8 resale unit is worth exploring, though you'll need to verify its individual construction and possession status directly with the seller or developer, since we have not independently verified Nikoo Homes 8's current completion status for this comparison.

3. Configurations & Pricing

Nikoo Homes 9's official price list spans eight configurations, from a 506 sq ft Studio at ₹60 Lakhs* to a 2,190 sq ft 3 BHK Loft at ₹2.56 Cr*, plus a larger Sky Villa duplex layout (~2,681 sq ft) outside the standard list. We do not have a verified, current configuration and price list for Nikoo Homes 8 to place side by side here with the same confidence, since Nikoo Homes 8 pricing (where resale units are available) is set by individual owners rather than a single official developer price sheet, and varies by floor, view, and negotiation. If configuration variety and a transparent, single price list matter to you, that favours Nikoo Homes 9; if you're specifically hunting for a particular unit type or floor in Nikoo Homes 8, expect to negotiate directly rather than compare list prices.

4. RERA and Legal Status

Nikoo Homes 9 carries its own Karnataka RERA registration, PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/100926/008930, which we verified directly from the developer's own master plan and floor plan documents for this phase. Nikoo Homes 8 would carry its own separate, earlier RERA registration number specific to that phase; we have not independently verified that number as part of this comparison, and it is important not to confuse the two. If you are considering a Nikoo Homes 8 resale unit, ask the seller for that phase's specific RERA number and confirm it on the Karnataka RERA portal (rera.karnataka.gov.in), rather than assuming Nikoo Homes 9's registration applies.

5. A Brief Note on Nikoo Homes 7

Bhartiya Urban has launched multiple Nikoo Homes phases at this township over time, and Nikoo Homes 7 is an earlier phase still. As with Nikoo Homes 8, we have not independently verified Nikoo Homes 7's current availability, pricing or RERA status for this comparison. The general pattern holds across all earlier phases: the newer the phase, the more likely you are to find fresh developer inventory; the older the phase, the more likely you are dealing with resale, and the more important it becomes to verify that specific phase's own legal and construction status rather than relying on comparisons to Nikoo Homes 9.

6. Financing & Loan Considerations Across Phases

Financing is another practical area where new-launch and resale purchases diverge, regardless of which Nikoo Homes phase you're considering. For Nikoo Homes 9, the developer's own material names HDFC, SBI and ICICI as lending partners, and a construction-linked payment plan lets you stagger disbursements against construction milestones, which typically also staggers your home loan drawdown and interest burden during the build period. For a Nikoo Homes 8 resale purchase, your loan eligibility and the lender's valuation will depend on that specific unit's existing occupancy certificate status, any remaining builder dues, and the seller's own loan closure (if the unit was itself mortgaged), which adds a few extra verification steps a new-launch purchase doesn't require. Either way, get a written loan pre-approval and, for resale, an encumbrance certificate before you finalise, so financing timelines don't derail a deal you've otherwise agreed to.

One more practical point worth flagging: taxes and charges differ in structure, not just amount, between the two paths. A new-launch Nikoo Homes 9 purchase attracts GST on the under-construction value in addition to stamp duty and registration on the agreement value, while a completed Nikoo Homes 8 resale unit (post-occupancy certificate) is typically GST-exempt but carries its own stamp duty and registration on the resale price, plus any applicable capital gains considerations for the seller that can indirectly affect negotiation room. Ask your advisor or CA to model the all-in cost for your specific scenario rather than comparing sticker prices alone.

7. Which Phase Should You Choose?

Choose Nikoo Homes 9 if you want: full configuration choice from Studio to Loft, a transparent single price list, a construction-linked payment plan, and the reassurance of a RERA number you can verify today on documents the developer has already filed. Choose a Nikoo Homes 8 resale unit if you specifically want a home in a more mature phase of the township, are comfortable negotiating directly with an individual owner, and have verified that specific unit's construction status, RERA registration and possession timeline yourself before committing. Neither choice is automatically "better" -- it depends on whether you value new-launch transparency or a potentially more immediate move-in timeline.

Our Honest Assessment

The comparison between Nikoo Homes 9 and Nikoo Homes 8 is really a comparison between buying new-launch (with a clear price list, full configuration choice and a verifiable RERA number, but a longer runway to possession) and buying resale in a more established phase of the same township (with less price transparency and a status that needs individual verification, but potentially closer to move-in). Both sit inside the same well-established 125-acre Bhartiya City township, so the amenities and location advantage are effectively shared. Our practical recommendation: if you're comparing the two seriously, ask any Nikoo Homes 8 resale seller for their unit's exact RERA number, current occupancy certificate status and possession date in writing, and compare that directly against Nikoo Homes 9's own committed terms, rather than assuming either phase's reputation transfers automatically to the other.
