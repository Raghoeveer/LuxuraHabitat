Shriram Shankari: Price, Layout & RERA Review

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published September 2, 2026•11 Min Read

Shriram Shankari price is one of the most-searched terms around this Guduvanchery launch, and for good reason: it is a new high-rise phase by an established developer on one of South Chennai's busiest arterial roads. This review covers the developer, the location, the real unit configurations and pricing, the honest floor plan situation, possession timeline, amenities and RERA verification, so you can decide whether Shriram Shankari is worth pursuing before you contact the sales team.

1. The Developer: Shriram Properties Limited

Shriram Properties Limited is one of South India's leading residential developers, with a long-standing, customer-centric track record built primarily across the mid-market and affordable housing segments in Bangalore, Chennai and other major cities. Shriram Shankari is a Chennai township by this same developer, positioned in Guduvanchery on the city's GST Road corridor, and it is registered under Tamil Nadu's RERA regime (TNRERA), not Karnataka's, a distinction worth keeping in mind if you are comparing this project against the developer's Bangalore projects like Shriram 107 SouthEast or Shriram Bannerghatta.

2. Location: Guduvanchery, South Chennai

The project sits on Nellikuppam Road, Perumattunallur Village, in Guduvanchery, directly off GST Road (NH45) in South Chennai. Guduvanchery has historically been a more affordable, industrial-adjacent pocket relative to Tambaram or OMR, but its position on GST Road, combined with the Kilambakkam integrated bus terminus and the ongoing 8-lane widening of GST Road itself, has been steadily pulling it into a stronger demand catchment for both end-users and investors.

At a glance, the proximity profile looks like this:

  • Connectivity: Guduvanchery Railway Station (5 min), Kilambakkam Bus Terminus (5 min), GST Road / NH45 (5 min), Chennai International Airport (35 min)
  • Employment hubs: Mahindra World City (15 min), MEPZ Tambaram (20 min), Oragadam Industrial Area (25 min)
  • Schools: SRM Public School, Velammal Vidyashram (5 min), SRM University (10 min)
  • Hospitals: SRM General Hospital (10 min), Hindu Mission Hospital (25 min)
  • Landmarks: Vandalur Zoo and Shriram Gateway (5 min)

Chennai Metro Phase 2's planned stations along the Tambaram-Chromepet-Kilambakkam-Vandalur corridor are frequently cited as a medium-term growth driver for Guduvanchery. That extension is not operational yet, so treat it as upside to watch rather than a reason to expect an immediate price jump.

3. The Project: A Township Sold in Phases, and a Naming Quirk Worth Knowing

Shriram Shankari is a 36-acre township, with 75% of the land kept as open and green space. Like several large Shriram townships, it has been developed and sold in phases over time, and the phase currently being marketed carries its own internal codename: Codename: The View, sometimes also referenced as Lakeside Residences. This is the launch this review, and the Luxura Habitat project page, actually covers: high-rise towers, specifically Tower 8 (1 BHK) and Tower 27 (2 BHK). An earlier phase of the same township, generally referred to as Phase 1, was launched several years earlier under a separate RERA registration and offered a wider 1, 2 and 3 BHK configuration mix. We've written a dedicated comparison of the two, Shriram Shankari Phase 1 vs Phase 2: RERA & Price Compared, since conflating the two phases' pricing or RERA numbers is a genuinely easy mistake to make when researching this project online.

Beyond the residential towers, the current launch integrates an approximately 18,000 sq.ft on-site commercial zone, a genuinely useful feature for a high-rise community this size, with a convenience store, ATM vestibules, a pharmacy, food outlets and an on-site creche.

4. Unit Configurations and the Real Floor Plan Situation

The current launch is marketed across two configuration tiers: 1 BHK in Tower 8 and 2 BHK in Tower 27. We want to be direct about something we found while researching this project: a clean, standalone unit-level floor plan for the 1 BHK configuration was not part of the material we reviewed. What is available is a genuine "Typical 2 BHK" plan, printed directly on the builder's own artwork as 1,035-1,100 sq.ft, along with two illustrative tower floor plates (Tower 23 and Tower 26) showing the stacked-unit arrangement typical of the township's high-rise towers. If you are seriously considering a 1 BHK unit specifically in Tower 8, ask the sales team directly for that unit's dedicated plan before booking, rather than assuming it mirrors the 2 BHK layout scaled down.

We also want to flag a naming inconsistency we found in the source marketing material for this launch: one section of the builder's own microsite describes the project as a "low-density township of low-rise blocks (Stilt + 4 floors)," while the overwhelming majority of the same material, including the meta descriptions, the about section, the master plan artwork and the tower floor plates themselves, consistently describes it as a high-rise township across numbered towers (Tower 8, Tower 23, Tower 26, Tower 27 all appear on actual plan artwork). We've gone with the high-rise description here, since it's what the concrete tower floor plates and the master plan actually show, but it's worth independently confirming your specific tower's exact floor count with the sales team before booking.

A sub-₹40 Lakh entry point for a 1 BHK is a genuinely competitive price for a high-rise, amenity-rich launch directly on GST Road, and it compares favourably against similarly-positioned inventory closer to Tambaram, where land costs run higher. As with any peripheral-but-improving corridor, the trade-off is that some of the connectivity upside, the Metro Phase 2 extension in particular, is not live yet, so today's pricing reflects a location that is still partway through its infrastructure build-out rather than one that has already fully arrived.

On financing, a 1 BHK at ₹36.3 Lakhs and a 2 BHK at ₹62 Lakhs both sit comfortably within standard home-loan eligibility for a salaried buyer in the mid-income bracket, assuming a conventional 80-85% loan-to-value ratio over a 20-year tenure. Budget separately for stamp duty, registration, GST on the under-construction component and monthly maintenance, none of which were disclosed in the source material we reviewed and should be confirmed directly with the sales team before booking.

6. Amenities

The amenity list spans a 12,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse with 45+ facilities, including an outdoor swimming pool, mini-theatre, meditation spaces and a cafeteria; sports facilities including a tennis court, badminton court, skating rink and basketball hoops; community and outdoor features like jogging tracks, kids' play parks, a community lounge and landscaped gardens; core infrastructure covering 24/7 security, 100% DG power backup, advanced water management and high-speed internet readiness; and, worth calling out on its own, the roughly 18,000 sq.ft integrated commercial zone with a convenience store, ATM vestibules, a pharmacy, food outlets and an on-site creche.

7. Possession Timeline

Shriram Shankari's current launch follows a phased handover schedule, with early units targeted for mid-2027 and full project completion targeted for December 2028, per the RERA-registered timeline. A phased schedule like this typically means some towers are handed over ahead of others, so if your timeline is a priority, ask specifically which phase your tower, Tower 8 or Tower 27, falls into, rather than assuming the earliest date applies to every unit in the launch.

8. Legal and RERA Verification

At Luxura Habitat, no project recommendation is made before legal verification. The current launch of Shriram Shankari is registered under TNRERA number TN/01/BUILDING/0418/2021. We recommend verifying this number independently on the official TNRERA portal (rera.tn.gov.in) before finalising a booking, a step worth taking regardless of how established the developer is. Importantly, this is a different registration from Phase 1 of the same township, which carries its own separate number, TN/01/Building/0035/2020, issued several years earlier. Quoting the wrong number for your specific tower would misstate which phase's legal disclosures and completion timeline actually apply to your money; see our Phase 1 vs Phase 2 comparison for the full breakdown. This project-level registration is also separate from any individual agent's own RERA registration, don't conflate the two when checking documentation.

9. Who This Project Actually Suits

Shriram Shankari's current launch is a strong fit for a buyer who wants a genuine high-rise, amenity-rich lifestyle product from an established, listed developer, at a price point below what similar inventory costs closer to Tambaram, and who is comfortable with a phased possession timeline running into 2027-2028. The integrated commercial zone is a real convenience differentiator for anyone who values not having to leave the community for everyday errands. It's a weaker fit for a buyer who needs a dedicated 1 BHK layout confirmed in writing before booking, since published artwork for that exact unit isn't yet available, or for someone who needs possession within the next year.

Our Honest Assessment

Shriram Shankari has real fundamentals in its favour: a listed, established developer, a genuine high-rise format with 75% open space, a well-rounded amenity list, an unusually useful integrated commercial zone, and a starting price that undercuts more established parts of the GST Road corridor. Where we'd urge caution is the floor plan situation for 1 BHK buyers specifically, insist on seeing your exact unit's plan before booking, and the fact that this township's phased history means it's easy to accidentally research the wrong RERA number or price list online if you're not clear on which phase you're looking at. As always, verify the RERA registration (TN/01/BUILDING/0418/2021) independently on the TNRERA portal, confirm current construction progress directly with the developer, and check title and encumbrance documents for your specific unit before booking.
