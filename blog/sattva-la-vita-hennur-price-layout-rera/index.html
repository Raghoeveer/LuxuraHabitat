Sattva La Vita Review: Price, Layout & RERA

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published November 22, 2026•10 Min Read

Row houses are a small share of new supply on the Hennur corridor, which is why Sattva La Vita gets attention: 63 four-bedroom homes on about 3 acres, each with its own walls, basement parking, backyard and terrace. This review covers the developer, the location, what each home contains, the master plan and amenities, specifications, pricing, RERA status and possession, plus the questions we would ask before paying a booking amount.

1. The Developer: Sattva Group

Sattva Group is headquartered in Bengaluru and began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva (the group's own site dates its current journey to 1993). It builds commercial, residential, hospitality and education projects across several Indian cities. The group describes itself as CRISIL 'AA' Stable and ICRA 'AA' Stable rated, ISO 9001, 14001 and 18001 certified, and a founding member of the Indian Green Building Council.

Most of Sattva's residential work is apartments, so La Vita is one of its few row-house projects. That matters for due diligence: ask to see a completed Sattva low-rise or villa project, not only its towers, when you judge build quality. You can see the group's other launches on our Sattva Group projects page.

2. Location: Byrathi, off Hennur Main Road

La Vita sits in Byrathi Village, just off Hennur Main Road. Hennur Main Road branches off the Outer Ring Road and runs north towards the airport, which puts the project between the Manyata and Hebbal job belt and the airport side of North Bengaluru. Read our Hennur connectivity guide for how the corridor's roads and metro plans fit together.

3. The Homes: 4 BHK G+2 Row Houses

Every home at La Vita is the same basic type: a 4 BHK row house with a basement, a ground floor and two upper floors, and an open terrace on top. Sattva Group lists the built-up area as 3,260 sq ft. Inside, each house has:

  • 4 bedrooms
  • A home gym, an entertainment room and a family lounge
  • A domestic help room
  • A basement with 2 car parks
  • A private backyard over 10 feet deep and an open terrace
  • Independent walls, with no shared walls with neighbours

Plans are published separately for east, north and west facing homes, each with basement, ground, first and second floor layouts. You can view all twelve plans in the floor plans section of the project page. Since there is only one home type, the real choice is facing and position, so ask which units are still available and whether any premium applies.

4. Master Plan and Amenities

The master plan places 63 row houses around a 9,700 sq ft clubhouse, with themed gardens and walkways kept free of vehicle movement. Sattva Group lists these amenities:

  • Clubhouse and leisure: indoor party area, reading lounge, indoor games, table tennis room
  • Fitness: swimming pool, gym, outdoor gym, meditation pavilion
  • Sports and play: multi-purpose court, cricket practice net, kids' play area
  • Landscape and safety: theme gardens, vehicle-free walkways, gated community

For a community of 63 homes, this is a generous amenity list. The flip side is that maintenance for a pool and clubhouse is shared across few households, so ask for the expected monthly maintenance charge and the corpus amount early.

5. Specifications

Sattva Group's page does not publish a specification sheet. The list below comes from a channel-partner site and a property portal, so check each line against the specification annexure in your sale agreement.

  • Structure: Seismic Zone II compliant RCC framed structure
  • Flooring: vitrified tiles in living areas; laminated wooden flooring in the bedrooms
  • Main door: wooden frame with teak finish, 8 ft high
  • Toilets: tile dado up to 8 ft, EWC, hot and cold mixers, shower partition
  • Power: 10 KVA three-phase supply per home with 3 KVA backup; full backup for common areas
  • Kitchen: the specification list says no granite counter and sink are provided, while a portal amenity list mentions a granite platform
  • Extra cost: gas piping and solar panels
  • Private lift: one portal claims each villa has an internal lift; the developer and the specification list do not mention one

6. Price and What a Quote Should Cover

Sattva Group shows "Request price" on its La Vita page and publishes no figure, and we do not quote unofficial prices. The price you are offered will depend on facing, position and current offers. A channel-partner site says its all-inclusive figure covers land cost, construction cost, 2 car parks, development charges, GST, legal charges, corpus fund and advance maintenance, with preferred location charges, stamp duty and registration on top.

A property portal lists a construction-linked payment plan. Ask for the milestone schedule and the list of banks that have approved the project. For the current cost sheet, use the pricing section on the project page.

7. RERA, Status and Possession

  • K-RERA number: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/041223/006446
  • Launch: December 2023, as listed on a property portal, the same month as the RERA number's date code
  • Status: Sattva Group's page still tags La Vita "Pre Launch", yet it posts monthly construction updates from January 2025 to October 2026. The photo above is from June 2026
  • Possession: handover is targeted from late 2028 onwards. The developer's page gives no date, so ask for the RERA completion date in writing

The "Pre Launch" tag alongside nearly two years of construction updates is likely a label that was never changed, but it is worth asking about. What counts for a buyer is the completion date registered against the RERA number and the construction stage of your own row house.

8. Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Only 63 homes on 3 acres, so the community stays low density
  • Independent walls, a private backyard and an open terrace in every home
  • Basement parking for 2 cars and vehicle-free walkways
  • A 9,700 sq ft clubhouse with swimming pool
  • About 30 minutes to the airport and 25 minutes to Hebbal ORR, per the developer

Cons

  • Peak-hour traffic at the ORR and Hennur Main Road junction
  • No metro yet: the Blue Line is under construction about 5.5 km away
  • No published price, and possession dates differ across listings
  • Gas piping and solar panels cost extra; the private lift is unconfirmed

9. Who La Vita Suits

La Vita suits a family that wants the space and privacy of an independent house but not the upkeep of a standalone plot: four bedrooms plus a gym, an entertainment room and staff quarters, inside a gated community with a pool. It works well for households with one earner in the Manyata, Hebbal or airport belt, and for buyers who prefer a small community over a large township.

It is a weaker fit if you need to move in before late 2028, if you want a metro station within walking distance, or if you need lift access to every floor without confirming it first. Buyers weighing an apartment instead can compare it in our Concorde Sienna vs Sattva La Vita and Orchid Salisbury vs Sattva La Vita comparisons.

10. Questions to Ask Before You Pay

  1. The RERA completion date currently on the K-RERA portal for PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/041223/006446
  2. The construction stage of your specific row house, and when the occupancy certificate will be applied for
  3. Exact built-up and carpet area of your unit, level by level
  4. Price difference between east, north and west facing homes, and any corner or park-facing charge
  5. Monthly maintenance charge and the corpus amount
  6. Whether a private lift is included, optional or not offered
  7. Title documents, khata type and the approved building plan
  8. Water source and sewage treatment arrangements

The same checklist is on the project page's status and booking section.

Our Assessment

Sattva La Vita offers something the Hennur corridor has little of: a small, gated row-house community with independent walls, private outdoor space and a proper clubhouse. The product itself is clear and well defined, with one home type, three facings and a published layout.

The gaps are on price and timing. There is no published price, the developer's page still says "Pre Launch" while showing construction progress, and the late 2028 handover target is not on the developer's page. None of these is unusual for a project at this stage, but each needs a written answer. Get the cost sheet, check the RERA completion date, and visit the site to see the row house you would be buying before you commit.
