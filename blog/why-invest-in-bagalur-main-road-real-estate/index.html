Why Bagalur Main Road is North Bangalore's Next Real Estate Hotspot

By the Luxura Habitat Team • July 14, 2026 • 6 Min Read

For over a decade, Bangalore property buyers focused heavily on the East (Whitefield) and the South (Sarjapur, Kanakapura Road). However, the narrative has shifted rapidly. Today, North Bangalore is the primary driver of real estate investment in the city.

Within the North, the Yelahanka-Bagalur corridor, specifically along Bagalur Main Road, is registering the highest rate of infrastructure expansion and land value appreciation. Let's analyze why early-stage property investors are focusing on this area.

1. The Airport Connectivity Advantage

The proximity of the Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) is the main growth driver. Prior to KIA's expansion, Bagalur was largely rural. Today, the Bagalur Main Road serves as a key connecting corridor to the airport, reducing travel times to approximately 20–25 minutes.

Crucially, this corridor allows commuters to bypass the heavily congested Hebbal flyover and Bellary Road highway traffic. For frequent travelers and aviation professionals, this makes the location highly practical.

2. Massive Employment Hubs (KIADB & Aerospace Parks)

Real estate value is directly linked to nearby employment. The Bagalur corridor is situated adjacent to the massive KIADB Aerospace, IT & Hardware Park.

  • Multinational Presence: Major corporations, including Boeing, Shell Technology, and Wipro, have established large campuses in this park.
  • Job Creation: Thousands of high-paying tech and aerospace engineering roles are being created in the immediate vicinity.
  • Rental Demand: The influx of corporate executives is creating strong demand for premium 3 BHK and 4 BHK residential apartments in the neighborhood.

3. Social Infrastructure and Education Enclaves

Unlike raw investment zones, the Yelahanka-Bagalur corridor has established educational and medical infrastructure:

  • Top Schools: Delhi Public School (DPS) Bangalore North, Stonehill International School, and Canadian International School are all within a 15-minute radius.
  • Universities: REVA University and Presidency College are nearby, drawing student and faculty populations.
  • Healthcare: Access to Aster CMI Hospital and Manipal Hospital Hebbal ensures quality healthcare is close by.

4. The shift to low-density, premium living

Post-pandemic, home buyers are moving away from congested high-rise layouts containing thousands of units. The Bagalur Main Road corridor offers developers the space to build low-density projects with a focus on open space and lifestyle amenities.

Developments like the Assetz Zen & Sato community are prime examples, utilizing Kyoto-inspired minimalism, large open lawns, and resort-style clubhouses on large plots of land. This type of layout is difficult to find in the crowded central areas of the city.

Conclusion: When to invest?

From an investment standpoint, the Bagalur Main Road corridor is currently in its sweet spot. The social infrastructure is established, the employment parks are active, and property values are appreciating steadily.

Investing now allows you to secure entry-level pricing before the upcoming metro expansion is completed, which historically triggers a second wave of rapid price increases.
