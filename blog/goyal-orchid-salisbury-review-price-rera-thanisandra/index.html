Goyal Orchid Salisbury: Price, Configuration, RERA & Our Investment Verdict

By Narayanan Rajesh•July 29, 2026•9 Min Read

In the Hennur–Thanisandra real estate market, Goyal Orchid Salisbury stands out as the largest and most comprehensive premium apartment project on Thanisandra Main Road. With 660+ apartments across 7 high-rise towers, it represents a scale rarely seen in gated residential communities in this belt. This is a complete independent review covering configuration, pricing, RERA status, location analysis, and our honest investment assessment.

1. The Developer: Goyal and Co

Goyal and Co is one of Bangalore's established Tier-1 residential developers, with a track record spanning 30+ years and multiple delivered communities across the city. Unlike some newer developers who have struggled with project delays in the post-COVID environment, Goyal and Co has maintained a reliable delivery record across their Orchid series of projects — including Orchid Whitefield, Orchid Brindavan, and Orchid Woods.

For rental investors especially, developer track record matters enormously: a delayed possession translates directly to lost rental income and carrying costs. Goyal's delivery consistency is a meaningful risk mitigator.

2. Location Analysis: Thanisandra Main Road

Orchid Salisbury is located on Agrahara Layout, Thanisandra Main Road — one of the most strategically positioned addresses in the Manyata Tech Park catchment zone:

  • Manyata Tech Park: ~4–6 km, approximately 10–15 minutes under normal traffic
  • Hebbal Flyover: ~8 km — gateway to NH-44 and KIAL
  • Outer Ring Road junction: ~5 km — providing east-west ORR connectivity
  • Kempegowda International Airport: ~30–35 km via NH-44, approximately 40–50 minutes
  • Nagavara Metro Station (planned): ~3–4 km

4. Amenities

The sheer scale of Orchid Salisbury enables amenities that smaller gated communities cannot justify:

  • Multiple swimming pools (adult and children's)
  • Clubhouse with indoor sports facilities
  • Gymnasium and wellness centre
  • Children's play areas across multiple levels
  • Landscaped gardens and walking tracks between towers
  • Dedicated parking for all units with EV charging provisions
  • 24-hour professional security with CCTV across common areas

5. Investment Assessment

For End-Users: Orchid Salisbury ticks every box for a Manyata-area family home. The location is strategic, the developer is reliable, and the community scale ensures a vibrant, well-managed residential environment. The entry price of ₹1.03 Cr for a 2 BHK is among the most competitive for a Tier-1 developer project in this belt.

For Rental Investors: At an entry price of ₹1.03–1.2 Cr for a 2 BHK, with expected rents of ₹22,000–₹28,000/month, the gross yield works out to approximately 2.8–3.5%. Add 12–16% annual capital appreciation and the total return is compelling on a 3–5 year horizon.

Our Verdict: Orchid Salisbury is one of the most complete value propositions in North Bangalore's premium mid-market segment. Strong developer, strong location, competitive pricing. For buyers with a ₹1–1.5 Cr budget targeting Manyata-belt exposure, it warrants serious consideration.
