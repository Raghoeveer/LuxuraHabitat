Goyal Orchid Salisbury Review: Price, Floor Plans, Location and Our Verdict

By Narayanan Rajesh, Luxura Habitat•Updated October 1, 2026•18 Min Read

Thanisandra Main Road has turned into one of North Bangalore's busiest residential streets. It sits between Hennur, Manyata Tech Park and the airport road, and has filled up with gated high-rises aimed at the IT workforce. Orchid Salisbury, launched in March 2024 by Goyal & Co. | Hariyana Group, is one of the larger ones: about 660 apartments in 7 towers on roughly 6 acres, right on the main road.

This review sets out what you actually get. We read every unit size, carpet area and tower allocation straight off the builder's 10 floor plans, and work out the real rate per square foot of carpet area. We go through the site layout, the 27 amenities and the specification sheet, look at construction progress from June 2025 drone photos, and are candid about the trade-offs. Whether you are buying a home or an investment, the aim is to help you decide on the numbers.

1. The developer: Goyal & Co. | Hariyana Group

Goyal & Co. started in Ahmedabad in 1971 and has since expanded to Mumbai and Bangalore. In Bangalore it operates as Goyal & Co. | Hariyana Group. The developer reports about 250 completed projects across apartments, commercial buildings, plotted land and leasing. It describes its approach as building communities rather than just buildings, with an emphasis on timely delivery and fair pricing.

The most useful fact for a Salisbury buyer is local. The builder's own location map marks six other Goyal communities within a few kilometres: Orchid Piccadilly, Footprints, Orchid Woods, Orchid Greens, Alanoville and Orchid Nirvana 2.0/3.0. You can walk around finished Goyal buildings in the same neighbourhood and see how they have aged, how the common areas are maintained and what residents say, before you pay a booking amount.

2. Location and distances

Orchid Salisbury has frontage on Thanisandra Main Road, with separate entry and exit gates. Thanisandra Main Road runs north from the Outer Ring Road at Nagawara, past Manyata Tech Park and Bharatiya City, towards Bagalur and the airport side. Hennur Bagalur Road runs parallel to the east. That puts the project in the Manyata catchment while still being on the airport side of the city.

3. Site layout and towers

The site is a long rectangle with its short side on Thanisandra Main Road. Seven towers run in a zig-zag chain through the middle, with a looping internal road around them and ramps down to basement parking. The clubhouse sits beside the entry gate with the banquet lawn in front of it. The swimming pool, party lawn and pavilion are in the middle of the site. The back half has the sports zone: a multipurpose court, pickleball court, celebration lawn, cricket pitch along the boundary and a fitness park in the far corner.

Each tower has up to 7 homes per floor (numbered 01 to 07). Towers A to E go up to the 14th floor; towers F and G stop at the 13th. Tower G, at the back-left corner, is the only tower whose floor plans show the 1,190 and 1,200 sq ft 2 BHK variants. Towers D and E hold the largest 1,675 sq ft 3 BHKs.

A note on density: about 660 homes on about 6 acres is roughly 110 homes per acre. That is normal for a mid-segment high-rise in this corridor, but it means the pool, courts and clubhouse will be shared by a lot of families. Busy weekends are likely.

A carpet share of about 66% is in line with most Bangalore high-rises with a full clubhouse. The important thing is to compare projects on carpet area. A "1,185 sq ft" 2 BHK here gives you 781 sq ft of usable floor inside the walls.

2 BHK Compact (930 sq ft): really a 1 BHK + study

Despite the name, the Compact has one bedroom (11'0" x 12'0" in Tower C), a separate study of about 8'7" x 10'0", two toilets, a kitchen with breakfast counter, a utility, a foyer and a sit-out. The study has a door and fits a single bed, so it can work as a small second bedroom, a home office or a nursery. This is the unit some 2024 listings described as a "1 BHK". Some portals also list a separate 970 sq ft 1 BHK (about 650 sq ft carpet), shown as sold out as of September 2026; it is not among the builder floor plans we have.

2 BHK (1,180 to 1,200 sq ft)

The standard 2 BHK is a conventional, efficient layout: two bedrooms of roughly 11' x 11'6" to 11' x 15', two toilets (one attached), a separate 8' x 10' kitchen with a utility, and a living-dining room around 19' deep with a sit-out. There are four versions across the towers; the differences are mostly orientation and a few square feet. The 1,200 sq ft Tower G variant has the widest living room (20'3").

3 BHK + 2T (1,420 to 1,430 sq ft): 2 bedrooms + study

"2T" means two toilets, not two terraces. This layout has two full bedrooms (the master is about 11' x 15'2" or 15'7" x 11'), a separate study of about 8' x 10', two toilets, a 10' x 8' kitchen with utility, and a large living-dining room (about 17'6" x 20'6"). It is the best-value layout for a family that wants a dedicated work-from-home room without paying for a full third bedroom and third toilet.

3 BHK (1,650 to 1,675 sq ft)

The largest homes have three bedrooms, three toilets, a walk-through dressing area off the master bedroom, two sit-outs, a 10' x 8' kitchen with utility and a living-dining room around 21'9" or 18'10" wide. They suit larger families or buyers upgrading from an older 2 BHK.

5. Price list and the real rate per sq ft

Orchid Salisbury launched in March 2024 at about ₹9,000 per sq ft of super built-up area. By 2026 the asking rate had climbed to about ₹12,000 per sq ft, a rise of roughly a third. In mid-2026, Square Yards listed the 930 sq ft unit at ₹1.13 Cr, 1,185 sq ft at ₹1.43 Cr, 1,430 sq ft at ₹1.72 Cr and 1,675 sq ft at ₹2.01 Cr. Housiey's September 2026 all-inclusive prices (base plus GST and possession charges) run from ₹1.16 Cr to ₹2.05 Cr.

Two points to note. First, the shear wall system is a plus: it is the standard for well-built high-rises and generally gives better seismic and structural performance than a plain column-and-beam frame. Second, the DG backup inside each home is 1 to 1.5 kW, which runs lights, fans and a fridge, not air conditioners. The "100% backup" in some marketing applies to the common areas.

9. Construction progress

Drone photos from 28 June 2025, about 15 months after launch, show most of the tower structures at or near full height, with tower cranes still on site and a couple of blocks still rising. That is healthy progress for a project with a December 2028 RERA date: the structure is usually the slowest part, and finishing, services and amenities fill the remaining time. Ask us for current site photos, and check the quarterly progress updates on the Karnataka RERA portal.

10. Pros and cons

Pros

  • Main-road frontage on Thanisandra, with schools and hospitals within 1 to 3 km and Bharatiya City close by
  • North Bangalore is one of the city's most sought-after and fastest-developing areas, good for both end-use and investment
  • Goyal & Co. has a long record and several finished projects in the same neighbourhood that you can inspect
  • Asking rates up roughly a third since the 2024 launch, a sign of steady demand
  • Uniform carpet rate across all layouts; no layout is poor value
  • Study rooms in the Compact and 3 BHK + 2T suit hybrid workers
  • Structure largely up by mid-2025, which lowers timeline risk

Cons

  • Heavy peak-hour traffic towards the Outer Ring Road, Hennur Road, Hebbal and Manyata Tech Park
  • Civic works and new construction in the area mean noise and dust for a few more years
  • Possession in December 2028 means paying EMI and rent together for a while
  • About 110 homes per acre; shared amenities will be busy
  • Only 1 to 1.5 kW backup per home
  • Some listings claim smart-home automation, which is not in the specification sheet; if it matters, get it in writing, and remember automated systems need maintenance

11. Investment view

The investment case rests on the Manyata Tech Park tenant base. In our Manyata rental yield analysis, 2 BHKs of 1,100 to 1,350 sq ft in this belt rent for roughly ₹28,000 to ₹36,000 a month in new gated projects. On a ₹1.43 Cr 2 BHK, that is a gross yield of about 2.3 to 3.0%, typical for Bangalore and not a reason to buy on its own.

The 930 sq ft Compact is the lower ticket for investors at about ₹1.13 Cr. Its study room makes it easy to rent to a single professional or a couple at roughly ₹22,000 to ₹28,000 a month, a similar gross yield of about 2.3 to 3.0%. Early buyers have done well on paper: the asking rate has risen from about ₹9,000 to about ₹12,000 per sq ft in roughly two years, but buyers entering now are paying that higher rate.

Capital growth will depend on North Bangalore's infrastructure (the Pink Line metro, road widening and the airport economy) and on how much competing supply lands on Thanisandra Main Road. Compare with nearby launches such as Concorde Neo, Meenakshi High Life and TVS Emerald Altura before deciding; our Concorde Neo vs Orchid Salisbury comparison covers the closest rival in detail. For the wider market, see the Hennur-Thanisandra market report.

12. Who should buy, and who should skip

Good fit

  • Manyata Tech Park, Hebbal and ORR-North professionals who want a short commute
  • First-time buyers with a budget of about ₹1.15 to 1.25 Cr all-in (the Compact)
  • Hybrid workers who need a separate study (the Compact or the 3 BHK + 2T)
  • Families who already live in North Bangalore and want to stay near their schools
  • Investors targeting steady IT-professional rentals

Probably skip if

  • You need to move in before 2029
  • You want low density, large open spaces or resort-style amenities
  • You work in South or East Bangalore (Electronic City, Whitefield, Sarjapur)
  • You need full power backup for air conditioning

13. Due-diligence checklist before you pay

  1. Check the right RERA number. The project ID is PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/271123/006440. Numbers containing "AG" on some listing sites are agent registrations, not the project's.
  2. Get carpet area in the agreement. Make sure the sale agreement states the RERA carpet area shown in the table above.
  3. Ask for the stamped cost sheet. Confirm today's rate per sq ft, floor-rise, car park charges, maintenance deposit and GST.
  4. Pick your tower carefully. Unit types differ by tower; check which side your unit faces (main road, internal lawns or neighbouring plots).
  5. Visit a finished Goyal project nearby. Orchid Piccadilly, Footprints or Orchid Woods will show you how Goyal buildings age.
  6. Read the payment plan. Construction-linked plans should tie payments to verifiable milestones.
  7. Check the RERA progress reports. Compare the quarterly updates with what you see on site.

Our verdict

Orchid Salisbury is a sensible, mid-segment project from a developer with real local history. It does not try to be a luxury township. What it offers is a main-road Thanisandra address, practical layouts with honest and uniform loading, a decent amenity list and construction that was well advanced by mid-2025. The 930 sq ft Compact (from about ₹1.13 Cr) and the 3 BHK + 2T with a study are the stand-out layouts. Prices have risen about a third since launch, so the value case now rests more on location and developer than on a launch discount.

The trade-offs are the usual ones for this corridor: peak-hour traffic, a fairly dense site and a late-2028 handover. If you work in North Bangalore and can wait for possession, Orchid Salisbury deserves a place on your shortlist. Confirm the current rate and visit both the site and a finished Goyal project nearby before you book.
