Brigade KIADB Review: Devanahalli 75-Acre Township Verdict

By Narayanan Rajesh•Published September 25, 2026•12 Min Read

Most pre-launch profiles are built from marketing decks. This one is different: it's built from an Environmental Clearance file, and that changes the conversation. Brigade KIADB Phase 2, the working name for Brigade Integrated Mixed Use Development, has a regulatory record that already confirms a 75.04-acre footprint and 7,195 planned homes at Devanahalli, a corridor where airport-belt values reached roughly ₹9,500 per sq ft in early 2026, up more than 20% year-on-year. Scale, here, is not just a claim. It is filed. This review walks through what's verified, what's developer-stated but not yet filed, and what's simply market chatter, so you know exactly which numbers to trust before you enquire.

The Short Answer

Brigade KIADB Phase 2 is a township-scale project by Brigade Group inside Devanahalli KIADB Industrial Area Phase II, with an Environmental Clearance recording 75.04 acres, roughly 20.5 million sq ft of built-up area at FAR 3.25, and 7,195 dwelling units alongside a 300-bed hospital and a 500-key hotelEC-Verified. Configurations lean 2 and 3 BHK on early intel, pricing is on request, and RERA registration is awaited. The verified scale and Brigade's delivery pedigree are the draw. The biggest trade-off is density: about 96 units per acre signals a very high-rise, high-population environment.

1. What Is Brigade KIADB, and Who Is It For?

Brigade KIADB Phase 2 is a self-contained integrated township rather than a standalone apartment cluster, which defines who it suits. With 7,195 homes plus a hospital and a hotel, it targets buyers who want a live-work-thrive ecosystem inside the airport and aerospace belt, end users betting on Devanahalli's long employment build-out, and investors taking a multi-year view on North Bangalore's fastest-rerating corridor. It is a poor fit for anyone seeking low density, a boutique community, or a quick ready-to-move purchase. This is scale living, by design.

2. Can You Trust Brigade Group to Deliver This?

Brigade Group is one of the strongest names in this review set: alongside Prestige, it holds one of the most extensive completed residential portfolios in Bangalore across the widest spread of corridors, per developer rankings. Township execution is genuinely its home turf — Brigade itself frames this project as a landmark chapter since its 2008 Brigade Gateway township in RajajinagarDeveloper-Stated. That materially de-risks delivery relative to a smaller builder. The caveat is that a township of this size takes many years and phases to complete, so early buyers should scrutinise the phasing plan and confirm which amenities and infrastructure land in the first phase versus the last.

3. Location & Connectivity: Inside the KIADB Aerospace Corridor

The site sits inside Devanahalli KIADB Phase II at Haraluru and Muddenahalli villages, and its location logic is unusually strong. Kempegowda International Airport, the KIADB aerospace and defence ecosystem, and ready industrial infrastructure anchor long-term employment demand, with Boeing India, Airbus India, Shell Technology Centre, Tata Advanced Systems and Brahmos Aerospace all sitting inside the same corridor, per the project's own location map. The Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) runs along the belt, and the Namma Metro Blue Line's airport extension, Phase 2B, targets a December 2027 opening, per metro tracking. The trade-off is that today, Devanahalli still relies on private transport, with social infrastructure maturing rather than mature.

4. Homes, Sizes & the Township's Five Pillars

Early channel intel points to a 2 and 3 BHK-led mix as the EC-confirmed coreEC-Verified, but market reports separately describe a wider Studio, 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK range from roughly 800 sq ft to 2,580 sq ftMarket-Reported — this is where confirmed and indicative facts genuinely diverge, so treat any specific floor plan as illustrative until RERA filing publishes exact areas. What the EC record does confirm is the built form: multiple towers with 1 to 4 basement levels plus ground and up to 30 upper floors, a stated maximum of 35.

Brigade's own pre-launch collateral organises the township around five pillars, all developer-stated rather than RERA-filed:

  • Live: Residential towers (one internal deck slide cites 27 floors and 1,614+ apartments for a specific cluster), a clubhouse, sports & recreation and community spaces.
  • Work: A second World Trade Center campus (W1 & W2 towers) — roughly 1 million sq ft as the business anchor, plus about 3 million sq ft of IT/ITES office space.
  • Thrive: A school and a hospital, cited as 300-bed in the EC filing and, inconsistently, as 200-bed on one internal marketing slide.
  • Experience: A 5 lakh sq ft retail mall with a multiplex cinema, dining and lifestyle destinations.
  • Hospitality: A 500-key hotel (EC filing) described elsewhere as a "5-star hotel" with an attached convention centre.

We're flagging the hospital bed-count and unit-count discrepancies deliberately: even within Brigade's own materials, some numbers vary (7,195 units per the EC filing vs. approximately 7,200 on the master-plan sector legend vs. 1,614+ for one residential cluster specifically). None of this suggests dishonesty, township master plans evolve, but it does mean you should treat every non-EC figure as indicative until the RERA filing reconciles them.

5. What Will Brigade KIADB Cost, All In?

Pricing is strictly on request, and no honest review will invent a number for an unlaunched township. The planning context: the Devanahalli airport belt trades around ₹9,500 per sq ft in 2026 on average, though a large-format Brigade township could price above or below that depending on phase and tower. Unverified market chatter circulates an indicative table (roughly ₹60.5 Lakhs for a 1 BHK up to ₹3.22 Crore for a 4 BHK "Ultima")Market-Reported, Unverified — treat this strictly as a planning reference, not a price list. On top of any base rate, budget Karnataka stamp duty of 5% plus cess, and a registration charge of 2% (raised from 1% on 31 August 2025), then add floor-rise, parking, club and GST. Township maintenance at this scale is also a recurring cost worth modelling before you commit.

6. Is Brigade KIADB RERA Approved, and When Is Possession?

No, Brigade KIADB Phase 2 is not RERA registered yet; Environmental Clearance has been granted under Category B1, but RERA registration is awaited, so it remains pre-RERAEC-Verified. Under Section 3 of the RERA Act, no booking or sale is legal before registration, regardless of how advanced the EC is. Possession is indicated around Q3 2031 on independent channel estimates, while Brigade's own pre-launch collateral separately cites a window from December 2030 to mid-2031, with official launch targeted for late 2026Developer-Stated. Neither is a filed commitment; wait for the RERA registration and phase-wise completion dates before treating any timeline as binding.

8. Your Pre-Booking Checklist

A pre-RERA township carries extra moving parts, so run this seven-point checklist before any payment and treat a failed row as a stop signal:

  1. Confirm the Karnataka RERA number is filed and active before paying anything.
  2. Read the phasing plan and confirm which amenities land in your phase.
  3. Get a dated, itemised price sheet with floor-rise, parking, club and GST.
  4. Model recurring township maintenance and common-area charges.
  5. Verify the EC conditions and the KIADB sector allotment for your chosen block.
  6. Confirm the exact unit size and floor for your chosen home at RERA filing.
  7. Track Namma Metro Blue Line Phase 2B progress against your possession horizon.

9. The Honest Risks & Trade-Offs

The defining trade-off is density. About 96 units per acre across towers rising to 35 floors is a genuinely high-population environment, with the lift queues, parking pressure and construction phasing that scale implies. The second risk is stage: even with EC granted, this is pre-RERA, so price, configuration and possession are not yet enforceable. Third, a township of 20.5 million sq ft delivers over many years, meaning early residents will likely live alongside active construction elsewhere in the township for a long time. None of these are hidden; they are the price of the scale and ecosystem on offer, and a buyer who genuinely wants low density should be honest with themselves that this township is not the place to find it.

Our Honest Assessment

Brigade KIADB Phase 2 is one of the more substantive pre-launch stories we've reviewed in North Bangalore: a regulator-verified 75-acre footprint, an integrated hospital-and-hotel program, and a top-tier builder in a corridor with real employment tailwinds. That foundation is genuinely strong, and it's rare to see EC-filed numbers this specific behind a project still this early. But it is still pre-RERA and unmistakably high-density, and even Brigade's own pre-launch materials don't fully agree with each other on unit counts and hospital bed capacity. The disciplined path here is to register interest, study the phasing plan carefully, wait for the RERA filing to fix price, configuration and possession, and then decide whether township scale genuinely fits how you want to live.
