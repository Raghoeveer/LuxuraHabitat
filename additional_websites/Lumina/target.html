About Project

A premium high-rise residential community with 75% open green spaces and modern amenities designed for contemporary lifestyle

Experience Premium Urban Living

Shriram Songs of the Earth is a meticulously planned nature-inspired residential community spanning 4 acres in Attibele, South Bangalore. Developed by Shriram Properties Limited, this project features 6 towers with 340 premium residences designed for low-density forest living.

Every aspect of the development reflects a commitment to quality and sustainability. With over 1,500 planned trees, a forest trail, and large central courtyard, it offers a unique living experience. Handover is scheduled for December 2030 onwards, and the project is fully RERA compliant (PR/120625/007819).

The Ultimate Guide to Shriram Songs of the Earth

Redefining Nature-Themed Luxury and Investment Potential in South Bangalore

Locality Information: The Rise of Attibele and Chandapura

Attibele and Chandapura, nestled in the vibrant southern corridor of Bangalore, are rapidly transforming from tranquil suburbs into bustling epicenters of modern urban living. Historically known for their serene landscapes, these twin localities are now at the forefront of Bangalore’s residential revolution. Shriram Songs of the Earth strategically anchors itself in this high-growth quadrant, offering residents a rare blend of pristine nature and cosmopolitan convenience.

What makes this locality truly stand out is its unpolluted environment coupled with meticulous urban planning. Unlike the saturated neighborhoods of central Bangalore, Attibele offers wide, uncongested roads, abundant green cover, and a noticeably lower air quality index, making it an idyllic setting for families. The area is witnessing an influx of premium educational institutions, world-class healthcare facilities, and vibrant commercial centers, ensuring that every lifestyle necessity is within a short, comfortable drive. Living here means waking up to the sounds of a 1,500+ tree mini-forest without ever feeling disconnected from the heartbeat of the metropolis.

Investment Potential: Why South Bangalore is the Next Big Hotspot

For astute investors and visionary homebuyers, Shriram Songs of the Earth represents more than just a luxurious residence; it is a highly lucrative asset poised for exponential appreciation. South Bangalore is currently undergoing a phenomenal infrastructural renaissance.

Real estate analysts project a substantial capital appreciation in the Attibele-Sarjapur belt over the next three to five years. The catalyst for this surge is the multi-billion-dollar government investment in local infrastructure. With starting prices at an incredibly competitive ₹1.02 Cr, the barrier to entry is low, while the ceiling for returns is exceptionally high. Rental yields in the vicinity are also steadily climbing, driven by the expanding workforce in nearby IT parks like Electronic City and the Bommasandra industrial corridor. Purchasing a property here is not merely acquiring a home; it’s securing a stake in Bangalore’s most promising economic growth engine.

Connectivity Analysis: Seamless Integration with the Metropolis

The true hallmark of premium real estate is connectivity, and Shriram Songs of the Earth excels unequivocally in this domain. The project is strategically positioned just minutes away from Hosur Road (NH 44), a vital arterial highway that facilitates effortless transit to both the heart of Bangalore and neighboring industrial nodes in Tamil Nadu.

The connectivity matrix is further strengthened by the Upcoming Metro Network, located a short drive from the development. This will seamlessly integrate South Bangalore with the broader Namma Metro network, drastically reducing commute times to key business districts. Additionally, the proximity to Electronic City ensures that inter-city and intra-city transit is readily accessible. Whether you are commuting for work, dropping children at school, or planning a weekend getaway, the unparalleled transport infrastructure guarantees that you spend less time in transit and more time enjoying life.

Infrastructure Developments: The PRR and Beyond

The defining infrastructural milestone set to redefine South Bangalore’s global standing is the upcoming Peripheral Ring Road (PRR). Situated at a convenient driving distance from Shriram Songs of the Earth, the PRR is expected to be a massive economic multiplier, acting as a magnet for massive commercial, hospitality, and retail investments, inevitably driving up property values in the surrounding radius.

Beyond the PRR, the region is benefiting from the rapid expansion of the Bommasandra-Jigani industrial corridor and Wipro SEZ at Sarjapur. This corridor is home to multinational giants. The influx of highly skilled professionals to these mega employment hubs is creating a robust, sustained demand for premium residential spaces.

Lifestyle Benefits: The Forest-Themed Paradigm

Shriram Songs of the Earth is designed around the progressive concept of a 'nature-themed city,' where every essential and aspirational need is met within a short radius of your doorstep. Spread across an expansive 4 acres with a remarkable forest theme, the project offers a sanctuary of peace amidst urban chaos.

The lifestyle here is defined by an array of world-class amenities curated for all age groups. Imagine starting your day with a brisk run on the dedicated forest trails, unwinding in the resort-style swimming pool, or working from the unique Work-from-Garden Pods. The colossal clubhouse serves as the social epicenter, featuring a state-of-the-art gymnasium, an elegant mini-theatre, and sophisticated indoor game zones. For children, the meticulously designed adventure play areas provide a safe and stimulating environment to grow and thrive.

Moreover, the community is safeguarded by multi-tier security systems, ensuring absolute peace of mind. By choosing Shriram Songs of the Earth, you are not just buying an apartment; you are embracing a holistic, elevated lifestyle that prioritizes your wellness, convenience, and joy.
