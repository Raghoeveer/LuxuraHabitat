Goyal Riviera Glade Overview

A premium low-density villa community by Goyal & Co on MVIT Road, Yelahanka.

Exclusive triplex villas on MVIT Road, Yelahanka

Goyal Riviera Glade is a new-launch gated community by Goyal & Co | Hariyana Group on MVIT Road, Bettahalasur / Chikkajala, Yelahanka, North Bengaluru. Spread across 9.47 acres with just 112 villas, the layout works out to a low density of approximately 12 units per acre — among the more spacious villa-plot ratios in the corridor.

The development exclusively offers 4 BHK luxury triplex villas (Ground + 1 + Terrace) across four variants — Rose, Iris, Lotus and Olive — each with its own private garden or backyard and no common walls with neighbouring villas. The community is anchored by the Sylvan Forest Clubhouse and designed for HNIs, CXOs, entrepreneurs and business owners seeking a private, low-density address in North Bengaluru.

The Investment Advantage

Why Goyal Riviera Glade is a compelling choice for HNI buyers and investors alike.

Goyal Riviera Glade is positioned directly on this corridor, giving early buyers a foothold in a locality with strong upside as surrounding infrastructure — including the KIADB Aerospace Park and the upcoming Metro corridor — continues to mature.
Connectivity to Employment Hubs MVIT Road offers practical daily access to Manyata Tech Park, the KIADB Aerospace Park and the Outer Ring Road, all roughly 20 minutes away — while Kempegowda International Airport itself is just 20–30 minutes by road, making Riviera Glade a realistic option for frequent flyers, aerospace-sector professionals and business owners who value a shorter, more predictable commute.
A Rare Low-Density Villa Product At just 112 villas across 9.47 acres — roughly 12 units per acre — Goyal Riviera Glade offers a genuinely low-density product that's increasingly rare in North Bengaluru's villa segment. Combined with private gardens, no common walls and a clubhouse-anchored master plan, it's designed to appeal to HNIs, CXOs and business owners who want privacy and scale without sacrificing community amenities.

About Goyal & Co | Hariyana Group

Goyal Riviera Glade extends the developer's footprint into the MVIT Road, Yelahanka corridor, joining Goyal Orchid Salisbury on Thanisandra Main Road as part of the group's wider "Orchid" and premium villa portfolio in the region.

With Riviera Glade, the developer has opted for an exclusively 4 BHK triplex villa format — a deliberate move toward a low-density, HNI-focused product rather than a high-rise or mixed-configuration layout.
