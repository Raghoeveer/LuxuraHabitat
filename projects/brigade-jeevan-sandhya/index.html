Brigade Group (Brigade Enterprises Limited) was founded in 1986 by M. R. Jaishankar and is headquartered in Bengaluru. Its first project, Brigade Towers, completed in 1989, was the city's first private high-rise building. In 2004, Brigade launched Brigade Gateway, Bengaluru's first integrated lifestyle enclave.

The company listed on the BSE and NSE in 2013 (ticker: BRIGADE) and now operates across residential development, commercial leasing and hospitality, with 300+ projects covering roughly 100 million Sq.Ft delivered across South India. Brigade is currently led by Pavitra Shankar (MD) and Nirupa Shankar (Joint MD), with M. R. Jaishankar continuing as Executive Chairman.

Brigade Jeevan Sandhya brings that scale to a boutique, single-tower ultra-luxury format on Kanakapura Road, near Vajarahalli Metro Station.
