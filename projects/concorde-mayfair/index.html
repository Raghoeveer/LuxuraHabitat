Introduction

Welcome to Concorde Mayfair, a spectacular upcoming residential enclave by the renowned Concorde Group. Located strategically on the bustling Bellary Road (Airport Road) in Yelahanka, North Bangalore, this premium 3.30-acre development redefines modern luxury living.

Unmatched Connectivity

Nestled in the high-growth corridor of North Bangalore, Concorde Mayfair guarantees exceptional connectivity. Its prime location provides effortless access to the Kempegowda International Airport and the upcoming Yelahanka & Jakkur Metro stations, alongside prominent IT parks and commercial centers.

Thoughtfully Designed Residences

Available in spacious 3 BHK configurations spanning 1452 Sq.Ft and 1667 Sq.Ft, each home is an architectural masterpiece. The layouts are optimized to provide maximum privacy while ensuring an open, breathable living experience with ample natural light and ventilation.

Investment Potential

North Bangalore is rapidly expanding, driven by massive infrastructure developments. Properties in Yelahanka are witnessing significant appreciation, making Concorde Mayfair an excellent choice for end-users and investors alike.
