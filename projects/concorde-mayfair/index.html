Concorde Mayfair Overview

A compact, mixed-use address on Bellary Road: homes, high-street retail and offices on one 3.17-acre site.

Downtown living on the airport road

Concorde Mayfair is Concorde Group's integrated development on Bellary Road in Yelahanka, North Bangalore. On a 3.17-acre site it combines 217 apartments in 2 BHK and 3 BHK layouts with commercial space and a high-street retail block, so everyday shopping and services are a short walk from your lift lobby.

The homes sit in four towers (A to D), each with two basements, a ground floor and 14 upper floors. About 61% of the land is left open, and the amenities are split between podium-level gardens and courts and the Evolve sky clubhouse on the 14th floor.

Built around smart, efficient homes

Every apartment has a 9.7 ft ceiling, an 8 ft entrance door and 7 ft double-glazed windows. Homes are Alexa enabled with digital door locks, and the plans are unusually efficient: about 67% of the super built-up area is usable carpet.

Master Plan

How 217 homes, a retail block and 40+ amenities fit on 3.17 acres.

  • Commercial block on the road. The retail and office building faces Bellary Road and shields the homes behind it from the highway.
  • Four residential towers. Towers A to D are arranged in a staggered row at the rear of the site.
  • Podium amenities between towers. Pool deck, courts, kids' play area and gardens sit in the courtyards.
  • Sky clubhouse on top. Evolve occupies the 14th floor, with terrace amenities on the level above.
  • Separate entry and exit. Dedicated in and out gates off Bellary Road, with a peripheral driveway and two levels of basement parking.
  • Green edges. A planted buffer along the boundary, using 25+ native species.

Evolve by Concorde

Evolve is Concorde's signature clubhouse format. At Mayfair it spans about 7,900 sq ft on the 14th floor, with terrace amenities on the 15th level, roughly 150 ft above the ground. The setting gives the gym, sky bar and party deck open views over North Bangalore.

Every home includes Club Evolve membership and part ownership of the club, so there is no separate club joining fee to negotiate.

Concorde Mayfair FAQs

46 answers on price, floor plans, amenities, location, RERA and possession.

Project basics

What is Concorde Mayfair?

Concorde Mayfair is a mixed-use project by Concorde Group on Bellary Road, Yelahanka, North Bangalore. It has 217 apartments in 2 BHK and 3 BHK layouts across four towers on 3.17 acres, plus commercial and high-street retail space on the same site. It is under construction, with possession expected in November 2028.

Who is the developer of Concorde Mayfair?

Concorde Group, a Bengaluru developer founded in 1998. It has delivered about 18 million sq ft across 29 completed projects, including Concorde Napa Valley, Mist Valley, Manhattans and Amber. In North Bangalore it is also building Concorde Sienna on Hennur Main Road.

Where exactly is Concorde Mayfair located?

On Bellary Road (NH 44, the Airport Road) in Yelahanka, North Bangalore, beside the six-lane elevated airport corridor. The builder's location map places it between Yelahanka New Town and Jakkur, a few minutes from the upcoming Yelahanka metro station.

How big is Concorde Mayfair?

The site is 3.17 acres, with about 61% left as open space. Some older listings, including an earlier version of this page, quoted 3.30 acres. We now use 3.17 acres, the figure from the detailed project disclosures.

How many towers and floors does Concorde Mayfair have?

Four towers, marked A to D on the builder's key plan. Each has two basements, a ground floor and 14 upper floors (2B+G+14). The Evolve clubhouse sits on the 14th floor, with terrace amenities above it.

How many apartments are there in Concorde Mayfair?

217 homes in total, across the four towers. That is a fairly low count for a Bellary Road high-rise, which means smaller lift lobbies and a quieter community than the large townships nearby.

Is Concorde Mayfair a residential or commercial project?

Both. It is an integrated development: residential towers plus a commercial block with high-street retail. The master plan places the commercial building along the Bellary Road frontage, with the four residential towers and podium amenities behind it.

Configurations and floor plans

What configurations are available at Concorde Mayfair?

2 BHK and 3 BHK apartments. The 3 BHK comes in two versions: with two toilets (3 BHK 2T) and with three toilets (3 BHK 3T). The builder's current campaign leads with the 3 BHK homes, so ask about 2 BHK availability when you enquire.

What is the size of a 2 BHK at Concorde Mayfair?

2 BHK homes range from about 1,170 to 1,278 sq ft super built-up. The builder's sample 2 BHK 2T (Type 1) is 1,181 sq ft super built-up with 787 sq ft of carpet area and a 29.5 sq ft sit-out.

What are the 3 BHK sizes at Concorde Mayfair?

3 BHK homes range from about 1,452 to 1,667 sq ft super built-up. The 3 BHK 2T (Type 1) is 1,457 sq ft with 987 sq ft carpet. The larger 3 BHK 3T (Type 3) is 1,667 sq ft with 1,123 sq ft carpet.

What is the carpet area efficiency at Concorde Mayfair?

About 67% across the three sample plans (787 of 1,181 sq ft, 987 of 1,457 sq ft and 1,123 of 1,667 sq ft). That is better than many new North Bangalore high-rises, where carpet often runs at 60% to 65% of super built-up area.

What is the difference between the 3 BHK 2T and 3 BHK 3T?

The 3 BHK 3T is about 210 sq ft larger. It adds a third toilet, so every bedroom has its own bathroom, and gets a bigger living room (18'10" x 12'9" against 16'1" x 11'11"), a larger master bedroom and a deeper sit-out.

Do Concorde Mayfair apartments have balconies?

Each sample plan has one sit-out off the dining area, of about 30 to 39 sq ft (for example 7'1" x 3'11" in the 2 BHK). They are small, so if a large balcony matters to you, check the exact unit plan before booking.

What is the ceiling height at Concorde Mayfair?

The apartments have a 9.7 ft ceiling height, an 8 ft main entrance door and 7 ft tall windows with double-glazed glass for noise reduction and better insulation.

Are Concorde Mayfair homes Vastu compliant?

The developer describes the homes as Vastu compliant. The sample 3 BHK plans are east facing and the sample 2 BHK is west facing. Confirm the facing and entrance direction of your specific unit.

Can I see the Concorde Mayfair floor plans?

Yes. The 2 BHK, 3 BHK 2T and 3 BHK 3T plans are in the configurations section of this page. Request the full brochure for the plans of every unit type and floor.

The 1,457 sq ft 3 BHK 2T starts at about ₹1.99 Cr, the figure Concorde uses in its own advertising. The 1,667 sq ft 3 BHK 3T is quoted at about ₹2.28 Cr.

2 BHK prices are on request. Concorde's current campaign advertises only the 3 BHK homes, so 2 BHK stock may be limited. Call or WhatsApp us for live availability and the current cost sheet.

What is the price per sq ft at Concorde Mayfair?

About ₹13,650 to ₹13,700 per sq ft of super built-up area, based on ₹1.99 Cr for 1,457 sq ft and ₹2.28 Cr for 1,667 sq ft. On carpet area it works out to roughly ₹20,150 to ₹20,300 per sq ft.

What is the all-in cost of a Concorde Mayfair apartment?

Add 5% GST, Karnataka stamp duty of about 5.6% including surcharge and cess, a 1% registration fee, plus parking, maintenance deposit and other charges if billed separately. For a ₹1.99 Cr 3 BHK, budget roughly ₹2.25 Cr to ₹2.30 Cr before interiors.

Are there any offers or payment plans at Concorde Mayfair?

Concorde runs periodic launch and festive offers. Ask us for the current payment schedule and any offer in force, and get every commitment written into the booking form.

Can I get a home loan for Concorde Mayfair?

Yes. It is a RERA-registered project, so major banks generally lend on it. Ask which lenders have already approved the project, since that speeds up sanction and disbursal.
