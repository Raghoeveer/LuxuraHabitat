Project Overview

Welcome to Assetz Codename Paradise

Welcome to Assetz Codename Paradise, now also marketed by the developer as Assetz Mizu & Ki, a premium residential development by Assetz Property Group off the Hennur Road corridor in North Bangalore. Spanning 8.2 acres, the project dedicates an impressive 55% of its total area to open green spaces and Balinese-themed aesthetics, with sweeping gardens, tranquil water features and shaded pavilions.

Breaking from traditional apartment living, the project introduces the concept of ‘Villaments’: residences that combine the privacy, scale and exclusivity of an independent villa with the security and community amenities of a premium apartment complex. Each spacious 4 BHK villament spans approximately 3,060 sq ft of super built-up area, with private backyards up to 500 sq ft, large private terraces and dedicated 2-car private garages. With only 188 exclusive villaments across a B+G+4 structure, this is a genuinely low-density address.

Why Invest in Assetz Codename Paradise?

The Villament Advantage

With only 188 units across 8.2 acres and 55% open space, Assetz Codename Paradise trades tower density for villa-scale privacy: a private garden, backyard and 2-car garage with every 4 BHK home. For buyers comparing high-rise apartments against standalone villas, the villament format is a deliberate middle path, offering independent-home privacy with the security and shared amenities of a managed community.

Assetz Property Group, established in 2006, is one of Bangalore's more prominent multinational developers with over 10 million sq ft under development, a recognized name that typically means more predictable construction quality and after-sales support.

Why Hennur Road

Hennur Road is a well-connected North Bangalore corridor with direct access to Manyata Tech Park, Karle Town Centre and Kirloskar Business Park, and an alternative toll-free, signal-free route to Kempegowda International Airport via Hennur-Bagalur Road.

Property values along the corridor have tracked the expansion of the IT belt and airport-linked infrastructure, making early entry into a credible developer's pre-launch project a reasonable position for both end-users and long-term investors.
