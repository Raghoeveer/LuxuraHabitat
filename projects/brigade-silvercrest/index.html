Silvercrest in 60 Seconds

The numbers a buyer or investor needs first, before the brochure.

A Pre-Launch Brigade Address Priced from ₹3.09 Cr

Brigade Silvercrest is Brigade Group's pre-launch residential project at Doddakalsandra Village, Raghuvanahalli, fronting an 18 m road off Kanakapura Main Road in South Bengaluru. The indicative price sheet lists three homes: a 2,010 Sq.Ft 3 BHK at ₹3.09 Cr, a 2,158 Sq.Ft 3 BHK Premium at ₹3.23 Cr and a 2,685 Sq.Ft 4 BHK at ₹4.26 Cr. That works out to roughly ₹15,000 to ₹15,900 per Sq.Ft before taxes.

Channel-partner listings add a few more numbers: a ₹15,370 per Sq.Ft pre-launch rate that they say rises to ₹17,500 after launch, EOI tokens of ₹5 to ₹7 lakh, and a slab-by-slab payment schedule. They also describe the building differently from the developer's site plan. We show every version below, labelled by source, so you can see where the figures agree and where they don't.

This page is written for buyers who start with the spreadsheet. Below you'll find the full price list with per-Sq.Ft rates, an estimated all-in cost for each home, the payment schedule in rupees, an illustrative EMI, a plain-English reading of the site plan, and a due-diligence checklist for a project that is not yet RERA registered.

One Project, Two Names: Silvercrest and Jeevan Sandhya

You'll also see this development marketed as Brigade Jeevan Sandhya. Both names refer to the same Brigade Group project at Doddakalsandra Village, Raghuvanahalli, with the same site, the same 3 BHK, 3 BHK Premium and 4 BHK homes, and the same price list. If you arrived from a link to our old Jeevan Sandhya page, you're in the right place: we've merged the two into this one page.

Jeevan Sandhya translates roughly as "evening of life". Brigade uses that name for the project's inter-generational positioning: large, low-density homes meant for families who want parents, children and grandparents under one roof at every stage of life. Silvercrest is the name used on the developer's price sheet and site plan, which is why this page leads with it.

Older Jeevan Sandhya listings describe a single 20-floor tower of about 135 homes from ₹3 Cr with December 2030 possession. Those figures come from earlier marketing and have been superseded by the numbers on this page. Whichever name appears on a brochure or booking form, check that the RERA registration, once issued, names the same project, survey numbers and unit sizes before you pay.

The Upgrade Maths Moving from the 3 BHK to the 3 BHK Premium adds about 148 Sq.Ft for roughly ₹14 lakh, a marginal cost of about ₹9,500 per Sq.Ft, well below the project's average rate. Moving from the Premium to the 4 BHK adds about 527 Sq.Ft for roughly ₹1.03 Cr, a marginal cost of about ₹19,500 per Sq.Ft. In plain terms, the 3 BHK Premium is the value sweet spot, and the 4 BHK carries a scarcity premium for the largest homes.
₹15,370 Now, ₹17,500 Later? Channel-partner listings quote a pre-launch rate of ₹15,370 per Sq.Ft, valid until the September 2026 launch, and say it will rise to ₹17,500 per Sq.Ft once construction starts. ₹15,370 matches the 3 BHK price exactly. At ₹17,500, the same three homes would cost about ₹3.52 Cr, ₹3.78 Cr and ₹4.70 Cr, which is ₹43 lakh to ₹55 lakh more (10 to 17%). Treat the higher rate as a sales claim until the developer publishes a launch price sheet. The September launch date has passed and no RERA number has been issued yet.
A Fourth Variant on One Listing One page of the same listing shows four homes instead of three: a 2,010 Sq.Ft 3 BHK Comfort at ₹3.09 Cr, a 2,040 Sq.Ft 3 BHK Large at ₹3.23 Cr, a 2,158 Sq.Ft 3.5 BHK at ₹3.55 Cr and the 2,685 Sq.Ft 4 BHK at ₹4.26 Cr. On that sheet the 2,040 Sq.Ft home costs about ₹15,830 per Sq.Ft and the 2,158 Sq.Ft home about ₹16,450, so the 3 BHK Premium would no longer be the value pick described above. That page also calls the sizes carpet area, while every other page calls them super built-up, and a few pages mention 4 BHK homes of up to 3,000 Sq.Ft. Ask which sheet is current before you compare.
Ask Which Area the Price Uses Pre-launch price sheets usually quote super built-up area, which includes a share of lobbies, lifts and the clubhouse. The carpet area you actually live in can be 25 to 35% smaller, which pushes the effective rate per usable Sq.Ft well above the headline number. Ask for the RERA carpet area of each configuration before comparing Silvercrest with another project.

*EMI assumes a loan of 80% of the base price at 8.5% a year over 20 years, for illustration only. Actual rates, eligibility and loan-to-value depend on your lender and profile. The first 20% (about ₹62 L to ₹85 L) plus stamp duty and registration normally comes from your own funds.

With a construction-linked plan, the bank releases the loan in stages as the building rises, so during construction you usually pay only interest on the amount disbursed (pre-EMI), and full EMIs start after the final disbursement or possession. Reading the Site Plan

What the developer's current site plan actually shows, feature by feature.

One Low-Rise Building The plan labels a single building of 2 basements, ground and 5 upper floors (2B+G+5), with two connected wings. That's far lower than the 20 to 39 floor towers common on Kanakapura Road, which usually means fewer homes per lift and a quieter building, but also a smaller total number of homes to share running costs. Channel-partner listings describe something very different: one G+28 tower over 2 basements, holding about 133 to 135 homes at 5 per floor. The three versions can't all be right, and the RERA filing will settle it.
Road Frontage & Entry A single entry and exit opens onto an 18 m wide road on the southern edge, with a guard room and service area beside the gate and a drop-off court in front of the building.
Cars Kept Below Ground A driveway loops around the building, with an entry ramp to the basement at the north-west and an exit ramp at the south-east. A short strip of surface parking sits along the western driveway.
High-Tension Line on the East The plan marks an overhead high-tension (HT) power line and its buffer zone running just outside the eastern boundary. The building sits outside the marked buffer, but buyers should ask for the statutory clearance distances and consider how the line affects east-facing views.

How Far to Trust These

The Maintenance Question A pool, clubhouse and staffed security cost roughly the same to run whether 100 or 1,000 families share them. On a small, low-rise site, those fixed costs are divided among fewer homes, so monthly maintenance per Sq.Ft can be higher than in a large township. Listings estimate ₹3.50 to ₹5.00 per Sq.Ft a month, which on these sizes is roughly ₹7,000 to ₹10,000 for the 3 BHK, ₹7,550 to ₹10,800 for the 3 BHK Premium and ₹9,400 to ₹13,400 for the 4 BHK. Above ₹7,500 a month, 18% GST usually applies to the bill, and 1 to 2 years are normally collected in advance at possession. Ask for the estimated rate and the corpus deposit at booking, and add them to your cost sheet.

Investment Case: Drivers & Risks

A balanced view for buyers weighing Silvercrest as an investment rather than only a home.

What Supports Value

  • Walk-to-metro on an operational Green Line
  • NICE Road about 2.5 km away for signal-free access to Electronic City and Mysore Road
  • Harohalli Industrial Area further south adding long-term job demand
  • Listed Tier-1 developer, which typically supports resale pricing
  • Large homes on a small site, a format in short supply on the corridor

What Could Hold It Back

  • Premium entry rate of ~₹15,000+ per Sq.Ft vs ~₹14,500 at nearby Purva Hallmark
  • Long indicative possession, October 2031
  • Building and unit data still inconsistent across versions
  • Adjacent high-tension line may weigh on some units' resale
  • Higher maintenance per home on a small site

Exit Options

  • Resale after possession and registration
  • Pre-possession transfer, only if the developer's policy allows it (ask about fees)
  • Rental after possession, to metro-commuting tenants
  • Hold for end use by the family
Rent and Yield, Run the Numbers Listings estimate post-possession rents of ₹55,000 to ₹70,000 a month for the 3 BHK, ₹70,000 to ₹85,000 for the 3 BHK Premium and ₹90,000 to ₹1.2 lakh for the 4 BHK; the same site's price page uses lower figures starting at ₹45,000. Against today's base prices, that is a gross yield of about 2.1 to 3.4% a year, below the 3.5 to 4.5% the listings advertise, and before maintenance, property tax and vacancy. Listings also claim 8 to 12% yearly price growth on Kanakapura Road. Treat both as sales estimates: Silvercrest makes more sense as an end-use or long-hold capital-growth buy than as a rental income play.

This is general analysis, not financial advice. Pre-launch investments carry more risk than registered, under-construction projects.

The Developer, from an Investor's Angle

Why the name behind a pre-launch matters more than the brochure.

Brigade Enterprises Limited has been listed on the NSE and BSE since 2013 (ticker: BRIGADE). For a pre-launch buyer, that listing is the most useful fact about the developer: quarterly results, debt levels, sales bookings and the project pipeline are all publicly disclosed, so you can check the company's financial health yourself rather than relying on a sales pitch.

The operating record is long. Founded in Bengaluru in 1986 by M. R. Jaishankar, Brigade built the city's first private high-rise, Brigade Towers, in 1989, and has since completed over 300 buildings and more than 100 million Sq.Ft across South India, spanning apartments, villas, integrated enclaves, senior living, offices, retail, hotels and schools. That breadth includes senior-friendly housing, which is relevant to how this project is positioned.

Leadership is still family-led: founder M. R. Jaishankar is Executive Chairman, Pavitra Shankar is Managing Director and Nirupa Shankar is Joint Managing Director. Brigade was the first developer in South India to earn ISO 9001 certification, in 1996, and several of its projects carry IGBC Gold or Platinum green ratings.

None of this removes pre-launch risk. Plans at this stage have already changed between versions, as the tower count and floor plans show. But a listed developer with a long delivery record is the strongest mitigation available before RERA registration.
