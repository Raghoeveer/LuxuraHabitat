About TVS Emerald Altura

A premium North Bangalore high-rise community with expansive open spaces and modern amenities for contemporary living

Experience Premium Urban Living

TVS Emerald Altura is a carefully planned residential community in Sathanur, North Bangalore. Developed by TVS Emerald, the project is planned across 10.06 acres with 12 towers, 975 premium residences, and 2B+G+12 high-rise living.

The community brings together efficient 2 and 3 BHK layouts, 71% open space, two clubhouses, wellness amenities, work-friendly spaces, and connectivity to Thanisandra, Bagalur Main Road, Hebbal, Manyata Tech Park, and Kempegowda International Airport.

Status, Pros & Questions to Ask Before You Pay

An honest checklist built from the developer's pages, property portals and broker listings

What Works

  • TVS Group developer, with freehold land and a clear title stated in the developer's FAQ
  • Large 10.06-acre site with 71% open space and two clubhouses of about 29,000 sq ft
  • Mivan construction on a raft foundation, EDGE certification and EV charging for 30% of slots
  • About 30% corner homes and senior-friendly planning (ramps, wide corridors, universal toilets)
  • Schools, REVA University and a hospital within about 1.6 km
  • Back-loaded payment plan: 10% on booking, most of the balance in July 2028

Things to Weigh

  • Long wait: possession is reported as March 2033 for all phases
  • The developer publishes a price only for 3 BHK (₹2.11 Cr* basic); 2 BHK pricing and availability must be asked for
  • Extra 10 to 15% on the basic price for charges and taxes
  • The nearest metro stations are still upcoming
  • Peak-hour traffic toward ORR, Hennur Road, Manyata and Hebbal is heavy
  • 975 homes means a dense community, not a boutique one

Questions to Ask Before You Pay

  1. What is the RERA-registered completion date for my tower, and is delivery phase-wise?
  2. Is a 2 BHK still available, and at what all-inclusive price?
  3. What is the RERA carpet area of my exact unit? The plan sheets show 713 to 1,277 sq ft, so confirm the figure for your tower and stack.
  4. What are the floor-rise, facing and corner premiums per sq ft?
  5. What exactly is in the 10 to 15% extra: maintenance per sq ft, corpus fund, clubhouse fee, GST, registration?
  6. How many car parks come with my home, and what does an extra park cost?
  7. Which banks have approved the project for home loans?
  8. Which towers face the water body, the entrance or Bagalur Main Road?
  9. Is the EDGE rating Level 1 certified or Level 2 pre-certified, and can I see the certificate?
  10. Is the payment due on 15 July 2028 linked to a construction stage, or is it time-based?
