About TVS Emerald Altura

A premium North Bangalore high-rise community with expansive open spaces and modern amenities for contemporary living

Experience Premium Urban Living

TVS Emerald Altura is a carefully planned residential community in Sathanur, North Bangalore. Developed by TVS Emerald, the project is planned across 10.06 acres with 12 towers, 975 premium residences, and 2B+G+12 high-rise living.

The community brings together efficient 2 and 3 BHK layouts, more than 70% open spaces, clubhouse-led recreation, wellness amenities, work-friendly spaces, and connectivity to Thanisandra, Bagalur Main Road, Hebbal, Manyata Tech Park, and Kempegowda International Airport.
