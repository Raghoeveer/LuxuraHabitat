Project Overview: Multi-Generational Living in Brigade Orchards

Two accessibility-first residential towers, Laurel & Maple, inside Brigade Orchards, Devanahalli.

Senior-Living Homes for Every Generation, Devanahalli

Brigade Orchards Laurel & Maple are two new residential towers, arranged around a shared clubhouse, courtyard and jogging track, inside Brigade Enterprises Ltd.'s larger Brigade Orchards master-planned township in Devanahalli (100.17 acres; some sources say 135). The towers are marketed as a multi-generational living format, with day-to-day senior-living operations by Primus Senior Living, alongside regular 1, 2 & 3 BHK family-sized apartments.

Every home is designed with accessibility-first features: wider doorways, lever-type door handles, dual-height door viewers, gently sloped level flooring, sliding doors for select bathrooms, grab bars in showers and toilets, and an SOS call bell. Brigade's specification sheets show Laurel's call bell wired to the nursing station, while Maple lists an SOS provision plus one wheelchair-friendly bedroom. Every tower has wheelchair-accessible passenger lifts and service lifts sized for a stretcher or bed. Residents also get the wider Brigade Orchards township's Signature Club Resort (about 1 km), sports arena and planned retail, office and school spaces. The project is RERA registered as PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/040326/008505, and portals list possession for March 2030.
