Project Overview & Low-Density Design

A single G+16 tower with just 135 exclusive residences in the heart of Koyambedu, Chennai.

135 exclusive residences on 1.96 acres in Koyambedu

Shriram Kinglife Koyambedu, formally registered as Shriram Codename King Life, is a benchmark for contemporary urban luxury in the heart of Koyambedu, Chennai. Set on a 1.96-acre estate, this single G+16 high-rise tower hosts a deliberately limited collection of 135 elite 3 and 4 BHK residences, ranging from 1,465 to 2,835 sq.ft, balancing elegant design with structural superiority.

Homes are engineered for 71-75% carpet area efficiency, translating to genuinely spacious interiors relative to the quoted super built-up area. Select floor plans also incorporate dedicated maid/servant quarters for enhanced privacy and convenience. Prices start from ₹2.36 Cr* onwards, positioning the project as an exceptional, low-density proposition for discerning homeowners who want a central Chennai address without a high-density tower.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Shriram Kinglife Koyambedu.

Shriram Kinglife Koyambedu (formally Shriram Codename King Life) is a low-density luxury residential development by Shriram Properties, spread across 1.96 acres in Koyambedu, Chennai. It comprises a single G+16 tower with just 135 exclusive 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments.

The project is located in Koyambedu, Chennai, just off Poonamallee High Road, about 1 km from Koyambedu Metro Station and the Koyambedu Wholesale Market.

Pricing starts from ₹2.36 Cr* onwards for the 3 BHK + 2T configuration (1465-1640 sq.ft), scaling up to ₹3.92 Cr* onwards for the 4 BHK + 4T + Maid configuration (2835 sq.ft).

Yes, the project is registered with the Tamil Nadu Real Estate Regulatory Authority under TNRERA/29/BLG/0191/2026.

Possession is scheduled for January 2030, as per the RERA-registered timeline.

The project offers four configurations: 3 BHK + 2T (1465-1640 sq.ft), 3 BHK + 3T (1925 sq.ft), 3 BHK + 3T + Maid (2065-2110 sq.ft), and 4 BHK + 4T + Maid (2835 sq.ft), with 71-75% carpet area efficiency.

The project is built using Mivan (aluminium formwork) construction technology, which delivers monolithic RCC structures with strong structural uniformity, seismic resistance and a faster, more precise finish than conventional brick-and-mortar construction.

Shriram Kinglife Koyambedu features a 20,000 sq.ft clubhouse with an adults' and kids' pool, a state-of-the-art gym, a rooftop yoga deck, a mini-theatre, a pet-friendly zone and landscaped gardens.

The project is developed by Shriram Properties, one of South India's leading listed residential developers with a long-standing track record across Bangalore, Chennai and other major cities.
