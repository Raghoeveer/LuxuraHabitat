Project Overview & Master Plan

A DTCP-approved plotted layout designed for buyers who want to build their own villa on their own timeline.

A plotted development, built for you to design later

Shriram Codename 10X is a DTCP-approved residential plotted layout by Shriram Properties Limited at Valarpuram, near Thirumazhisai, in Chennai West, just off the Chennai-Bengaluru Highway (NH4). Spread across approximately 9.73 acres, the layout offers 166 residential plots with roughly 72% of the land set aside for open, landscaped and amenity space.

Unlike an apartment purchase, buying here means registering a DTCP-approved plot of your chosen size and building your own villa on it, on your own schedule. There are no fixed floor plans, unit layouts or a builder-driven possession date to track. The master plan marks certain zones as "Future Development," a detail we're stating plainly rather than glossing over, since it's a genuine part of the layout's current phase. The Investment Advantage

Why plots on this corridor appeal to both self-builders and land investors.

Astride an Active Industrial Corridor Shriram Codename 10X sits roughly a 5-minute drive from the 8-lane Chennai-Bengaluru Highway (NH4) and the proposed Chennai-Bengaluru Expressway, placing it directly on one of Tamil Nadu's busiest industrial and logistics arteries. The Sriperumbudur Industrial Hub, home to over 100 manufacturing MNCs including Hyundai and Saint-Gobain, sits close by, as do the Oragadam SEZ and SIPCOT Tech Parks, anchored by Renault Nissan, Daimler and Royal Enfield. This concentration of large-scale industrial employers is exactly the kind of demand driver that tends to support land and plot values over time: a growing, stable workforce needs housing, and plotted land near an expanding industrial base is a genuinely different investment thesis from a rental-yield apartment play in a purely residential micro-market.

Unlike an apartment, a DTCP-approved plot lets you time your own construction, hold the land as-is while it appreciates, or build a villa exactly to your own specification once you're ready, flexibility that a fixed-floor-plan apartment purchase simply doesn't offer.
Why Plots Near Industrial Corridors Tend to Hold Value Land banking near active industrial and SEZ corridors has historically been one of the more resilient real estate strategies in Tamil Nadu, precisely because the demand driver (employment) is durable and doesn't depend on speculative residential launches alone. As Sriperumbudur and Oragadam continue to expand their manufacturing base, the surrounding residential catchment, including layouts like Codename 10X, stands to benefit from that steady, employment-linked housing demand rather than a purely cyclical real estate market.

A DTCP-approved, gated layout with wide roads, underground utilities and dedicated amenity zones also de-risks the purchase relative to buying raw agricultural land, since the statutory conversion and infrastructure work is already in place before you build.
Build Your Own Villa, On Your Own Terms For end-users, the appeal of a plotted development like Shriram Codename 10X is the ability to design and build a villa exactly to your family's needs, size, layout and finish, rather than accepting a fixed apartment floor plan. Combined with 72% open and landscaped space across the layout, wide asphalted internal roads, and a full amenity set spanning sports, recreation and community zones, this is positioned for buyers who want a gated, well-planned community setting with the freedom to build later.
