Project Overview: A Godrej Plotted Township at the Southern End of OMR

950+ premium plots on 47 acres beside Wonderla Chennai, Thiruporur, by Godrej Properties.

Own the Land, Design the Home

Godrej Wonderla, also listed as Godrej Plots Thiruporur, is a large-scale premium plotted development by Godrej Properties on about 47 acres right next to the Wonderla Chennai amusement park. Instead of buying a fixed apartment, you buy a serviced plot of 750 to 2,400 sq ft inside a gated, professionally planned layout, and then build the home you want: a compact cottage, a duplex villa or a full-ground bungalow with a garden. The developer calls them plug and play lifestyle plots, meaning roads, utilities and amenities are planned to be in place when you start building.

The scale is what sets it apart from most plotted layouts on OMR. More than 950 plots are arranged around a central 15,000+ sq ft Victorian-themed clubhouse, the Grand Club on the master plan, with a swimming pool, multipurpose hall, tennis courts, pet parks, a yoga lawn, a kids' play area and jogging trails threaded through landscaped greens. In all, the developer lists 62+ lifestyle amenities, and the site overlooks forest and open green land on its edges.

Location is the other half of the story. Thiruporur sits at the southern end of Chennai's OMR IT corridor, with the OMR junction about 10 minutes away, SIPCOT Siruseri about 25 minutes and easy access to the East Coast Road. Wonderla Chennai, a ₹611 crore theme park, opened next door in December 2025. On 30 June 2026, Godrej Properties announced the purchase of a 47-acre land parcel off OMR for a primarily plotted project with about ₹500 crore of revenue potential. The project is at pre-launch: an EOI window is expected to open soon, the launch is planned for December 2026, and the TNRERA registration number is yet to be issued.

The Investment Case, and the Risks

Why buyers are looking at Godrej plots at the southern end of OMR, and what to weigh before paying an EOI.

OMR's Growth Is Moving South Chennai's IT corridor has steadily pushed south from Perungudi and Sholinganallur to Navalur, Siruseri and Kelambakkam, and land prices have followed. Thiruporur is the next stretch along the same road, still largely open land, now anchored by Wonderla Chennai and a Godrej-scale gated township. Metro Phase 2 is targeted to reach Siruseri by 2027, which brings rail within a short drive. Our OMR villa investment guide covers how rents and resale values have moved along the corridor.
Branded Land Holds Value Differently A plot has no building to depreciate, and a plot inside a large, well-maintained Godrej community with a clubhouse is easier to finance, value and resell than one in an unbranded roadside layout. That brand premium is real, but you pay for it upfront: entry prices of roughly ₹4,900 to ₹5,100 per sq ft are well above many unbranded layouts listed in Thiruporur. Compare the two on total cost, including infrastructure and amenities, not just the headline rate.
Pre-Launch Means Pre-RERA The project is not yet registered with TNRERA, and the possession date, final layout and price list are still to come. Pay only a refundable EOI to a Godrej Properties account until the registration is issued, and read the RERA-approved layout before paying a booking amount. Tamil Nadu has also tightened rules on real estate advertising; see TNRERA's new ad penalty rules for what a compliant project listing must show.
