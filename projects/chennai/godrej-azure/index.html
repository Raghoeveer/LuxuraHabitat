Project Overview & Master Plan Details

An 8.8-acre sea-view residential development by Godrej Properties in Padur, on Chennai's IT Corridor.

Sea-View Living on Chennai's IT Corridor, Padur OMR

Godrej Azure is a new-launch residential development by Godrej Properties, planned across 8.8 acres with 60%+ of the land kept as open space, in Padur, on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR), Chennai's established IT Corridor. Every residence is designed with a private balcony, giving residents panoramic views of the sea and surrounding greenery, a rare combination this close to Chennai's IT employment belt.

The project sits close to the SIPCOT IT Park and Siruseri IT Park, putting a large concentration of Chennai's IT and ITES employers within a short commute. Godrej Azure is registered under TNRERA (TNRERA/35/BLG/0354/2025) with possession expected in May 2030. As with any new-launch project, treat published imagery and timelines as indicative until the developer's own construction-linked disclosures catch up, and verify current status directly before booking.
