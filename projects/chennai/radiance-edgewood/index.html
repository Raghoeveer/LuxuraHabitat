The Investment Advantage

Why Radiance Edgewood is a compelling choice for home buyers and investors on the OMR corridor.

Why Invest in the OMR-Siruseri Corridor Navalur sits within Chennai's established OMR (Old Mahabalipuram Road) IT corridor, one of the city's largest concentrations of technology campuses and business parks. Radiance Edgewood is barely 5 minutes from Siruseri SIPCOT IT Park and the TCS Siruseri Campus, placing residents close to a large, stable base of employment without the density and traffic pressure of the older OMR stretches closer to the city.

The corridor's continued expansion, ongoing road-widening on Vandalur-Kelambakkam Road, and steady growth of IT/ITES employment around Siruseri and Navalur keep housing demand consistent in this belt. A gated villa community with only 83 units on 5.5 acres, next to a reserved forest, gives buyers a lower-density, greener alternative to the high-rise apartment supply that dominates most of OMR.
Villas vs Apartments: The OMR Resale Case Row-villa supply on OMR is far more limited than apartment supply. Most new launches in this corridor are high-rise apartment towers, so that scarcity is itself a structural advantage for a project like Radiance Edgewood: buyers who specifically want a private villa with no shared walls, an independent terrace, and a small, low-density community of 83 homes have very few comparable options in Navalur and Siruseri.

Villas also typically carry a meaningfully higher land-to-built-up-area ratio than apartments, which tends to support land-value-driven appreciation over the long term, in addition to any construction-quality premium. Buyers should independently verify current resale trends for OMR villa communities before treating this as a guarantee, and should not rely on this page alone for investment decisions.
Rental Yield Potential Near Siruseri IT Park The steady presence of IT and ITES professionals working at Siruseri SIPCOT IT Park and TCS Siruseri Campus supports consistent rental demand for well-located homes in Navalur. Senior professionals and relocating families in particular tend to prefer villas over apartments when budgets allow, valuing the privacy, independent parking and larger built-up area that Radiance Edgewood's 3 and 4 BHK configurations offer.

With amenities spanning a clubhouse, swimming pool, sports court and landscaped gardens, the project is positioned to appeal to both end-users and investors seeking a rental-ready home in a corridor with an established, IT-driven tenant base.
