Project Overview & Master Plan Details

An 8.9-acre, 7-tower pre-launch development by Shriram Properties in Kuthambakkam, West Chennai.

An 8.9-Acre "15-Minute City" in Kuthambakkam, West Chennai

In short: Shriram Codename Pudhiya Chennai is a TNRERA-registered (TN/02/Building/0319/2023) apartment project by Shriram Properties in Kuthambakkam, near Thirumazhisai. It has 1,053 homes in 7 towers on 8.9 acres, 1 to 3 BHK from ₹38 Lakhs*, and possession targeted for July 2028. Published figures vary by source, and we show them side by side below.

Shriram Codename Pudhiya Chennai (also marketed as Shriram Swargam and Codename: Puthiya Chennai) is a pre-launch high-rise development by Shriram Properties Limited, planned across 8.9 acres with 75% of the land kept as open and green space, in Kuthambakkam, near Thirumazhisai, on the Padur side of West Chennai. The development comprises 1,053 apartments across 7 independent high-rise towers, designed around a "15-minute city" concept intended to keep daily errands, recreation and commuting within a short walk or ride of home, close to the 8-lane Chennai-Bangalore Expressway (NH4).

This is a pre-launch project, registered under TNRERA (TN/02/Building/0319/2023, registered 27 July 2023) with possession targeted for July 2028 on a phased handover basis, in line with the RERA-registered deadline. As with any pre-launch purchase, treat published imagery, layouts and this timeline as indicative until the developer's own construction-linked disclosures catch up, and verify current status directly before booking. Beyond the residential towers, the project integrates an approximately 18,000 sq.ft on-site commercial zone with everyday retail, an ATM vestibule, a pharmacy, food outlets and a creche, alongside a 12,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse with 45+ facilities. Explore the master plan, unit plans and gallery below, and enquire for the current price list.

On its own project page, Shriram Properties promises 1, 2 and 3 BHK homes with zero space wastage, a sleek modern facade, premium finishes, excellent ventilation and Vastu compliance, and lists an environmental clearance dated 15 December 2022. Broker listings describe each tower as basement + stilt + 13 floors.

The Investment Case, Pros & Cons

Why Kuthambakkam and the wider Padur, West Chennai corridor matters for buyers and investors.

A Fast-Transforming Western Corridor Padur and Kuthambakkam sit on the western edge of Chennai, in a corridor that market watchers frequently compare to OMR's own early growth phase: relatively affordable entry pricing today, ahead of infrastructure that is still being built out. The proposed Parandur Greenfield International Airport, 42.3 km away by road per the developer, and the established Sriperumbudur-Oragadam industrial belt are the two biggest catalysts, alongside the upcoming TIDEL Park Pattabiram IT hub. None of these are fully operational yet, so treat them as genuine medium-to-long-term upside rather than a reason to expect an immediate price jump.
Jobs Within Reach The project microsite cites the Porur IT cluster (DLF, L&T, Commerce Zone and RMZ campuses) at over 1,00,000 employees, Sriperumbudur's Fortune 500 manufacturers at over 2,00,000 and Oragadam Industrial Park at over 1,50,000, with Chennai's third Tidel Park about 30 minutes away. SIPCOT Irungattukottai (7.1 km) and Sriperumbudur SIPCOT (8 km) are the closest large employers. These are the developer's figures, so treat them as a rough guide to the rental and resale catchment.
Growth Drivers Ahead The proposed Thirumazhisai Metro station, the 8-lane Chennai-Bangalore Expressway (NH4) already running through the area, and the longer-term Parandur airport project are all cited as growth drivers for this stretch of West Chennai. Several of these are planned rather than operational today, so this is a medium-to-long-term thesis, not a reason to expect immediate returns, but it is a meaningful part of the long-term appreciation case for a pre-launch project at this price point.
