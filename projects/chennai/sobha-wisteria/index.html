About Sobha Wisteria, Egattur

A pre-launch, 8.5-acre residential community by Sobha Limited on Chennai's OMR IT corridor.

Large-Format 3 & 4 BHK Homes on OMR

Sobha Wisteria, also advertised as Sobha OMR, is a pre-launch residential project by Sobha Limited in Egattur, on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR). Launch listings describe about 650 apartments across roughly 8.5 acres, offered only as 3 BHK and 4 BHK homes from about 1,750 to 2,700 sq ft. Prices start at about ₹1.69 Cr* for a 3 BHK, and possession is expected by December 2029.

Egattur sits between Navalur and Kelambakkam, on the stretch of OMR next to SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri, where TCS, Cognizant and several other large employers have campuses. The Marina Mall, Hiranandani's Egattur township, schools and hospitals are all a short drive away, and Corridor 3 of Chennai Metro Phase 2 is planned to end at SIPCOT. Listings variously place the site in Egattur, Navalur or Siruseri; all three are adjacent stretches of OMR. For families who want a bigger home from a branded developer close to OMR's workplaces, it is a strong fit.

Because this is a pre-launch project, the TNRERA number, master plan and official price sheet are not public yet. Everything below is taken from launch listings and should be confirmed against the developer's documents before booking.

Sobha Wisteria: Pros, Cons & Who It Suits

What makes this launch worth a look, what counts against it, and what to check before you pay.

Pros
  • Large-format 3 and 4 BHK homes from a branded developer, scarce on this 2 BHK-heavy stretch of OMR (per launch listings)
  • Next to SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri, with the Purple Line metro planned to end at SIPCOT
  • Sobha's in-house, backward-integrated construction, which tends to support resale value
  • The Marina Mall, schools and hospitals within a few kilometres
Cons
  • No TNRERA registration yet, and the announced March 2026 launch date has passed
  • Sources disagree on land, unit count, towers, BHK mix and possession (2029 or 2030)
  • Road-only commute for now: Taramani MRTS is about 19 km and the nearest operating metro about 20 km away
  • Price scepticism: a Reddit thread calls the quoted rate (about ₹15,000 per sq ft) too high for a 2030 handover (forum opinion)
  • Over 30 km from central Chennai, with heavy OMR traffic at peak hours
Who It Suits IT professionals working at SIPCOT, Navalur or Sholinganallur who want a 3 or 4 BHK from a branded developer, and families who can wait until 2029 or 2030 for handover.
Who Should Look Elsewhere Buyers who want a smaller or ready-to-move home, who rely on rail for the daily commute, or whose budget is closer to the OMR average of about ₹7,400 per sq ft.
Compare Before You Decide Nearby benchmarks include House of Hiranandani on OMR and DRA Marina 100, slated for delivery around September 2028. Visiting two or three of these makes it easier to judge whether Sobha's premium is justified.
Questions to Ask Before You Pay
  • The TNRERA registration number, and whether it shows about 650 homes (3 & 4 BHK) or 906 homes (1 to 4 BHK)
  • The carpet area and carpet-area rate for each layout, not just the super built-up size
  • Floor-rise, preferential location, car parking, corpus and maintenance charges on a written cost sheet
  • Which towers, if any, have a sea view: some brokers claim every home does, while the nearest named beaches are 12 to 14 km away
  • Environmental clearance, and any coastal (CRZ) restrictions that apply to the plot
  • Drainage and the monsoon flood history of the plot and its approach road

Sobha Limited was founded in 1995 by P.N.C. Menon, who had earlier built an interiors business in Oman, and is headquartered in Bengaluru. The company listed on Indian stock exchanges in 2006 and builds apartments, villas, row houses, plotted developments and commercial projects across several Indian cities, including Chennai, where it launched its first projects in 2012.

Sobha is best known for its backward-integrated model: it makes its own concrete, runs its own interiors, glazing and finishing units, and builds with in-house teams rather than outsourcing. That control over quality is the main reason Sobha homes are often valued above comparable projects on resale. A marketing-partner site credits Sobha with over 500 completed projects across 27 cities in 14 states, about 7,621 employees (March 2026) and 1,456 quality checks on each home before handover.

Sobha Wisteria brings that approach to Egattur on OMR. As with any pre-launch project, verify the TNRERA registration, approved plans and possession timeline directly before booking.
