Project Overview & Master Plan Details

A premium high-rise township by Shriram Properties on Chennai's West Porur corridor in Mangadu.

A 4.98-acre high-rise community in Mangadu, West Chennai

Shriram 122 West is a premium high-rise apartment township by Shriram Properties Limited, developed across 4.98 acres with 75% of the land kept as open and green space, in Mangadu on Chennai's West Porur corridor. The project offers 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK homes across towers built to 2 Basement + Ground + 19 Floors, positioned within easy reach of Porur, the DLF IT Park corridor and Poonamallee High Road.

The development is registered under TNRERA (TN/29/BUILDING/0459/2023) and follows a phased handover schedule, with early units targeted for 2027 and full project completion targeted for June 2028. Beyond the residential towers, the project includes an approximately 18,000 sq.ft on-site commercial zone with everyday retail, an ATM vestibule, a pharmacy, food outlets and a creche, alongside a 20,000 sq.ft clubhouse and an extensive amenity list. The Investment Advantage

Why Mangadu's position on the Porur corridor matters for buyers and investors.

Why Invest in Mangadu, West Chennai Mangadu sits just off the Porur corridor, roughly 10-15 minutes from Porur and the DLF IT Park cluster, putting an established West Chennai employment and IT hub within a genuinely short commute while still offering an entry price meaningfully below what similar-sized homes cost inside Porur itself. The Outer Ring Road and Poonamallee High Road both run through the same corridor, adding further connectivity options beyond a single arterial road.
A Trusted, Listed Developer Shriram Properties Limited is one of South India's leading residential developers, with a long track record in the mid-market and affordable housing segments across Bangalore, Chennai and other major cities. A TNRERA-registered, phased handover schedule and a recognisable, listed developer name reduce a common risk buyers face when evaluating a corridor that is still developing.
Growth Drivers Ahead Upcoming Metro connectivity into the corridor and the proximity of the planned Parandur Airport are both widely cited growth drivers for West Chennai. Neither is operational yet, so treat them as medium-term upside rather than a reason to expect immediate returns, but they are a meaningful part of the long-term rental yield and appreciation case for this corridor.
