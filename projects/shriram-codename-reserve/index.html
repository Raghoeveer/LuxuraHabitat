Project Overview

Villa-Style Privacy, Community Convenience

Shriram Codename Reserve is an exclusive pre-launch villament development by Shriram Properties, spread across 5 acres on the Bannerghatta Road corridor in South Bengaluru. The project brings together the privacy and spaciousness of a standalone villa, private entrances, gardens and duplex layouts, with the convenience and security of a thoughtfully planned, gated community, a format the developer calls a villament: villa plus apartment.

The collection offers 3 BHK and 4 BHK duplex villaments, with a Simplex option also available on request. Bannerghatta Road is one of South Bengaluru's rapidly developing corridors, with convenient access to IT hubs, schools, hospitals, retail and key road networks, positioning this project as a low-density alternative to the high-rise apartment towers more common across the city.

The Investment Advantage

Why an exclusive, low-density villament on Bannerghatta Road matters for early registrants.

Why Bannerghatta Road, South Bengaluru Bannerghatta Road is one of South Bengaluru's rapidly developing corridors, with growing retail, healthcare and connectivity infrastructure feeding demand from the wider South Bengaluru employment and residential belt. A 5-acre, low-density villament collection on this stretch is a genuinely different product from the high-rise apartment towers that dominate most nearby corridors, which can support both a distinctive end-user appeal and long-term resale differentiation.
Villa-Style Privacy at Community Scale A duplex villament format, private entrance, private deck or garden and a two-level layout, offers a meaningfully different everyday living experience than a stacked apartment, while the shared clubhouse, pool and gated perimeter still provide the security and amenity access of a planned community. That combination is relatively rare in this size band and can be a strong differentiator for end-users who have outgrown an apartment but aren't ready for the maintenance overhead of a stand-alone villa plot.
Pre-Launch Entry Point (With a Caveat) Registering interest ahead of a formal launch can offer early access to pricing and unit selection before wider release.
