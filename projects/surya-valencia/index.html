Surya Valencia Overview

Luxury inspired by Spain, designed for a life meant to be well lived.

Own a piece of Spain in North Bengaluru

Surya Valencia is an exclusive villa community by Surya Developers near Rajankunte, Yelahanka, designed around timeless Spanish architecture — arched facades, terracotta-toned exteriors and courtyard-style layouts — spread across a master-planned community of 122 villas developed in two RERA-registered phases.

Every villa comes with a signature triple-height sky ceiling above the living room and a private backyard of 300–700 sq ft. The 5 BHK Regal Edition takes it further with its own private swimming pool, a pre-fit lift, home automation, a sky pergola and a twin terrace — a genuinely rare combination in this price band on the North Bengaluru growth corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Surya Valencia, Rajankunte.

Surya Valencia is a Spanish-themed villa community near Rajankunte, Yelahanka, North Bengaluru, offering 4 BHK Estate Villas and 5 BHK Regal Edition Villas across a layout of 122 homes, with a 40,000 sq ft clubhouse managed by Illesium Clubs.

The project is located near Rajankunte, Yelahanka, in North Bengaluru, close to Taj Urban Resort and Angsana Oasis, with easy access to the airport, STRR and PRR.

Two configurations: 4 BHK Estate Villas at 3,345 sq ft starting from ₹3.54 Cr, and 5 BHK Regal Edition Villas at 3,893 sq ft starting from ₹4.59 Cr.

The 5 BHK Regal Edition adds a private swimming pool, a pre-fit lift, home automation, a sky pergola and a twin terrace on top of the shared features both villa types get, like the triple-height sky ceiling and private backyard.

Yes. Surya Valencia is developed in two phases, each separately RERA registered. Phase I covers 44 villas across 3.64 acres under RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/171224/007305. Phase II covers 78 villas across 5.32 acres under RERA number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/020125/007343. Always verify current status directly on the Karnataka RERA portal before making a purchase decision.

The 40,000 sq ft clubhouse is managed by Illesium Clubs and anchors a master-planned layout that includes an azure pool, party lawn with amphitheatre, yoga and meditation corner, outdoor fitness area, putting green, reflexology walk, and multiple landscaped gardens and trellised walkways.

Private backyards range from approximately 300 to 700 sq ft depending on the villa's plot position within the layout.

Every villa is built with dedicated space for a lift across its floors; the 5 BHK Regal Edition comes with the lift pre-fit, while other villas can have one installed in the reserved space.

Both the 4 BHK and 5 BHK villas are laid out across a ground floor, first floor, second floor and a terrace/third floor, with bedrooms, family lounges and balconies distributed across the upper levels.

Rajankunte sits on Bengaluru's fast-growing northern corridor near the airport, STRR and PRR, and Spanish-themed private-pool villas are a relatively rare product in this belt, which supports both lifestyle appeal and long-term demand.

Surya Valencia is developed by Surya Developers, a real estate firm based in Yelahanka, Bengaluru.

Possession is scheduled by phase: Phase I (44 villas across 3.64 acres) is scheduled for December 2028, and Phase II (78 villas across 5.32 acres) is scheduled for December 2030.
