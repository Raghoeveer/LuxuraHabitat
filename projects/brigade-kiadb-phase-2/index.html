Project Overview: Brigade KIADB Phase 2, Devanahalli

A regulator-verified 75-acre township inside North Bangalore's aerospace and defence corridor.

Bengaluru's Next Landmark Chapter, Since Brigade Gateway in 2008

Most pre-launch profiles are built from marketing decks. Brigade KIADB Phase 2's profile is unusually grounded in a filed Environmental Clearance record, which already confirms a 75.04-acre footprint, roughly 20.5 million sq ft of built-up area at FAR 3.25, and 7,195 planned dwelling units, alongside a 300-bed hospital and a 500-key hotel. Brigade's own pre-launch positioning frames this as a landmark chapter in the same lineage as Brigade Gateway, its flagship 2008 township in Rajajinagar.

The site sits inside Devanahalli KIADB Industrial Area Phase II, spanning Haraluru and Muddenahalli villages, directly adjacent to global aerospace and technology investment: Boeing, Airbus, Shell Technology Centre, Tata Advanced Systems and Brahmos Aerospace all sit inside the same KIADB corridor. Brigade's own master-plan collateral describes a self-contained "live-work-thrive" township built around five pillars — residential towers, a second World Trade Center office campus, a school and hospital, a retail mall, and a 5-star hotel with convention facilities. As with any pre-launch township, RERA registration, final unit sizes and official pricing are still awaited; enquire below for the current status before booking.

Honest Risks & Trade-Offs

Scale and ambition come with real trade-offs. None of these are hidden; they are the price of the ecosystem on offer.

High Density, High-Rise Living

At roughly 96 units per acre across towers rising up to 30-35 floors, this is a genuinely high-population environment, with the lift queues, parking pressure and construction phasing that scale implies. Buyers who prioritise low density or a boutique community should weigh this honestly before booking.

Still Pre-RERA

Even with Environmental Clearance granted, this is pre-RERA: price, configuration and possession are not yet legally enforceable commitments. Under Section 3 of the RERA Act, no booking or sale is legal before registration.

Multi-Year, Multi-Phase Delivery

A township of 20.5 million sq ft delivers over many years and phases. Early residents should expect to live alongside active construction elsewhere in the township for a long time, even after their own phase is complete.

Figures Vary Across Sources

Some numbers differ even within Brigade's own pre-launch materials — e.g. a 300-bed hospital in the EC filing versus 200-bed on one internal deck slide, and 7,195 units (EC) versus ~7,200 (master-plan sector legend) versus 1,614+ for one residential cluster specifically. Treat all non-EC figures as indicative until the RERA filing reconciles them.
