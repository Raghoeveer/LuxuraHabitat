Project Overview

A low-density, high-rise pre-launch by one of Bengaluru's oldest listed developers.

Low-Density Towers, Metro-Adjacent Living

Purva Hallmark (also referred to in the market as Purva Diamond Vajarahalli) is a pre-launch high-rise residential project by Puravankara Limited at Vajarahalli, off Kanakapura Main Road, South Bengaluru. Planned as towers with 2 basements, ground and 29 floors, the project will house approximately 250 residences on about 3.6 acres — a genuinely low-density plan for a high-rise of this scale.

The standout feature is metro proximity: the site sits roughly 280 metres from Thalaghattapura Metro Station on the Green Line, with direct access to Kanakapura Main Road (NH 948) and the NICE Road Junction just 1.8km away. As this is a pre-launch listing, floor plans, tower elevations and the final RERA-registered project name are still being finalized by the developer — enquire for the latest updates.

Puravankara Limited was founded in 1975 by Ravi Puravankara and is headquartered in Bengaluru. The company listed on the NSE and BSE in 2007 (ticker: PURVA) and operates through three brands — Puravankara (luxury), Provident Housing (mid-segment) and Purva Land (plotted developments).

The group has expanded across Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi, Mumbai, Coimbatore, Pune, Mangaluru and Goa, with an international presence in Sri Lanka, Dubai and the UAE. In FY2024, Puravankara reported record annual sales bookings of ₹5,914 Crore, a 90% year-on-year increase.

Purva Hallmark brings that scale to Vajarahalli, off Kanakapura Main Road — a low-density, metro-adjacent tower pre-launch in one of South Bengaluru's fastest-improving corridors.
