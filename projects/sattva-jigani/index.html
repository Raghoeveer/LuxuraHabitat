About Sattva Jigani (Sattva Amritya)

A compact, high-rise Sattva community in the Jigani industrial and tech belt of South Bangalore.

Tall towers in a low-rise market

Sattva Jigani, also called Sattva Codename Jigani and branded Sattva Amritya on the brochure unit plans, is a pre-launch apartment project by Sattva Group on Jigani Main Road, directly behind the HCL Technologies campus. It plans about 432 homes in three towers (A, B and C) of basement, ground and 31 floors, roughly 100 metres tall, on about 6.37 acres.

Jigani has historically been a market of low-rise buildings, plotted layouts and independent houses serving the Jigani and Bommasandra industrial areas. Sattva Jigani is one of the first branded 31-floor projects here, so upper floors get open horizon views towards Bannerghatta National Park and Hennagara Lake rather than into the next building.

Homes come in four formats: a compact 1 BHK, a 1,377 sq ft 2 BHK, a 1,524 sq ft 2.5 BHK with a study, and a 1,797 sq ft 3 BHK with three toilets. The developer describes the layouts as Vastu-compliant with no common walls and multi-side ventilation, and select lower-floor homes come with large private terraces. Around 25% of the land is planned as green cover, with podium gardens and walking trails around a multi-level clubhouse of about 12,000 sq ft.

The Investment Case & Trade-offs

What supports Sattva Jigani as a home or an investment, and what to weigh before you pay an EOI.

Why the location works Jigani sits on a large job base: the Jigani and Bommasandra industrial areas, HCL's campus next door and Electronic City's tech parks a short drive away. Most housing stock here is older low-rise or plotted, so a branded, amenity-rich high-rise is genuinely scarce supply in this micro-market.
Infrastructure tailwinds The Yellow Line metro to Bommasandra opened in August 2025, and the master plan marks a proposed 60 m wide road along the site's western edge. Better metro access and road upgrades are what typically re-rate an industrial suburb into a residential one.
Trade-off 1: a premium price for Jigani Locality guides put Jigani's average apartment rate at about ₹5,650 per sq ft (range ₹4,400 to ₹6,750). At about ₹10,800, Sattva Jigani asks nearly double. You are paying for brand, height, amenities and newness, so resale comparisons in the area will not support the price for some years.
Trade-off 2: pre-launch risk There is no RERA number yet, possession is four to five years away, and prices, unit mix and even the project name can change at launch. Keep any EOI refundable, and commit only after the RERA filing confirms approvals, completion date and the final cost sheet.

Location and investment

The developer puts the Bommasandra end of the Namma Metro Yellow Line about 7.4 to 8 km away, roughly 15 to 20 minutes by road. The Yellow Line runs from RV Road through Electronic City to Bommasandra and opened fully in August 2025.

Jigani Industrial Estate is about 2 km (5 minutes) away and Bommasandra Industrial Area about 7.4 to 8 km (15 to 20 minutes). Electronic City's tech campuses, including Infosys, HCL and Biocon, are reached via Jigani-Bommasandra Road and Hosur Road, and the area also links to NICE Road and Bannerghatta Road. Jigani and Koppa Gate railway stations are close by.

It depends on your horizon. In its favour: a branded 31-floor project in a market of mostly low-rise buildings, a large industrial and tech workforce nearby, and metro access at Bommasandra. The caution: at about ₹10,800 per sq ft it is priced well above Jigani's average apartment rate of roughly ₹5,650 per sq ft, so near-term rental yields will be modest and appreciation depends on the locality re-rating. It suits long-term end users more than short-term investors.
