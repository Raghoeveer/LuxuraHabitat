About Orchid Salisbury

A premium high-rise residential community designed for contemporary living and strategic urban connectivity

Experience Premium Urban Living

Orchid Salisbury is a meticulously planned premium residential community spanning 6.07 to 6.3 acres in Thanisandra, Bangalore. Developed by the renowned Goyal and Co, this project features 7 independent high-rise towers built on a 2B+G+13/14 floor structural layout, offering 660-663 premium residences.

Every aspect of the development reflects a commitment to quality, sustainability, and lifestyle excellence. With RERA registration ID PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/271123/006440, your investment is fully compliant and secured.
