About Orchid Salisbury

A 660-home, 7-tower gated community on Thanisandra Main Road by Goyal & Co. | Hariyana Group

High-Rise Living on Thanisandra Main Road

Orchid Salisbury is a high-rise gated community by Goyal & Co. | Hariyana Group on Thanisandra Main Road, near Yelahanka in North Bangalore. About 660 apartments sit in 7 towers (A to G) on roughly 6 acres. Towers A to E rise to 14 floors and towers F and G to 13 floors, above basement parking.

Homes range from a 930 sq ft 2 BHK Compact (one bedroom plus a study) to a 1,675 sq ft 3 BHK with three toilets and a dressing area. Current asking prices work out to about ₹12,000 per sq ft, up from about ₹9,000 at launch, so prices start at about ₹1.13 Cr. The project launched in March 2024 under RERA ID PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/271123/006440, and the RERA possession date is December 2028.

The site plan puts a clubhouse at the entry with 8 indoor amenities, plus 19 outdoor amenities: a swimming pool, a cricket pitch, a pickleball court, a pet park, an amphitheatre and several lawns, all set around a looping internal road.

Pros & Cons

An honest look before you book

What works

  • Main-road frontage on Thanisandra, with schools and hospitals within 1 to 3 km and Bharatiya City close by
  • North Bangalore is one of the city's fastest-growing corridors, which suits both end-use and investment
  • Goyal & Co. has a long record and several finished projects in the same neighbourhood
  • Asking rates up roughly a third since the 2024 launch, a sign of steady demand
  • Study rooms in the Compact and 3 BHK + 2T layouts

What to weigh

  • Peak-hour traffic towards the Outer Ring Road, Hennur Road, Hebbal and Manyata Tech Park
  • Civic works and new construction nearby can mean noise and dust for a few years
  • Possession is December 2028, so buyers carry the EMI or rent overlap until then
  • About 660 homes on ~6 acres is a fairly dense plan; amenities will be shared by many families
  • Carpet areas are about 66% of super built-up, so compare carpet, not super built-up, across projects
