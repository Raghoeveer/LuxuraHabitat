Project Overview: Prestige Parklane, KIADB Hi-Tech, Defence & Aerospace Park

A rare mixed residential and enterprise master plan on Devanahalli's aerospace corridor.

9 Towers, 1,788 Apartments, and a 20-Acre Enterprise Estate Next Door

Prestige Parklane occupies 32.26 acres inside KIADB's Hi-Tech, Defence and Aerospace Park, Phase 2, at Muddenahalli Village, Channarayapatna Hobli, Devanahalli, North Bengaluru. Of this, 11.91 acres form the residential precinct: nine towers of 3 basements plus Ground plus 24 upper floors (76.55 m), arranged in an arc around a landscaped central park, with 1,788 apartments across six configurations, from a compact 1 BHK Classic to the largest 3 BHK Ultima layout.

The remaining 20.35 acres is a dedicated enterprise estate: three data centres (each B+GF+6UF, 46.20 m), a warehouse block, a manufacturing block, and a dedicated 220 KV on-site substation, separated from the towers by a main boulevard and tree-lined internal roads. It is an unusual pairing for a Devanahalli launch, and one we address directly in our FAQ below.

Prestige Parklane launched on 25 September 2026 and carries Karnataka RERA registration PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/150926/008941, granted 15 September 2026, with completion declared 31 December 2030 per the filing. As with any new launch, verify current status on the Karnataka RERA portal before booking, and treat the pricing below as indicative until Prestige Group publishes its formal price list.

The Investment Case for Prestige Parklane

Why a large-format, mixed-use launch on the KIADB aerospace corridor matters for buyers and investors.

A Corridor With Compounding Price Growth Devanahalli's average listing price has risen from roughly Rs 5,500/sq ft in 2020 to about Rs 9,250/sq ft in 2026, a 72.7% gain over five years, driven by airport expansion, the now-open STRR, and the aerospace and electronics manufacturing buildout around KIADB.
Employment Density Right Next Door Foxconn alone has hired 30,000 people in just 8 months in this corridor, alongside Boeing, Safran and Collins in the adjoining aerospace park. Prestige Parklane's own enterprise estate, three data centres, a warehouse and a manufacturing block, adds further employment density within walking distance of the towers, a genuinely different demand driver than a purely residential launch.
A Track Record on This Exact Corridor Prestige Group has already delivered Golfshire and Park Drive on this same Devanahalli corridor, and in June 2026 was signed to build the convention centre, hotels and offices inside Bengaluru Airport City. A repeat developer with local delivery history is a materially different risk profile than a first-time entrant to the corridor.
