Concorde Sienna Overview

An iconic vertical landmark on Hennur Main Road, designed for elevated living.

Iconic high-rise living on Hennur Main Road

Concorde Sienna is a pre-launch residential project by Concorde Group on Hennur Main Road, Kannuru, North Bengaluru. Spread across 3.05 acres, it uses a single vertical tower format — rising to approximately 92 metres — to leave up to 70% of the surface area open for landscaped greenery, sports zones and community spaces.

The project offers exclusively 2 BHK and 3 BHK smart residences, with a limited inventory of approximately 269 to 270 units. Layouts are optimised for privacy, natural light and cross-ventilation, backed by a sky-deck clubhouse, infinity pool and co-working space for the new-age professional.

The Investment Advantage

Why Concorde Sienna is a compelling choice for home buyers and investors alike.

Why Invest On Hennur Main Road Hennur Main Road has emerged as one of North Bengaluru's most closely watched residential corridors, sitting between the established Hebbal/Manyata belt and the fast-developing Thanisandra-Bagalur stretch. Concorde Sienna is positioned right on this corridor in Kannuru, giving early pre-launch buyers a foothold in a locality with strong upside as surrounding infrastructure matures. The area already combines established residential neighbourhoods with newer gated communities, a lake and nala buffer that keeps the immediate surroundings green, and steadily improving retail and social infrastructure.
Connectivity to Employment Hubs Hennur Main Road offers practical daily access to Manyata Tech Park — one of Bengaluru's largest IT employment clusters — as well as the Outer Ring Road and Hebbal interchange, both key arteries for commuting across North and Central Bengaluru. Kempegowda International Airport is roughly 16.5 km away, making Concorde Sienna a realistic option for frequent flyers and IT professionals who want a shorter, more predictable commute than the more congested inner-city corridors.
Pre-Launch Entry Advantage Buying at the pre-launch stage typically offers the most competitive per-square-foot pricing in a project's lifecycle, before prices step up at each subsequent construction milestone. With a single-tower format, 70% open space and a sky-deck clubhouse positioned to appeal to both end-users and investors, Concorde Sienna is well placed to see steady demand from professionals working along the Hennur-Manyata-Hebbal belt, supporting both rental income and long-term capital appreciation.

About Concorde Group

Concorde Group is a Bangalore-based real estate developer with residential projects across the city, built around the brand promise "Experiences for life." Concorde Sienna extends the developer's presence in North Bengaluru, joining Concorde Mayfair in Yelahanka as part of the group's growing footprint along the Hennur-Yelahanka-Bellary Road belt.

With Concorde Sienna, the developer has opted for a single, vertically-landmarked tower rather than a sprawling low-rise layout — a design choice aimed at maximising open, green and amenity space on a compact 3.05-acre plot on Hennur Main Road.
