Project Overview: Purva Northern Lights, KIADB Aerospace Park

A Bali-inspired, resort-style high-rise township for North Bangalore's tech and aerospace workforce.

8 Towers, 2,973 Homes, Built for a Walk-to-Work Lifestyle

Purva Northern Lights is Puravankara Limited's newly launched high-rise residential township inside the KIADB Aerospace Park corridor of North Bangalore, developed jointly with land partner KVN Property Holdings LLP, which holds the 24.59-acre parcel. The masterplan is arranged around a Bali-inspired, terraced landscape, with roughly 77-80% of the total acreage reserved for open green space, botanical trails and central lawns, so towers sit apart rather than crowding each other's views.

Across 8 towers of B+G+29/30 floors, the project offers 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartments from 408 sq ft studios to 4,131 sq ft Grand Duplex residences, engineered for 70-75% space efficiency with minimal shared walls for cross-ventilation. The township's centrepiece is a nearly 1 lakh sq ft dual clubhouse (a 75,000 sq ft Grand Clubhouse plus a 25,000 sq ft Poolside Clubhouse), alongside a 76,000 sq ft in-township retail boulevard for daily convenience shopping. RERA approval was granted on 12 March 2026 across three registered phases; verify current status independently before booking.

The Investment Case for Purva Northern Lights

Why a large, RERA-approved township inside the KIADB Aerospace Park corridor matters for buyers and investors.

Employer-Anchored Rental Demand A concentrated tenant base from Boeing, Shell, Airbus, Foxconn and other Aerospace Park employers gives this corridor occupancy stability that few Bangalore micro-markets can match. Realistic gross rental yields here sit in the mid-single digits (roughly 3-4%), broadly in line with the Bangalore average; the case for renting out a unit rests more on occupancy stability than headline yield, and 2 BHK units typically lease fastest.
A Trusted, Large-Scale Joint Development Puravankara's ~50-year track record (about 92 completed projects, ~54 million sq ft, 80,000+ families served) combined with KVN Property Holdings' clear-titled, E-Khata-approved 24.59-acre land parcel reduces two of the biggest risks buyers face in a new-launch project: developer delivery risk and land-title risk.
Payment Plan & Aerospace Park Growth The 10:10:70:10 construction-linked payment plan spreads out upfront cash flow, while continued government investment in KIADB Aerospace Park (Boeing, Airbus, Foxconn's expanding factory) and the upcoming Blue Line Metro support both end-use demand and long-term resale value in this corridor.

The project spans 24.59 acres and features 8 high-rise towers (B+G+29/30 floors). The township offers approximately 2,973 apartments across three RERA-registered phases.

The project offers four configurations: 1 BHK (408 sq ft), 2 BHK (1,097-1,428 sq ft), 3 BHK (1,686-2,178 sq ft) and 4 BHK Grand Duplex (4,017-4,131 sq ft), across 11 named floor plan layouts.

The project targets a high 70-75% usable internal (carpet) area relative to saleable area, with floor plans designed to minimise long corridors and wasted corners. Units follow Vaastu Shastra principles, with East- and West-facing entrances and bedrooms/kitchens positioned for natural light and airflow.

Prices start from approximately ₹80 Lakhs* for a 1 BHK and go up to around ₹2.64 Crores* for the largest 4 BHK Grand Duplex, with 2 BHK from about ₹1.1 Crore* and 3 BHK from about ₹1.9 Crore*. Prices are subject to change and vary by tower, floor and view; enquire for the latest price list.

Purva Northern Lights is a joint development between Puravankara Limited, a Bengaluru-based developer founded in 1975 with around 92 completed projects and 54+ million sq ft delivered, and KVN Property Holdings LLP, the Bengaluru-based land-owning partner that holds the 24.59-acre parcel inside KIADB Aerospace Park.

The project follows a construction-linked 10:10:70:10 payment plan: 10% on booking, 10% on agreement within 30 days, 70% linked to construction milestones (slabs/flooring), and the final 10% at possession.
