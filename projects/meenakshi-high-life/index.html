Project Overview

A low-density high-rise community built for buyers who want to live within walking distance of Manyata Tech Park.

Walk-to-Work Living, Behind Manyata Tech Park

Meenakshi High Life is a 313-unit residential project by Meenakshi Infraa Projects (Meenakshi Realty), built across 2 high-rise towers on approximately 3.7 acres on Rachenahalli Main Road, near the Jakkur / Hebbal extension corridor of North Bangalore. At roughly 53-54 units per acre, this is a comparatively low-density layout for a high-rise development, translating into more generous common areas and landscaping per resident.

The project's central pitch is proximity: Rachenahalli sits directly behind Manyata Tech Park, approximately 1.5 km from the nearest campus blocks, positioning Meenakshi High Life as a genuine walk-to-work option for the tech park's workforce.
