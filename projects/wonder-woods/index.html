Project Overview

A premium, large-format township designed for Vaastu-first, privacy-conscious modern living.

1,261 Homes, Built for Everyday Comfort

Aparna Wonder Woods is a premium high-rise development by Aparna Constructions at Mallasandra, off Kanakapura Road, South Bengaluru.
