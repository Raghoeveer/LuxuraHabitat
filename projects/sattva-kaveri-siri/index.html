Thoughtfully Planned Layout

Sattva Kaveri Siri's master plan is designed to maximise open space, ensure privacy, and provide connectivity within the community. Each plot is positioned to offer excellent exposure, ventilation, and easy access to common amenities.

The layout features 9 to 12 metre internal roads, six parks, four entry and exit points, and a clubhouse for the community's social life. Phase 2 sits beside a 60 metre bypass road shown on the developer's Phase 2 plan.

Status, Pros & Questions to Ask Before You Pay

An honest checklist for plot buyers, built from the developer's pages, listings and buyer discussions.

What Works

  • Branded developer: Sattva lists CRISIL and ICRA "AA Stable" ratings
  • 9 to 12 m roads, six parks and four entry/exit points
  • Phase 1 shows OC received and ready for registration on the developer's page
  • Entry price near ₹28 L* to ₹31 L* for a 600 sq ft plot
  • Railway station about 4 to 5 km; expressway at the town entrance
  • A Reddit buyer on a sub-₹1 Cr budget saw it as one of few branded plot options they could afford (forum opinion)

Things to Weigh

  • Phase 2 is still under construction; possession is listed as Dec 2026 by most and Dec 2030 by one site
  • Public transport is limited, a point even a broker write-up admits
  • Bengaluru is about 98 km by road, so this is not a daily commute
  • Phase 2 lists a "semi clubhouse"; the 25,000 sq ft clubhouse is on the Phase 1 page
  • Stock may be thin: one advisory site marks Phase 2 sold out
  • Appreciation figures quoted online are sales claims, not forecasts

Questions to Ask Before You Pay

  1. Which authority approved the layout, and is the khata an A-khata? (A forum comment says MUDA; no listing confirms it.)
  2. What is the written Phase 2 handover date, and is sale-deed registration open now?
  3. What is the all-in rate per sq ft for my plot, and what premium do corner, park-facing or east-facing plots carry?
  4. Will Phase 2 owners use the 25,000 sq ft clubhouse or only the semi clubhouse, and what are the maintenance charges?
  5. What are the building rules: permitted floors, setbacks and any minimum build period?
  6. What is the water source, the power utility and the cost of individual connections?
  7. Is the plot low-lying or near a canal, and has the site ever waterlogged?
  8. Which banks have approved the project for plot and composite loans?
  9. Do I need to cross a public road to reach the amenities?
  10. What have Phase 1 plots resold for, compared with their launch price?

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Facts are drawn from the developer's Phase 1 and Phase 2 pages, property portals, broker microsites and a Reddit buyer thread, labelled where they differ.
