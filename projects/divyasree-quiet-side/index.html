Project Overview

A quiet, low-density address on the Bellary Road corridor, coming soon from Divyasree.

A Peaceful New Address on Bellary Road

Divyasree The Quiet Side is a pre-launch residential development by Divyasree spread across 5.5 acres in Yelahanka New Town, North Bangalore. The project comprises 3 towers with a Basement + Ground + 23 floor structure and 270 apartments, positioned on the Bellary Road (NH-44) stretch near Vidyaranyapura, Kodigehalli and Sathanur, between Hebbal and Yelahanka, close to Kristu Jayanti (Deemed to be University) and Manyata Tech Park.

True to the developer's own positioning, “A Life of Peace. A World of Comfort,” the project is designed around low-density living, with just 4 units per floor and no common wall, doubling ventilation and natural light into every home. The developer describes the philosophy simply: “Live in Quiet. Live in Harmony.”

The project is currently at the coming-soon, pre-launch stage. Floor plans, exact pricing and RERA registration are yet to be released; this page will be updated as official details are confirmed. Register your interest below to be notified first.

The Investment Advantage

Why the Bellary Road stretch near Hebbal and Yelahanka matters for early registrants.

Why This Stretch of Bellary Road Divyasree The Quiet Side sits on the Bellary Road (NH-44) corridor between Hebbal and Yelahanka, within reach of Manyata Tech Park via Outer Ring Road and close to established social infrastructure like Kristu Jayanti College. This stretch, closer to Vidyaranyapura and Kodigehalli than the core Yelahanka New Town area further north, has historically offered relatively accessible entry pricing on a corridor with strong IT-driven rental and resale demand from Manyata and Hebbal.
Low-Density, No-Common-Wall Design At just 4 apartments per floor with no common wall, Divyasree The Quiet Side is designed at a meaningfully lower density than many towers on the same corridor, which is unusual for a 270-unit, 3-tower project. That translates into double-side ventilation and more natural light in every home, an end-user appeal that can also support resale value versus higher-density neighbours.
