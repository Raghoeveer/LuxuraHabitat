Divyasree The Quiet Side: Low-Density Luxury in North Bangalore

Divyasree The Quiet Side is an upcoming premium residential project by Divyasree Developers in Yelahanka New Town, North Bangalore. Spread across 5.5 acres, the project has 3 elegant towers of B+G+23 floors and only 270 luxury apartments and penthouses, designed for families who want peaceful surroundings and spacious homes away from crowded city living.

Homes come in 3 BHK (1,900 sq ft), 3.5 BHK (2,100 to 2,400 sq ft) and penthouse (2,900 sq ft) layouts, with prices from ₹2.65 Cr onwards. The developer's own positioning sums up the idea: “A Life of Peace. A World of Comfort.” Each floor has just 4 homes with no common wall, which brings double-side ventilation and natural light into every home.

Yelahanka New Town is known for its greenery, wide roads and well-developed social infrastructure, with quick access to Hebbal, Manyata Tech Park, Jakkur, Bellary Road and Kempegowda International Airport. The project is currently in its pre-launch EOI phase, with launch expected in 2026 and possession from 2030 onwards. RERA registration is under process.

Design Philosophy: Privacy, Natural Light & Efficient Space

With only 270 homes on 5.5 acres, the project is planned to reduce congestion and create a quieter living environment. Large windows, open balconies and efficient layouts maximise airflow and daylight, while the plans minimise wasted space so every square foot is usable. Spacious living areas, carefully placed bedrooms and separate utility zones balance modern luxury with practical everyday living.

Best units for end-use: for long-term living, mid and higher-floor apartments offer better views, improved ventilation and a quieter experience. With only 3 towers, buyers can expect a more private lifestyle than in larger township developments, suited to professionals working in Hebbal, Manyata Tech Park and along Airport Road.

How Yelahanka Grew

Yelahanka was long known for its calm suburban setting, lakes and defence establishments. Kempegowda International Airport changed that: once it opened, business parks, residential projects, hotels and commercial hubs followed across North Bangalore. Through the late 2010s and early 2020s, Hebbal, Yelahanka, Jakkur and Thanisandra grew quickly on the back of Manyata Tech Park, Kirloskar Business Park and a wave of IT and startup offices.

Infrastructure such as the proposed Peripheral Ring Road, the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR), metro expansion and road widening has cut travel times between North Bangalore and the rest of the city. Today Yelahanka New Town is one of Bangalore's most preferred residential areas, with green surroundings, organised layouts, wide roads, good schools and hospitals, and smooth airport access.
