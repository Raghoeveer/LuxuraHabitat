Project Overview: Nikoo Homes 9, KIADB Bagalur

The newest phase inside Bhartiya City's 125-acre integrated township.

A New Phase Inside an Established Township

Bhartiya City Nikoo Homes 9, officially registered as "Nikoo Homes 9, Alpine, Bhartiya Garden Hill: Phase 1", is Bhartiya Urban's newest residential launch in KIADB, Bagalur, North Bangalore. It occupies roughly 16 acres within the larger 125-acre Bhartiya City township, an integrated development that already includes the Bhartiya Mall of Centre, Chaman Bhartiya International School, and the Bhartiya Centre of Information Technology (BCIT) commercial campus.

The phase is planned across 10 towers (2 basements plus Ground plus 17 to 20 upper floors) with 1,584 units, spanning eight configurations from a compact 506 sq ft Studio to a 2,190 sq ft 3 BHK Loft with double-height living. A limited number of larger Sky Villa duplex units (around 2,681 sq ft) also appear in the developer's floor plan set for Tower 3. Unlike a standalone apartment block, residents step directly into Bhartiya City's existing township infrastructure rather than waiting for amenities to be built out over time.

Nikoo Homes 9 is registered with Karnataka RERA under number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/100926/008930. As with any new-launch project, we recommend verifying the current registration status directly on the Karnataka RERA portal before booking, and confirming the possession timeline with the sales team, since it was not disclosed in the material available to us.
