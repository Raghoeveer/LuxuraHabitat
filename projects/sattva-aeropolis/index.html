Project Overview & Master Plan Details

A thoughtfully planned residential experience with premium amenities and a connected lifestyle.

A large, amenity-led township on the airport corridor

Sattva Aeropolis (also searched as Salarpuria Aeropolis) is an apartment township by Salarpuria Sattva at Boovanahalli, Udayagiri, off NH-44 in Devanahalli. It covers about 11 acres (the developer's website says 10.32 acres) with roughly 1,001 homes in towers of ground plus 11 floors, about 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport and in the same employment belt as the KIADB Aerospace Park.

The project is being delivered in two RERA-registered phases. Phase 1 offers efficient 1 BHK (666 to 672 sq ft), 2 BHK (906 to 943 sq ft) and 3 BHK (1,012 sq ft) homes. The official Phase 1 brochure also prints carpet areas: 388 sq ft for the 1 BHK, 570 to 572 sq ft for the 2 BHK and 645 sq ft for the 3 BHK. Phase 2, with 554 homes, adds compact Studio (448 to 500 sq ft) and 1 BHK (550 to 658 sq ft) units for first-time buyers and rental investors. For an independent take on pricing, risks and fit, read our Sattva Aeropolis review.

The Investment Case & Trade-offs

What supports Sattva Aeropolis as a home or an investment, and what to weigh before booking.

Why the corridor works Land in the Devanahalli airport belt has moved from about ₹4,500 per sq ft in 2024 to roughly ₹7,000 to ₹9,600 in 2026, driven by the aerospace park, airport expansion, STRR and the upcoming Blue Line metro. New Devanahalli apartment launches are quoting about ₹11,000 to ₹13,000 per sq ft, so Aeropolis Phase 1 at roughly ₹8,600 to ₹9,000 per sq ft on stated sizes sits at the value end of the market. Two caveats: a property portal puts the project's own average nearer ₹11,139 per sq ft and the Devanahalli average at about ₹9,844 (July to September 2026), and on carpet area the Phase 1 starting prices work out to roughly ₹14,000 to ₹15,500 per sq ft.
Rental yield potential Airport-corridor apartments currently yield about 3.2% to 3.8% gross (a property portal reports 3.22% for Devanahalli). On a ₹60 Lakh 1 BHK that implies roughly ₹16,000 to ₹19,000 a month, and on an ₹81 Lakh 2 BHK roughly ₹21,500 to ₹25,500, before maintenance and vacancy. Compact Studio and 1 BHK homes suit the airport, aerospace and logistics workforce.
Trade-off 1: scale and density About 1,001 homes on 11 acres means a busy community with heavy shared infrastructure. Long-term quality depends on the residents' association and facility manager, so ask for the projected maintenance rate per sq ft and how facility management will be staffed after handover.
Trade-off 2: timelines and location Phase 1's RERA completion date of July 2026 has passed, so verify your specific tower's handover and OC status rather than relying on the project-level date. And while airport access is superb, the eastern and southern tech belts are a long commute.
Trade-off 3: what the base price leaves out The brochure excludes the kitchen counter, sink and mixer, gives only conduit for an AC point and charges extra for DG back-up. Add these, plus GST, stamp duty and registration, before comparing with projects that quote fitted kitchens.
Trade-off 4: flight path and noise The brochure sells "unobstructed views of the airport". Being this close to the runways can also mean aircraft noise. Visit at different times of day and ask which wings face the airport.

Location and connectivity

Sattva Aeropolis is at Boovanahalli, Udayagiri, off NH-44 (International Airport Road) in Devanahalli, next to the KIADB Aerospace Park belt in North Bangalore.

It is about 15 minutes by road from Kempegowda International Airport in typical traffic. The developer gives the distance as 3.4 km and the drive as 5 to 6 minutes, while a broker's road estimate is 20 to 30 minutes.

The project sits in the same airport-side belt as the KIADB Aerospace Park, about 10 minutes from its main campuses by our estimate; the developer's brochure measures it at about 12 km. The park hosts major aerospace employers including Boeing and RTX, and a new Wistron plant is expected to add about 3,000 jobs.

The Namma Metro Blue Line to the airport is under construction along the Airport Road corridor. The developer lists the upcoming Doddajala station about 6 minutes away, and a broker puts the line about 3 km from the project. Check the expected opening date, since metro timelines in Bengaluru often slip.

Hebbal is roughly 30 minutes and Manyata Tech Park about 35 minutes off-peak. Whitefield, Outer Ring Road and Electronic City are long drives, so the project fits people working in the north far better than those commuting east or south.

Around 50,000 professionals are estimated to work within about 10 km of the airport, across the aerospace park, airport operations, logistics, hardware parks and IT campuses at Devanahalli Business Park.

Investment and buying decision

It has a strong case for airport-corridor investors: an established developer, a sub-₹1 Cr entry and one of Bengaluru's fastest-growing employment belts. Land in the Devanahalli belt has moved from about ₹4,500 per sq ft in 2024 to roughly ₹7,000 to ₹9,600 in 2026. The trade-offs are high density and a far-north location.

Airport-corridor apartments currently yield about 3.2% to 3.8% gross. On a ₹60 Lakh 1 BHK that implies roughly ₹16,000 to ₹19,000 a month, and on an ₹81 Lakh 2 BHK roughly ₹21,500 to ₹25,500 a month, before maintenance and vacancy. These are estimates, not quotes.

Newer Devanahalli apartment launches are quoting about ₹11,000 to ₹13,000 per sq ft, so Aeropolis Phase 1 at roughly ₹8,600 to ₹9,000 per sq ft on stated sizes is towards the value end of the market. Confirm the area basis before comparing.

Airport, aerospace and logistics professionals, first-time buyers who want a branded home under ₹1 Cr, and investors targeting the airport-corridor rental pool. Phase 1 suits those wanting a nearer-term home; Phase 2 suits buyers who want the lowest ticket size and can wait until 2028.

Buyers who want low-density, boutique living, or who commute daily to Whitefield, Outer Ring Road or Electronic City, are likely to be better served elsewhere.

The developer has not published a maintenance rate. On a 1,001-home community, ask for the projected per-sq-ft charge, the facility management company and how the handover to the residents' association will work.
