Project Overview

A meticulously planned residential experience offering an exclusive community and premium lifestyle.

Exclusive 4 BHK Luxury Row Villas

Sattva La Vita brings together privacy, community, and unmatched wellness. Situated just off Hennur Main Road in Byrathi Village, this low-density enclave has just 63 row houses on 3 acres. Enjoy lush private gardens, extensive fitness zones, and world-class lifestyle amenities, built to elevate your everyday routines.

Each home has independent walls (no shared walls with neighbours), 4 bedrooms, a gym, an entertainment room, a family lounge and a domestic help room. Backyards extend over 10 feet and every house gets its own open terrace. Inside the community, walkways are kept free of vehicle movement. The project is registered with K-RERA as PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/041223/006446.

Why Invest in Hennur & North Bangalore?

Why buyers look at the Hennur corridor, and what to weigh up.

Why Invest in Hennur

Hennur is rapidly emerging as a preferred residential destination in Bangalore. Hennur Main Road branches off the Outer Ring Road and heads north towards the airport, connecting to Hebbal, Indiranagar, MG Road and the cantonment areas. IT parks, hospitals and malls are within a short drive.

Demand in Hennur is driven by road upgrades, the airport metro line under construction and North Bengaluru's tech parks. Row houses are a small share of supply on this corridor, which is part of La Vita's appeal. As with any property, returns are not guaranteed, so compare the all-in price with nearby villa and apartment projects.

Advantages of North Bangalore

North Bangalore has evolved into the city's most promising growth corridor, spearheaded by the presence of the Kempegowda International Airport. It boasts world-class infrastructure including signal-free corridors, the upcoming Suburban Rail, and the Namma Metro Blue Line.

The region is home to colossal employment hubs like Manyata Tech Park, KIADB Aerospace Park, and Devanahalli Business Park. With major IT and aerospace employers in the region, North Bengaluru draws a large workforce, which supports rental demand.

About the Builder: Sattva Group

A Bengaluru developer active since 1986.

Sattva Group is a Bengaluru-headquartered developer that began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva (the group's own site dates its current journey to 1993). It develops commercial, residential, hospitality and education projects across several Indian cities.

The group describes itself as a CRISIL 'AA' Stable and ICRA 'AA' Stable rated company, ISO 9001, 14001 and 18001 certified, and a founding member of the Indian Green Building Council (IGBC).
