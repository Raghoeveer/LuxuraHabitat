About Sattva Thippapura

A new plotted residential community in Doddaballapur, North Bangalore's emerging industrial and infrastructure corridor

A New-Launch Plotted Development by Sattva Group

Sattva Thippapura is a newly launched plotted residential development in Thippapura, Doddaballapur, on the northern edge of Bangalore. Spread across approximately 25 acres, the layout offers 500 residential plots in standard, corner and premium categories, giving buyers the freedom to design and build their own home at their own pace.

Developed by Sattva Group, the project is positioned to benefit from Doddaballapur's growing industrial base, its proximity to NH-44 (the Bangalore-Hyderabad highway), and the town's ongoing connectivity upgrades toward Kempegowda International Airport and the wider North Bangalore corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Sattva Thippapura, Doddaballapur.

Sattva Thippapura is a new plotted residential development by Sattva Group in Thippapura, Doddaballapur, North Bangalore, offering 500 residential plots across approximately 25 acres.

The project is located in Thippapura, Doddaballapur, around 8 km from NH-44 (the Bangalore-Hyderabad Highway) and roughly 30 km from Kempegowda International Airport.

It is a plotted residential development, not apartments. Buyers purchase a serviced residential plot within the gated layout and build their own home, choosing from standard, corner or premium plot categories.

Sattva Thippapura offers standard, corner and premium plot categories. Exact plot dimensions have not yet been announced and will be confirmed closer to RERA registration. Contact our sales team for the latest details.

Standard plots start from ₹84 Lakh onwards, as an indicative new-launch price. Corner and premium plots are priced on request. Final pricing is subject to change until RERA registration is complete.

As a new launch, Sattva Thippapura's RERA registration application is currently in process. Contact our sales team for the latest RERA registration number and approval status before booking.

Possession is currently expected around 2030, in line with the project's new-launch timeline. This date may be revised once RERA registration and development milestones are confirmed.

Planned amenities include a swimming pool, fitness center and yoga studio, landscaped gardens, a children's play area, a clubhouse with indoor games, jogging tracks, 24/7 CCTV security and internal roads throughout the layout.

Doddaballapur's established industrial base, proximity to NH-44 and ongoing North Bangalore infrastructure growth make plotted developments in this corridor worth evaluating for both self-build end-use and long-term land appreciation. As with any new launch, review the final RERA registration and layout before committing.
