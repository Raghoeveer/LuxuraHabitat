Sattva Vasanta Cove Overview

A compact, investment-focused residential development by Sattva Group on Devanahalli's aerospace growth corridor.

Smart-Sized Homes on the Aerospace Growth Corridor

Sattva Vasanta Cove is a new-launch residential development by Sattva Group, located off Bellary Road (NH-44) in Devanahalli, North Bengaluru — within close range of the KIADB Aerospace Park, Foxconn's upcoming facility and Prestige Tech Cloud. Kempegowda International Airport is approximately 5-10 minutes away by road.

Spread across 1.3 acres, the project comprises 2 towers rising 2 basements + ground + 12/13 floors, with 200 units split between 163 Studio apartments and 37 1 BHK apartments. Based on the developer's own configuration table, Studio homes range from 533-625 sq ft and 1 BHK homes from 887-952 sq ft — a compact, efficiently planned format aimed at young professionals, first-time buyers and rental-yield investors.

The project is currently at its early launch stage, with possession targeted for December 2030 as per its RERA registration.

The Investment Advantage

Why Sattva Vasanta Cove is a compelling choice for first-time buyers and rental-yield investors.

Why Invest in Devanahalli's Aerospace Corridor Devanahalli's growth story has moved beyond the airport itself — the KIADB Aerospace Park, an upcoming Foxconn facility and Prestige Tech Cloud are drawing a large manufacturing and technology workforce to the immediate vicinity of Sattva Vasanta Cove. That workforce needs housing close to the workplace, and a Studio/1 BHK-only project sitting directly off Bellary Road is well positioned to capture that demand.
Small-Ticket Entry Advantage With Studio homes starting around ₹59 Lakhs, Sattva Vasanta Cove offers one of the more accessible entry points into Sattva Group's Devanahalli portfolio, which also includes the larger-format Sattva Aeropolis and Sattva City. That makes it a realistic first purchase for young professionals as well as a lower-ticket-size option for investors diversifying across the airport corridor.
Rental Demand from the Aerospace Workforce Studio and 1 BHK formats are typically the first to lease out to single professionals and dual-income couples working nearby. As KIADB Aerospace Park and Foxconn ramp up hiring, Sattva Vasanta Cove's proximity — combined with direct NH-44 access and a full lifestyle clubhouse — positions it to see steady rental interest from this workforce.

About Sattva Group

One of Bengaluru's most established developers, with a growing Devanahalli portfolio.

Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group) is one of Bengaluru's most trusted and established real estate developers, with a large portfolio of residential and commercial projects across the city. Sattva Vasanta Cove extends the developer's presence in the Devanahalli airport corridor, joining Sattva Aeropolis and Sattva City as part of the group's growing footprint in North Bengaluru.

With Vasanta Cove, the developer has focused on a compact, efficient Studio and 1 BHK format — a departure from the larger multi-configuration launches typical of the corridor — built around a ~12,000 sq ft clubhouse with co-working and creative zones. The positioning reflects the area's fast-growing base of aerospace and technology professionals looking for a self-contained, well-connected home close to work.
