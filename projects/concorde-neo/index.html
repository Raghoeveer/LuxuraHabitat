Concorde Neo Overview

An iconic vertical masterpiece by Concorde Group on Thanisandra Main Road.

Iconic vertical living on Thanisandra Main Road

Concorde Neo is a pre-launch residential project by Concorde Group on Thanisandra Main Road, RK Hegde Nagar / Bellahalli, North Bengaluru. Spread across 4.02 acres, it uses a 6-tower vertical format — each tower rising 2 basements plus ground/stilt plus 18 floors — to leave approximately 78% of the surface area open for landscaped greenery and active sports zones.

The project offers exclusively 2 BHK and 3 BHK smart residences, with a total of 424 units. Layouts are optimised for privacy, natural light and cross-ventilation, backed by the multi-level Sky Evolve Clubhouse, a co-working space and business lounge for the new-age professional.

The Investment Advantage

Why Concorde Neo is a compelling choice for home buyers and investors alike.

Why Invest On Thanisandra Main Road Thanisandra Main Road has emerged as one of North Bengaluru's most closely watched apartment corridors, sitting between the established Hebbal/Manyata belt and the fast-developing Yelahanka-Bellary Road stretch. Concorde Neo is positioned right on this corridor at Bellahalli/RK Hegde Nagar, giving early pre-launch buyers a foothold in a locality with strong upside as surrounding infrastructure matures — the same road that is also home to Goyal Orchid Salisbury, underlining the corridor's pull for Tier-1 developers.
Connectivity to Employment Hubs Thanisandra Main Road offers practical daily access to Manyata Tech Park — one of Bengaluru's largest IT employment clusters — as well as the Outer Ring Road and Hebbal interchange, both key arteries for commuting across North and Central Bengaluru. Kempegowda International Airport is roughly 26 km away, making Concorde Neo a realistic option for frequent flyers and IT professionals who want a shorter, more predictable commute.
Pre-Launch Entry Advantage Buying at the pre-launch stage typically offers the most competitive per-square-foot pricing in a project's lifecycle, before prices step up at each subsequent construction milestone. With 6 towers, ~78% open space and a Sky Evolve Clubhouse positioned to appeal to both end-users and investors, Concorde Neo is well placed to see steady demand from professionals working along the Thanisandra-Manyata-Hebbal belt, supporting both rental income and long-term capital appreciation.
