Concorde Neo Overview

A 6-tower, 424-home community by Concorde Group on Thanisandra Main Road, North Bengaluru.

Mid-market 2 & 3 BHK homes on a fast-growing corridor

Concorde Neo sits on Thanisandra Main Road at RK Hegde Nagar / Bellahalli, a corridor that has become one of North Bengaluru's busiest new-launch stretches over the last two years. Manyata Tech Park, Hebbal and the Bellary Road (NH 44) approach to the airport are all within reasonable reach, and REVA University is close by.

The project places 424 homes in six towers (A to F), each 2 basements + ground + 18 floors, on about 4.02 acres. Keeping the towers in a compact central cluster leaves roughly 78% of the site open for a pool, sports courts, play areas and gardens. Homes are 2 BHK and 3 BHK only, in seven floor plans from 1,125 to 1,539 sq ft super built-up (727 to 1,024 sq ft carpet). The project was RERA registered in September 2024, launched in October 2024 and is due for possession between October and December 2028.
