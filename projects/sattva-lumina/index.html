Why Invest in Rajankunte & North Bangalore?

A fast-growing corridor with exceptional appreciation potential.

Why Invest in Rajankunte

Rajankunte is rapidly emerging as a preferred residential destination in Bangalore. Located strategically along the Doddaballapura Main Road, it offers an escape from city congestion while keeping you seamlessly connected to essential hubs. The area features pristine lakes, lush green covers, and a tranquil environment.

Real estate in Rajankunte is experiencing steady appreciation driven by improved infrastructure, widening roads, and the expanding perimeter of North Bangalore's tech zones. Investing in a premium project like Sattva Lumina here means securing property at competitive launch prices with high future returns.

Advantages of North Bangalore

North Bangalore has evolved into the city’s most promising growth corridor, spearheaded by the presence of the Kempegowda International Airport. It boasts world-class infrastructure including signal-free corridors, the upcoming Suburban Rail, and the Namma Metro Blue Line.

The region is home to colossal employment hubs like Manyata Tech Park, KIADB Aerospace Park, and Devanahalli Business Park. With major IT and aerospace companies setting up operations, North Bangalore attracts a huge workforce, ensuring robust rental yields and massive long-term capital appreciation for property owners.
