About the Project

Sattva Lumina Overview: Why Families Track It on Doddaballapura Road

A mid-size township that sits in the sweet spot: not too dense for comfort, not too small to support a full clubhouse.

Sattva Lumina offers Studio, 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK+2T and 3 BHK+3T apartments. It suits families looking for a neighbourhood they can grow into, couples planning to stay long-term, and buyers who put comfort and developer reputation ahead of hype. If you want a home that supports everyday routines like school runs, office commutes, grocery trips and evening walks, Lumina is a natural fit.

The site sits in Rajanukunte (often spelt Rajankunte) on the Yelahanka to Doddaballapura road, with a lake and mature tree cover around it. The master plan places the clubhouse right beside the single entry, keeps cars mostly in two basement levels, and runs green open space along the length of the plot between towers.

Unlike most North Bangalore launches, Lumina starts at a Studio format priced from about ₹38 Lakh. That opens the project to rental investors and first-time buyers as well as families shopping for a 3 BHK, which also tends to keep resale demand broad.

Sattva Lumina is in Rajanukunte (also spelt Rajankunte), on Yelahanka Doddaballapura Road in North Bangalore. The site address is 5H88+44, Kalmi Factory, Rajanukunte, Yelahanka Doddaballapura Road, Bengaluru 560064. Yelahanka town is a short drive south and Kempegowda International Airport is roughly 20 to 30 minutes away depending on route and traffic.

It is a gated high-rise apartment township by Sattva Group on about 13 acres (the master plan lists 12.83 acres). Eight towers of 2 basements plus ground plus 30 floors (one tower goes to 31) hold 1,549 apartments, with about 70% of the land kept as open space and a multi-level clubhouse near the entrance.

Marketing material quotes possession from 2027 onwards, while the completion date committed in the RERA registration is January 2029. Large multi-tower projects usually hand over tower by tower, so ask for the committed date of your specific tower and make sure it is written into your agreement for sale.

There are five formats: Studio (420 to 434 sq ft), 1 BHK (650 to 684 sq ft), 2 BHK (1,142 to 1,185 sq ft), 3 BHK with 2 toilets (1,506 to 1,536 sq ft) and 3 BHK with 3 toilets (1,795 to 1,809 sq ft). All sizes are super built-up areas; the carpet area is roughly 60 to 65 percent of that figure.

Share your budget and preferred configuration through the enquiry form or WhatsApp, take a site and sample-flat visit, then shortlist a tower, floor band and facing. Ask for the stamped cost sheet and payment schedule, check the RERA registration on the Karnataka RERA portal, pay the booking amount by cheque or bank transfer to the developer's project account only, and sign the agreement for sale within the timeline RERA allows.

Sattva Lumina is a new launch in its pre-launch and early construction phase. It is registered with Karnataka RERA under PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/060924/007009, and its plan is approved by the Local Planning Authority (LPA).

The project is marketed at about 13 acres. The master plan drawing states the site area precisely as 51,951.09 square metres, which is 12.83 acres.

The project is RERA-registered and LPA-approved, and both require the promoter to file title and land documents with the authorities, which is a good starting signal. It is still not a substitute for your own check. Ask for the title deed, encumbrance certificate, land-conversion order and any joint development agreement, and have an independent property lawyer review them. Banks also run their own legal check before approving a home loan.

A launch offer is currently being extended to the first few buyers. Offers change quickly during a launch, so ask for the current offer to be printed on your stamped cost sheet rather than accepting a verbal promise.

It is worth shortlisting if you want a branded, amenity-rich community in North Bangalore at a lower ticket size than Hebbal or Thanisandra, with a Studio entry from ₹38 Lakh and family 3 BHKs up to about ₹1.44 Cr. It is less suited to buyers who want a low-density community, since 1,549 homes on 13 acres is a large neighbourhood, or who need to move in before 2027.

Sattva Lumina is a residential apartment project by Sattva Group, the Bengaluru developer that started in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva. The group has built residential, commercial and plotted projects across Bangalore and cities such as Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata and Goa.

Rajanukunte sits on the Doddaballapura Road growth axis between Yelahanka town and the airport belt. Daily needs such as schools and hospitals are available in Yelahanka, and employment hubs such as Bagmane Tech Park, Manyata Tech Park and the aerospace and airport-linked parks are within commuting range. Road widening, the Satellite Town Ring Road and planned metro extensions support the medium-term story, but these are future drivers, so buy for the location as it is today and treat infrastructure as upside.

Studios and 1 BHKs suit rental investors, first-time buyers and airport-belt professionals. The 2 BHK is the most liquid format for resale and suits small families. The 3 BHK+2T is a practical family home, and the 3 BHK+3T suits families that need a third bathroom for parents or help. Choose by who will live in the home over the next ten years, then compare carpet area and balcony area, not only the super built-up figure.

The plan includes a multi-level clubhouse with swimming pool, gym, yoga room, spa, party hall and party terrace, cafe, library, co-working space and indoor games, plus outdoor tennis and multi-sport courts, children's play areas, jogging and cycling tracks, landscaped gardens and a tree-lined avenue, with 40+ amenities in total. In practice this means kids play within the gated community, fitness does not need a commute, and evening walks happen inside the campus. Confirm which amenities are delivered at handover and which follow in later phases.

The towers are RCC-framed high-rises with two basement levels for parking and services. The detailed specification sheet covering flooring, doors and windows, bathroom fittings, electrical points and power backup is shared with the cost sheet, so ask for it in writing and check that it is attached to your agreement for sale. A visit to a completed Sattva project nearby is the best way to judge finish quality.

Quoted rates run between about ₹12,400 and ₹15,600 per sq ft of carpet area (roughly ₹7,700 to ₹10,000 per sq ft of super built-up area), depending on tower, floor and orientation, giving tentative tickets of ₹38 to 42 Lakh for a Studio, ₹57 to 63 Lakh for a 1 BHK, ₹93 to 99 Lakh for a 2 BHK, ₹1.18 to 1.23 Cr for a 3 BHK+2T and ₹1.39 to 1.44 Cr for a 3 BHK+3T. On top of this, plan for GST on under-construction homes, stamp duty and registration, car parking, floor-rise and preferred-location charges, the maintenance deposit and corpus, and utility connection charges.

The RERA-committed completion date of January 2029 is the legally binding one, and RERA requires the developer to pay interest if it misses it without a valid extension. The 2027 onwards date is a marketing target for early towers. Track quarterly progress updates on the Karnataka RERA portal and prefer a construction-linked payment plan so your money moves with the building.

Compared with branded launches around Hebbal, Thanisandra or Hennur, Sattva Lumina offers a lower entry price and a Studio format that most competitors skip. Compared with smaller projects in Yelahanka, it offers a far bigger amenity set but higher density. Its closest trade-off is distance: it is further out than Yelahanka New Town projects, which shows up in commute time to central Bengaluru.

The main watchpoints are density (1,549 homes means busy lifts and common areas at peak hours), a long construction period to 2029, the gap between super built-up and carpet area, phased delivery of amenities, and dependence on future infrastructure for the strongest appreciation. A nala runs through the site with a 30 metre buffer in the master plan, so check drainage and the position of your tower relative to it.

Drive the route to your workplace at peak hours, compare the all-inclusive cost per sq ft of carpet area with two or three nearby launches, and check rental demand for your chosen configuration in Yelahanka. Then verify the RERA registration, title documents and the tower-wise completion date. If the numbers work without assuming future metro or road upgrades, the project is a sound fit.
