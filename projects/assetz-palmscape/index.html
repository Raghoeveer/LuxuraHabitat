Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Assetz Palmscape, Devanahalli.

Assetz Palmscape is a premium gated-community villa plot development by Assetz Property Group on IVC Road, Devanahalli, North Bangalore, offering 450+ plots with 60%+ open spaces and a Miyawaki forest.

The project sits on IVC Road in Devanahalli, with direct access to the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR), Peripheral Ring Road (PRR) and NH-44, and is around 15 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

It is a plotted villa development, not apartments. You buy a serviced plot within the gated community and build your own villa, choosing a layout and BHK configuration that fits your chosen plot size.

Plots are available in multiple sizes, including 1,200 sq ft, 1,500 sq ft and 2,400 sq ft options, so you can pick a footprint that matches the villa you plan to build.

Pricing is currently available on request as the project is in pre-launch, with an EOI (Expression of Interest) token of ₹3 Lakhs to register early interest. Contact us for the latest price list.

Residents get a grand clubhouse with a swimming pool and banquet hall, jogging and cycling tracks, outdoor sports courts, a fitness zone, landscaped gardens, a yoga and meditation deck, and a dedicated senior-citizen relaxation zone.

The layout dedicates 60%+ of the community to open spaces, including a Miyawaki forest, wide asphalted internal roads, manicured lawns and children's play areas.

Assetz Palmscape is a registered real estate project. For the current RERA registration number and project approvals, please contact our sales team directly.

Devanahalli is one of North Bangalore's fastest-growing corridors thanks to the airport, upcoming tech parks and highway upgrades, which makes gated-community plots here attractive for both long-term land appreciation and self-build flexibility.

You can register your Expression of Interest, request the brochure and latest price list, or book a site visit using the enquiry form, WhatsApp button or call button on this page.
