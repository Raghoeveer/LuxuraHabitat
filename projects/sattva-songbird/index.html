Sattva Songbird Phase 2 Overview

A premium residential development by Sattva Group on Budigere Cross's fast-growing airport-road corridor.

Premium Living on the Budigere Cross Growth Corridor

Sattva Songbird Phase 2 is a new-launch residential development by the reputed Sattva Group, positioned directly opposite the KRN Complex on Old Madras Road (NH-75), Budigere Cross, East Bengaluru. The address offers seamless connectivity to Whitefield, ITPL and the wider Old Madras Road IT corridor, with Kempegowda International Airport approximately 45 minutes away.

This phase spans 4.33 acres with 381 two-side-open apartments across 2 towers, keeping approximately 84% of the land as open, landscaped green space. Based on the project's own floor-plan brochure, verified configurations range from compact Studio homes through 1, 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK layouts (roughly 468 to 1,813 sq ft) — a broader mix than a typical single-format launch, so buyers across budgets have a genuine option here. Sattva Songbird Phase 2 forms part of a larger 50-acre Sattva Songbird integrated township, which also includes independent rowhouses and further towers in other phases.

The project is currently under construction in a single phase, offering an early-bird opportunity for homebuyers and investors seeking premium real estate in East Bengaluru's airport corridor.

The Investment Advantage

Why Sattva Songbird is a compelling choice for home buyers and investors alike.

Why Invest in Budigere Cross Budigere Cross has emerged as one of East Bengaluru's fastest-growing residential corridors, sitting on Old Madras Road (NH-75) between the established Whitefield-ITPL belt and the airport road towards Hoskote. Sattva Songbird is positioned right on this corridor, giving early buyers a foothold in a locality with strong upside as surrounding infrastructure — including an upcoming retail mall and multiplex within the township — matures.
Connectivity to Employment Hubs The location offers practical daily access to Whitefield and ITPL, as well as Bagmane Tech Park and RMZ Infinity — some of Bengaluru's largest IT employment clusters. Kempegowda International Airport is roughly 45 minutes away via Budigere Road, making Sattva Songbird a realistic option for both IT professionals and frequent flyers.
New-Launch Entry Advantage Buying at the new-launch stage typically offers the most competitive per-square-foot pricing before prices step up at each construction milestone. With 84% open space, 3 dedicated clubhouses and a genuinely broad Studio-to-3-BHK unit mix, Sattva Songbird is well placed to see steady demand from both end-users and investors along the Budigere-Whitefield corridor.

About Sattva Group
