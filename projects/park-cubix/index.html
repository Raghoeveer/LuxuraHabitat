Sattva Park Cubix Overview

A landmark 18-acre township by Sattva Group on Devanahalli's airport corridor.

Where premium living meets the airport lifestyle

Sattva Park Cubix is an 18-acre integrated township developed by Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva), positioned directly opposite the KRN Complex on Devanahalli's Airport Road — minutes from Kempegowda International Airport. Comprising 1,608 homes across multiple towers, the township uses a two-side open design for superior cross-ventilation and natural light.

With approximately 79% of the 18-acre parcel kept as open, green and landscaped space around 2 large central parks, Park Cubix offers rare breathing room within an urban township. Phase 1 is Ready to Occupy — ideal for end-users seeking immediate possession — while Phase 2 is currently in its pre-launch stage, offering early-bird opportunities for investors.
