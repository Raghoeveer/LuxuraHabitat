Sattva Park Cubix Overview

The lowest-priced ready township from a branded developer on Devanahalli's airport corridor.

A ready airport-corridor home at a corridor-low price

Sattva Park Cubix is an 18-acre township in Devanahalli Town, on NH 207 opposite KRN Complex, built by Salarpuria Sattva Group. It holds approximately 1,620 apartments in basement + ground + 14 floor towers, arranged around the edge of the site and a large central courtyard. Homes range from 436 sq ft studios to 1,498 sq ft 3 BHKs in Phase 1, and up to 1,702 sq ft in Phase 2.

The project launched in September 2017 at about ₹3,990 per sq ft. Phase 1 was completed in December 2022 and now resells at ₹6,890 to ₹8,050 per sq ft. That is still 35% to 45% below branded Devanahalli projects, a discount that reflects specific, known utility issues covered openly further down this page. Phase 2 is under construction for possession between late 2028 and late 2029.

The three recurring complaints

1. Water arrives by tanker

Phase 1 has no functional borewell and no municipal connection. Roughly 810 occupied homes depend on private water tankers, and tanker rates move with the monsoon and with local supply control. This is a running cost every owner and tenant shares.

Ask the association for three years of tanker spending and the current status of the borewell or Cauvery connection.

2. Electricity at commercial rates

The complex draws power through a high-tension connection that the developer still manages after handover. Residents are billed at commercial tariffs and cannot access state residential subsidies such as Gruha Jyothi. Over a long hold the difference adds up.

Ask a resident in a similar-sized flat for an actual electricity bill.

3. Monsoon seepage

Red-brick masonry without adequate waterproofing has caused wall dampness inside some units and waterlogging in floor lobbies during heavy rain.

Visit during heavy rain and inspect the unit, the floor lobby and the basement.

What residents like

Open space and a working clubhouse

The open space is real, and the clubhouse, pools, tennis, basketball and squash courts, walking tracks and creche all run day to day.

About Salarpuria Sattva Group

Founded in 1986, headquartered in Bengaluru since 1993, with over 48 million sq ft delivered.

Salarpuria Sattva Group, now branded Sattva Group, was founded in Kolkata in 1986 and set up its Bengaluru headquarters in 1993. It has delivered over 48 million sq ft of commercial office parks, retail centres and residential communities across India, and is better known in Bengaluru for commercial and IT park development than for high-density housing.

Park Cubix is the group's main residential project in the Devanahalli micro-market. It launched in September 2017 and is being built in two phases on a single 18-acre master plan. The developer still controls the high-tension electricity connection serving the complex, which continues to affect residents' power bills after handover.

Sattva is building several other projects on the same corridor, including Sattva City, Sattva Aeropolis and Sattva Vasanta Skye.
