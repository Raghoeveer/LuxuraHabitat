About Brigade Banashankari

A large, high-rise Brigade community in one of South Bangalore's best-established residential neighbourhoods.

A rare large land parcel in Banashankari

Brigade Banashankari is an upcoming premium apartment project by Brigade Group in Banashankari 5th Stage, South Bangalore. On 24 September 2025 Brigade announced that it had signed a joint development agreement for about 7.5 acres here, with an estimated gross development value of about ₹1,200 crore. Brochure graphics place the site off Dr. Vishnuvardhan Road, between Kanakapura Road and the Outer Ring Road.

Banashankari is one of Bangalore's original BDA-planned neighbourhoods, known for wide roads, temples, parks, good schools and long-settled families. Most homes here are independent houses and smaller apartment buildings, so a 7.5-acre gated high-rise community with resort-style amenities is genuinely scarce supply in this part of the city.

The project brief describes residential towers rising above twenty floors, over 75% of the land kept as open space, landscaped green spaces across the community and a clubhouse with multiple lifestyle amenities. It lists 2 BHK homes of 1,200 to 1,350 sq ft, 3 BHK homes with three toilets of 1,700 to 2,000 sq ft and a 3 BHK Large of 1,800 to 2,000 sq ft, with possession planned for December 2029. Other material in circulation shows larger 3.5, 4 and 4.5 BHK homes and a later possession date, which we set out side by side in the source comparison below.

For our independent view on price, risks and fit, read the Brigade Banashankari review, and for the bigger picture on the neighbourhood see Is Banashankari 5th Stage a good place to buy a flat? You can also compare the wider corridor on our Kanakapura Road area guide.

The Investment Case & Trade-offs

What supports Brigade Banashankari as a home or an investment, and what to weigh before you register interest.

Why the location works Banashankari is a mature, BDA-planned neighbourhood with schools, hospitals, temples and markets already in place, close to JP Nagar and Jayanagar. Large land parcels are rare here, so a 7.5-acre branded gated community stands out against older, smaller apartment buildings.
Infrastructure already in place The Green Line metro along Kanakapura Road is running, with Doddakallasandra about 3 km away, and the Outer Ring Road and NICE Road junction are within 4 to 6 km. Unlike many peripheral launches, you are not buying on the promise of future roads.
Trade-off 1: a premium over local resale Resale listings put a Banashankari 5th Stage 2 BHK at about ₹55 to ₹90 lakh and a 3 BHK at about ₹90 lakh to ₹1.40 crore, and inner-south Bangalore at roughly ₹6,000 to ₹10,500 per sq ft. At about ₹12,200 to ₹14,400 per sq ft, you are paying for the brand, amenities and newness, so resale comparables will not support the price for some years.
Trade-off 2: pre-launch uncertainty There is no RERA number yet, sources disagree on the unit mix and tower count, and possession is quoted anywhere from December 2029 to December 2031. Keep any expression of interest refundable and commit only after the RERA filing confirms approvals, sizes, completion date and the final cost sheet.

Brigade Group was founded in 1986 by M.R. Jaishankar, its Founder and Chairman, and is headquartered in Bengaluru. Over nearly four decades it has completed more than 275 projects covering over 80 million sq ft across Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mysuru, Kochi and other cities, spanning apartments, villas, offices, malls, hotels and schools. Its best-known developments include Brigade Gateway, Brigade Exotica, Brigade Orchards, Orion Mall and the World Trade Center Bengaluru, and it partners with hotel brands such as Sheraton, Grand Mercure and Holiday Inn.

Announcing the Banashankari project, Brigade Enterprises Managing Director Pavitra Shankar said it aims to deliver thoughtfully designed homes that enhance urban living. The project is part of a residential pipeline of about 12.6 million sq ft across Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad. On the same South Bangalore corridor, Brigade is also developing Brigade Silvercrest in Raghuvanahalli, off Kanakapura Road.
