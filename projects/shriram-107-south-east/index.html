Project Overview & Master Plan Details

A 22-tower community by Shriram Properties, built across three phases in Attibele, South East Bangalore.

A large-format community 15 minutes from Electronic City

Shriram 107 SouthEast is a 22-tower, 2,200+ unit residential community spread across 19 acres in Attibele, off Hosur Road in South East Bangalore. Developed by Shriram Properties in joint venture with Garden City Realty, the project is built in three phases. Phase 1 ("Dil Chahta Hai") and Phase 2 ("Dil Chahta Hai Dobara") have both already received their Occupancy Certificate and are ready to move in, giving prospective buyers a rare chance to see the actual delivered product before booking into the currently-selling Phase 3.

Phase 3, branded Shriram 107 SouthEast, is under construction with a G+14 elevation over 2 basement levels and an expected handover of January 2027. The layout keeps 78% of the 19-acre footprint as open space, anchored by an 18,000 sq.ft (G+2) clubhouse with a skating rink and lake-facing amenity zones. The Investment Advantage

Why Attibele's position on the Electronic City South corridor matters for buyers and investors.

Why Invest In Attibele, South East Bangalore Attibele sits just off Hosur Road, roughly 15 minutes from Electronic City via the E-City Elevated Flyover, putting one of Bangalore's largest IT employment hubs within a genuinely short commute while still offering an entry price meaningfully below what similar-sized homes cost inside Electronic City itself. The corridor is also flanked by established industrial hubs at Attibele, Bommasandra-Jigni and Hosur, which adds a second, non-IT source of rental and resale demand that many purely IT-driven micro-markets don't have.
A Track Record Already on the Ground Unlike a first-of-its-kind launch on an unproven corridor, Shriram 107 SouthEast's Phase 1 and Phase 2 have already received their Occupancy Certificate and are ready to move in on the very same 19-acre site. That gives a Phase 3 buyer something rare in an under-construction purchase: the ability to walk the completed phases, see the finished clubhouse and landscaping in person, and judge the developer's actual delivered quality rather than relying on renders alone.
Rental & Resale Demand Drivers With Electronic City's IT workforce, the Bommasandra-Jigni and Hosur industrial belts, and Attibele's own industrial hub all within commuting distance, Shriram 107 SouthEast is positioned to draw rental demand from more than one employment segment. As the E-City and Bommasandra Metro stations move from under-construction to operational, connectivity into this corridor is only expected to improve further, a factor worth weighing into a longer-term hold.
