About Sattva City

A landmark township by Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group) on Doddajala's most coveted growth corridor

Where Premium Living Meets the Airport Lifestyle

Sattva City is a 50-acre integrated township by Sattva Group, the Bengaluru developer that began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva. It sits at Meenakunte Hosur, Doddajala, on Airport Road, opposite the KRN Complex and about 1 km from the Sadahalli toll plaza. Kempegowda International Airport is roughly 8 km away, about 15 minutes off-peak.

The township has 3,460 apartments in 13 high-rise towers, each with two basements, a ground floor and 16 to 21 residential floors (2B+G+16 to 2B+G+21). Homes run from a 1,161 sq ft 1 BHK to 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK layouts and a 6,266 sq ft duplex penthouse. About 84% of the land is kept as open, landscaped space around two large central parks.

Sattva City was RERA-registered on 27 February 2026, formally launched on 1 March 2026 and is under construction, with RERA possession set for February 2032. Sattva's latest launch brochure lists 2, 3 and 4 BHK luxury homes from ₹1.79 Cr, with prices going up to about ₹3.67 Cr for a 4 BHK, and introduces a staggered 20:5 payment plan.

Market Outlook: What Channel Partners Are Projecting

The investment case as it is being pitched in 2026 market reports. These are projections, not guarantees.

🏦 Developer Credit Rating

Market reports cite a CRISIL AA/Stable rating upgrade for Sattva Group in late 2025, and present it as a sign of strong liquidity and execution certainty for a long build like this one.

🚗 Before-Toll Location

Because the township sits before the Sadahalli toll plaza, daily commuters to Hebbal or Manyata avoid the toll. Channel partners estimate the saving at about ₹35,000 to ₹50,000 a year in toll fees and idling fuel, against ₹110+ per round trip for projects beyond the toll.

🌳 Open-Space Premium

With 84% open space against the 60% to 65% typical of Devanahalli projects, reports project a resale premium of around 15% over high-density neighbours by 2030. They also claim the green cover keeps the site 2 to 3°C cooler.

💧 Lake-View Units

Units overlooking Doddajala Lake are said to be selling fastest. Reports claim water-view homes in Bengaluru have historically appreciated about 12% faster than garden-facing units.

🚇 Metro Effect

With Doddajala Metro Station expected around late 2026 or early 2027, channel partners project a 10% to 15% jump in capital values once trial runs begin, and about 15% over the next 24 months.

🏠 Rental Demand & Area Prices

Reports project rental yields of about 4.2% against a Bengaluru average of 3.2%, with demand expected from ITC (opposite the site), Northgate Office Park and employees of firms such as Boeing, Salesforce and Foxconn. Doddajala apartment rates are quoted as rising from about ₹5,500 per sq ft in 2021 to ₹7,800 to ₹8,500 in 2026, with 12% to 18% annual growth expected.

These figures come from channel-partner and market reports and have not been independently verified by Luxura Habitat. Our independent Sattva City review uses more conservative numbers, including a 3% to 3.6% gross rental yield.

Sattva City (sometimes listed as Sattva Hamlet City) is a 50-acre integrated township in Doddajala, North Bengaluru, developed by Sattva Group, which began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva. It has 3,460 apartments in 13 high-rise towers, with 1 BHK to 4 BHK homes and duplex penthouses, 84% open space, 4 clubhouses and 200+ amenities.

Sattva City is at Meenakunte Hosur, Doddajala, on Airport Road in North Bengaluru, opposite the KRN Complex and about 1 km from the Sadahalli toll plaza. Devanahalli town is about 5 km away and Kempegowda International Airport about 8 km.
