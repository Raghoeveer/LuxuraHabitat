About Sattva City

A landmark township by Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group) on Doddajala's most coveted growth corridor

Where Premium Living Meets the Airport Lifestyle

Sattva City is an 50-acre integrated township developed by the reputed Sattva Group, ideally positioned in Doddajala — the fastest-growing residential and commercial hub of North Bengaluru. Situated directly opposite the KRN Complex and within minutes of Kempegowda International Airport, this address delivers unmatched convenience for frequent flyers, professionals, and families alike.

Comprising 3,460 thoughtfully configured 2, 3 & 4 BHK homes spread across multiple towers, Park City embraces two-side open design for superior cross-ventilation and natural light. With approximately 84% of the land kept as open, green, and landscaped areas, residents enjoy rare breathing room within an urban township.

Sattva City is a New Launch project and is currently Under Construction. As a single-phase development, it offers an exclusive early-bird opportunity for investors and homebuyers seeking premium real estate in the airport corridor.

Everything you need to know about Sattva City, Doddajala

Sattva City (also commonly referred to as Sattva Hamlet City) is a premium 50-acre integrated residential township developed by Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group), one of Bengaluru's most trusted and established real estate developers. Located in Doddajala, North Bengaluru, the project offers 3,460 apartments in 2, 3 & 4 BHK configurations across multiple towers with 84% open space, 4 grand clubhouses, and 200+ lifestyle amenities.

Sattva City is located in Doddajala, North Bengaluru — situated directly opposite the KRN Complex, on Airport Road (NH-207). The project is in the heart of the Doddajala Growth Corridor, just minutes from Kempegowda International Airport, making it one of the most strategically positioned residential communities in Bengaluru.

Sattva City offers 2, 3 & 4 BHK apartments in the following size ranges:
