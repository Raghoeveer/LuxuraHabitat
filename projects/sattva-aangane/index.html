Project Overview: Sattva Aangane, Old Madras Road

A new expression of expansive living by Sattva Group, currently at the pre-launch, EOI stage.

Expansive Living, Codename Courtyard

Sattva Aangane is Sattva Group's newest residential development on Old Madras Road, Bengaluru, currently marketed under the pre-launch codename "Courtyard." Spread across 10 acres with 80% open space and a 2-acre central green, the project is designed around three-side open residences with zero shared walls, giving every home better light, ventilation and privacy than a typical shared-wall layout.

The project offers 3 & 4 BHK homes, including signature duplex residences, across multiple towers, each with its own sky-level amenity deck. A 40,000 sq.ft clubhouse anchors the community, alongside a 25m lap pool, tennis and pickleball courts, badminton and squash courts, yoga and wellness spaces, and dedicated pet park and kids' play areas. As with any pre-launch project, RERA registration, possession timelines and final pricing are still being finalised by the developer; verify current status independently before booking, and enquire below for the latest details.
