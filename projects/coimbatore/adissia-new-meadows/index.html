Project Overview: 225 Plots in Kovaipudur

A DTCP & RERA-approved, Ready-to-Construct plotted community by Adissia Developers in Kovaipudur, Coimbatore.

DTCP & RERA-Approved Plots, Ready to Construct

Adissia New Meadows is a premium, DTCP & RERA-approved plotted residential community by Adissia Developers Pvt. Ltd., spread across 11.25 acres in Kovaipudur, one of Coimbatore's more peaceful southern suburbs, known for its scenic views of the Western Ghats. The layout is planned across Phase I and Phase II, with 225 freehold residential plots in four indicative sizes, a grand entrance arch, blacktop internal roads, dedicated Open Space Reservation (OSR) and park zones, and 12+ lifestyle amenities.

Unlike a pre-launch project, Adissia New Meadows carries an active RERA registration (TN/11/LO/1533/2026) and is at Ready to Construct status — meaning internal infrastructure such as roads, drainage and rainwater harvesting is already in place, and plots are available for immediate registration and construction. As with any land purchase, buyers should independently verify the RERA registration, DTCP layout approval and title documents for their specific plot before booking.
