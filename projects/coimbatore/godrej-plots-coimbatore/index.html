Freehold Plots Opposite Coimbatore Golf Club

Godrej Plots Coimbatore is a pre-launch plotted development by Godrej Properties, planned across 44 acres in Chettipalayam, South Coimbatore, directly opposite the well-established Coimbatore Golf Club. The layout is designed around 700+ freehold residential plots in four indicative typologies (Cottage, Bungalow, Mansion and Estate), giving buyers the freedom to design and build at their own pace within a gated, professionally planned community.

Land acquisition for the project was announced in March 2026, and the Expression of Interest (EOI) window has been open since July 2026. The official commercial launch is expected in mid-to-late 2026, once final DTCP and TNRERA approvals are in place; TNRERA registration is currently in progress. As with any pre-launch project, treat published imagery, layouts and timelines as indicative until the developer's own construction-linked disclosures catch up, and verify current status directly before making any payment. The Investment Advantage

Why South Coimbatore and the Golf Club micro-market matter for buyers and investors.

An Established, High-Income Micro-Market Coimbatore Golf Club memberships typically run ₹15-20 Lakhs and see very high weekend occupancy, reflecting the affluence of this South Coimbatore corridor. Coimbatore is also widely regarded as one of Tamil Nadu's highest per-capita-income cities, with many traditional textile and mill-owning families now diversifying into real estate.
A Growing Employment & Logistics Base Larsen & Toubro operates a campus of roughly 2,000 acres in the region, and Coimbatore has emerged as a major logistics and warehousing hub after Chennai, with Amazon and Flipkart facilities generating employment for nearly 30,000 people, a durable driver of housing and land demand.
Infrastructure Tailwinds A major airport expansion, including Terminal 2, is planned across roughly 630 acres along the Avinashi Road corridor, and a proposed international cricket stadium would become Tamil Nadu's second such venue outside Chennai, both meaningful long-term catalysts for this corridor.
A Trusted, Listed Developer Godrej Properties is part of the Godrej Group and one of India's leading listed real estate developers, marking its first plotted development in Coimbatore. A recognisable, listed developer name is a meaningful risk reducer for buyers evaluating a pre-launch plotted project.
