Project Overview: A British-Themed Plotted Address on the Avinashi Road Belt

168 DTCP & TNRERA-approved villa plots on 9.1 acres in Neelambur, Coimbatore, by Adissia Developers.

More Than Another Plotted Layout

Adissia Regal Arch is a fully gated villa plot community that Adissia Developers Pvt. Ltd. has built around a single idea: a British-inspired streetscape you would normally only find in a high-end villa project, offered as open plots on which you build your own home. The entrance is a grand arch with arched windows and lantern lamp posts, the common areas are laid out as formal clipped lawns and lamp-lit avenues, and the layout is planned for 168 plots across 9.1 acres, roughly 18 to 19 plots per acre before roads and open spaces.

The location is the other half of the story. Neelambur sits on the eastern edge of Coimbatore where Avinashi Road meets NH-544 and the L&T bypass, which puts Coimbatore International Airport about 15 minutes away, KPR Tech Park about 9 minutes away, and Peelamedu and the city's IT corridor within easy reach. It is also the city's gateway to the Coimbatore to Tiruppur industrial corridor, which gives the area a deep base of textile, engineering and manufacturing employment alongside the IT parks.

The layout is DTCP approved and registered with Tamil Nadu RERA under TN/11/Layout/0852/2026, dated 4 March 2026. It is at the pre-launch stage: the developer is quoting from ₹23 Lakh per cent, plots are registered within 15 days of booking (or on loan sanction), and the official master plan is marked "coming soon". As with any plot purchase, verify the RERA registration, DTCP approval and title documents for your specific plot before booking.

The Investment Case, and the Risks

Why buyers are looking at plots on the Avinashi Road and NH-544 belt, and what to weigh before booking at pre-launch.

Coimbatore's Most Connected Growth Corridor Avinashi Road is Coimbatore's main east-west spine, linking the city centre, Peelamedu, the airport and the IT clusters before continuing as NH-544 towards Tiruppur and Salem. Neelambur sits at the point where that corridor meets the bypass, so a plot here is within a short drive of the airport, the eastern tech parks and the industrial belt at the same time. Our analysis of Coimbatore's market found that the Avinashi Road belt's growth has been driven by employment access and road infrastructure rather than transit speculation, which is covered in Coimbatore Metro Rejected Again: What It Means for Property Investors.
Employment From Two Directions Most growth corridors depend on one kind of employer. Neelambur draws on two: IT and services through KPR Tech Park, Sri Sakthi Tech Park and the Peelamedu corridor, and manufacturing through the Coimbatore to Tiruppur textile and engineering belt along NH-544. A diversified employment base usually supports steadier rental and resale demand for homes built on these plots.
Pre-Launch Pricing, With Pre-Launch Risk The developer quotes a pre-launch price from ₹23 Lakh per cent and compares it with a stated market price of ₹30 Lakh per cent. Treat that gap as the developer's claim, not a guaranteed gain, and compare it with recent registered values in Neelambur. Pre-launch also means the master plan is not yet published and the timeline for completing layout roads and amenities is not stated, so get both in writing in your booking agreement.
Plots or Apartments? A villa plot gives you land ownership, design freedom and no building depreciation, but no rental income until you build. If you are weighing a plot here against a ready apartment, our framework will help: Plots vs Apartments: Which Is the Better Investment in Coimbatore?
