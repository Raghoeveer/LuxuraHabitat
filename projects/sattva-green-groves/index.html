Project Overview

A RERA-approved plotted development for buyers who want to build their own home in a 45-acre master-planned community.

Own the Land in a 45-Acre Master-Planned Community

Sattva Green Groves is a premium plotted development by Sattva Group at Nelamangala, off Tumkur Road, roughly 500 metres from the highway and minutes from the Nelamangala Toll Booth — the entry point to Bengaluru from the Tumkur Road corridor. Spread across approximately 45 acres with 735 RERA-registered plots, the layout is built for buyers who want full ownership freedom to design and build their own home, rather than a ready-made unit.

The layout's standout feature is its green infrastructure: 17 individually named parks and open zones threaded through the community, from a dedicated Birds Park and Urban Forest to a Fitness Park, Elders Park and Community Leisure & Pets Park.
