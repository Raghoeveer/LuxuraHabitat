Project Overview

A RERA-approved plotted development for buyers who want to build their own home in a 45-acre master-planned community.

Own the Land in a 45-Acre Master-Planned Community

Sattva Green Groves is a premium plotted development by Sattva Group at Nelamangala, off Tumkur Road, roughly 500 metres from the highway and minutes from the Nelamangala Toll Booth, the entry point to Bengaluru from the Tumkur Road corridor. Spread across approximately 45 acres with 735 RERA-registered plots, the layout is built for buyers who want full ownership freedom to design and build their own home, rather than a ready-made unit.

The layout's standout feature is its green infrastructure: 17 individually named parks and open zones threaded through the community, from a dedicated Birds Park and Urban Forest to a Fitness Park, Elders Park and Community Leisure & Pets Park. Sattva Group is a Bengaluru-headquartered developer that began in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva (its own site dates its current journey to 1993). It builds apartments, villas, plotted layouts and commercial space, and describes itself as CRISIL 'AA' Stable and ICRA 'AA' Stable rated, ISO 9001, 14001 and 18001 certified, and a founding member of the Indian Green Building Council.

Sattva Green Groves brings that expertise to the Tumkur Road / Nelamangala corridor as a RERA-registered plotted development, focused on wide roads, an extensive 17-park green network and a well-equipped ~25,000 Sq Ft clubhouse, built for buyers who want full ownership freedom on their own timeline.

The group's other plotted development in North Bangalore, Sattva Ekatra (Sattva Thippapura) in Doddaballapur, follows the same approach, giving buyers a second, verifiable Sattva Group plotted layout to compare before deciding.
