Welcome to Assetz Meru & Meadows

Welcome to Assetz Meru & Meadows, a premium residential development by Assetz Property Group on Kanakapura Main Road, near NICE Road, in South Bangalore. Why Invest in Assetz Meru & Meadows?

A high-rise twin-tower address from an established developer, on a corridor with genuine connectivity.

The Assetz Advantage

Assetz Property Group is an established Bangalore-based developer with a long-running presence in the city's residential and commercial real estate market. For buyers, a recognized developer name typically means more predictable construction quality, documentation standards and after-sales support — factors that matter as much as the floor plan itself.

Meru & Meadows applies that brand to a genuinely low-density high-rise format: just two towers across ~5 acres, with 87% of the land kept open. That's a deliberate trade-off — fewer towers, more green space — aimed at buyers who want a high-rise address without a crowded, wall-to-wall tower footprint.

Why Kanakapura Main Road

Kanakapura Main Road sits within a short drive of NICE Road, giving residents a bypass route around central Bangalore's congestion while staying connected to JP Nagar, Banashankari and Jayanagar's established schools, hospitals and retail.

As South Bangalore's inner core continues to see limited new land supply, corridors like Kanakapura Main Road are where new large-format, high-rise launches are increasingly concentrated — making early entry into a credible developer's project a reasonable long-term position for both end-users and investors.
