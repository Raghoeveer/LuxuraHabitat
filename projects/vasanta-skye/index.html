Sattva Vasanta Skye Overview

A landmark 16-acre township by Sattva Group on Devanahalli's Bellary Road growth corridor.

Premium Township Living on Bellary Road

Sattva Vasanta Skye is a 16-acre integrated township developed by Sattva Group, positioned in Devanahalli, North Bengaluru — directly opposite the National Public School (NPS) and adjacent to NH-44, approximately 10 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

The township comprises 1,077 two-side-open apartments across multiple towers, with approximately 80% of the land kept as open, landscaped green space around 2 large central parks. Based on the developer's pricing sheet, specification table and stamped tower floor plans, verified configurations span 1 BHK (~707 sq ft), 2 BHK (1,177-1,270 sq ft), 2.5 BHK (~1,186-1,213 sq ft), 3 BHK (1,483-1,937 sq ft, with select duplex units up to ~2,395 sq ft) and 4 BHK (2,100-2,623 sq ft) — a broader mix than the "2, 3 & 4 BHK" line used in some of the developer's own marketing copy.

Sattva Vasanta Skye is a new-launch project currently under construction in a single phase, offering an early-bird opportunity for homebuyers and investors on the Devanahalli airport corridor.

About Sattva Group

One of Bengaluru's most established developers, with a growing Devanahalli portfolio.

Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group) is one of Bengaluru's most trusted and established real estate developers, with a large portfolio of residential and commercial projects across the city. Sattva Vasanta Skye extends the developer's presence in the Devanahalli airport corridor, joining Sattva Aeropolis, Sattva City and Sattva Vasanta Cove as part of the group's growing footprint in North Bengaluru.

At 16 acres and 1,077 homes, Vasanta Skye is the largest of the developer's Devanahalli launches by unit count, built around 4 clubhouses and 2 central parks with a full 1 to 4 BHK unit mix — distinct from the smaller, Studio/1 BHK-focused Sattva Vasanta Cove nearby on the same Bellary Road corridor. Buyers researching Sattva's Devanahalli projects should confirm which specific project — and which RERA number — applies to any floor plan or price quoted.
