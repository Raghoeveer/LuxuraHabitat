Sattva Vasanta Skye Overview

A 16-acre Sattva Group township in Kannamangala Village, on Devanahalli's Bellary Road growth corridor.

Premium Township Living on Bellary Road

Sattva Vasanta Skye is a new residential township by Sattva Group in Kannamangala Village, Devanahalli, on Bellary Road near Kempegowda International Airport. The project covers 16 acres and offers Studio, 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartments, which makes it one of the few Devanahalli launches where a first-time buyer and a large family can choose from the same township.

The layout places 1,077 homes in G+13 and G+14 towers around a landscaped central court, with roughly 80% of the land kept open. Based on the developer's Block and Wing floor plans, super built-up areas run from 454 sq ft for a Studio to 2,416 sq ft for the largest 4 BHK. The project is RERA approved under PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/080525/007730, prices start at ₹78 Lakhs onwards, and the expected possession date is May 2031, which gives buyers time to plan their finances around a construction-linked schedule.

Homes are planned for natural light, cross ventilation and efficient use of space, and the sample apartment interiors are shown in the gallery below.

About Sattva Group

One of Bengaluru's most established developers, with a growing Devanahalli portfolio.

Sattva Group (formerly Salarpuria Sattva Group), founded in 1986, is one of Bengaluru's most trusted real estate developers, with a large portfolio of residential and commercial projects across the city. Sattva Vasanta Skye extends the developer's presence in the Devanahalli airport corridor, joining Sattva Aeropolis, Sattva City and Sattva Vasanta Cove in North Bengaluru.

At 16 acres and 1,077 homes, Vasanta Skye is the largest of these Devanahalli launches by unit count, with a full Studio to 4 BHK mix. Sattva Vasanta Cove, nearby on the same Bellary Road corridor, is a much smaller Studio and 1 BHK project with its own RERA number, so confirm which project any floor plan or price quote refers to.
